# FIGURE 7.20 The X Configuration screen

![](bbox=[0.301, 0.143, 0.803, 0.467])

The installation program uses Xconfiguration to probe and determine the type of video card and monitor on the system. If Xconfiguration cannot identify your type of video card or monitor, it displays a list of hardware from which you can choose. Hundreds of cards and monitors are listed; scroll down the list to find your video card and monitor. If your monitor is not listed, you may nevertheless to configure X. Select Unlisted Card, and specify the characteristics of your video card. Alternatively, select Custom Monitor, and specify the horizontal and vertical sync ranges of your monitor.

You can test the configuration to see that it works. To do so, click This Configuration. You can also select a custom resolution or color depth. To do so, click Customize X Configuration.

Enable the Use Graphical Login box to cause Linux to display an X login when the system starts. If you prefer a text-based login, disable the Use Graphical Login box.

If you cannot configure X or you prefer not to try, you can postpone the configuration by selecting Skip X Configuration.
