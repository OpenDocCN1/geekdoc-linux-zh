# Taking Red Hat Courses

One of the most effective ways to prepare for RHCE certification is to take the appropriate Red Hat courses. This approach is particularly attractive if your employer will cover all or most of the cost of courses. However, as Table 1.1, if you're new to Unix and Linux, this approach can be quite expensive. And, unless you live near one of Red Hat's training locations, you must add the cost of travel, lodging, and food to the already considerable cost of the courses. For many, the time and cost of taking courses is prohibitive. Fortunately, other options exist.

TABLE 1.1 Approximate Tuition for Red Hat Courses

| Course | Tuition |
|---|--|
| RH033 | $1,998 |
| RH133 | $2,098 |
| RH253 | $2,198 |
| RH300 | $2,498 |
| Total | $8,792 |

In the U.S., you may be able to deduct some or all of the costs of taking certification courses from your taxable income. In some cases, you may even be eligible for a tax credit. Consult your tax advisor for details.
