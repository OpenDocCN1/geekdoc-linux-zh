The TCP/IP Family of Protocols TCP/IP can be thought of as consisting of four protocol layers: network access, Internet, transport, and application. The most important Internet protocols are IP and ICMP. The most important transport protocols are UDP and TCP.

TCP/IP Services are associated with ports, which can be privileged or nonprivileged. Certain ports are well-known and listed in the file /etc/services. Services can be provided by the kernel or by a daemon, which can run continuously or be started on demand by the inet service. You can control Red Hat Linux TCP/IP services by using scripts that reside in /etc/rc.d/init.d.
