To help get the library started, iPodLibrary.com already offers a couple of podBooks for downloading:

+   * *iKnow F1*. This book provides all kinds of info about Formula One racing, including historical rules changes, listings of race winners and other title holders, and even a Formula One glossary.
+   * *Apple_Bytes*. If you’re a fan of Apple history and trivia, you’ll get a kick out of this book. It offers a vast array of info, useful and otherwise about Apple, including listings of code names, product timelines, and even the legendary story of the Dogcow.
