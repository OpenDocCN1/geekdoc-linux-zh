# Share Your iPod with a Splitter

If you're traveling with friends or family, or you have two children who actually like the same music, you can look into getting a special plug that lets you connect your iPod's headphone jack to more than one set of headphones or ear buds. These devices are called splitters or Y-connectors. XtremeMac makes one, the iShare Earbud Splitter, that comes in iPod white. The iShare has a well-made look, with gold connectors for good sound quality. See www.xtrememac.com for more information. The iShare sells for about $13.
