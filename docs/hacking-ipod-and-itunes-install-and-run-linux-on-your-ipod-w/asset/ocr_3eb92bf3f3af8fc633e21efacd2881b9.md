Wow, it's so shiny! There's lots of cool stuff to see, and plenty to click. You can click the feature boxes at the top, any of the tiny album covers, or the items in the various lists, such as names of artists, albums, and songs. If you wonder whether some text on the screen is a link, just point the mouse at it. If you see an underline when the cursor is hovering, it's a link. The store's home page includes five small windows labeled New Releases, Exclusives, Pre-Releases, Just Added, and Staff Favorites. Each of these windows shows four albums, but they all scroll to reveal more when you click the arrows on the left or right. If you would rather all the albums in the window at once rather than watching them scroll past, click the See All link at the top right corner of the miniature window.

Take a look at the navigation controls at the top of the window. Use the cute little picture of a house to get back to the store's home page any time. The arrows pointing right and left are back and forward buttons for the store. They're pretty handy—use them the same way you do in a Web browser.
