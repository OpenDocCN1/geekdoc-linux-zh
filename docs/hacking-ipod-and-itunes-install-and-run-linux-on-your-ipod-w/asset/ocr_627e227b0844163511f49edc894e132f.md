# XPlay

Another neat program for Windows users is XPlay, from Mediafour Corporation (www.mediafour.com/products/xplay/). XPlay has the extra added wrinkle of working with Mac-formatted iPods as well as those connected to Windows computers. XPlay has a "Copy To" button in its toolbar that lets you move music from your iPod to your hard disk. XPlay sells for $30, and there's a free test drive version available.
