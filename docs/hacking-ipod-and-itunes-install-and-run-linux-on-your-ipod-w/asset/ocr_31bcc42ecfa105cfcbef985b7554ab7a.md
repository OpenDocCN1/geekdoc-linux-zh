# ## Get News, Weather, Movie Listings and More

**Note**  This hack works on Macintosh computers only.

Pod2Go by Kevin Wojniak ([kainjow.com/pod2go/website/]) is a brilliant gem of a program for your iPod. iPod2Go grabs news and other information from the Internet and your Mac and copies it to your iPod. Then, when you're on the train, sitting at lunch, or waiting for your bail bondsman to arrive, you can read what you've downloaded.
