+   1. Click the Search button in the toolbar. The Search Companion appears on the left side of the window, usually accompanied by that eager dog (which you can change to a woman in a hovercar, by the way, but that’s a hack for a different book).
+   2. Near the bottom of the Search Companion area, click “Change preferences”.
+   3. Click “Change files and folders search behavior”.
+   4. Click the “Advanced” button, and then click OK. This will take you back to the main Search screen.
