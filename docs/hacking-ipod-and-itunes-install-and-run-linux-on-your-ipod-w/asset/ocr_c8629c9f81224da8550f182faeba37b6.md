PocketMac iPod Edition on the Mac works by installing itself as an iSync component. PocketMac puts an icon on the iSync toolbar. When you click the icon, its settings appear, and you can set sync preferences, just as with other iSync devices (Figure 4-22).

PocketMac for Macintosh isn’t all about Entourage. You can also use it to sync e-mail from Apple’s Mail program, PDFs, and Stickies. And because PocketMac works in iSync, you don’t have to remember to run yet another application to get everything synchronized.
