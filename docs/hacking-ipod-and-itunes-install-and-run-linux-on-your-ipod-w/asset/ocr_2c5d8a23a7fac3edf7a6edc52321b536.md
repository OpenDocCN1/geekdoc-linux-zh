# Read and Write iPod "books"

Yes, it's true that you're reading an iPod book, and there are several other iPod books out there for your enjoyment. But the folks at iPodLibrary.com are talking about a different kind of iPod book: the kind that lives on your iPod. The creators of iPodLibrary.com are looking for people to create and submit what they call "funky little podBooks": collections of interesting content that you can carry around with you on your favorite music player (see Figure 2-18).
