Linux on your iPod is certainly cool, but you can still use it the regular way, too. To put your iPod into its familiar mode, just reboot normally, without holding down any buttons while it’s starting up. To get back to Linux, hold down Previous as the iPod boots.

In Linux, you can make things happen on the iPod by using the command-line tool `telnet` to talk to your iPod over the FireWire cable.
