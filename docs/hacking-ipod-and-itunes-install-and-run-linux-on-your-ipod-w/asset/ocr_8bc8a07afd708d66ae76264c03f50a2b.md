# ## Summary

Adding AppleScripts to iTunes can make your music listening and management a lot smoother, especially for tasks like cleaning out duplicates and dead tracks. If you want something to look at while you're listening to great music, the Visualizer has an almost infinite variety of nifty graphics to see. And if you're a programmer type, or you want to be, you can learn how to create your own scripts and visual plug-ins for iTunes.
