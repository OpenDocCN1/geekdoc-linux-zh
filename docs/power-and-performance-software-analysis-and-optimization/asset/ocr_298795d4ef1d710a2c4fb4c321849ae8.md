Donald Knuth ethos this sentiment, his numbers are slightly different, with 90% of the time spent in 10% of the code. This task is not as straightforward as it may be, described in Chapters 1–3. Modern hardware and software platforms are far too complicated for a programmer to reliably estimate where 10% or 20% resides. In the first chapter, the concept of premature optimization was defined to be an optimization conducted without performing the necessary analysis to actually locate these areas. In order to isolate these areas, and avoid premature optimization, system data must be collected and interpreted, a process referred to as profiling.
