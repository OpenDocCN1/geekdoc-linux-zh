+   - Desktop environments can make your life easier— they automate some of the routine day-to-day tasks and provide a friendly face for some of the more difficult ones. If your system doesn't have KDE, don't fret—chances are that either KDE can be installed on it (if you're nice enough to your system administrator), or it already comes with a nice desktop environment with comparable capabilities.
