+   - **uptime**—The uptime command provides a summary of information about the state of your operating system, including the length of time it has been online, the number of users logged in, and the average load on your system for the past 15 minutes.
+   
+   - **who**—who enables you to see all the users who are logged in to your computer, the date that they connected, and from what network address they are connected. If your machine seems slow, you might want to use who to find out where all the processor time is going.
+   
+   - **Other utilities**—There are far too many utilities on the system to cover them all in this book. Luckily, the man pages provide extensive documentation for most programs on the system. In addition, KDE has its own comprehensive documentation system.
