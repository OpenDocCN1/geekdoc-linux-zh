Influence of some important process criterions like duty cycle, open voltage, electrode polarity and current were analyzed. Nadaliman and Khan [32] described the properties of Cu-TaC (two compositions with TaC 30% and 55% wt.) tool electrodes fabricated by powder metallurgy method. Density, electrical and thermal conductivity of the sintered and green compacted electrodes was measured and compared. The electrical conductivity, thermal conductivity and density for the green pellet was measured in the range of 94.96-189.92 $\Omega$'m-1, 29.70-33.20 W/m K and 6.13-9.80 g/cm3 respectively which were more than for the sintered pellets. The most conductive green pellet had 30% TaC.
