# **Thermally Enhanced AJM**

*Patel et al.* [19] found that there were some materials like Inconel 718 and Titanium (Ti6Al4V) that were not easily machinable; such materials were needed to have low hardness so that the abrasives can penetrate the surface thus causing the erosion to take place. For this there was some thermal assistance provided by heating the workpiece up to a temperature of 850°C by using an oxy Acetylene gas flame, thus resulting in lower hardness on the surface and increased MRR using abrasive water jet machining. Though there was a drastic change in the temperature during the process, but no stresses and crack were discovered in the machined part.
