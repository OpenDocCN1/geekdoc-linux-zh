# WIRE ELECTRIC DISCHARGE MACHINING (WEDM)

While the material removal mechanisms of EDM and WEDM are similar, their functional characteristics are not identical [8]. In WEDM process electrical sparks are produced between a thin, travelling wire which acts as the tool electrode and the workpiece to erode the material in order to generate desired shape.
