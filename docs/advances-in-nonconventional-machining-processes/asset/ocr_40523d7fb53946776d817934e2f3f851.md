# # FABRICATION OF ELECTRODES USING ALTERNATE APPROACHES

The physical properties of electrode influence the machining performance. High electrical conductivity, high specific heat, good machinability, high erosion resistance etc. are the properties which an ideal electrode must possess. A single material cannot fulfill all these requirements. Traditional machining methods are mostly used for fabricating the electrodes for EDM [38]. Complexity of the geometry and the precision required commonly determines the manufacturing cost of the electrodes.
