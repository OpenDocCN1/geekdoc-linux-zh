# **Principle of Laser Beam Machining**

When a laser beam is focused through a lens on workpiece, the heat energy is absorbed which melts the selected surface, melts it, vaporizes it instantaneously and penetrates it. Thus, it can also be called a thermal cutting process as shown in Fig. (1).
