# Use of Cryogenic in AJM

It has been discussed earlier that the softer metals cannot be easily machined having precise dimension using abrasive in AJM. Due to the small abrasives having high kinetic energy and high velocity penetrate the surface especially when hitting normal to the surface. Thus, it is found that a large amount of material cannot be machined having ductile or soft material properties by the same principle as of brittle or hard material erosion is carried out. Thus, to work on such materials cryogenics has been introduced so that the material removal mechanism can somehow be changed.
