# Types of Laser Drilling

There are two types of laser beam drilling process present and these are percussion and trepan drilling. Percussion drilling punches directly through the workpiece material with no relative movement of the laser or workpiece. Trepan drilling involves cutting around the circumference of the hole to be generated as shown in Fig. (2).
