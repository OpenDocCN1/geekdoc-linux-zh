> *Give a man a fish and you feed him for a day. Teach him to use the Net and he won't bother you for weeks.*

Everything is connected to the Internet, even refrigerators.¹ And of course the big buzz phrase in the technology biz is the “Internet of Things,” or IoT. So it is time to turn our attention to network programming in the embedded space. Linux, as a Unix derivative, has extensive support for networking.
