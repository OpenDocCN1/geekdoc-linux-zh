BusyBox is highly modular and configurable. While it is capable of implementing over 300 hundred shell commands, by no means are you required to have all of them in your system. The configuration process lets you choose exactly which commands will be included in your system. Table 14.1 is the full list of commands available in recent releases of BusyBox.
