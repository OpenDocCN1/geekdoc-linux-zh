# Lesson 9 Follow-up

In this lesson, you customized the Bash shell and performed various operations in it. You also worked with shell scripts, redirected input and output in shells, and used control statements in scripts to automate repetitive tasks. This will enable you to efficiently perform your job as a system administrator.

+   1. Why do you use variables in scripts?

Answers will vary, but may include: variables are used to define changeable values, read user-defined input, and customize the shell environment.

+   2. What is the most prominent feature of the Bash shell? Explain.

Answers will vary, but may include: command history—for its ability to recall recent com-mands, tab completion—for its ability to complete filenames and commands, or command line shortcuts—because they save time.

+   3. What are the various tasks you can perform by running a shell script?

Answers will vary, but may include: setting reminders, sending mail, and customizing the system environment.
