# # ACTIVITY 2-4
## Managing Group Accounts

**Before You Begin:**

+   1. Log in to the CLI terminal as root.
2. On the terminal, create two groups named **Finance** and **Marketing**.
3. Enter `clear` to clear the terminal screen.

**Scenario:**

You are assigned the task of changing the GID for the finance department so that it matches the department number. In addition, you need to remove the marketing group from the system because the work of the marketing department will be outsourced to another company from now on.

| What You Do | How You Do It |
| :--- | :--- |
| **1.** Change the GID to match the finance department number, 555. | a. Enter `groupmod -g 555 Finance` to alter the GID of the finance department.<br><br>b. Enter `tail -20 /etc/group` to verify that the change was made.<br><br>c. Observe that the GID of the finance department has been changed. |
| **2.** Delete the user group, Marketing. | a. Enter `groupdel Marketing`<br><br>b. Enter `cat /etc/group \| less` to verify that the **Marketing** group was deleted.<br><br>c. Press the **Spacebar** till you reach the end of the file to verify that the **Marketing** group is no longer listed.<br><br>d. Press **Q** to quit the display.<br><br>e. Clear the terminal screen. |
