Tools such as passwd and chpass enable you to modify various features of accounts, or you can edit the configuration files directly. You can delete an account by using rmuser or by editing the configuration files and performing various cleanup steps. FreeBSD's groups also require creation, maintenance, and deletion through mechanisms that sometimes parallel those used in account operations. However you go through the mechanics of account creation and maintenance, these steps fit into your system's overall security, so you should plan your accounts and groups to fit your site's security policies.
