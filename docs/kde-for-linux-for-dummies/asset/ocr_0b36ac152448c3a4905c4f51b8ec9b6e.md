Don't keep your password with your other ISP information. In fact, don't get in the bad habit of writing down passwords. An even worse habit is writing down your passwords and leaving them someplace convenient, like your top desk drawer or taped to the monitor. Passwords work because they're secret; making them public by writing them down defeats the purpose of requiring passworded access.
