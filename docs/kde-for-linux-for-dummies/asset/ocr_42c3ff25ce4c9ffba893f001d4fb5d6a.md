# Giving Your Windows a Metal Edge

![](bbox=[0.24, 0.11, 0.92, 0.51])

Figure 18-5: The Mechanical theme captures the feel of a futuristic factory.

The Metal Edge theme (shown in Figure 18-6) gets its name from its window border treatment that simulates metal pipe or rounded metal edging. The dominant color in this theme is a teal blue that appears in the panel, windows, and in the mottled blue and green wallpaper. The stark white of the window background contrasts with the teal blue of the window surround, which gives this theme a crisp appearance.
