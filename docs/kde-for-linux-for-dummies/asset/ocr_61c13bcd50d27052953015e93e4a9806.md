# ## Process Management

The KDE Process Management utility enables you to view and manage the processes running on your system. It´s a powerful tool for experienced system administrators, but it´s not something the average user should mess with. Choose KDE Menü►Utilities►Process Management to open the KPM window (as shown in Figure 10-15). The window displays a graphical representation of CPU load, memory, and swap space usage, plus a complete list of the running processes and their status. Right-click a process and choose from the commands on the pop-up menu to do things such as kill, terminate, or hang up the selected process. Be careful and make sure you know what effect your actions will have.
