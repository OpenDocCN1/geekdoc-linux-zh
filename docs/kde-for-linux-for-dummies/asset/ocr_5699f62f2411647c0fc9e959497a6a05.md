2. Click on the small plus sign to expand the tree one level.

If you haven't already downloaded the newsgroups list from your server, KRN asks if you want to do this now. Click Yes. Depending on the speed of your connection and the number of groups your ISP carries, downloading may take a while.

You can only read newsgroups that your ISP carries. For some people, this means that they have a relatively small selection of groups to read. Many ISPs will add newsgroups at the request of their subscribers; others won't. If your ISP won't carry the groups you want to read, consider subscribing to a third-party service such as the ones offered at www.newsguy.com or www.deja.com.
