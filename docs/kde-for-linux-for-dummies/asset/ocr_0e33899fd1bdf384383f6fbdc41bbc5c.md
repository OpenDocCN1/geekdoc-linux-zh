+   - **Viewing the contents of an existing archive file:** Choose File->Open and click OK. The contents of the archive file appear in the Archiver window. # # Calculator

If you need to do some number crunching on a scientific calculator, just open KCalc (KDE Menu->Utilities->Calculator). When the KCalc utility appears on-screen (as shown in Figure 10-10), just use your mouse pointer to click on-screen calculator buttons instead of using your finger to press the buttons of a handheld calculator. Clicking the large KCalc button beside the answer opens the KCalc Configuration dialog box, where you can adjust settings that change the precision and switch between trigonometry or statistical modes or select a different font to display the results.

# # Cut & Paste History

If you do a lot of cutting and pasting, you'll love the KClipper utility. When you launch the KClipper utility, the program docks a small icon into the panel and begins tracking your cut, copy, and paste activities. KClipper expands the standard KDE Clipboard to make room for multiple items. As a result, you can cut or copy multiple text passages before stopping to paste any of them to a new location.
