To launch a program using the KDE menu, follow these simple steps:

+   1. **Click the KDE Menu button in the panel.**
+   The KDE Menu button is the one with the KDE logo (a big K and a gear) on it. The button is normally located at the left end of the panel. When you click the KDE Menu button, the KDE application starter menu appears.
+   2. **Point to a menu item (such as Applications) with a right-facing arrow.**
+   Pointing means to move the mouse pointer over the menu item and let it rest there for a second or so. A cascading submenu appears, as shown in Figure 2-1.
+   3. **Click the desired program in the submenu.**
+   KDE closes the menu and launches the selected program. Normally, the program appears in its own window on your desktop although some utilities might show up in the panel or other locations. Depending on the speed of your system and the size of the selected program, the program may appear immediately or it may take nearly a minute to show up on your desktop.
