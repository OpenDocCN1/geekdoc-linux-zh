+   1. Click the Reply to Author button (see Figure 9-6).
+   
+   The KMail composition window appears. You see that the text of the e-mail to which you're replying is already quoted in the message, and the e-mail address of the sender is already in the To: field.
+   
+   2. Delete the parts of the quoted message that you don't want to answer directly and compose your reply.
+   
+   3. Click Send Message after you're finished.
+   
+   KMail sends your reply to the person named in the To: field.

If the message was originally sent to several people and you want to reply to all of them, use the Reply to All Recipients button (shown in Figure 9-6) instead of the Reply to Author button, and proceed as you would with a single reply.
