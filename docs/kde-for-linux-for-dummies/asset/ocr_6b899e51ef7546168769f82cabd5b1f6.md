# In this part...

Nobody knows what the human appendix was originally for, but there’s no doubt what this one’s for—hooking you up to more resources for making contact with the KDE/Linux community. Here you’ll find tips on how to get hold of and install KDE, where to find more know-how and know-who on the Internet, and a catalog of the goodies you’ll find on the CD-ROM that accompanies this book. With these resources and KDE For Linux For Dummies, you’re ready to rock.
