# In this part . . .

In some ways, the cyberworld is mercifully different from the real world. Case in point: Here, too, appearances matter - but in the world of KDE, you *can* master them - they actually have a practical reason beyond making an on-screen fashion statement.

This part shows you how to set up your Panel to your liking, design a desktop tailored to the way you actually work (what a concept!), and use KDE's themes to best advantage. Form follows function. Beautiful.
