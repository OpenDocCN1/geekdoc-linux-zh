# Mechanizing with the Mechanical Theme

The hallmarks of the Mechanical theme (shown in Figure 18-5) are its nicely executed charcoal gray color scheme and the wallpaper image by artist Neil Blevins depicting a futuristic factory scene. The text and active titlebar color are a light gray-green. The titlebar buttons have a stamped metal look and the window borders are thin, but functional.
