# Naming the desktop pager buttons

Virtual desktops are a great way to get more space to spread out your windows. Instead of shuffling stacks of open windows on your computer desktop, you can create up to eight virtual desktops, each with a different collection of windows, and switch back and forth between them. You can keep your Web surfing on one desktop and your budget proposal on another.

But wait, is your e-mail program open on desktop Two or desktop Five? You can solve that problem by renaming the panel buttons you use to access your virtual desktops. To manage the number of virtual desktops and assign them meaningful names, follow these steps:

+   1. Click the KDE Menu button and choose Panel \ Configure, or right-click the panel and choose Configure from the pop-up menu that appears.
+      
+      The KPanel Configuration dialog box appears.
+   2. Click the **Desktops** tab.
+      
+      The Desktops tab appears in the KPanel Configuration dialog box and displays the settings shown in Figure 5-5.
