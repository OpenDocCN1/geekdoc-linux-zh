# RealPlayer

As recently as two or three years ago, thinking that someday local radio and TV stations would be able to broadcast worldwide via the Internet was a nice fantasy. With the advent of streaming technology, that fantasy has come true in an explosive way. (In fact, the soundtrack to this chapter is WFMT, the Chicago classical radio station that Kate listened to in college; even though she's 2,000 miles away, the magic of streaming audio brings Chicago traffic reports to her desktop.)

RealPlayer is a wonderful program that handles all sorts of input. You can use it to listen to streamed audio (as in Figure 12-4), watch video clips, or just listen to sound files stored in the .mp3 format. Visit the RealPlayer Web site at www.real.com to learn more.
