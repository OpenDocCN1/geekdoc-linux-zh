# Downloading new e-mail from your ISP

Getting your mail with KMail is also unbelievably easy. If you have KMail open, click the Get New Mail button (shown in Figure 9-5)! KMail then contacts your ISP to get any new mail that’s been sent.

![](bbox=[0.18, 0.21, 0.79, 0.65])

Figure 9-5: Use the Get New Mail button to check whether you have any mail waiting in your ISP’s inbox.

After you send KMail to go get your e-mail, click the name of your inbox in the upper-left corner of the KMail screen. If you have new mail, the subject lines and senders’ names appear in the upper-right window of the screen. Click any of these messages to display the e-mail message in the large window at the bottom of the screen.
