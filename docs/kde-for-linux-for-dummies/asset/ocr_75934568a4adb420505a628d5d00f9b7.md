# Resizing your photo

There are two ways to resize an image: make it smaller or make it bigger. KView offers crop tools to cut an image’s size and zoom tools to enlarge it.

# Cropping an image

*Cropping* is the most effective way to fix an uninspiring photograph. Who hasn’t taken a shot that needed cropping? Perhaps the classic example is the “child at a famous place” shot, where little Tanya fits neatly to the lower-right corner of the picture while the Disneyland Matterhorn looms overhead. You have two choices with a picture like this: leave it as it is to prove that you actually were at Disneyland or crop it so you have a picture of Tanya on the happiest day of her four-year-old life. (Here’s a hint: There are millions of pictures of the Matterhorn, but only one of that grinning little girl.)
