You work on your computer every day — you need a little time to relax and play! KDE offers several ways to bring that fun into your computing life — who knows, you may even find handy ways to use these great programs to enhance your work productivity!

Some of the most popular software programs are those that allow you to edit images, like photographs or drawings, and those that let you play music through your computer speakers. (Of course, games are the real reason we all buy computers, aren’t they?) KDE has all of these things for you — yes, even the games — as part of the basic distribution. If you’re longing for a little more, search through the various Web sites described in Appendix B; you’ll find new games and programs designed to make your leisure time more electronically enhanced.

> This chapter discusses various programs that work with image and sound files. In all cases, it’s assumed that you’ll use these programs in conjunction with your own creative efforts or with files you’ve obtained legally. Copyright law applies to digital media as much as it does to print or broadcast media; you really don’t want to get into trouble for altering a copyrighted image and claiming it as your own, or for obtaining a copyrighted file illegally. Trust us and the fine lawyers at IDG Books.
