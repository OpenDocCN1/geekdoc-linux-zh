# Grateful for the Dead (Theme)

The Dead theme (shown in Figure 18-3) tends toward the macabre with its heavy, dark gray color scheme, and red, dripping blood on the top edge of the panel and the active titlebar. The window borders and toolbar buttons are composed of bones. The wallpaper image depicts skeletons, barely visible in the darkness of a dungeon. This theme is designed to give you the creeps.
