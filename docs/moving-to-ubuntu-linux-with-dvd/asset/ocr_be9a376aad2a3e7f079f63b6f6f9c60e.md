# **Chapter 13**  
OpenOffice.org Writer  
(It Was a Dark and Stormy Night . . .)

*I just love using this infamous opening from Edward George Bulwer-Lytton's Paul Clifford (written in 1830) to introduce a chapter on word processing. Those famous words, "It was a dark and stormy night," were made even more famous (infamous?) by Charles M. Schulz's Snoopy, that barnstorm-ing, literary beagle. It just seems fitting considering this chapter's topic—word processors.*

*Word processors run the gamut in terms of complexity, from simple programs that aren't much more than text editors to full-blown desktop publishing systems. Users coming from the Microsoft world are most likely to use OpenOffice Writer, part of the OpenOffice.org suite.*
