Before we move on to other things, we need to look at the buttons on the right side of the **title bar**. You use the leftmost icon, the one with an under-score (or a small straight line, if you prefer), to minimize a window (remember that you can pause over the top with your mouse button to get the tooltip). The icon with a square in the center maximizes a window, causing it to take up every bit of space on your desktop except for the space taken up by your panels. Finally, the X does pretty much what you would expect it to: It closes a running application.

# On to the Menu Bar

Directly below the *title bar* is the *menu bar*. The menu bar generally has a number of labels (such as File, Edit, View, and so on), each grouping the various tasks you can do into some kind of sensible order.
