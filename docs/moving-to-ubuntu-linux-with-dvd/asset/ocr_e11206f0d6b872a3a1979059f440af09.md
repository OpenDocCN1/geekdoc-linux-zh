**Figure 2-13** Ubiquity's first screen is mostly a welcome screen, but take note of what it has to say, especially when it comes to backups.

The warnings about making sure you have taken a backup of your data cannot be repeated too many times. Make a backup, then make another backup.

Click the **Forward** button and you are taken to the installation language selection screen (see **Figure 2-14**). This language will also be the default language when the system is fully installed. English is selected by default, but Ubuntu has been internationalized for dozens of languages. There's no Klingon here, but Esperanto is available.
