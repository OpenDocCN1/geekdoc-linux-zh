Click **Forward** and **Ubiquity's** partitioning tool starts. A small window appears titled **Starting up the partitioner**. A progress bar keeps you posted as your disks are scanned and analyzed. A few seconds later, the main partitioning window appears. It is labeled “Select a disk.” Most people will have just one disk but if you have multiple disks, select one here and click Forward. The next window, labeled “Prepare disk space,” lets you select the amount of space you are willing to allocate (see Figure 2-18).
