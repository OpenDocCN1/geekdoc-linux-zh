The default Server Type for sending is SMTP, and that is almost certainly what you need. Enter the hostname as provided by your ISP (or system administrator), and click Next. The Account Management screen follows with your new email account listed as it is displayed in Evolution. If you prefer, you can change this to be a name rather than an email address. When you are finished, click Forward.

The final step is to select your time zone. Select an area on the map (preferably near to where you live) to narrow down your search. The map zooms in to the area you clicked, allowing you to fine-tune your selection (see Figure 11-4). Make your final selection (you can use the drop-down box to aid in your selection), click Forward again, then click Apply, and you are done.
