If you don't like the results, close the image by clicking the Close button in the corner (usually an X unless you have changed your desktop theme or style). A warning box pops up, telling you that changes have been made and you might want to save your work (more on that in a moment). Your options are Save, Don't Save, and Cancel. Click Don't Save and it goes away. Then start over with another logo. You might try changing the background color or the gradient this time.
