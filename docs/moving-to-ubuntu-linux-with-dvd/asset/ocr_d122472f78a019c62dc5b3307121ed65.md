Let's look at each individually, starting with controls. These represent most of what you see when you are looking at a theme. The colors, style of sliders, shape of buttons, and tabs . . . all these are elements that fall under this category. I think of it as the look and feel of the GNOME interface.
Window border is just that—the frame and the layout of the buttons that go at the top of the window title. Changing the window border can also define whether frames have square or rounded corners.
