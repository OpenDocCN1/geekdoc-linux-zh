# Same GNOME

Same GNOME is one great little puzzle game that takes no time to learn, but forever to master (see Figure 19-12). You start with a grid of brightly colored marbles. When you move your mouse cursor over a marble, all of the similarly colored marbles in a group start to spin. Click that group and the marbles disappear: Any marbles that were above your group fall below. The net effect of this is that you are creating other groupings, which can then be removed. The idea is to clear the grid of marbles in as few moves as possible. A little strategic planning mixed with some good spatial visualization is the key to success. If you don't like the colored marbles, switch to the planets view (my personal favorite).
