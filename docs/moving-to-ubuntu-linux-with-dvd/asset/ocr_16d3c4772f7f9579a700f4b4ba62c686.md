> Tip  When you look at your files and folders in Nautilus, they are arranged alphabetically. Usually, this is what you want, but you can also sort them by size, modification date, file type, and more. Just click View on the Nautilus menu bar and check out the Arrange Items submenu. You can even choose to arrange them manually.

One of the directories under the root is called home and inside that directory, you’ll discover other directories, one for each login name on your system.
