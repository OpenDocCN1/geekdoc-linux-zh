The most common thing you will do is add an `x` option. This shows all processes, controlled by your terminal or not, as well as those of other users. The administrator also wants to know about the `1` option, which stands for long. It is particularly useful because it shows the parent process of every pro- cess, because every process has another process that launched (or spawned) it. This is the parent process of the process ID. In sysdaim short form, this is the `PPID` of the PID. When your system starts up, the first process is called `init`.
