I still find it interesting to consider the terms that have entered the language when referring to creating CDs. We rip, and then we burn. Considering the violent-sounding nature of the process, anything that simplifies the process and makes things a little friendlier is certainly welcome. For this part of the chapter, I’m going to assume that you have already ripped a number of songs and now you want to create a custom collection of your music to listen to in the car or on your living room stereo.
