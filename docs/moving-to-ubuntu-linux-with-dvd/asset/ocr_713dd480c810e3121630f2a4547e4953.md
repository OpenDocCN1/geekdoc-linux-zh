**Figure 2–17** Both you and your computer need a name. The username you specify here is that of the primary user. You can add users later.

Enter your full name in the “What is your name?” field. In the second field labeled “What name do you want to use to log in?” select a username, preferably eight characters or fewer (although this isn’t a hard and fast rule), and tab to the Password field. When you enter the password, it is echoed back as stars (or asterisks) so don’t worry if you can’t see what you are typing. After this, you must enter the password again. This is to make sure that you typed what you thought you typed in the first password field. Finally, under the heading “What is the name of this computer?” enter a hostname for your system. By default, the installer will append “-desktop” to your username, but you are free to select something else.
