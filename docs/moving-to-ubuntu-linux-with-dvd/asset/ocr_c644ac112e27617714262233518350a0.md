# Commands to Know and Love, Part 1

+   - **date** Date and time.
+   - **df -H** Show me how much free space my disks have, in a nice, human-readable format.
+   - **who** Who is logged on to the system?
+   - **w** Similar to who but with different information.
+   - **cal** Show me a calendar.
