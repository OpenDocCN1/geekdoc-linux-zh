# To subscribe to newsgroups with pine:

+   1. pine
+   For starters, type pine at the shell prompt.

+   2. L
+   Press L to see the folder list. You'll notice that you now have a news section at the bottom of the folder list.

+   3. ↓
+   Scroll down to the News on section.

+   4. A
+   Press A to subscribe to a newsgroup. You have to subscribe for groups to show up in your list.

+   5. Ctrl T
+   Press Ctrl T to get a list of all newsgroups, and then scroll through the list with the arrow keys until you find one you want to try out, as Figure 12.10 shows. Note that this can be pretty time-consuming on a slow connection.

+   6. S
+   Press S to subscribe to the newsgroup you highlighted in the previous step.

+   7. Continue subscribing to any groups that look interesting by following the same process.

+   8. Q
+   Press Q (and confirm with Y) when you're finished and ready to exit pine, or just move to the next section to read your new newsgroups.
