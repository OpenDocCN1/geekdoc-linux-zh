# Splitting Files with split

Suppose you're futzing with your new digital camera and want to share a photo of your new computer (what else is cool enough to take pictures of?!) via email with your friends and family. You access the file, attach it to an email message, and then—argh!—your ISP fails to send the file because it's too big. While you could modify the file itself—reduce the physical size, reduce the number of colors used, or crop out nonessential parts, for example—you can also just split the file with split. For example, if the ISP tells you that no files larger than .51 MB will be accepted, you can use split to send the file in chunks—all using one easy command (Code Listing 6.23).

```
[jdoe@frazz split]$ ls -lh
total 1.1M
-rw-rwxr-x 1 jdoe jdoe1.0M Jan 1 12:42 mongopicure.jpg*
[jdoe@frazz split]$ ls -l
total 1060
-rw-rwxr-x 1 jdoe jdoe1879300 Jan 1 12:42 mongopicure.jpg*
[jdoe@frazz split]$ split -b 500k mongopicure.jpg
[jdoe@frazz split]$ ls -lh
total 2.1M
-rw-rwxr-x 1 jdoe jdoe1.0M Jan 1 12:42 mongopicure.jpg*
-rw-rw-r-- 1 jdoe jdoe500K Jan 1 13:03 xaa
-rw-rw-r-- 1 jdoe jdoe500K Jan 1 13:03 xab
-rw-rw-r-- 1 jdoe jdoe54K Jan 1 13:03 xac
[jdoe@frazz split]$ split -b 500k mongopicure.jpg chunk
[jdoe@frazz split]$ ls -lh
total 3.2M
-rw-rw-r-- 1 jdoe jdoe500K Jan 1 13:03 chunk aa
-rw-rw-r-- 1 jdoe jdoe500K Jan 1 13:03 chunk ab
-rw-rw-r-- 1 jdoe jdoe54K Jan 1 13:03 chunk ac
-rw-rwxr-x 1 jdoe jdoe1.0M Jan 1 12:42 mongopicure.jpg*
-rw-rw-r-- 1 jdoe jdoe500K Jan 1 13:03 xaa
-rw-rw-r-- 1 jdoe jdoe500K Jan 1 13:03 xab
-rw-rw-r-- 1 jdoe jdoe54K Jan 1 13:03 xac
[jdoe@frazz split]$ cat chunk aa chunk ab chunk ac > reconstitutedpicture.jpg
[jdoe@frazz split]$ cmp mongopicure.jpg reconstitutedpicture.jpg
[jdoe@frazz split]$
```
