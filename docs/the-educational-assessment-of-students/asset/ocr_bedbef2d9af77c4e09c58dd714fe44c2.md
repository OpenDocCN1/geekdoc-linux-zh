# ABOUT THIS CHAPTER

Suppose you have taught a student but the student just doesn't get it: why? Have you wondered how you could assess why a student doesn't understand the ideas you are teaching? How can you assess what the problem is? These are the issues we discuss in this chapter.

We begin the chapter by discussing the purposes of diagnostic assessment and formative evaluation using six different approaches. We discuss how these approaches complement one another and fit into a teaching sequence. Each approach is discussed and we describe how you may implement it. We close the chapter by discussing suggestions for interviewing students expressly to discover why they do not understand some concepts and principles.
