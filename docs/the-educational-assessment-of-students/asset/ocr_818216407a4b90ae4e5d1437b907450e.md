- no sense that the writer cares about the topic; the writing is flat, lifeless, stiff, or mechanical.
- no sense that the piece was written for an audience.
- no hint of the writer behind the words; there are few if any places where the reader feels connected to the writer. The writing doesn't get the reader involved. |
