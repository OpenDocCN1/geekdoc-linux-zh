In the Customer Portal interface, you will notice some of the issues are marked with the Ansible logo. This indicates that there is an Ansible remediation playbook available. The issues with dimmed, crossed-out Ansible logo do not have an Ansible remediation playbook, but may have manual mitigation or remediation instructions in the details.

# Configuration

The Configuration tab controls configuration settings for the Red Hat Insights service on the Customer Portal. For example, you can configure it to send you a weekly summary report in email, adjust systems registered through your Red Hat Satellite are displayed in the Customer Portal, and so on.

# Viewing Issues Reported by Red Hat Insights

To view issues by Red Hat Insights, the overall process is as follows:

+   1. Log on to Red Hat Customer Portal and access the Red Hat Insights page at https://access.redhat.com/insights.
2. On the portal, navigate to the Overview tab. View all your registered systems in the Newest Systems section. The Actions Summary section provides percentages of actions required based on low, medium, and high severity.
3. Navigate to the Actions tab, which provides more information about any security, stability, or critical issues. Click on the graph representing the percentage to see more details for these issues.
4. Navigate to the Inventory tab. page lists all the registered systems.
5. Click the registered system. The page lists all the issues affecting the system.

# INTERPRETING RED HAT INSIGHTS REPORTS

In Red Hat Insights, its rules determine the issues it looks for on your systems. Red Hat frequently adds new rules to Red Hat Insights to check for newly identified issues. Rules may look for incidents that have occurred on your system that a problem, or they may proactively anticipate problems based on your system's current configuration.
