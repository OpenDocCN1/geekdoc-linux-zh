We have already seen that the file managers in Linux, both Konqueror and Nautilus, endeavor to be your central point for a lot of program functions, from simply managing files to acting as a Web browser. Add one more function to Konqueror’s list, gPhoto2 front end.

To get images off your digital camera using Konqueror, the steps are few and easy. Connect your camera and turn it on. Linux detects most digital cameras, especially most USB cameras, right away. Open Konqueror. You can probably just type "camera:/" into the address bar. There should be a folder for your camera. In that folder will be either more directories or the image files on your camera, depending on how your camera stores images (see Figure 9.3). You can then copy images from the camera to a directory on your hard drive in the same way that you would normally copy files in Konqueror.
