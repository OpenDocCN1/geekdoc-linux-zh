Hephaestus

O deep-devisting child of Mario sage,
Small will have I to do, or thou to bear,
What yet we must. Beyond the haunt of man
Unto this Web site, with letters grimly forged,
I must transfix and shackle up thy limbs.
What else would thou propose we do for fun?

(Hermes flits down with a delivery.)

Hermes

Yea, I got a delivery for thee, Prometheus.

Hephaestus

What in the name of Samus Aran,
Greatest space fighter for Zeus, could it be?

Prometheus

Tears can avail us nothing; strive not thou,
Nor waste thine efforts thus unaidingly.
We must play the games the great Zeus
Provides us with. He that is great and mighty
Has bestowed upon us a copy of World of Warcraft!

Hephaestus

Would that some other were endowed therewith!
We shall totally rule with our Paladins!

Hermes

Enough of useless ruth! Why tarriest thou?
Why not sign here for thy delivery?

(Prometheus signs and Hermes flits away.)

# Scene: In the great caverns of the game room of Prometheus

Prometheus

Zeus, why curse us thus? In sooth this game
Of thine standeth assailed of all that here is
Installed on mine machine of mighty Linux.

Hephaestus

I know it; I gainsay no word hereof.
This game is not in the support of Linux.
Smite harder, wedge it home—no faltering here!

Prometheus

I have smote this game with all terminal commands in my power,
Yet no load of the program will come of my toil.

Hephaestus

Alas, Prometheus! I lament thy pain.
Try thy game in Windows and be done with it.
