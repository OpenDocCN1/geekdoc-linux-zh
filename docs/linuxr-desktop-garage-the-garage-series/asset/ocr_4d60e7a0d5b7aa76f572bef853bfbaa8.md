# The Contacts

Most e-mail programs have an address book or contacts function. In Evolution, by default, the Contacts component (see Figure 7.5) is organized like an old Rolodex, with individual contacts made to appear like index cards. You will find Evolution's Contacts function to be similar to other address book programs and very easy to work with.

While in the Contacts component view, the toolbar has no special buttons. I guess someone, somewhere is telling us that our contacts are not special. In the menu, you can choose to view as cards, as a list, or by company, or you can define your own custom view.
