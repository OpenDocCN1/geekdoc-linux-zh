# KDE Control Center

> ### Program Info
> Fedora/GNOME menu  *Preferences > Control Center*
> Mandrake/KDE menu  *System > Configuration > Configure Your Desktop*
> Terminal command  kcontrol

One-stop shopping. Everyone likes that. The KDE Control Center makes configuring and customizing your desktop one-stop shopping. In the KDE Control Center, you can change most of the preferences for your desktop environment, from a simple task such as changing your desktop wallpaper to more complex jobs such as installing fonts and adjusting your screen resolution settings.

As we discussed in earlier topics, the KDE version in Mandrake is different than the KDE version in Fedora. Most of the changes were in an effort to standardize icons, themes, labels, and tools between GNOME and KDE with their “Bluecurve” theme.
