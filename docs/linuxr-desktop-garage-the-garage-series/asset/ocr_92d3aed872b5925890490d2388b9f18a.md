## Scene: A rocky ravine in the mountains of Amazon

Prometheus

*Lo, the Internet's bound and limitary depth,*  
*The Amazonian steppe, the waste unshopped of men!*  
*Look to it now, Hephaestus—thine it is.*  
*Thy Consumer obeying, this archfiel to clench*
