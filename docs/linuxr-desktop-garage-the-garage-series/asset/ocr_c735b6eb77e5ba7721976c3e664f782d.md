# # Filtering Spam

There isn’t a specific spam-filtering tool in Evolution. You need to create rules yourself to try to filter the good from the bad. You can create filters by going to Tools > Filters.

# **TOOL KIT, 6.1:**

**Set up Your Evolution E-mail Account**

If you stepped through the setup wizard the first time you started Evolution, your e-mail address should be set up. If you canceled out of the wizard, want to edit your account, want to add an additional account, or just want to play along, you can come with me, but you are responsible for refreshments. You also need to know your e-mail address, your mail account type, your incoming and outgoing mail server addresses, your username, and your password.

+   1.  Open the settings by choosing Tools > Settings > Mail Accounts on the menu bar.
+   2.  Click Add to add an account. The same wizard that comes up the first time you start Evolution pops up, the Evolution Setup Assistant (see Figure 6.2). Step through the wizard and fill out all the fields with your information.
+   3.  To edit your account, highlight the account on the list in the Mail Accounts section and click the Edit button.
+   4.  The Account Editor dialog box has several tabs: Identity, Receiving Mail, Sending Options, Sending Mail, Defaults, and Security. Each tab is easy to go through and complete. You don’t need all the options on every tab to set up your e-mail account; you need only your basic e-mail account info.
