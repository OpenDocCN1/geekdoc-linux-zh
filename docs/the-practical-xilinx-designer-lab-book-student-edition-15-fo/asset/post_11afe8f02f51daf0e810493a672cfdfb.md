The Flow Engine window appears which has a graphical rendition of the stages required to map a netlist into an FPLD (in this case, Figure 2.30 shows the steps needed to map into an XC5000 CPLD). The Foundation Series Implementation tools run these stages.

Translate: The EDIF netlist is converted to an internal netlist format, design-rule checks are performed, and various optimizations of the logic circuit are tried to increase the speed of the circuit and/or decrease the number of gates used.

Fit: The gates in the netlist are assigned to specific macrocells and their interconnections are routed through the FastCONNECT matrix.

Timing: The propagation delays through the macrocells and the FastCONNECT matrix are computed and stored in a file for use during a timing simulation.

Bitstream: The stream of bits is generated which will be downloaded into the chip to make it carry out the logic functions described in the ABEL or schematic file.
