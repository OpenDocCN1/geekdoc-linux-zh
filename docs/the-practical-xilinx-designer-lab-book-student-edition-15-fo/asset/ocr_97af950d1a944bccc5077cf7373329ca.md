The right-hand pane has a Flow tab for controlling the activation of tools used in various phases of the design flow, a Contents tab which shows the status of the files in your project, a Reports tab which lists the report files generated by the tools as they do their work, and a Synthesis tab which activates the logic synthesizer for various HDL macros included in your design. The bottom pane lists diagnostic messages output by the Foundation software as it processes your design files. For now, you will spend most of your time working in the Files and Flow tabs of the left and right panes, respectively.
