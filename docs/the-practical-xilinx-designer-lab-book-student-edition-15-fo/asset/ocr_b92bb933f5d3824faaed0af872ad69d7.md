Of course, it is one thing to say that the Boolean operators will be replaced with schematic symbols, but it is another to do it. As an example, let's generate the schematic drawing of the Boolean equation for Hwy3. We start by noting that the Hwy3 output is the logical AND of two terms:

(Hooterville · Mayberry + Hooterville · Mayberry) (1.1)
(Siler City · Mt. Pilot) (1.2)

Therefore, the Hwy3 output is generated by an AND gate that has the previous two terms as its inputs (see Figure 1.7a).

We can continue the process by noting that Eq. (1.1) is the logical OR of Hooterville · Mayberry and Hooterville · Mayberry. So an OR gate can be added to the drawing that has these two terms as inputs and whose output is connected to one of the inputs of the first AND gate (see Figure 1.7b). In addition, Eq. (1.2) is the logical AND of Siler City and Mt. Pilot. The output of this AND gate is connected to the other input of our first AND gate.
