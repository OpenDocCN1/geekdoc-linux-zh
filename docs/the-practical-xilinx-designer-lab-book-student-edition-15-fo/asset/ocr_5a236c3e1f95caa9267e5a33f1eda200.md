5. The jump-to-subroutine (jsr) instruction was added that saves the address of the next instruction on the stack and then transfers to a new address in the PROGRAM region. The ret instruction was added that removes the saved address from the stack and restores it to the PC.

6. The return-from-interrupt (reti) instruction was added that restores the accumulator and program counter values from the stack and then resumes instruction execution at the address in the PC. This instruction reverses the operations performed when an interrupt service routine is initiated (i.e., the PC and ACC are saved on the stack).
