The steps for wiring a circuit should be completed in the following order:

+   1. Turn the power off before you begin to build anything.
+   2. Make sure the power is off before you begin to build anything.
+   3. Connect the +5 V and ground leads of your power supply to the power and ground bus bars of your breadboard. (These are the leftmost and rightmost columns of the breadboard.)
+   4. Plug the chips you will be using into the breadboard. Point all the chips in the same direction with pin 1 at the upper left corner. (Pin 1 is often identified by a dot next to it on the chip package.)
