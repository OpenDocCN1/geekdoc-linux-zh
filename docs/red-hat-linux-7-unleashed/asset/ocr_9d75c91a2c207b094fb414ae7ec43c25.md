Most interactive editors permit a form of global search-and-replace, and some even make it easy to operate on more than one file. Perhaps that's a superior automation for your needs. If not, `cshtr` is a minimal command-line alternative that is maximally simple to use.

#        to HOST and retrieve FILE into the local directory.
