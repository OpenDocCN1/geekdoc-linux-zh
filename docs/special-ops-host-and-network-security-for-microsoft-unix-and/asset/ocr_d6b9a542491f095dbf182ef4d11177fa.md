**Q:** Can I find wireless devices based on their MAC address alone?

**A:** Several vendors that are wireless-only companies have registered their Company_id MAC prefix with IEEE OUI and can be positively linked with a wireless device. Examples of this scenario are Aironet (pre–Cisco buyout) and Agere (pre–Lucent buyout). If you have a dump of MAC addresses on your network from your switch logs or traffic monitoring, you can look for these addresses to pick out wireless devices.
