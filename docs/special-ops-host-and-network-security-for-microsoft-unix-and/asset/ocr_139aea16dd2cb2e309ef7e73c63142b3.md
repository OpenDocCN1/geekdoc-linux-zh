**Q:** Is secure DNS implementations have been developed; the most popular Domain Name System Security (DNSSEC). DNSSEC works by following the DNS authority hierarchy and including encryption to verify each server’s communication. Encryption within DNS has not been fully supported, especially at the top root and TLD servers. Because of the lack of support, DNS security solutions have not gained popularity. In the words of D.J. Bernstein,"I’m not going to bother implementing DNSSEC until I see (1) a stable, sensible DNSSEC protocol and (2) a detailed, concrete, credible plan for central DNSSEC deployment." In the eyes of many administrators, DNSSEC is currently a lost until there is trust at the root or TLD levels.

Optimistic Encryption (OE) is a new and ingenious method of privacy that depends on the trusted DNS hierarchy for key distribution. Free S/WAN Encryption tunnels support OE encryption (you can find a good explanation at www.freeswan.org/freeswan_trees/freeswan-1.91/doc/optimism.spec). Due to the current privacy concerns and government Internet monitoring, OE is becoming more popular and becoming deployed privately throughout many networks.
