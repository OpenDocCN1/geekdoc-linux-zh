# Analyzing Your Firewall and IDS Logs

Depending on their placement and configuration, your firewall and IDS logs may hold your most valuable network information—information about all the users on your network. You will want to notice of permissive segments (subnets that have too much access to the rest of the network) and possibly more important, trouble segments (subnets that have users who try to access resources they shouldn't, which are most often engineering and software subnets).

If you already have IDS sensors, you might have a good snapshot of your network's composition. However, revisiting these logs during this portion of the process is a good exercise. Reacquaint yourself with what your traffic looks like a NIDS sensor; this will be valuable when we talk about the NIDS configuration.
