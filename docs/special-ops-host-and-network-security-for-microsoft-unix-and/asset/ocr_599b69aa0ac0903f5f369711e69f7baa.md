There are many examples of fairly straightforward tasks that can be assigned to these managers; enforcement of acceptable-use policies (though the detection of violations is and will be an IT responsibility) is one of the most common ways to involve management in information security. And as you'll see in this section, company-wide awareness campaigns also leave room for engaging management in your information security posture.

Much can be done to protect your users from inadvertently causing harm to the company by implementing technology-based safeguards as those described earlier in this chapter, in many cases the user becomes the last line of defense. If we could magically teach users to never leave their unsecured and to recognize and delete suspicious e-mails, a considerable portion of major incidents would come before fruition.

In this section, we are not going to concentrate on the messages themselves, because these run the gamut from the universal, such as anti-virus updates and dealing with suspicious e-mails or system behavior, to more specialized information, such as maintaining the security of proprietary information. In your position, you are the best judge of what risks in your organization can be mitigated by user education, and so I forget the content and spend time looking at the methods themselves. I touch on three common approaches to disseminating security awareness materials, and let you decide which methods or combinations fit your organization and user base:

+   - Centralized corporate IT department
- Distributed department campaigning
- Pure enforcement

# Creating Awareness via a Centralized Corporate IT Department

In this approach, corporate IT assumes responsibility for developing and distributing security awareness campaigns. Typically, this is implemented secondarily to centralized help-desk awareness programs. Your organization may already have produced mouse pads, buttons, or posters that include the help-desk telephone number and instructions to contact this number for any computer issues.
