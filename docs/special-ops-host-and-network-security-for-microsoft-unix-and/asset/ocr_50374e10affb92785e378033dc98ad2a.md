# Wardial to Discover Corporate Assets

+   - There are several decent wardial technologies available, but the DOS-based freeway applications are typically the best for identifying live dialup systems.

+   - Typically, wardial is performed after hours to avoid interrupting the normal course of business or alerting any employees. Additionally, after-hours dialing is preferred because that is typically the remote desktop applications are in use for employees who work from home.

+   - Oftentimes, a lot of information is given away in the carrier banners, such as company name, host or application name, and operating system, making it easy to identify the system that has dial-up access.

+   - The outlined methodology to accurately remove dial-up systems for an organization and assess the security controls surrounding them is key when conducting a wardial. In this process, systems that do not need dial-up access can be identified and analog lines via dial-up, proper controls can be assessed to ensure that assets are protected by hackers.

# Managing Asset Exposure

+   - Exposure or exposure level refers to a host or network's relationship with another host or network and the bi-directional visibility they share. When generically, the term exposure level should take inbound and outbound exposure into account. The goal of understanding the exposure of your assets is to determine which exposures are accepted risk and which ones are not. This will allow for exposure remediation and subsequently a reduction in overall risk.
