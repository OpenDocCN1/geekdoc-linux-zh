# Attacking and Defending DNS • Chapter 8 45

Most of the general top-level domains (gTLD) are the original TLDs issued in 1995. Over the years, many TLD entries have been added when required justification has been approved. See Table 8.5 for a list of the added TLDs.

# Table 8.5 General TLDs

| gTLD | Introduced | Purpose | Operator |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| .aero | 2001 | Air-transport industry | Societe Internationale de Aeronautautiques SC, (SITA) |
| .biz | 2001 | Businesses | NeuLevel |
| .com | 1995 | Unrestricted (but intended for commercial registrants) | VerSign, Inc. |
| .coop | 2001 | Cooperatives | DotCooperation, LLC |
| .edu | 1995 | United States educational institutions | EDUCAUSE |
| .gov | 1995 | United States government | U.S. General Services Administration |
| .info | 2001 | Unrestricted use | Afilias |
| .int | 1998 | Organizations established by international treaties between governments | Internet Assigned Numbers Authority |
| .mil | 1995 | United States military | U.S. DoD Network Information Center |
| .museum | 2001 | Museums | Museum Domain Management Association (MuseDom) |
| .name | 2001 | For registration by individuals | Global Name Registry, LTD |
| .net | 1995 | Unrestricted (but intended for network providers, etc.) | VerSign, Inc |
| .org | 1995 | Unrestricted (but intended for organizations that do not fit elsewhere) | Public Interest Registry; was operated by VerSign Global Registry Services until the end of 2002 |
| .pro | 2002 | Accountants, lawyers, physicians, and other professionals | RegistryPro, LTD |
