The GLBA requires federal agencies to establish guidelines or rules regarding administrative, technical, and physical information safeguards. Some of these safeguards are implemented in agency-issued rules, others as guidelines.

Though various agencies issued guidelines or rules, most were required to “implement the standards prescribed under section 501(b) in the same manner, to the extent practicable…” However, the FTC and SEC were explicitly required to issue standards under section 501(b) by rule instead. The following is an excerpt from section 501(b) of the GLBA Act:

> ...Each agency... shall establish appropriate standards for financial institutions subject to their jurisdiction relating to administrative, technical, and physical safeguards.

+   1. Insure the security and confidentiality of customer information;
2. Protect against any anticipated threats or hazards to the security or integrity of such information; and
3. Protect against unauthorized access to or use of such information that could result in substantial harm or inconvenience to any customer.
