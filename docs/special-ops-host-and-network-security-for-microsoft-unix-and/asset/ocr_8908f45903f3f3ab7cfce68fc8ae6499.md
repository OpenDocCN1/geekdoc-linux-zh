When profiling your network, it will be important to keep an eye on bandwidth utilization, well as device utilization, for given subnet. Additionally, it would be nice to see statistics from your switches that will tell how much traffic a particular port or VLAN is generating. Most SNMP tools will be able to show a breakdown of protocol usage, which will be helpful in determining what protocol or application is used on a given subnet.

Many free and commercial tools are available; however, each of them has their own special use. Multi-Router Traffic Grapher (MRTG) is terrific for monitoring traffic on network links; however offers no benefit if you are trying to determine what the percentage of utilization a router's CPU is. MRTG is a free tool and can be downloaded at http://people.ee.ethz.ch/~oetik/webtools/mrtg The package will work on both UNIX and NET systems and was written in Perl and C. Using MRTG will give you an accurate representation of how much traffic is on your network links.
