# Internal Security Assessment Methodology

Assessing your internal network does not mean attempting to identify and remediate all vulnerabilities in your network. The sheer number of systems on your internal network, multiplied by an astronomical rate of network changes sets up this strategy for failure. Because securing every host on the internal network may not plausible for most organizations, a number of departments within every company can be determined to deserve special attention. For various reasons, these departments host data could pose significant risk to the welfare of the organization. Assessing your internal network will require you to make difficult decisions about what to secure based on your available resources. You will need to secure the assets, from the right risks, with the right measures.

# Enumerating Business Operations

Before beginning discussion of how to correct internal security issues in your most sensitive environments, you need to determine what you have and where you are most vulnerable. Every location where your organization hosts sensitive data will have different profiles that you will need to take into account when developing solutions for securing them. For example, a financial services company will be more concerned about protecting the privacy and integrity of their clientele's fiscal data but a company whose primary product is software applications will prioritize securing their development networks.

If you are new to the organization, you should quickly begin to schedule interviews and make informal and formal requests for this information. Remember, you can't secure what you don't understand.
