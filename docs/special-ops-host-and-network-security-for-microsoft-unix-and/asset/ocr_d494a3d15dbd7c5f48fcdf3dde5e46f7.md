After you rename the Administrator account, the next step is to delete the Guest account. The Guest account is another attacking point for numerous tools that can provide sensitive information to the attacker even if the account is disabled. In the case of securing the OS, less is always better. Here are the steps necessary for deleting the Guest account:

+   - Open the **Users** folder within the **Local Users and Groups** plug-in and right-click on the **Guest** under the name column. Notice that it has a red X on the account meaning it is currently disabled.
- Scroll down to click **Delete**. You will then be prompted to remove, as shown in Figure 4.18. Click **Yes**.
