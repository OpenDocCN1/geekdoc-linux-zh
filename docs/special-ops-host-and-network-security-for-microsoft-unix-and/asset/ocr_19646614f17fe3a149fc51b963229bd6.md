# Reviewing the Mechanics of DNS

+   - Different types of DNS records produce a wide variety of information.

+   - ICANN is the central IP and domain authority, but registrars have received delegate authority.

+   - The redundancy of top-level domains (TLD) should fit your business needs for redundancy.

+   - DNS packets are broken up into four sections. The FLAG bits significantly impact the way DNS traffic is passed from client to server.

+   - DNS will use TCP packets during zone transfers and zone queries larger than 576 bytes.
