# Creating New Policies

You have already prepared the majority of the work necessary to create your policies, through gathering support, scoping your project and participants, and you should select controls. You now can begin to create your policies, and you should start by planning a structure for your policy documents. Many successful policies use a “hierarchy” structure, where one central document defines specific principles and goals of the policy program. Smaller, more specific documents tie this central policy document to address certain areas, such as e-mail or user management policies. This helps to maintain a structure for policies and impose a consistency and readability throughout all policies in your program.

When creating your policies, you should have already identified your high-risk areas. These require additional attention to the policies you choose. It sounds obvious, but spend more time on these high-risk areas, such that you cover all the necessary elements. Pay close attention to the controls you require and make certain that the policies in high-risk areas are especially clear and succinct.
