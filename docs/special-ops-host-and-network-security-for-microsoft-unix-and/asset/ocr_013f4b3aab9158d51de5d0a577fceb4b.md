Sniffer Pro has the capability to decode the raw packet dumps into an intelligible interface, giving the network administrator the ability to interpret the results. Another solid feature of this commercial tool is the capability to provide a graph-based view of the protocols in use on your network, as well as the percent of utilization. This concept is crucial to helping you understand the flow of traffic on your networks. Although other network monitors allow the aggregation of this data, Sniffer Pro provides the information by default and in an easy-to-read format.
