### Search of Open Relays
Do you think you need an SMTP relay? A surprising number of network managers think that they do due to the fact that they have POP3 or IMAP4 clients. Or perhaps another SMTP host in the organization is using that as an SMTP relay. However, SMTP can be configured to relay only for the required clients and no one else. *There is no valid reason to have an SMTP relay that is open to the Internet.*
nRight now, as you are reading this, some spammer’s “bot” is scanning through all IP addresses in an IP address subnet looking for hosts that support SMTP relay. Once they find one, they will use that SMTP host as a relay for hundreds, thousands, or maybe millions of spam or UCE (unsolicited commercial e-mail) messages. For more information about spam, see www.cauce.org.\nUnfortunately, if your SMTP open relay is ever discovered, and it will be, the source of those millions of spam messages is going to originate from your server. This will use your bandwidth and your resources to deliver these messages.
