# Get Up to Speed on Red Hat Linux 7.2—in a Weekend!

The big day is Monday. The day you get to show off what you know about Red Hat Linux. The problem is, you’re not really up to speed. Maybe it’s been a while since you worked with Linux. Or maybe you just like a challenge. In any event, we’ve got a solution for you—Red Hat Linux 7.2 Weekend Crash Course. Open the book Friday evening and on Sunday afternoon, after completing 30 fast, focused sessions, you’ll be able to jump right in and install, use, and manage Red Hat Linux. It’s as simple as that.
