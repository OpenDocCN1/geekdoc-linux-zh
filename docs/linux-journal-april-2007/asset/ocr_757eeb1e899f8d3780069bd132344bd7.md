# ## 2 Systems in One

Built on open standards, the Gemini 2U elegantly accommodates two discrete motherboards in a 25" chassis uniquely designed for easy access from the rear.

Gemini 2U represents the realization of interlacing power and superior environmental specifications, with considerably less power consumption, less heat and less noise. Remarkably, it all fits nicely into any standard rack. Front to back, Gemini 2U is both powerful and efficient.
