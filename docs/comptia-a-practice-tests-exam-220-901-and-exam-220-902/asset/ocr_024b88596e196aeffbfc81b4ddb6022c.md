108. C. A soft reset is the gentlest of the resets. In fact, it will retain data for running applications. Many iPhone users will know the soft reset as the way they turn their phone off and on normally. Press and hold the Sleep/Wake button until the red slider appears and then drag the slider to turn the device off. To turn the phone back on, press and hold the Sleep/Wake button again until you see the Apple logo.
