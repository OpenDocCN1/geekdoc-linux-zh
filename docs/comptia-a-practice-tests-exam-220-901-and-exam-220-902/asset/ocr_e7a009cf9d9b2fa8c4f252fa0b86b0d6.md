+   - Temperature, humidity level awareness and proper ventilation
+   - Power surges, brownouts, blackouts
+       - Battery backup
+       - Surge suppressor
+   - Protection from airborne particles
+       - Enclosures
+       - Air filters/mask
+   - Dust and debris
+       - Compressed air
+       - Vacuums
+   - Compliance to local government regulations
