**88. A.** This is an example of spear phish ing, which is a specific form of social engineering. With spear phish ing, the attacker uses information that the target would be less likely to question because it appears to be coming from a trusted source. (When, in reality, the attacker in this case has hacked a friend’s email account.) Because it appears far more likely to be a legitimate message, it cuts through your standard defenses like a spear, and the likelihood that you would click this link is higher.

**89. A.** Social engineering is a process in which an attacker attempts to acquire information about your network and system by social means, such as talking to people in the organization. A social engineering attack may occur over the phone, by email, or in person. When the attempt is made through email or instant messaging, this is known as phish ing, and it’s often made to look as if a message is coming from sites where users are likely to have accounts (banks, eBay, and PayPal are popular).
