# **1.4.2 &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Election Committee**

The Election Committee is an ad hoc committee of LSB contributors, who are appointed by the Steering Committee to elect a new chair when the chair position becomes vacant or the term of office expires. The committee shall have two to six members and is responsible for selecting one or more candidates for chair at least 30 days in advance of the election date.
