# Introduction

Now that you have been given a tour of the Open Office Writer, you are ready to move on to the next application in the Open Office suite, the spreadsheet application, Calc. While word processing is probably the most common task performed on a PC by both home and business users, spreadsheets are by far the most commonly used business applications. Virtually any business of any size needs to keep financial records and perform financial calculations. For most Windows users, this means using Microsoft Excel, which is shown in Figure 9.1.
