That brings us the last option on the desktop configuration, that of screensavers. This is probably somewhat familiar to you because it is quite similar to selecting and configuring screensavers in Windows. You simply select the screensaver you want and the number of minutes KDE should wait before launching the screensaver. For our example, we will pick six minutes. You then decide whether or not to require a password. At this point, your screen should look very much like the one shown in Figure 4.7.
