In either case you will end up at a screen much like the one shown in Figure 13.24.

From here you can create a new e-mail message, a new contact item for your Contact List, a new appointment, or even a new folder. The folder is really important. After a time your Inbox might become quite full. There may be e-mail messages you don't want to discard, but which are crowding your Inbox. You can create folders and move your messages into these folders, thus keeping them organized.

The Edit menu, shown in Figure 13.25, enables you to do the basic text editing functions you have become accustomed to. This includes Select All, Copy, Cut, Paste, and so on. It also has a few options for actions that are strictly e-mail related. You can mark your messages. You can mark them as having been read, unread, important, and others. This is another function that is virtually identical to Microsoft Outlook.
