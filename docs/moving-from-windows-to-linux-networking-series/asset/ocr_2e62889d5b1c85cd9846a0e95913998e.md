After surfing the Net a while, you could end up with lot of bookmarks. It can become quite difficult to find one when you want it. If you go to the Manage Bookmark option, you will see a screen much like the one shown in **Figure 13.15**. This screen enables you to organize your bookmarks. You can create new folders and organize your bookmarks by subject matter. You also can delete bookmarks from here.

The next menu may be the most important one in Mozilla. It is certainly the most complex. This is the Tools menu, shown in **Figure 13.16**. There is a similar menu found in Internet Explorer. The first option does not require any explanation because it searches the Web as its title suggests.

The rest of the options under Tools are managers for various functions you may encounter when visiting a particular Web site. For example, on some Web pages you will fill out forms with some sort of information. Form Manager enables you to customize that experience.
