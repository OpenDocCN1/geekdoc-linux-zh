# To duplicate a layer:

+   1. Open the Layers & Channels dialog box by right clicking on the image and choosing Layers-> Channels from the pop-up menu (**Figure 3**).
2. In the layer window, click the name of the layer you want to duplicate. The layer will become highlighted and active.
3. Click the Duplicate button or press Ctrl+C on the keyboard.
or
In the layer window, right-click on the name of the layer you want to duplicate and choose Duplicate Layer from the pop-up menu (**Figure 7**).
The duplicate layer will appear in the layer window with the word "copy" after the original layer's name (**Figure 8**).
