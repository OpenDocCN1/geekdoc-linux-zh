# The GIMP Screen

When you launch the GIMP the only thing that appears on the screen is the GIMP toolbox. To start a new image, in the GIMP toolbox choose File->New or press Ctrl+N on the keyboard. To open an existing image, in the GIMP toolbox choose File->Open or press Ctrl+O.
