# Layer Masks and Filters

Using layer masks, you can apply semi-transparent textures to images. As you probably remember, black areas of a layer mask are transparent, white areas are opaque, and gray areas are semi-transparent. When you apply a filter to a layer mask, the black, white, and gray the filter translates to an interesting texture (To find out layer masks and how to use them, turn to pages 200–206.)

![](img/5164bc49fb24f10213e18cc994b7b40c_259_0.png)
