# Merging and Flattening Layers

Merging and flattening layers perform essentially the same thing, but with one very important difference. When layers are merged, only the ones that are visible (with the eye icon showing in the Layers & Channels dialog box) are combined into one layer. When layers are flattened, all visible and invisible are combined into one layer.

To merge all visible layers:

+   Right-click on the image and choose Layers→Merge Visible Layers from the pop-up menu (Figure 32).

or

+   In the Layers & Channels dialog box, right-click in the layer window and choose Merge Visible Layers from the pop-up menu (Figure 33).

or

+   Press Ctrl+M on the keyboard.

The Layer Merge Options dialog box will appear (Figure 34).
