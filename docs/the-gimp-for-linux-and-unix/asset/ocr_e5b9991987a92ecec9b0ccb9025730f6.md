# Chapter 5

To zoom in:

+   1. Select the Magnify Tool from the Toolbox or press Shift+M on the keyboard.

![](bbox=[0.415, 0.096, 0.483, 0.137])

+   2. Click in the image window.

or
Right-click on the image and choose View→Zoom In from the pop-up menu (Figure 2).

or
Press the = (equals) key on the keyboard.

+   Tip
- To quickly view the image at its actual size, press 1 on the keyboard.

![](bbox=[0.532, 0.053, 0.933, 0.355])

Figure 2. Choose View→Zoom In from the pop-up menu.

To zoom out:

+   1. Select the Magnify Tool from the Toolbox or press Shift+M on the keyboard.

+   2. Press down the Shift key on the keyboard, then click in the image window.

or
Right-click on the image and choose View→Zoom Out from the pop-up menu (Figure 3).

or
Press the – (hyphen) key on the keyboard.

+   Tip
- To quickly view the image at its actual size, press 1 on the keyboard.

![](bbox=[0.532, 0.513, 0.933, 0.81])

Figure 3. Choose View→Zoom Out from the pop-up menu.
