4. To create a sharp corner at a control point:

A. Position the mouse crosshair over a handle (Figure 28).

B. Press and hold down the Shift key.

C. Press the left mouse button and drag the handle to a new position (Figure 29). Notice that instead of curving, a sharp bend appears in the selection segment at the control point.

![](bbox=[0.61, 0.068, 0.86, 0.272])

D. Release the left mouse button when you are happy with the shape of the bend (Figure 30). Once you are satisfied with the shape of the selection segments, the final step is to activate the bezier selection.

Figure 29. Press the left mouse button and drag the handle to a new position. As you drag, a bend forms at the curve.

# To activate the bezier selection:

Click inside the bezier selection area to activate the selection. The control points will disappear and the selection segments will change to a dashed, moving boundary (Figure 31).

![](bbox=[0.61, 0.38, 0.86, 0.585])

Figure 30. Release the mouse button and Shift key when you are happy with the curve.

+   * Tips

+   - Bezier selections are complicated and time consuming to create. Once you've created one, you'll probably want to use it again in the future, so save it. Turn to page 86 for directions how to do this.

+   - The Bezier Selection Tool supports both antialiasing and feathering. To learn more about these options and turn them on, turn to page 62.

+   - You can add to, subtract from, or intersect a selection. For details how to do this turn to pages 77–79.
