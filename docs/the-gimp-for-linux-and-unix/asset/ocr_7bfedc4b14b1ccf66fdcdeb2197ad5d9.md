# Getting Images Out of the Way

If you are working on several images at one time, you may want to get a few of the image windows out of the way but not close them. There are two ways to do this: minimize the window or roll it

Figure 40. Click the button on the Control Panel that corresponds to the image you want to view.

# To minimize an image window:

Click the Minimize button in the upper right-hand corner of the image window (Figure 39).

# To view a minimized image window:

Depending upon the type of Linux/Unix distribution you are using, a minimized window will appear differently. For RedHat Linux or KDE, just click the button on the Control Panel to view a minimized window (Figure 40).

# To roll up an image window:

Double-click on the Title Bar. The image will roll up, displaying only the Title Bar (Figure 41).

# Tip

+   - To roll the image window, double-click on the Title Bar again.

# To close an image window:

Right-click on the image window and choose File→Close from the pop-up menu (Figure 42).

or

Press Ctrl+W on the keyboard.

or

Click the Close button in the upper right-hand corner of the image window (Figure 43).
