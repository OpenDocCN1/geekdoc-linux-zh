# Deactivating and Restoring a Selection

It's easy to deactivate and restore selections with a few clicks of the mouse or key presses on the keyboard.

# To deactivate a selection:

Right-click on the image and choose Select→None from the pop-up menu (Figure 82).

or Press Shift+Ctrl+A on the keyboard.

or Select the Rectangular, Elliptical, or Free-hand Selection Tools from the GIMP Toolbox and click on the image.

# Removing a Selection Before You Finish It

If you are making a selection and realize you didn't want to create it, there's a way out. Before releasing the left mouse button (which is pressed when dragging a selection boundary), press and hold down the right mouse button. Release, the left mouse button and then the right mouse button. The selection you were will disappear.

While the finger coordination is a bit tricky, this can be useful when dealing with complex, multiple selections.
