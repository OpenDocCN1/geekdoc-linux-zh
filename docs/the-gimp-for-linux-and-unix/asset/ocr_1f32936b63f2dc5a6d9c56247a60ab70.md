# The Crop Tool

When an image is cropped, unneeded edges or portions of the image are cut away.

To crop an image:

+   1. Click the Crop Tool in the Toolbox to select it or press Shift+C on the keyboard. The mouse pointer will change to a crosshair.

+   2. Position the mouse at the upper-left corner of the area you want to keep.

+   3. Press the left mouse button and drag diagonally to the right to select the area you want to keep (Figure 7). Horizontal and vertical lines forming a cropping rectangle will appear, indicating the area that you have selected. The rectangle will contain four handles—black squares—that let you resize and move the cropping rectangle. Also, a Crop Information dialog box will open (Figure 8).

+   4. If you need to make the cropping rectangle smaller or larger, you can resize it:
