# Two Special Plug-ins

There are many special graphics plug-ins that come with the GIMP, but we will discuss just two of them here: Gfig and IfsCompose. Gfig is a self-contained drawing program that works with the GIMP to let you create geometric patterns and shapes. IfsCompose lets you repetitive, complex geometric shapes and patterns, such as snowflakes and fern leaves.
