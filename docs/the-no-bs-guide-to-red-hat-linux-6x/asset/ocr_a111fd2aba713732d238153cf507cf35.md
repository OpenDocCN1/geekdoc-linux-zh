Let's take a quick tour of the GnoRPM interface, starting with the main window as shown in Figure 14-1. The Package panel on the left side of the window shows the packages installed on your system. You can navigate the tree-like structure by clicking on the plus (+) and minus (-) signs. Clicking on a folder icon will display the packages from that category in the Display panel, to the right of the Package panel. The toolbar provides buttons for the most common RPM operations, such as "Install," "Uninstall," "Query," and "Verify." Generally, you select one or more icons in the Display panel and then press one of the buttons on the toolbar to act on them.
