# ## Who Makes Notebooks?

Although many name-brand computer manufacturers, such as IBM, Compaq, and Dell, market notebook computers, you might be surprised to learn that many branded laptops are actually made under contract by other companies! This is actually good news, because it means that there might be other models quite similar to the one you purchase that can share upgrades, add-ons, or additional peripherals. Notebooks are notorious for requiring specifically manufactured hardware such as batteries or RAM, or replacement items such as floppy drives or keyboards.

According to International Data Corp., in 1998 the majority of laptops sold in the U.S. were made by Toshiba, Compaq, Dell, IBM, and Gateway. The rest of the market was split among other vendors.

In some cases, you might be able to save quite a bit of money by buying a similar off-brand notebook. The trade-off may be in service, warranty, or convenience, such as being able to buy only through mail order, or only receiving technical support from a Web page. You’ll have to make that choice.
