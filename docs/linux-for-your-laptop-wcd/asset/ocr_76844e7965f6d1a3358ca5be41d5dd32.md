# **HP 340cbi**

The HP340-series printer is a veteran portable printer that has been around for a while. Built with typical HP ruggedness, this printer is smaller than some of the smallest of the HP inkjet models, such as the HP400. This printer features the following:

+   * Removable NiCad battery
+   * IrDA port (an attachment to the Centronics port on the printer)
+   * Standard parallel-printer cable
+   * Dimensions: 12.2 inches × 2.6 inches × 5.8 inches
+   * Weight: 4.3 to 5.4 lbs.
+   * Speed: 2 pages per minute for quality black-and-white and 4 to 7 minutes per page for color
+   * Dots per inch: 600×300 for black-and-white and 300×300 for color
+   * Pages per charge: not available
+   * Paper weight range: 16- to 24-lb. paper
+   * Pages per cartridge: not available
+   * Time to charge: not available
+   * Lifespan of battery: not available
+   * Sheet-feeder capacity: 30 sheets of 16-lb. paper

This printer, although labeled as a portable, would not be a welcome companion in your backpack or shopping bag. However, it is fairly rugged, and it’s more likely than most to survive shaking, banging, and vibration. Happily, it is quite compatible with Linux as a local or networked printer. Unhappily, its IrDA port does not work with the current Linux IrDA implementation or utilities. This is unfortunate, because this printer is a workhorse, includes a 30-page sheet feeder, and otherwise works well attached to a notebook or a LAN.
