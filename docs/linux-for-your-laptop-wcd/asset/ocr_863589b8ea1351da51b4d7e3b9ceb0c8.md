In the Send field, type in your username, and then click on the Done button. Next, click on the ord: entry, click on Edit, and enter your password in the Send field. Click on Done to finish. Note that that script only needs part of a recognized phrase, such as ogin: for login:, but requires your entire username and password. If the script looks okay, click on Done, verify the save, and click on Done again. You'll then see the main netcfg window, as shown in Figure 15.10.

To start your PPP connection, click to select the ppp0 interface, and then click on the Activate button. You should hear your modem dialing (especially if you use the `ATML11kW` command to set the volume and save the settings). To end your PPP session, select the interface and then click on the Deactivate button.
