# DVD on the Horizon?

Despite the insipid machinations by the U.S. motion picture industry over the cracking of Digital Video Disc (DVD) encryption, Linux users might soon have a legal way to play and enjoy DVD movies. Although you can still download, build, and install the DeCSS decryption package and related programs from numerous sites on the Internet, a company named InterVideo announced in early 2000 that an inexpensive, binary-only (of course) driver would be developed and released for Linux six months later. Check http://www.intervideo.com/ for the latest details.

If you have a Creative Labs Dxr2 DVD card for your PC, you can download drivers from http://opensource.creative.com/. Note that Creative’s product is a hardware DVD decoder and will not work with notebooks!
