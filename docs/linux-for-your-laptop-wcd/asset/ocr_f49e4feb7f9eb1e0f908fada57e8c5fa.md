# Resource Information

**http://home.snafu.de/wehe/energy_laptops.html**

Technical details about laptop batteries, alternative power sources, and UPS devices.

**http://www.linuxdoc.org/HOWTO/mini/Battery-Powered.html**

Extensive information about batteries, notebooks, and Linux-specific software.

**http://www.linux.org/help/ldp/howto/IR-HOWTO-9.html**

A short blurb about saving power by controlling Linux IrDA.

**http://www.planetit.com/techcenters/docs/mobile_computing/expert/PIT19990428S0018**

A short article on power-saving tips for laptop users (it’s not Linux-centric). An interesting idiosyncrasy of Windows (as if there aren’t enough) is that apparently you can save power by keeping a CD-ROM in the CD drive bay!

**apmsleep man page**

Power-management control for mobile Linux users. Loads of details about using APM from the command line.

**asapm man page**

Details how to configure this X11 client to support standby and suspend modes when power reaches critical levels on your notebook.

**http://www.laptoptravel.com**

A sample online store catering to laptop users’ needs, such as offering solar panels for laptops.

**Replacement laptop batteries**

Many different online dealers cater to replacing your laptop’s battery. Check your manufacturer’s price first, and then compare!
