This book would not have been possible without the support of Lynette Quinn, my mentor in the publishing business. Her enthusiasm helps make writing about Linux easy. But the real writing talent comes from those programmers who write software for Linux. Laptop users owe a large debt of gratitude to David Hinds for his work on providing PCMCIA support for Linux. Kudos also from a new Apple iBook user to Benjamin Herrenschmidt for his work on making Linux work on Motorola's PowerPC platforms. Thanks are also due to the talented programmers of The XFree86 Project, Inc. for crafting the best networking graphics system in the world. Finally, thanks to Greg Pryzby, Tim Bogart, and numerous fellow Northern Virginia Linux Users Group members for their camaraderie and fellowship (and for not making me feel like an idiot, even after I posted online gaffs to the mailing list).
