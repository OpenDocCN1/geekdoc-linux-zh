# ## Supported Hardware

As I mentioned, there are Linux distributions for a variety of computers and central processing units (CPUs). Although they are best known for their support of Intel-based computers, there are also Linux distributions from different vendors supporting the Digital (now Compaq) Alpha, Sun Microsystems’ SPARC, the ARM series, and Motorola’s Dragonball series of CPUs.

For Motorola PowerPC chip users, there are Linux distributions for a variety of Macintosh operating system hardware, including legacy Motorola, StarMax, PowerComputing, and Umax computers. Linux also runs on various IBM reduced-instruction set computers (RISCs).

In general, if you have an Intel-based x486 computer or better with 16MB of RAM and 500MB of free hard drive space, you can run Linux.
