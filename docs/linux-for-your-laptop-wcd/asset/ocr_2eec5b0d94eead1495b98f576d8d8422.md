# Tuxtops: Laptops with Linux

![](bbox=[0.08, 0.12, 0.57, 0.61])

**Tuxtops**’ corporate mission is to create the frontier of Linux on mobile computers. Our goal is to make mobile computers running Linux just as enjoyable, just as robust, and just as fast as Linux running on other computer platforms.

When you turn on a Tuxtops computer for the first time, the Windows X subsystem will be working properly, all the special laptop power management features will be enabled and operational, the correct sound drivers will be installed and configured, everything ready to go to work for you. You will have every major system setting, including the root password, your network settings, time zone, and ssh keys configured in under 5 minutes, and on the very first boot.

For more information please visit www.Tuxtops.com

# COMPANY HISTORY

Tuxtops™ was founded in November 1999 to provide top quality laptop computers with Linux preinstalled and preconfigured on them.

Our corporate mission is make computing on mobile computers with Linux just as enjoyable an experience as any other Linux supported platform.

# Our Name

Tuxtops™ is derived from the name of the Linux mascot, Tux the penguin, and of course, laptops.

From here on in, things are only going to become easier for all Linux enabled mobile computer users. We are fully committed to keeping mobile computers and its associated technology on the very bleeding edge of Linux.

Tuxtops currently offers three models to meet your mobile computing needs: Value, Ultralight, and Premium. You choose the features you want and we’ll make sure they work, right out of the box.

# But I Like Some Windows Features and Software!

Don’t worry, we realize that many of our customers may not want to give up some of their favorite software packages.

That’s why we offer a Dual- Boot package, Windows98 and Linux Red Hat.
