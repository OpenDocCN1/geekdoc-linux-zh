# Resource Information

**time.nist.gov**
Need to get the most accurate time in the world, provided by an atomic clock? This is the place to point your rdate command!

**The Clock Mini-HOWTO**
Ron Bean's short but technical discussion of computer clocks and Linux.
**http://www.isbister.com/worldtime/**
One of many Web sites offering world time zone, date, and time information.
**http://aa.usno.navy.mil/AA/faq/docs/world_tzones.html**
A world time zone map from the U.S. Naval Observatory, with links to astronomical clients.
**http://aa.usno.navy.mil/AA/**
Detailed explanations about the sun, moon, time, calendars, and navigation. Links are provided to information about weather, maps, and tides.
**http://developer.apple.com/techpubs/mac/OSUtilities/OSUtilities-94.html**
Everything you need to know about time on the Macintosh.
“Understanding Why Time Is Running Out,” p. 243, *Sams Teach Yourself Linux Programming in 24 Hours*, by Warren W. Gay, Sams Publishing, 1999
An interesting explanation of when the Linux time value may cause problems.
**http://www.linuxsxa.org.au/tips/time.html**
A roundup of tips on using Linux, clocks, and time, along with time zones.
**http://docs.linux.cz/programming/c/glibc-manual-0.02/library_19.html**
An explanation of the TZ environment variable.
**http://www.redhat.com/legal/y2k_statement.html**
Red Hat, Inc.'s Y2K statement.
**http://tycho.usno.navy.mil/voice.html**
Lists phone numbers for the U.S. Naval Observatory's voice clock.
