# PART I
# Putting Linux on Your Notebook

Chapters in this part aim to help you understand your notebook, its components, and associated technology in preparation for installing Linux. You will learn how to choose a notebook for Linux, and will read about the number of ways Linux may be installed. You'll gain experience in devising partitioning strategies, learn about different types of Linux installations, and be given a step-by-step walk-through of a sample install. You'll also learn about configuring Linux to work with other notebook components after the install. The in-formation outlined in these chapters will help make you an expert at installing Linux on your notebook.

+   1  **Choosing a Notebook for Linux**
+   2  **Taking Inventory**
+   3  **Basic Installation Preliminaries**
+   4  **General Installation Procedures**
+   5  **Starting the Installation**
+   6  **Post-Installation Issues**
