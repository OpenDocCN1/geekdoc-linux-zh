Several laptop models from manufacturers such as Sony appeared on the market in 1999 with FireWire ports. A typical use is for the transfer of high-bandwidth data, such as digital video. Unfortunately, at the time this chapter was written, there was no Linux FireWire support (and there were few FireWire devices on the market, aside from several digital video cameras, a few PCI adapters, and a couple external hard drives).

There is no stable support for FireWire under Linux (yet). You can check the GNU/Linux IEEE 1394 Development Project, hosted by Emanuel Pirkler at http://ec1.ipt. uni-klu.ac.at/ieee1394/index.html
