> Figure 15.14 Select your IP address assignment by clicking on Dynamic IP Address or by entering an IP address.

In the dialog box, type in an account name. Next, enter your ISP's phone number. Click on the drop-down menu to select a connection negotation type, such as script- based (use PAP if that is what your ISP requires). When finished, click on the IP tab at the top of the dialog box. You'll see the window shown in Figure 15.14.

If your ISP has given you a static IP address, enter it here. If your ISP, like most, assigned the address dynamically, click on Dynamic IP Address. When finished, select the DNS tab. You'll see the Domain Name Service setup dialog box, as shown in Figure 15.15.

Enter your ISP's domain name, such as erols.com, along with the IP addresses of your ISP's DNS servers. These entries will be placed in your system's /etc/resolv.conf file during your PPP connection. They help your Internet clients “resolve” plain hostnames, such as www.primetech.com, into numerical IP addresses.

When finished, select the Login Script tab. Enter the script of your login to your ISP, as shown in Figure 15.16.

Select Expect from the drop-down field on the left, enter ogin, and click on the Add button. Next, click to select Send, and then enter your username. Repeat this step, except with the phrase ssword: and your password. This is similar to the netcfg script discussed in the preceding section. When finished, click on the OK button.
