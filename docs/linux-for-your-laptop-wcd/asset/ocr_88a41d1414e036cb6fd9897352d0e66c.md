Want to protect your notebook and, if it is stolen, perhaps track the thief? Browse to http://savelaptop.zzzweb.com/, and register your laptop with Moonicer Salem's server. You can then download and install his laptop-tracking software for Linux. The tracker command, according to Salem, "assists another user or yourself in finding your laptop by sending vital selection information to our servers each time your laptop is connected to the Internet."

The tracker command is a small 40,000-character binary file. It uses a simple command line consisting of your username and password at Salem's server, your laptop's bar code, and a designated network interface to watch, such as eth0 or ppp0. This command should be renamed and then run at startup from an initialization script. Should someone steal your Linux laptop and then get on the Internet, you might be able to track the logon's point of origin. Versions of the software are also available for other operating systems.

# ## Protecting Your Notebook

There's nothing like good protection when toting your notebook around. Some notebooks are easy to carry, and others slip through damp fingers very quickly. Fortunately, manufacturers are beginning to realize that many notebooks are routinely slung into bags, plopped onto car seats, and precariously carried around.

# ### Can't Afford a ToughBook? Try an iBook!

You can buy ruggedized notebooks, often categorized as "Industrial" or "Field Grade," but unless you're the U.S. Army and can afford to spend $10,000 on parachute-deployable computer equipment, you will probably have to make do with commercial consumer offerings. In the Intel-based class of notebooks, the Panasonic ToughBooks are a slam-dunk choice, and Linux works well with most models. If you're a PowerPC user, the Apple iBooks (a new model was introduced while this book was being written), like the ToughBooks, feature a built-in handle. This is unique among all notebooks and is very handy if you need to walk across campus or strap a computer to your saddle. Both note- books are rugged and feature tough exteriors, but the ToughBook line gets the nod for the most protection against moisture and dust.
