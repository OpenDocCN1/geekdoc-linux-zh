# Maintaining Your Battery

Proper maintenance of your notebook's battery is essential if you want to get the most life out of it and avoid early replacement. Of course, you should never let your battery get wet or try to take it apart. Batteries that are no longer any good should be disposed of properly. Many of the general guidelines for batteries, aside from recharging, also apply to your notebook. Here's a list of do's and don'ts for battery treatment:

+   - Do charge your battery in an environment between 60 and 80 degrees Fahrenheit.
+   - Do drain and store your NiMH battery if you aren't going to use it for several weeks.
+   - Do drain your LiIon battery to 50 percent and store it if you aren't going to use it for more than several weeks.
+   - Do expect shorter battery life if the battery is cold. (Always allow a computer to come up to room temperature if it has been stored in a cold environment.)
+   - Do fully charge any new battery and then discharge it. Repeat this three times.
