# # Listening to Music

Nearly every notebook with an internal or external CD drive supports the playing of audio CDs. Using a laptop’s internal CD drive is more convenient, because sound is generally sent directly to the notebook’s speakers and headphone jack. Some notebooks support the direct play of music CDs without the need for the laptop to be turned on! If you must use an external CD drive with your laptop, you’ll most likely have to use a patch cable connecting the CD drive’s audio or headphone jack to your notebook’s line-in jack.
