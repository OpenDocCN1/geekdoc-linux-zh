# ## Working with an External Monitor

If you really need extra screen real estate or you want to use a larger display, you can hook your notebook up to an external monitor (as long as your notebook or replicator port provides an external video out port). You might need to make a change in your notebook's BIOS during a reboot in order to enable the use of an external display. You might also need to toggle the display of your notebook's LCD on or off by using a special $F n$ key combination on your laptop's keyboard.
