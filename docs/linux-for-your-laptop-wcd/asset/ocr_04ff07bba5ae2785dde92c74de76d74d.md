Linux may also be configured to boot from a PCMCIA device. See section 5.3, "Booting from a PCMCIA Device," in the Linux PCMCIA-HOWTO document. You'll find a copy under the /usr/doc/HOWTO directory or at http://www.linuxdoc.org.
