# # Merits of Three Buttons

The most desirable pointer for use with Linux and the X Window System is the three-button mouse. In right-handed terms, the left mouse button is mouse button 1. It is used to click buttons, click and drag to select text, double-click to launch programs, and so on. The middle mouse button, or mouse button 2, is used to paste text and graphics or grab scrollbars in xterm terminal windows. The right mouse button, or mouse button 3, is used by many X11 window managers for alternative menus, such as when changing the properties of a right-clicked object.

The majority of notebooks on the market have only two mouse buttons, probably due to the fact that most notebooks, aside from Apple’s line of Macintosh notebooks (which have only one mouse button), are manufactured in a monopolistic software market. Because Windows and its variants use only two mouse buttons, why include a third mouse button at additional cost? Thankfully, at least one manufacturer, IBM, sees fit to produce laptops with three buttons. This makes life with Linux a bit easier!
