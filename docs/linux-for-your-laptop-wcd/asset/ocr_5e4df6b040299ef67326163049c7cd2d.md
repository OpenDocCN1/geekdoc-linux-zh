The graphical Red Hat installer offers several choices of system. Choose a system by clicking on its icon. If you’re upgrading, click on the Upgrade icon. Remember that workstation installations require more hard drive space than server systems. If you’d rather choose your own software, click on the Custom button, and then click on the Next button. You’ll see the dialog box shown in Figure 5.12.

You’ll see the two partitions reported earlier by FIPS. Click to highlight the larger partition (the desired Linux partition), and then click on the Delete button to delete it. Next, click on the Add button. You’ll see the dialog box shown in Figure 5.13.
