# Choosing
components

When the first IBM Compatibles hit the market, the revolution of open architecture hardware like Linux revolutionized the world of operating systems. Now, it's possible to buy individual components and customize a computer to your exact requirements, and to build your operating system similarly. Rather than relying on what a designer far removed from your situation thought best, you could build something that works the way you want it.

If you've never built your own computer or customised your operating system, don't panic! You're capable of tapping Tab into BIOS and learning how to read is no difficult. Simply grab your screwdrivers and put aside a frame that is built specifically around your requirements. In the case of the example that follows, that means a future-proof mid-range system with room to grow as your needs expand, alongside an operating system that you've created exactly to your tastes.
