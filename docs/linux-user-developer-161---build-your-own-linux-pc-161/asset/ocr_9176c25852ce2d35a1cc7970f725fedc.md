# A Good Day To Pi Hard

Many teams explored means of movement, and Niklas Henderson impressed us with a segmented robot intended to give fine control through the use of a central pivot. This was our first venture into robotics, Niklas told us.

"We got a Raspberry 2 and a pencil down the middle, that allows it to bend and it creates steering for the robot. We were hoping that it would be able to bend slightly more, but the bending is a bit limited - a design we didn't expect... Imagine having three sections to it - that means in the middle bit it can bend the right way, and allows it to go in a circle. Like a snake!"
