# # Let's encrypt enters public base

# 100,000 certificates issued within a week

Let's encrypt, the initiative to create an automated certificate authority to make it easier and easier for developers to add SSL to their sites, has entered public base.

"We have more to do before we are comfortable dropping the label," says the Internet Security Group's Josh Aa, "particularly on the client experience. Automation is a strategy of ours, and we know how to scale to a wide range of platforms. We are monitoring feedback from users closely, and making improvements as quickly as possible."

Using automated verification to verify issuing certificates for free, Let's Enc is off to a grand start. In the first week of 100,000 individual certificates, figures, including the 26,000 issued during a closed beta period.

Interested parties are invited out and apply for the service by visiting letsencrypt.org and following the documentation.
