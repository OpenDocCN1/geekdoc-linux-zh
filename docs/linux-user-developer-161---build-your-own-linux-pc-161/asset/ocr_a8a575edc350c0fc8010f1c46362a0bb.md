# Audio editors

We're looking at the most feature-packed and stable audio editors for Linux to process, and save dialogue or perhaps compose a simple song

# Audacity

One of the most successful open source projects, the branchich of Carnegie Mellon University the most renowned digital audio editor and post-processing software for many years too early to establish Audacity as a reference point, so we will see how others look in front of it first.

Download: bit.ly/1fFTZmQF

# Rezound

Rezound is a lesser-known audio editor, maybe due to the fact that it hasn't been updated for too long, yet it offers a stack of built-in filters and effects. LADSPA plugins work great with most popular file formats. We'll also try to capture sound with it and process it for later use.

Download: bit.ly/1Qqrv15

# KWave

It's certainly simpler than the previous apps, but KWave integrates very well into the KDE desktop and handles the capturing, importing and exporting of audio beautifully. It has tools for editing your recordings, offers encoder setup, handles large files and much more.

Download: bit.ly/1Nv00hj

# Sweep

Another powerful and relatively undiscovered app with capturing and editing capabilities. Sweep doesn't have many filters (although LADSPA plugins can be used), but it is easy to use and for navigating inside an audio file. Sweep is also an aging software, but still works nicely on modern systems.

Download: bit.ly/1Z4wQRX
