
# COMPILER

## Free Pascal Compiler 3.0.0

Speedy cross-platform compiler for the user-friendly language

Free Pascal Compiler - FPC - a compiler both for the Object Pascal dialect, Free Pascal, and for other Delphi dialects. FPC compiles to many platforms and processors, with no change to the source code. For cross-platform Pascal as well, the IDE - built with Free Pascal - works perfectly. Binaries are available for some distributions already, FPC hadn't reached the Ubuntu PPA when we tested, but by adding Debian experimental to our /etc/sources.list and installing with:

```bash
$ apt-get install -t experimental fpc
```

For other distros, a complete package and install script is on the download site. FPC has astonishing met our demands (your PC has more than enough power) with assembler and supports Raspberry Pi without anyone can run at all, even if you have a much more powerful build.

Free Pascal is an object-oriented language, with PostgreSQL and MySQL -it's growing all beyond the teaching language of the Seventies. The documentation is, and if you've never used Pascal a quick look at one of the tutorials, then grab the score for one of the games at pascaldevelopment.com.

### Pros
- Blazing IDE that includes many dialects, integrates with assembler and supports databases

### Cons
- Not many Pascal projects to join, but hopefully that will change in the near future

### Great for...
- Language learners and those who want to review freeppascal.org

## Hedgewars 0.9.22

Action, strategy and violent hedgeogs

Featuring "pink hedgeogs", with attitude battle the depths of hell to the depths of space. This is a strategy game. Obviously, the combination of all war (the aim is to destroy all enemies) and hedgeogs with bazookas makes for surreal carnage, with the level of development thought to you losing a lot of time spent exploring endless.

Installation is easy through the package manager. Hedgewars has been around for a long time, and is packed for most distributions, with new releases quite regularly appearing. Your first task is to read the manual on the Hedgewars wiki, then start the training missions. Get proficient in the art of nodes, parachutes and desert enemies, and can play against others locally or over the network, or at operations. We use 22 different weapons and numerous maps, as well as an infinite number of randomly-generated maps. Customization include "280 costumes, 32 games, 143 types of fonts and unique voice packs" in the main game, as well as community communications, and the possibility of creating your own artwork.
