# 04 Does it work?

When you take the photo in the dark, it may
appear that the NoLR module does not work. The picture is
a right vision spy-style photo; in fact, it is perfectly
black. What you need to change this is an infrared light source.
The LISPARI is an adapter for the camera module which is
designed to provide additional illumination when taking
recording video in the dark. It features extra mounting points,
which are perfect for using custom mounts or a geon-
holder. There are two versions: a standard for the
camera and the NoLR version. The infrared LISPARI (with)
clear LEDs) has 12 LEDs arranged around the camera
module to offer a spread of light when used in low/zero light
conditions, making perfect for capturing night activity.

# 05 Connecting to the LISPARI

The LISPARI is easy to connect: simply use the
female-to-male leads and attach them to the pins on
LISPARI. Connecting to the Raspberry Pi is easy, too the
pins required are 5V, GND and the GPIO 10. In this project
we use the BCS pin numbering system - the physical pin
number is provided here for use. The 5V is connected to physical
pin, the ground is connected to pin 32 or 19, although
several GPIO pins you can use. The final is the GPIO 10
in 19 on the board. Now you have the
LISPARI connected and are ready to take a picture.

import time
