# # Capture photos at night with the NoIR Pi camera

# Set up and deploy a mobile solution to take night-time photos of your garden hidden wildlife

![](bbox=[0.068, 0.675, 0.165, 0.765])

You are already familiar with the Raspberry Pi camera module. However, it is also available in the NoIR edition.
NoIR in this instance is perfect for 'no infrared' usage. With this module, you can take photos and video footage in the dark, similar to a night vision camera. Many of us are fortunate to have a wide variety of animals visiting during the day, but also a large number of nocturnal visitors to your garden. This tutorial shows how to set up your camera, then add and combine infrared light source and PIR sensor to enable the camera to photograph night-time wildlife. Each photo that is captured is saved to the Pi and a 'time stamp' is also added to monitor and track the time the animal visited your garden.

## Attach the NoIR camera module
To get started, add your camera to the Raspberry Pi. It is vital to ensure that you remove all static charge you have built up by touching, such as a radiator fan, first. The blue-coloured labels help identify the HDMI port.
