# PROBLEM 5B
Walking East and North!

Suppose we have an $n \times n$ grid. How many paths are there, following edges of the grid, from the bottom left corner to the top right corner that may touch, but not go above, the diagonal shown in Figure 1.1?

Here is a problem that engaged the Swiss mathematician Leonhard Euler.* By a *triangulation* of a polygon, we mean a way of dividing the polygon in triangles by non-intersecting diagonals, that is, lines joining two vertices.
