# **PROBLEM 15.8**

Can 18 dominoes be placed on a $6 \times 6$ board “faultlessly”?

# **Solution**

Label the internal “struts,” that is, the short lines that a domino might cover, by the numbers 1 to 60, as indicated in Figure 15.2. If 18 (nonoverlapping) dominos are placed on the board, then 18 of these struts will be covered, leaving 42 exposed. These lie on the 10 lines of struts and so, by the pigeonhole principle, at least one of the 10 lines will contain at least 5 exposed struts.

If one of these lines has exactly five exposed struts, then the sixth must be crossed by a domino as in Figure 15.3.

Apart from that one domino, each other domino occupies either two of the squares we have labeled with an A, or two labeled with a B.
