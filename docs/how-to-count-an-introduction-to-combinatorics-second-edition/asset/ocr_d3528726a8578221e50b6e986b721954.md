It is now not difficult to see how to extend this formula to deal with the general case of the number of elements in a union of $n$ sets. The only complication is with the notation needed to deal with this general situation. The theorem gets its name from the inclusion and exclusion processes corresponding to the alternate + and $-$ signs.

# **The Inclusion–Exclusion Theorem**

For all sets $A_1$, $A_2, \dots, A_n$,
