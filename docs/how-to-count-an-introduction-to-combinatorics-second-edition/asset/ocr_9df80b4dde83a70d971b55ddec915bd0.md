# PROBLEM 9A
The Bridges of Königsberg

Is there a route that would have taken the burghers of Königsberg over each of the bridges exactly once?

This problem was solved by Leonhard Euler in 1735. Euler's solution of this problem is regarded as the origin of the branch of mathematics known as graph theory. The “graphs” of graph theory are rather different from the “graphs” of functions you will be familiar with, but in both cases, graph abbreviates graphical representation. In the case of the Königsberg bridges problem, all that really matters is how the different parts of the city are connected by the bridges.
