+   *   i. Suppose that $G$ is drawn in the plane with $f$faces. Since each closed path of $G$ contains at least four edges, each face is bounded by at least four edges and, as each edge is a boundary of at most two faces, hence $4f \le 2e$ and so $2f \le e$. By Euler's formula $f = e-v+2$, and hence $2(e-v+2) \le e$; from it follows that $e \le 2v-4$.
