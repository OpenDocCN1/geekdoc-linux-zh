**17.1.5A** Suppose there is a board, $B$, whose rook polynomial is $1+4 x+7 x^{2}+2 x^{3}+x^{4}$. Since $r_{1}(B)=4$, the board has four white squares. Hence there are $C(4,2)=6$ ways in which two rooks can be put on the board, possibly including cases where they are in the same row or the same column. Hence, $r_{2}(B) \leq 6$. It follows that the term $7 x^{2}$ cannot occur in the rook polynomial of $B$.
