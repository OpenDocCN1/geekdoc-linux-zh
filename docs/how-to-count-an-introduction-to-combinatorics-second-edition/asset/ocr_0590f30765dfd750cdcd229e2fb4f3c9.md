In this way we obtain a one–one correspondence between the partitions of *n* into unequal odd parts and partitions of *n* − *k*² into even parts of size at most 2*k*, for some integer *k* with 1 ≤ *k* ≤ ⌊√*n*⌋. Thus, if *q<sub>d,δ</sub>(n)* is the number of partitions of *n* into even parts of size at most 2*k*, we have that

$$ P_{d,δ}(n) = \sum_{k=0}^{\lfloor\sqrt{n}\rfloor} q_{2k,δ}(n-k^2). $$ (8.16)

It follows from Equation 8.16 that the generating function *P<sub>d,δ</sub>* for the sequence { *p<sub>d,δ</sub>(n)* } is given by

$$ P_{d,δ}(x) = \sum_{n=0}^{∞} \left(\sum_{k=0}^{\lfloor\sqrt{n}\rfloor} q_{2k,δ}(n-k^2)\right) x^n. $$ (8.17)

For a given value of *k*, the sum in Equation 8.17 includes a term *q<sub>2k,δ</sub>(n − k²)* *x<sup>n</sup>*, provided that *k*² ≤ *n*. Hence, when we change the order of summation we get

$$ P_{d,δ}(x) = \sum_{k=0}^{∞} \left(\sum_{n=k^2}^{∞} q_{2k,δ}(n-k^2) x^n\right). $$ (8.18)

Now let *Q<sub>2k,δ</sub>* be the generating function for the sequence { *q<sub>2k,δ</sub>(n)* }. Thus,

$$ Q_{2k,δ}(x) = \sum_{n=0}^{∞} q_{2k,δ}(n) x^n $$

and hence multiplying through by *x<sup>k²</sup>* and then replacing *n* by *n* − *k²* throughout, we have

$$ x^{k^2}Q_{2k,δ}(x) = \sum_{n=0}^{∞} q_{2k,δ}(n) x^{n+k^2} = \sum_{n=k^2}^{∞} q_{2k,δ}(n-k^2) x^n. $$ (8.19)

It follows from Equations 8.18 and 8.19 that

$$ P_{d,δ}(x) = \sum_{k=0}^{∞} x^{k^2} Q_{2k,δ}(x). $$ (8.20)
