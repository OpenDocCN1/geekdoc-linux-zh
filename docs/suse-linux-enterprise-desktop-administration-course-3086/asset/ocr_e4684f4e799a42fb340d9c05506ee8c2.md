# Novell iFolder Account

You must configure at least one Novell iFolder account before you can create Novell iFolders. A Novell iFolder account is created when a Novell iFolder services administrator provisions you as a Novell iFolder user for a Novell iFolder server. The administrator provides you with a username and password. For an enterprise, this might be your username or e-mail address and the related password. A Novell iFolder session begins when you log in to a Novell iFolder services account and ends when you log out, or when you exit the Novell iFolder client.
