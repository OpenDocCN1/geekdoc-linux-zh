# Manage Icons in GNOME

You can manage icons on your desktop in different ways. For simplicity, we will describe only the most important methods.

You can find icons in the following areas on your desktop:

+   - Desktop
+   - Panel
+   - Main Menu

**Desktop** To create an icon for an application on your desktop, select the item in your application menu, drag it to a free space on your desktop, and release the mouse button.

Notice the small plus icon at the mouse pointer when moving the icon. This indicates that a copy of the icon will be created.

To create a new icon, right-click a free space on your desktop. The menu shown in Figure 2-10 pops up.
