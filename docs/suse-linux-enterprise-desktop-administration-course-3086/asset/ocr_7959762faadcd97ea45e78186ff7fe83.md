# Objective 3—Log Out and Shut Down

When you are ready to log out of the system, do the following:

+   1. Open the KDE menu by selecting the first (left) icon in the bottom panel, as shown in Figure C-6.
+   2. At the bottom of the KDE menu, select Log Out. You can also right-click on the window background and select the same option from the menu.
+   3. After you select Log Out, a confirmation dialog, shown in Figure C-7, appears.
