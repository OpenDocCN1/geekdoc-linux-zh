We have just found a binary fraction from a decimal fraction by multiplying by 2 repeatedly. To find a pental fraction from a decimal fraction, we begin with a similar equation and multiply by 5 repeatedly.

By definition, the base five coefficients make this equation true:

$.3333333...=b_{1} \cdot({ }^{1} / 5)^{1}+b_{2} \cdot\left({ }^{1} / 5\right)^{2}+b_{3} \cdot\left({ }^{1} / 5\right)^{3}+...$
