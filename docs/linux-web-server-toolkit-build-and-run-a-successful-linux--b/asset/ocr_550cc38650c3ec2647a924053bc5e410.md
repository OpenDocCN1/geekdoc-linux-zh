# Creating a Plan for Your Site

Once you have the Web site "mission statement" in mind, you can begin to create a plan for getting your site up and running. That plan can include a schedule of when you things to happen. It can be as simple as a Post-it note or as large as a full-wall Gantt chart.

You may need to wait a little to add the details to your plan: you can fill them as you read the rest of this book. The schedule will likely have changes and input from many members of your organization. Some of the things that you should consider in your plan include the items in the following sections.

Web sites are notorious for growing faster than anticipated. Quick decisions are often required in order to move forward with the site. By creating processes and refining how you will create, manage, and update your site, your site can be responsive to new needs without looking though it was all done in a day.
