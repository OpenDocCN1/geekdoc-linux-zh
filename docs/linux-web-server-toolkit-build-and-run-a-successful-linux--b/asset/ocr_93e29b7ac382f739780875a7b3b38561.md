# Summary

This chapter has touched on the business aspects of having a Web site and the importance of bringing people to your site. You've learned about advertising on the Web and getting your site listed on the most popular search engines. In this chapter you've learned the following:

+   - Including your URL on your printed advertising is accepted practice these days. In addition, online advertising reaches users who already browse the Web. Many online forums exist where you can purchase space to promote your Web site.

+   - Listing your site in search engines on the Web is an important part of promoting your site. These listings are free to you, and allow people who are searching for your organization or for products and services like those you offer to find your site rapidly.

+   - Bringing visitors back to your site for return visits is important. The more they learn about you, the more likely they are to buy your product, support your cause, and so forth. Good Web sites with interactive pages and updated information keep people coming back for more.

In the next chapter, you'll learn the basics of how to create simple documents on your Web server that will showcase your organization. These documents are written in HTML, the document language used by everything on the Web.
