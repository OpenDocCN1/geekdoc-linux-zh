Some examples of information that you might include on your site include the ideas in the following list. Some of these ideas are harder to convert to Web (HTML) format than others. In every case, consider who is responsible for creating and maintaining each piece of information, and possible legal ramifications for the things you choose to make publicly available on your Web site:

+   - Product information sheets and brochures
- News and press releases
- Biographies of your employees
- Information on how to order your products and services
- Summary information on your company's products and services
- Company mission statement or overview
- Annual reports (for publicly held companies)
- Lists of favorable press stories and product reviews (with links possible; don't copy the review to your page without permission)
- Lists of dealers or resellers for your products
- Stories of customers who use your products or services
- Newsletter for customers and dealers
- Questions and answers about the company and/or your products
- Technical support information about using your product
- Contact information for different departments in your company, including the Webmaster
- Technical information sheets/white papers/research reports
- References who have used and can recommend your services
- Sample audio clips or video clips showing your offerings
- Downloads of sample software or related tools to show what you do
