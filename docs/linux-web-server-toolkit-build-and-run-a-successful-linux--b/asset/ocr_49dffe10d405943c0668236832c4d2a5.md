# Chapter 7: Connecting to the Internet

You can drill an ISP technical representative with a few of your hardest questions (don't judge the ISP by the salesperson's answers). But it's also important to look at the following telling signs:

+   - How long has the ISP been in business? An ISP with four years' experience is an old-timer. Does it have a reputation among businesses? Does it have a reputation among area technical specialists? Top technical people will know the grapevine if a big ISP is blowing smoke without the expertise to back it up.
- How large is the ISP's support staff? How experienced are the key members of that staff? A larger staff indicates a commitment to solving problems. Even a small staff of professionals is comfort during a storm.
- Can you contact references of the ISP? Ask for a reference that matches your needs closely as possible. That is, if you're planning a partial T1 connection, have technical but not Internet expertise in-house, and want to colocate your server at the ISP, ask to talk to someone who's in a similar situation.

# COST

To misquote a phrase, the bitterness of extended technical problems with an inexperienced ISP remains long after the sweetness of low rates is forgotten. The wrangling of telephone companies, high rates for high-speed digital service is likely to remain part of the Internet access landscape in the foreseeable future.

While competition among ISPs flares up in different areas regularly and causes a shakeout of the latest entrants hoping to cash in the Internet, stable, quality companies with even rates year after year.

Nevertheless, you and should price-shop among those ISPs that meet your selection criteria. Comparing basic access rates for a given speed of connection is fairly trivial; most ISPs produce price lists with these figures. The more important thing to check is the full range of services and fees associated with using an ISP. Check the following list of questions related to services and potential challenges:

+   - What is the basic monthly line charge for my desired line speed?
- What is the setup fee to connect at that?
- What equipment must I provide for your side of the connection and how much do you anticipate it costing? Can you provide discounts on hardware purchased through you or do you recommend a specific model or vendor?
- What are payment terms and methods for my monthly connection fee?
- Do I receive any sort of discount if you experience downtime or equipment problems that block access to my site? What are your policies on that?
