# WHEN IS YOUR SITE EXPERIENCING THE MOST HITS?

Knowing the heaviest traffic time for your site may be more important than knowing the slow time. It's the time you better be certain that your site is up and running at full speed. It's the time you want all the latest information in place, and it's the time when an Internet connection outage can least be tolerated.

# IS THE USE OF THE SITE GROWING OVER TIME?

By reviewing statistics over consecutive days, weeks, and months, you can easily track whether your site is becoming more popular. Save statistical information or graphs so you can compare different periods.

Remember that if you change your site substantially (such as redesigning it with a new look that uses many more or fewer graphics), the hits shown in your statistics are not as relevant across several time periods. However, you can still track trends.

If you are comparing daily totals, compare the same day of the week. Because traffic often varies during every week, just as business does from quarter to quarter, your daily comparisons should be, for example, on several consecutive Wednesdays. Comparing weekly totals may be an easier way for you to see growth trends.
