# **Lemma 3.1** *It holds that*

$$\begin{cases}
\left[1+\xi\left(\frac{\sigma}{s}-1\right)\right]^2s\ge 4(1-\xi)\xi\sigma, & \text{for }s>0,\\[6pt]
\left[1+\xi\left(\frac{\sigma}{s_*}-1\right)\right]^2s\le \max\left\{\sigma,\left[1+\xi\left(\frac{\sigma}{s_*}-1\right)\right]^2s_*\right\}, & \text{for }s\in[s_*,\sigma],
\end{cases}$$

where $0<\xi<0.8$ and $s_*>0$ is a constant.

*Proof.* Define a differentiable function

$$f(s)=\left[1+\frac{\xi}{s}(\sigma-s)\right]^2s$$

for $s>0$. It follows that

$$\dot f(s)=\left[1+\frac{\xi}{s}(\sigma-s)\right]\left[1-\frac{\xi}{s}(\sigma+s)\right]$$

for $s>0$. Denote

$$s^*=\frac{\xi\sigma}{1-\xi}.$$

Then,

$$\dot f(s)\begin{cases}<0, & \text{if }0<s\le s^*,\\=0, & \text{if }s=s^*,\\>0, & \text{if }s^*\le s.\end{cases}$$

So, it shows that $s^*$ must be the minimum point of the function $f(s)$ on the interval $(0,+\infty)$. Then, it holds that

$$\left[1+\frac{\xi}{s}(\sigma-s)\right]^2s\ge 4(1-\xi)\xi\sigma,$$

for $s>0$. And, it holds that

$$\left[1+\frac{\xi}{s}(\sigma-s)\right]^2s\le \max\left\{\sigma,\left[1+\frac{\xi}{s_*}(\sigma-s_*)\right]^2s_*\right\}$$

for all $s\in[s_*,\sigma]$. The proof is complete.
