Meanwhile,

\[\Phi_i^{(l)}(k)=\phi_i(k)-\frac{\xi\lambda_i^{(l)}(k)\displaystyle\sum_{j=1}^{l-1}\lambda_j\left(z_j^{(l)}(k)\right)^2}{w_i^T(k)Cw_i(k)\displaystyle\sum_{j=1}^{p}\lambda_j\left(z_j^{(l)}(k)\right)^2}.\]

Since \(\lim_{k\to\infty}\phi_i=0\), \((i=1,\ldots,p)\), by Lemmas 4.6 and 4.7, we have \(\lim_{k\to\infty}\Phi_i^{(l)}(k)=0\). So, it follows that \(\lim_{k\to\infty}\Psi_i^{(l)}(k)=0\). Thus, there must exist a constant \(\hat K_i\) so that

\[\begin{aligned} w_i^T(k+1)Cw_i(k+1)\ &\geq \sum_{i=1}^{p}\left[1-\xi+\frac{\xi\lambda_i}{\displaystyle\sum_{j=1}^{p}\lambda_j\left(z_j^{(l)}(k)\right)^2}\right]^2\left(z_i^{(l)}(k)\right)^2\lambda_i \\ &\geq \left[1-\xi+\frac{\xi\lambda_p}{\displaystyle\sum_{j=1}^{p}\lambda_j\left(z_j^{(l)}(k)\right)^2}\right]^2\sum_{i=1}^{p}\left(z_i^{(l)}(k)\right)^2\lambda_i \\ &\geq \min_{s\geq 0}\left[1-\xi+\frac{\xi\lambda_p}{s}\right]^2s, \end{aligned}\]

for all \(k>\hat K_i\). By Lemma 4.1, it holds that

\[w_i^T(k+1)Cw_i(k+1)\geq 4(1-\xi)\xi\lambda_p,\]

for \(k\geq\hat K_i\). Since \(\lambda_1\lVert w_i(k)\rVert^2\geq w_i^T(k+1)Cw_i(k+1)\), it follows that

\[\lVert w_i(k+1)\rVert\geq\sqrt{\frac{4(1-\xi)\xi\lambda_p}{\lambda_1}}>0,\]

for \(k\geq\hat K_i\), where \(0<\xi<1\).

Then, from Lemma 4.6, it is easy to see that

\[\lVert w_i(k+1)\rVert\leq (1-\xi)^k\lVert w_i(0)\rVert+\xi\frac{M_1}{\lambda_p}\sum_{i=1}^{k}\frac{(1-\xi)^{i-1}}{\lVert w_1(k-i)\rVert}.\]
