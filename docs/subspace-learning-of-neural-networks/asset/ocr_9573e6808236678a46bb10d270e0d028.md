$\theta_1=\ln\left(\frac{(1+\eta a)(a+3b)}{(1+\eta a)(a+3b)+\eta ab\bar K\,Kurt(s_i)}\right)^2>0.$

**Proof:** Since $z(0)\notin V_q^1$, there must exist $j\in Q$ so that $z_j(0)\ne 0$. For any $z_i(k)\ne 0$, $i\in P$, and $z_i^2(k)>\bar p>0$ for all $k\ge 0$, from (6.7), it follows that

$$\left[\frac{z_i(k+1)}{z_j(k+1)}\right]^2=\left[\frac{1+\eta a-\eta a\|z(k)\|^2+\eta b\bigl(Kurt(s_i(k))z_i^2(k)+3\|z(k)\|^2\bigr)}{1+\eta a-\eta a\|z(k)\|^2+\eta b\bigl(Kurt(s_j(k))z_j^2(k)+3\|z(k)\|^2\bigr)}\right]^2\cdot\left[\frac{z_i(k)}{z_j(k)}\right]^2,$$

for all $k\ge 0$. By Lemma 6.5 and $i\in P$, that is, $Kurt(s_i)<0$, it follows that

$$\left[\frac{z_i(k+1)}{z_j(k+1)}\right]^2\leq\left[\frac{1+\eta a-\eta a\|z(k)\|^2+\eta b\bigl(Kurt(s_i(k))\bar p+3\|z(k)\|^2\bigr)}{1+\eta a-\eta a\|z(k)\|^2+3\eta b\|z(k)\|^2}\right]^2\cdot\left[\frac{z_i(k)}{z_j(k)}\right]^2$$
$$\leq\left[\frac{1+\eta a+\eta b\bar p\,Kurt(s_i)+3\eta b\|z(k)\|^2}{1+\eta a+3\eta b\|z(k)\|^2}\right]^2\cdot\left[\frac{z_i(k)}{z_j(k)}\right]^2,$$

for all $k\ge 0$. Since $\|z(k)\|^2<\frac{1+\eta a}{\eta a}$, by Lemma 6.2, it follows that

$$\left[\frac{z_i(k+1)}{z_j(k+1)}\right]^2\leq\left[\frac{1+\eta a+\eta b\bar p\,Kurt(s_i)+3\eta b\cdot\frac{1+\eta a}{\eta a}}{1+\eta a+3\eta b\|z(k)\|^2}\right]^2\cdot\left[\frac{z_i(k)}{z_j(k)}\right]^2$$
$$\leq\left[\frac{(1+\eta a)(a+3b)+\eta ab\bar p\,Kurt(s_i)}{(1+\eta a)(a+3b)}\right]^2\cdot\left[\frac{z_i(k)}{z_j(k)}\right]^2$$
$$\leq\left[\frac{(1+\eta a)(a+3b)+\eta ab\bar p\,Kurt(s_i)}{(1+\eta a)(a+3b)}\right]^{2(k+1)}\cdot\left[\frac{z_i(0)}{z_j(0)}\right]^2$$
$$=\left[\frac{z_i(0)}{z_j(0)}\right]^2\cdot e^{-\theta_1(k+1)},$$

for all $k\ge 0$, where

$$\theta_1=\ln\left(\frac{(1+\eta a)(a+3b)}{(1+\eta a)(a+3b)+\eta ab\bar p\,Kurt(s_i)}\right)^2>0.$

Since $z(0)\in S_{z_1}$, it holds that

$$|z_i(k+1)|^2\leq |z_j(k+1)|^2\cdot\left[\frac{z_i(0)}{z_j(0)}\right]^2\cdot e^{-\theta_1 k}<\Pi_1\cdot e^{-\theta_1 k},$$

where

$$\Pi_1=\frac{1+\eta a}{\eta a}\cdot\left[\frac{z_i(0)}{z_j(0)}\right]^2.$

The proof is complete.
