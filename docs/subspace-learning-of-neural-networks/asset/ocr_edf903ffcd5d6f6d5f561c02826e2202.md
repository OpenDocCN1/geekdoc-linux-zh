# **Lemma 3.6** *There exists a positive constant* \(\Pi_2>0\) *such that*

\[Q(k)\leq \Pi_2\cdot e^{-\theta_1 k},\]

for \(k\geq 0\).

*Proof :* From (3.11),

\[Q(k)=\sum_{i=m+1}^{n}\lambda_i\left[2(1-\xi)+\frac{\xi(\lambda_i+\sigma)}{w^T(k)Cw(k)}\right]\left[\frac{\xi(\sigma-\lambda_i)}{w^T(k)Cw(k)}\right]\hat z_i^2(k)\]

\[\leq \sum_{i=m+1}^{n}\lambda_i\left[2+\frac{2\xi\sigma}{w^T(k)Cw(k)}\right]\left[\frac{2\xi\sigma}{w^T(k)Cw(k)}\right]\hat z_i^2(k)\]

\[\leq 2\left[1+\frac{\xi\sigma}{w^T(k)Cw(k)}\right]\left[\frac{\xi\sigma}{w^T(k)Cw(k)}\right]\cdot\sum_{i=m+1}^{n}\lambda_i\hat z_i^2(k)\]

for \(k\geq 0\). By Theorem 3.1 and Lemma 3.3,

\[Q(k)\leq 2\left[1+\frac{\xi\sigma}{4(1-\xi)\xi\lambda_p}\right]\left[\frac{\xi\sigma}{4(1-\xi)\xi\lambda_p}\right]\sum_{i=m+1}^{n}\lambda_i\hat z_i^2(k)\]

\[\leq 2\Pi_1\cdot\left[1+\frac{\sigma}{4(1-\xi)\lambda_p}\right]\left[\frac{\sigma}{4(1-\xi)\lambda_p}\right]e^{-\theta_1 k}\]

\[\leq \Pi_2e^{-\theta_1 k}\]

for \(k\geq 0\), where

\[\Pi_2=2\Pi_1\cdot\left[1+\frac{\sigma}{4(1-\xi)\lambda_p}\right]\left[\frac{\sigma}{4(1-\xi)\lambda_p}\right].\]

The proof is complete.
