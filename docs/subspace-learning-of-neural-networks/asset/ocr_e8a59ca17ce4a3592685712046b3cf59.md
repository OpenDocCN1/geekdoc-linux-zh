where \(-b_{(k)}^2\le 0\) for all \(k\). Since the matrix \(D_k^2\) is positive semidefinite in practice, we have \(w^T(k)D_k^2w(k)\ge 0\). Thus, the objective declines. The proof is completed.

# Theorem 10.2

Denote a performance vector by \(c=A^T w_*\), where \(w_*\) is the weight vector estimated by the updating rule (10.6). If for that specific time delay \(\tau_i\), relations (10.3) hold, then \(c=\beta e_i\), where \(\beta\) is a nonzero scalar and \(e_i\) is such a vector that the \(i\)th element is \(\pm1\) and the other elements are 0.

**Proof.** The \(w_*\) and \(b_*\) estimated by the algorithm satisfy

\[
w_* = E\left\{(x_k-b_kx_{\tau_k})(x_k-b_kx_{\tau_k})^T\right\}w_* . \tag{10.9}
\]

and

\[
w_*^T E\left\{x_kx_{\tau_k}^T+x_{\tau_k}x_k^T-2b_kx_{\tau_k}x_{\tau_k}^T\right\}w_*-2b\sigma_n^2=0. \tag{10.10}
\]

From (10.10), we have

\[
c_i^2E\{s_i(k)s_i(k-\tau_i)\}-b_k\sum_{j=1}^{N}c_j^2E\{s_j^2(k-\tau_j)\}=0.
\]

If \(c_i=0\), then \(c_j=0\), \(\forall j\ne i\), which is impossible. Assume that \(c_i\ne0\), multiplying both sides of equation (10.9) by nonsingular matrix \(A^T\), a basic condition for the resolvability of the BSE problem, it gives that

\[
A^Tw_*=A^TE\{x_kx_k^T-b_k(x_kx_{\tau_k}^T+x_{\tau_k}x_k^T)+b_k^2x_{\tau_k}x_{\tau_k}^T\}w_*.
\]

Hence, taking into account that \(E\{x_kx_k^T\}=I\), we have

\[
c_j=\left[1+(A^TA)_j^2E\{s_j^2(k-\tau_j)\}+b_k^2\sigma_n^2\right]c_j,\qquad \forall j\ne i,
\]

where \((A^TA)_j\) denotes the \(j\)th diagonal element. Note that \((A^TA)_jE\{s_j^2(k-\tau_i)\}+\sigma_n^2=1\); we know it is clear that \(c_j=0\), \(\forall j\ne i\). Taking \(c_i\ne0\) into consideration, we have \(c=\beta e_i\). Theorem 10.2 indicates that the algorithm (5) actually leads to the extraction of the desired source signals.
