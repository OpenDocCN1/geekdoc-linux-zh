# **Lemma 6.9** Suppose that \(z(0)\in S_{\Sigma_1}\), \(z(0)\notin V_l^1\) and \(\Gamma_{\Sigma}>\max\{\operatorname{Kurt}(s_i)+3,3\}\). If \(\operatorname{Kurt}(s_{j_0})z_{j_0}^2(0)>\operatorname{Kurt}(s_j)z_j^2(0)\), \(j\in Q\) and \(j\in I/j_0\), it holds that

\[\eta a-V(k+1)<\zeta^k\cdot[\eta a-V(0)],\]
where \(V(k)=\eta[a-b(\operatorname{Kurt}(s_{j_0})+3)]z_{j_0}^2(k)\) and \(\zeta=\max\{|(1-\gamma)^2-\eta a\gamma|,\ |1-2\eta a|\}\), when \(k\) is large enough.

**Proof.** By Lemmas 6.6 and 6.8, it follows that

\[\lim_{k\to+\infty}z_i(k)=0,\quad(i\in P\text{ and }\operatorname{Kurt}(s_i)<0),\]
\[\lim_{k\to+\infty}z_j(k)=0,\quad(j\in(Q\cup O)/j_0\text{ and }\operatorname{Kurt}(s_j)\ge 0),\]

where \(j_0\in Q\), \(\operatorname{Kurt}(s_{j_0})z_{j_0}^2(0)>\operatorname{Kurt}(s_j)z_j^2(0)\). From (6.7), when \(k\) is large enough, it follows that

\[z_{j_0}(k+1)=z_{j_0}(k)+[\eta a-\eta a z_{j_0}^2(k)+\eta b(\operatorname{Kurt}(s_{j_0})z_{j_0}^2(k)+3z_{j_0}^2(k))]z_{j_0}(k)\]
\[\qquad=\{1+\eta a-\eta[a-b(\operatorname{Kurt}(s_{j_0})+3)]z_{j_0}^2(k)\}z_{j_0}(k).\]

Since \(z(0)\in S_{\Sigma_1}\), there must exist a small constant \(\varrho\) so that \(0<\varrho<1\) and \(\varrho\le V(k)\). Thus, it can be checked that

\[0<\varrho<V(k)\le \eta[a-b(\operatorname{Kurt}(s_{j_0})+3)]\frac{1+\eta a}{\eta a}\]
\[\qquad=\frac{a-b(\operatorname{Kurt}(s_{j_0})+3)}{a}(1+\eta a)\]
\[\qquad\le 2,\]

where \(0<\eta a<1\) and \(a>b(\operatorname{Kurt}(s_{j_0})+3)\). Then, it follows that

\[\eta a-V(k+1)\quad=\quad \eta a-\eta[a-b(\operatorname{Kurt}(s_{j_0})+3)]z_{j_0}^2(k+1)\]
\[\qquad=\quad \eta a-\eta[a-b(\operatorname{Kurt}(s_{j_0})+3)]\cdot\]
\[\qquad\qquad\{1+\eta a-\eta[a-b(\operatorname{Kurt}(s_{j_0})+3)]z_{j_0}^2(k)\}^2z_{j_0}^2(k).\]

It is easy to get that

\[\eta a-V(k+1)=[\eta a-V(k)]\{[1-V(k)]^2-\eta aV(k)\}\]
\[\qquad\le |\eta a-V(k)|\,|[1-V(k)]^2-\eta aV(k)|.\]

By Lemma 6.3, it follows that

\[|\eta a-V(k+1)|\le |\eta a-V(k)|\cdot|[1-V(k)]^2-\eta aV(k)|\]
\[\qquad\le |\eta a-V(k)|\cdot\zeta\]
\[\qquad=\zeta^k|\eta a-V(0)|,\]

where \(0<\zeta<1\). The proof is complete.
