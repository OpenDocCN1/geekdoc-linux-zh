The easiest way to work with the Styles and Formatting tool is to highlight the text you want to style and double-click the required style in the window. There are quite a few to choose from, but you might find them restrictive if you have more specialized needs. To start defining your own styles, press Ctrl+F11 to bring up the Style Catalogue, shown in Figure 6.6, where you add, modify and delete styles for pages, paragraphs, lists, characters and frames.
