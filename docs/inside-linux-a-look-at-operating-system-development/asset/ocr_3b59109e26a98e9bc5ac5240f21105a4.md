# 4.4.2 Mapped

If the programs do not co-operate, through malice or by mistake, they may corrupt one another by writing into the other's space. A number of methods have been developed to provide hardware-based protection.

Most of these rely on some mapping from the *logical address* generated by the program to the *physical address* used to access memory. When the *logical address* is not always mapped to some *physical address*, it is referred to as a *virtual address*.
