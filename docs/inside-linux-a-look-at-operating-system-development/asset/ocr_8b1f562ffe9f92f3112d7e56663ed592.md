## Prologue to Linux

Linux did not appear out of thin air. It is the outcome of years of computer system development. If we look back, we can identify a number of seminal ideas that have been incorporated into Linux from other systems.

# ### 2.1 Multics

In 1964-1965, General Electric, Bell Telephone Laboratories, and Project MAC at MIT entered into a partnership to build an operating system for a large computer. This system was to be known as Multics—an acronym for Multiplexed Information and Computing Service.

Official support for this operating system has now been withdrawn by all of the original parties, but there still exist a few operational Multics sites. There is also a group of Multics fans and users which supports a net news group$^1$.

A number of books and papers have been written about Multics, most significantly Organic[k37]. Some of these explore the demise of Multics. None suggests Multics had fundamental design problems and most extol a number of original ideas that are slowly making their way into other operating systems.
