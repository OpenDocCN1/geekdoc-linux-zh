# **INSIDE LINUX**

A Look at Operating System Development

*Inside Linux* provides the reader with an inside look at operating system design and implementation considerations.

*Inside Linux* looks at the history of operating systems, how they are used, and the details of one operating system—Linux. The contents interweave discussions of history, theory and practice, allowing the reader to see what happens inside the system.

*Inside Linux* will give the computer professional a deeper understanding of how Unix systems in general (and Linux in particular) operate.

# **About the Author:**

Randolph Benton has been programming since 1969—writing more tasking kernels in assembly code than he wants to admit. His first high-level language operating system was the UCSD P-system.

Dr. Benton has been working with Unix for nearly 16 years, and he's been enjoying (and contributing to) Linux for the last 2 years.
