# Deadlock avoidance

All of the above methods are static in that they do not depend on what other processes are doing. Deadlock avoidance relies on a dynamic analysis of when it is safe to allocate a resource.

One can visualize deadlock in terms of processes moving into a corner out of which they cannot escape. This idea can be shown graphically in two dimensions for two processes.
