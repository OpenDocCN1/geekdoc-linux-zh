Another classic example comes from the field of transaction processing. Imagine a scenario where Alice and Bob each approach a different bank teller at the same instant. Alice deposits $100 and Bob withdraws $100 from their joint account. A naive bank operation could follow any of the steps shown in Figures 4.5 through 4.8. Only in the last two cases are the read-modify-write sequence performed as atomic operations, i.e., without interruption.
