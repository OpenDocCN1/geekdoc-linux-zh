The Masters of Software Engineering program at Seattle University$^9$ offers a computer science survey course to students who lack formal computer science education. I had the opportunity to teach this class for several years and discovered some measure of what computer professionals don't know about computer science.

Nenetews have been a surprisingly good source of information—both how Linux works$^{10}$ and what is important to Linux users$^{11}$. Many of the discussions in those groups have prompted sections of this book. Sections 2.1 and 2.2 draw heavily from Salus[44]. Section 5.2.3 draws upon the Kernel Hacker's Guide[25]. Some of the code examples in Section 5.2.4 were derived from examples in Schimmel[45]. Section 9.1 was originally written for *The Linux Sampler*[18]. It is reprinted with permission of *Linux Journal*.

Thanks to the skipper and crew of the racing sloop Sunwood for their forbearance as I'd disappear below deck between races to work on my manuscript.
