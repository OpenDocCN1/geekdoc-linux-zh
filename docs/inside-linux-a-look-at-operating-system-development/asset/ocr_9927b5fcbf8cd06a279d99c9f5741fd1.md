# Biography

+   Gordon Bell—V.P. of Engineering at DEC. While at DEC, he played an important role in the development of the PDP-11 and VAX-11 series of computers. In spite of DEC's limited support of Unix on these systems, these systems were the foundation for Unix development for over 15 years.

+   Stephen R. Bourne—Author of the Bourne Shell. Much to the consternation of Unix fans, there were two “Stephen R. Bourne”s at Bell Labs. A trivia quiz asked the phone extension of this one.

+   Fred Brooks—Leader of the OS/360 project and author of the Mythical Man-Month, a commentary on large software development products. Dr. Brooks is now involved in virtual reality systems at the University of North Carolina.

+   John Lions—Author of book which examined the early Unix system kernel, published by the University of New South Wales for students of operating systems theory. Copies of this book have been widely distributed.

+   Dennis Ritchie—Unix creator at Bell Labs. His name is generally paired with that of Ken Thompson. If one had to make a distinction, Ritchie has shown an inclination towards languages and Thompson towards the kernel.
