# Unsubscribing from a Newsgroup

When you decide that you do not wish to belong to a newsgroup, you can unsubscribe from it. You will probably want to unsubscribe from many newsgroups if your .newsrc file was initialized for you; the list of such newsgroups is likely to be long and diverse.

To unsubscribe from a newsgroup, select News from the COLLECTION list, press RETURN to display the newsgroup FOLDER LIST, highlight the newsgroup you want to unsubscribe from, and press d (Delete). Unsubscribing from a newsgroup does not remove the newsgroup from the .newsrc file; it simply tells pine not to include that newsgroup in the FOLDER LISTsqcup. If you decide to subscribe to the same newsgroup again, pine remembers what messages you deleted, and you can resume reading the posts where you left off.

# Netnews with Mozilla

You can also use Mozilla to read netnews. Use the Account Wizard to set up Mozilla to read and post netnews. How you display Account Wizard depends on whether you already have a mail and/or newsgroup account set up with Mozilla. In either case select Mozilla menu:Mail & Newsgroups. If Mozilla displays the Account Wizard, you are set. When Mozilla displays the message center window, select message center menu:Edit=Mail & Newsgroups Account Settings to see the Mail & Newsgroups Account Settings window. Click Add Account and Mozilla displays the Account Wizard window.

From the Account window click Newsgroup account and then Next. Follow the prompts in the news windows, verify the information you entered on the final window, and click Finish to close the Account Wizard window. Click OK to close the Account Settings window.

If the message center window is not already displayed, select Mozilla menu:TasksMail & Newsgroups to display it. The news folder is generally at the bottom of the tree list on the left, labeled with the name of your server. To add newsgroups, click the news folder and click Subscribe from the pop-up menu. To subscribe to individual newsgroups, expand a news directory by clicking the plus (+) to the left of the news directory's name. Enter or pick the groups you want to subscribe to by double-clicking the name of the newsgroup. Click OK when you are done subscribing to newsgroups. Mozilla returns you to the message center window. Double-click the newsgroup you want to visit, and Mozilla displays items from the newsgroup.

+   23. The message center window displays the name of the highlighted folder on its titlebar.
