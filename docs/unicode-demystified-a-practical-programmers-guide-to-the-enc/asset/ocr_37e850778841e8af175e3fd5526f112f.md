In the manifesto, after explaining a little about the project and what has been accomplished so far, Mr. Stallman continues.

# Why I Must Write GNU

I consider that the golden rule requires that if I like a program I share it with other people who like it. Software-sellers want to divide the users and conquer them, making each user agree not to share with others. I refuse to break solidarity with other users in this way. I cannot in good conscience sign a nondisclosure agreement or a software license agreement. For I worked within the Artificial Intelligence Lab to resist such tendencies and other hostilities, but eventually they had gone too far: I could not remain in an institution where such things are for me against finishing it.
