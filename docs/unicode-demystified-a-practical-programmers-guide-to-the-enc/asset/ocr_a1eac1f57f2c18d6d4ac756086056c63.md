Toolbars
A toolbar (usually a rectangular area) appears upon your workspace or within a window and contains displays, icons, text, applets, and more that you can work with. There are many kinds of toolbars; see Table 8-23 on page 340. One type of toolbar has no name other than toolbar. Figure 8-2 on page 269 shows the KDE Helpcenter window toolbar: the bar that has left and right arrows at its end to, toward the top of the window. Above the toolbar is a menubar, another type of toolbar. The titlebar, because it is not part of the window, is not a toolbar. Rather, a titlebar is part of the window decoration and is placed by the window manager.
