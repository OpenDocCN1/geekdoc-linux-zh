# Toolbar Handles

A toolbar handle appears as several faint, diagonal lines on a narrow bar (Figure 8-29). The handle is positioned either at the top or left end of a toolbar or panel applet, depending on its orientation. Right click a toolbar handle to bring up the Toolbar menu (page 311), or drag a toolbar handle to move the toolbar. In addition to moving the toolbar to one of the sides of its window, in most cases you can move it anywhere in the workspace. Return a toolbar to its window by dragging it back into the window close to one of the sides and releasing it. The toolbar handle snaps into place.
