Nautilus displays a subwindow and, optionally, a side pane (Figure 7-6). The side pane, to the left of the subwindow, has three possible—History, Notes, and Tree—each which changes what appears in the side pane. The figure shows part of the directory structure as a tree on its side (the Tree tab). The subwindow shows the contents of the directory that is highlighted in the tree view. Click a directory in the side pane to display its contents in the subwindow.

To the left of entries in the side pane are little triangles/arrows that point out right or down. Click a right arrow to expand the associated directory one level. Click a down arrow to close the directory.

Three control bars are usually at the top of the Nautilus window (Figure 7-6): a menubar, a toolbar, and a location bar. Table 7-6 on page 220 describes the dropown menus presented by the menubar. The toolbar holds navigational tools and content specifies (page 225). The location bar shows the name of the directory you are viewing and accepts the name of a directory that you want to view (page 224). Refer to Table 7-11 on page 244 for information about customizing Nautilus.

You can display icons or a list of filenames in the subwindow. Choose which you prefer by making a selection from the drop-down list that appears on the right end of the location bar. View as icons is shown in Figure 7-6 and View as list is shown in Figure 7-7. At the left end of the toolbar are icons for Back, Forward, Up, Refresh, Home, Web Search, and Stop.
