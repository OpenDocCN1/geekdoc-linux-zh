The default kicker displays a row of buttons with an icon on each; a small group of numbered squares representing the workspaces (the pager); a group of rectangles, each listing the name of a program that is running on the displayed workspace (the taskbar); and perhaps the Clipboard tool, Kandalf (useful tips) and a click. At one or both ends may be an arrow that you can use to move kicker out of sight toward the end, except that it leaves in place so you can recall kicker when you want to. Refer to page 338 for more information on panels.

Figure 8-8 and Table 8-3 gives more information on some of the more common buttons/icons, applets, and other items on kicker and extension panels. Buttons and applets are of two types: one that execute a program and one that display a menu of programs. You can tell which one display a menu by the little black triangle pointing up at the upper left of the button. The most important and most used menu is the K menu, which is discussed on page 274.
