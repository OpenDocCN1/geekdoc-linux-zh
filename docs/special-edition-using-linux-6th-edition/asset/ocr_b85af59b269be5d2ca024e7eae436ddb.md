Support preferences.

The Supply Connection-Quality Data To RealServers check box tells your RealPlayer client to send statistical information back to the person who maintains the audio/video servers to which your client connects. Server maintainers can use this information to monitor the quality of their transmissions and make improvements if required. Some people might not like the idea that information about their system is being sent to remote sites, so this box can be checked to disable this feature.
