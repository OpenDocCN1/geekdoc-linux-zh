This doesn’t cause a carrier drop per se, but it does mean that no data gets through during the retrain period. If you have an external modem, you can generally spot a retrain by a blinking light, but with an internal modem, you have no clue when a retrain is in progress. You can often spot connection quality problems after the fact, though, by using a terminal program and issuing an appropriate command.
