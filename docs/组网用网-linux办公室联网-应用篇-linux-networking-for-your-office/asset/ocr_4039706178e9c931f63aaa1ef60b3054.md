# Linux:
Networking for
Your Office

SMALL OFFICE / HOME OFFICE SOLUTIONS

If you've been looking for a cost-effective way to network a handful of computers, your search stops here---- welcome to Linux!

Linux: Networking for Your Office shows you how to network a cluster of Windows®, Macintosh®, or UNIX machines with Linux at the core—and even give the networked machines Internet capabilities. Learn to use your Linux machine as a file server, print server, IP masquerading router, and firewall. You even get a CD with Linux and additional tools to get you up and running!

Roderick W. Smith has been using computers since high school, including systems using operating systems as diverse as AppleDOS, MacOS, TOS, MS-DOS, Windows 95 and 98, Windows NT®, OS/2, BeOS, VMS, Ultrix, and of course Linux. Rod has been using Linux since 1994, and has used it as his primary OS since 1996. He has experience in assorted contexts integrating Linux with various flavors of Linux, Windows, MacOS, OS/2, and BeOS on Ethernet-based networks.

Rod is the author of Que's Special Edition Using Corel® WordPerfect® 8 for Linux. He has also published articles in computer magazines, most notably Linux Journal. He holds a bachelor's degree from Oberlin College, a master's degree from the University of Michigan, and a doctorate from Tufts University.
