As the category suggests, these lists are quite technical in nature. Do not post questions of a general nature to any lists considered "technical lists." Virtually all these lists have guidelines for the way they are to be used. Therefore, you should read the charter that is e-mailed to you when subscribing to any of these lists before posting to them.

> **CAUTION**
> 
> The technical lists tend to be primarily only for advanced FreeBSD users. Posting how-to questions and such to this list will get you no answers at best and will get you flamed at worst.

# IRC Channels

Several IRC channels are available for FreeBSD. I’m not going to list all of them here because some of them are general discussion channels in which the topics are rarely about FreeBSD. Here are the two in which you are likely to be able to get help for FreeBSD:

+   - On EFNet: #freebsdhelp
+   - On Undernet: #freebsd

Note that depending on the time of day you show up, these channels may have a lot of people in them and still be very quiet. Though it may look like people are ignoring you, that’s probably not the case. Many people are working and are simply logged in to the channel as well. Also, during periods of high Net congestion, IRC can have very high lag times between when you post your message and when some people actually get it. Keep this in mind as well when you don’t seem to be getting any responses (you might consider switching to a different server if it seems your lag time is very high).
