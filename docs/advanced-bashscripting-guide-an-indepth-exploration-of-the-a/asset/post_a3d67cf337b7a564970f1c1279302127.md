This tutorial assumes no previous knowledge of scripting or programming, but progresses rapidly toward an intermediate/advanced level of instruction (...all the sneaking in little snippets of UNIX wisdom and lore). It serves as a textbook, a manual for self-study, and a reference and source of knowledge on scripting techniques. The exercises and well-commented examples invite active reader participation, under the premise that the only way to learn scripting is to write scripts.

The latest update of this document, as an archived “tarball” including the SGML source and rendered HTML, may be downloaded from [the author’s home site](). See the [change log](_) for a revision history.
