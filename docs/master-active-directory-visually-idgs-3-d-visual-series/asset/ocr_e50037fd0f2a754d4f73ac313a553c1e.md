# DENYING LOGON LOCALLY

L ogging on locally means that a user can sit down at a computer and log onto the machine. Whereas users may be able to logon to a computer over the network, you can choose to deny the logon locally right to various users and groups as desired. This prevents certain users and groups from physically sitting down at computers and logging onto them.
