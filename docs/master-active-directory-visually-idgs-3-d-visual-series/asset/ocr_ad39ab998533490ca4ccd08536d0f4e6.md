Next, when you are satisfied with your first level OUs, you should plan your nested OUs. Begin with second-level and work your way down as necessary. Remember, try to keep the OUs to a minimum and always ask the

question “How does the OU benefit our administrative model” before creating it. Some models call for nested OUs to be created by office or divisions.
