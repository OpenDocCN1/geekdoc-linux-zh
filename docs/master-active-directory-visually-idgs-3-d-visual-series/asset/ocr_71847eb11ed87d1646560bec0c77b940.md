# About the Author

Curt Simmons, Microsoft Certified Systems Engineer (MCSE) and Microsoft Certified Trainer (MCT), is a Windows expert who spends his most of his time writing technical books. Curt has been working closely with Windows 2000 and the Active Directory since beta 1 was released, and he is author of a dozen high-level computing books on Microsoft technologies. Curt lives in a little Texas town outside of Dallas with his wife and daughter, and you can visit him on the Internet at curtssimmons.hypermart.net.
