Your other domains create the hierarchical tree

After you establish your root domain, your other domains fall into the hierarchical tree. For example, wilsonadams.com has domains in Tulsa, San Diego, Atlanta, and Tampa. You need to give these domains descriptive names that become child domains of the root domain, wilsonadams.com. You name your child domains using the same principles you did for the root domain — choose names that describe the domain

This design creates a contiguous namespace where network users can find resources in any domain. You can learn more about planning your domain structure in Chapter 9.
