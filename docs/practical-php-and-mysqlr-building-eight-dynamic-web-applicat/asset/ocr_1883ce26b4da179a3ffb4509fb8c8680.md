# # Creating a Shopping Cart

For many developers, the humble shopping cart holds a special place in their hearts. Although PHP and MySQL are the fodder for a range of Web applications, many developers learned their trade with the ambition to write the ubiquitous shopping cart. If there is a Zen to Web development, it is likely to be experienced while writing a shopping cart.

Although a common sight on the Web, shopping carts do come in a variety of different flavors. The various incarnations typically differ in ways that are specific to the type of business using the software. For example, the sale of items such as books, CDs, and DVDs differs from the sale of cables, food, building materials, and janitorial products. The core difference is quantity: you generally buy only a single book or DVD at time, but it is not uncommon for a restaurant to buy 10 packs of dinner rolls.
