+   ✦ SCSI: SCSI rules in the server market. A server system without SCSI drives is unthinkable to me and to many other experienced server administrators.

+   ✦ Fiber Channel disks: The Fiber Channel disk is the hottest and youngest drive technology, but it is not widely used because of high price and interconnectivity issues. However, Fiber Channel drives are taking market share from SCSI in the enterprise or high-end storage arena. If you need Fiber Channel disks, you should consider a very high-end drive subsystem, such as a storage area network (SAN) or a storage appliance.

Note In this chapter I assume that you are an EIDE or SCSI user.
