✦ `^www\.(\[^\]+)\.host\.`\com(.*)`—**This will match a string, such as `www.username.host.com STATUS=java` and the **$1** will be assigned to host and **$2** will hold everything followed by the `www.username.host.com` part of the string. The **$2** will hold `STATUS=java`.
