# ## 2.9 HIDDEN FILES

JUST AS MOST OF THE STUFF IN THE UNIVERSE IS INVISIBLE DARK MATTER, SO THE MAJORITY OF
DIRECTORIES AND FILES IN YOUR Home DIRECTORY ARE HIDDEN. TO SEE THESE, DOUBLE-CLICK
YOUR NEW DESKTOP ICON TO RUN NAUTILUS, THEN PRESS Ctrl + H OR OPEN THE VIEW MENU
AND CLICK 'SHOW HIDDEN FILES'. YOU WILL SEE THAT THERE ARE QUITE A FEW HIDDEN
FOLDERS IN YOUR Home DIRECTORY, AND THERE WILL BE MANY MORE WHEN YOU'VE FINISHED
INSTALLING THE VARIOUS APPLICATIONS IN THE NEXT CHAPTER. NOTICE THAT THESE HIDDEN
FILES AND DIRECTORIES ARE ALL PREFIXED WITH A `.`

PERSONAL APPLICATION LAUNCHERS ARE STORED IN THIS HIDDEN PART OF YOUR Home
DIRECTORY, IN THE `LOCAL/SHARE/APPLICATIONS` FOLDER. HAVE A LOOK AT IT:

+   1) NAVIGATE TO THAT FOLDER NOW, AND OPEN TERMINAL.

+   2) ENTER `LS` TO LIST THE FILES IN THE FOLDER. ONE OF THEM IS
+   `NAUTILUS_AS_ROOT.DESKTOP`.

+   3) ENTER `leafpad Nautilus_as Root.desktop` (REMEMBER YOU CAN COPY-AND-PASTE THE FILENAME
+   INTO THIS COMMAND).

+   4) THE TEXT OF THE LAUNCHER SHOULD BE DISPLAYED.

+   5) CLOSE LEAFPAD, AND CLOSE TERMINAL.

+   6) IN NAUTILUS, RETURN TO YOUR Home DIRECTORY (BY CLICKING HOME IN THE TOOLBAR),
+   AND PRESS Ctrl + H AGAIN TO TURN OFF THE HIDDEN FILES VIEW.

+   7) FINALLY CLOSE NAUTILUS.
