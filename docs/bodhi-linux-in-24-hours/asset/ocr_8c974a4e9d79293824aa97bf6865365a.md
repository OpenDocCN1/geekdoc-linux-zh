+   14) CLICK THE RIGHT ARROW TO REACH THE ‘STYLE’ TAB, AND HERE YOU SHOULD SELECT ‘INVISIBLE’.

+   15) CLICK APPLY, AND THEN CLOSE.

THE ICONS ON YOUR SHELF SHOULD NOW BE CLUSTERED AT THE LEFT EDGE OF THE SCREEN, AT THE TOP, AND THE SHELF ITSELF SHOULD BE INVISIBLE. AND NOW YOU CAN ACCESS ALL THE GADGETS AND APPLICATIONS PLACED ON IT WITH A SINGLE CLICK. ONE OF THESE – THE SETTINGS GADGET – YOU’LL BE USING IN THE NEXT SECTION.

**NOTE:** THE SYSTEM (POWER SWITCH) GADGET IS NO LONGER ON THE SHELF. SO TO SWITCH OFF YOUR COMPUTER, YOU’LL EITHER HAVE TO PRESS THE COMPUTER’S POWER BUTTON OR USE THE MENU SYSTEM (MAIN MENU > SYSTEM > POWER OFF). LATER IN THIS CHAPTER YOU WILL BE CREATING A KEY BINDING TO SWITCH OFF.
