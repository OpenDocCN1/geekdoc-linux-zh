A world-writable mail drop allows users to receive mail directly, instead
of only through your network’s mail system. It is faster, but can be a
security risk.

If in doubt, do not select world writable mail drop.

Procmail lets you filter incoming messages and deliver them to folders
other than your Inbox.

You can use other tools to filter, so, if in doubt, do not use procmail.
