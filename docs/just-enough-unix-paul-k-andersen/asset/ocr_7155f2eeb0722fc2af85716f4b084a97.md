# 4.4 Getting Acquainted with the Mouse

If you have previously worked with a personal computer, you are undoubtedly familiar with the use of a mouse with a GUI. As you move the mouse on its pad or the table top, an arrow-shaped pointer also moves on the screen.

Unlike many personal computers—which typically work with a mouse having just one or two buttons—the Motif Window Manager is designed to operate with a three-button mouse (Figure 4–4).
