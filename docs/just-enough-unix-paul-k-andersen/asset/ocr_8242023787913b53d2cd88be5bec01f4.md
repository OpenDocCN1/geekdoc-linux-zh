# 19.2 The Netscape Window

You should take a moment to acquaint yourself with the various parts of the Netscape window (Figure 19–2). Be sure you can locate and identify the following:

+   * **Menu Bar.** Nine pull-down menus are available from the menu bar near the top of the window. Reading from left to right, these are the File, Edit, View, Go, Bookmarks, Options, Directory, Window, and Help menus.
+   * **Toolbar Buttons.** Nine buttons are located in a row beneath the menu bar. These are the Back, Forward, Home, Reload, Images, Open, Print, Find, and Stop buttons.
+   * **Netscape Company Logo.** This is located to the right of the toolbar buttons.
+   * **Location Field.** The URL of the currently displayed page appears in this area.
+   * **Directory Buttons.** These buttons provide links to interesting information and helpful tools. Handbook and Net Search are especially useful.
