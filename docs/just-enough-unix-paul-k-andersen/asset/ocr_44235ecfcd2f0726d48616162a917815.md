# 10.6 Buffers and More Buffers

Recall that vi actually works on a copy of the file in the work buffer. While using vi, you have access to other buffers as well. There are 36 of these:

+   • unnamed buffer
+   • named buffers "a", "b", "c", . . . , "z"
+   • numbered buffers "1", "2", "3", . . ."9"
