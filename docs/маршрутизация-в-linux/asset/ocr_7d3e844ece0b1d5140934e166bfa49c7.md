I would first like to thank the members of the IP Multicast mailing list available from www.stardust.com/Multicast for their great help as I was trying to familiarize myself with IP Multicast. I’d also like to thank the folks who peer-reviewed what I intended to cover before the writing started. Their comments helped to shape much of what you find in this book.

Additional thanks go to the technical reviewers who helped keep me on my toes throughout the project. It’s their job not only to keep me from looking silly but to putting large mistakes in print, but also to make sure that you, the reader, get the most benefit possible from the pages within.

Last but not least, I would like to thank my co-authors, Joe and Ron. I was ill through a good part of this project, and after a long search we brought these two fine gentlemen in to ensure that I not only was the book finished in a timely manner, but that it remained the best possible quality and usefulness to the readers. This book is far greater than the sum total of its parts thanks in part to their hard work.
