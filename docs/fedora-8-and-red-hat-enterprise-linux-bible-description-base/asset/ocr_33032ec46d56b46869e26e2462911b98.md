• **NFS (system-config-nfs)** — Set up directories from your system to be shared with other computers on your network using the NFS service.

• **Services (system-config-services)** — Display and change which services are running on your Fedora or RHEL system at different run levels.

• **Authentication (authconfig-gtk)** — Change how users are authenticated on your system. Usually, Shadow Passwords and MD5 Passwords are selected. However, if your network supports LDAP, Kerberos, SMB, NIS, or Hesiod authentication, you can select to use any of those authentication types.

• **Boot Loader (system-config-boot)** — Configure the default boot entry and timeout value (in seconds) for your GRUB boot loader.

• **Date & Time (system-config-date)** — Set the date and time or choose to have an NTP server keep system time in sync. Figure 10-2 shows the Date/Time Properties window.

• **Display (system-config-display)** — Change the settings for your X desktop, including color depth and resolution for your display. You can also choose settings for your video card and monitor.

• **Firewall and SELinux (system-config-securitylevel)** — Configure your firewall to allow or deny services to computers from the network. Also disable or enable SELinux, along with various services supported by SELinux.

• **Hardware (hwbrowser)** — View information about your hard drives, network devices, sound cards, and other hardware. (Install the hwbrowser package to see this item.)
