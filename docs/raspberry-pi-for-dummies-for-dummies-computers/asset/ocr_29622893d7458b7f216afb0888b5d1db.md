The Format menu includes options for character formatting (which includes font and font effects, underlining, superscript, rotation, links, and background colors), paragraph formatting (which includes indents and spacing, alignment, text flow, and borders), bullets and numbering, page formatting (including page size, background colors and images, headers, and footers) for multicumn layouts, and alignment.

Using these two menus, you can achieve most of what you need. The most options are also replicated with icons on the menu bars at the top of the screen.

If you use styles to structure your document (using Heading 1 for the most important headings and Heading 2 for subheadings, you can use the Navigator to jump to different parts of your document easily. Tap F5 to open it. The Navigator also allows you to jump to tables, links, and bookmarks.

Using the File menu, you can save your document as a PDF (.pdf) format (or export it). The great thing about this is that it preserves the formatting of the file, so you can share your document with people who might not have the same fonts or software, and guarantee they will see exactly what you see. Most people have software for reading PDF files, but the drawback is that very few people have software for editing them. For that reason, this format is only suitable for circulating final copies of documents you want people to read but not edit.
