The cannon is used to fire T-shirts into the audience during the timeouts, which last for up to a minute-and-a-half. They wanted a design that would enable more than one T-shirt to be fired in that period, and that would also enable the cannon to tweet a photo when a T-shirt was fired.

The resulting design uses four PVC tubes for the barrels, and compressed air to fire the T-shirts. The cannon has three buttons: one to choose which barrel to fire from, and the other two that are pressed together to fire a shirt. As an additional safety measure, a key is required to disarm the cannon. The Raspberry Pi is used to control the device, with software written in Python that uses the GPIO libraries.
