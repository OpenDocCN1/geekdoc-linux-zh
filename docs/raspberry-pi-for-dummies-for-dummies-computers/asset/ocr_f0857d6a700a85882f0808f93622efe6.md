# #Naming Your Sprites

Whenever you're programming, you should give things meaningful names so that you (and others) can easily understand what your program does. Scratch gives your sprites names like *Sprite* and *Sprite2*, but you can rename them. To rename a sprite, click its name above the Scripts Area (refer to Figure 10-3) and type its new name. Your sprite's costumes are called *costume1*, *costume2*, and so on. If you've created different costumes for your sprite, you should also give them sensible names so that you can easily tell which is which. To go to your sprite's costumes tab, click the name beside a costume (refer to Figure 10-3), and type the new name.

For the space game, you need to create a flying saucer sprite name *ship* and a sprite named *fireball* to represent the ship's weapon. The baddie, a sprite called *alien*, has two costumes: *alienok*, which is looking menacing, *alienhit*, which shows the same entity after it is hit by the fireball.

To make it easier to see what you're doing, we recommend you drag your ship to the bottom of the screen, drag the alien to the top, and put the fireball somewhere in the middle. Roughly reflects where they will be in the finished game.
