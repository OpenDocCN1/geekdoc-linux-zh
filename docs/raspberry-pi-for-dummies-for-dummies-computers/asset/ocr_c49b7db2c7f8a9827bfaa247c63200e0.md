# Chapter 8
Playing Audio and Video on the Raspberry Pi

In this chapter, we show how you can turn your Raspberry Pi into a media center, capable of playing high-definition video files and music.

To do that, we use dedicated media player software based on the Kodi software that powers some-top boxes and smart TVs. You can use it to play music and video that you have on storage devices connected to your Raspberry Pi, or to play back media from other devices on your home network. To a more limited degree, you can use it to play back streaming TV shows and radio stations from the Internet.

At the end of this chapter, we also show you how to play music on your Raspberry Pi in the desktop environment.
