# Connecting an HDMI or DVI display

On the top surface of your board, on the bottom edge, there is the HDMI connector.
Refer to Figures 3-1, 3-2 and 3-3.) If you're using a Raspberry Pi Zero, you should have an adapter to convert the Mini HDMI to an HDMI socket. Insert your HDMI
cable in the Raspberry Pi board or the Pi Zero converter, and then insert the other
to your monitor.

If you have a DVI display rather than a HDMI display, you need to use an adapter on the screen end of the cable. The adapter itself is a simple plug, so you just plug the HDMI cable into the adapter and then plug the adapter into your monitor and turn the silver screws on the adapter to hold the cable in place.
