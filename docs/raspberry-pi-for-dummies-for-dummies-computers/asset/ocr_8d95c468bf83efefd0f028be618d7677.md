On the Raspberry Pi 3, Pi 2, and Model B+, it's the same socket as the audio output
on the bottom of the board. You'll need to use a special RCA cable for this socket
and you can't just connect an audio cable.

Connect your RCA cable to the socket, and the other end to the Video In socket
on your television, which is likely to be silver—and-yellow.

You might need to use your TV's remote control to switch your television over
the external internal signal coming from the Raspberry Pi. If you're using HDMI on
y your TV, you might need to turn on the TV so that the Raspberry Pi can detect
when you switch it off.
