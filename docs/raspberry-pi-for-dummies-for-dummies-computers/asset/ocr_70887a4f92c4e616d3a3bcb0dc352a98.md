# Searching within web pages

To find a word or phrase within a web page, press Ctrl+F after the page has loaded.
The Find bar opens at the top of the search, with a box for you to type into.
The first occurrence of the text you're looking for is highlighted on the page in orange,
and you can press the Enter key or click the Down button in the Find bar
to the next one. You can close the Find bar by clicking the Close button
(an X) on the far right end of the Find bar or pressing the ESC key.
