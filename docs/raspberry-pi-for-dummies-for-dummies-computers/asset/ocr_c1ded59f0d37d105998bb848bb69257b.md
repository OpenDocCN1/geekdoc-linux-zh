It's easy to change the size of windows — so that you can see more than one at a time, for example. Move the mouse cursor to one of the edges until the Mouse icon changes, and you can click and drag it inwards or outwards to reshape the window. You can also click and drag a corner to change the window's height and width at the same time. To arrange the windows by side, you can move them around by clicking and dragging the title bars at the top of them.
