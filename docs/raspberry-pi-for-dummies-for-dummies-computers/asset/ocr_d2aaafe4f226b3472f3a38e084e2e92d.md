# # Playing Minecraft

Minecraft is preinstalled in Raspian. You start it by clicking it on the Applications menu at the top left of the screen, where Minecraft Pi is filed under Games. When you start Minecraft on the Raspberry Pi, the title gives you two options:

+   **Start Game:** Generates your own world to explore. You can also use this option to choose a previously generated world to visit, when you replay Minecraft. To choose between different worlds, click and drag them left and right to position your chosen one in the middle, and then click it to open it.

+   **Join Game:** Lets you join other players on a local network. A discussion of this option is outside the scope of this chapter, but this option can enable collaborative or competitive play in a Minecraft world.

Click Start Game and choose Create New, and Minecraft generates a new world for you, with its own, distinctive terrain of mountains, forests, and oceans. When it's finished, you see a first-person view of it. (See Figure 13–1)

You can change your perspective to see the player's character in the game. Press the Esc key to open the Game menu, and then click the icon beside the Speaker icon in the top left to change the perspective.

When you've finished, you can exit the game by pressing the Esc key to open the game menu and then choosing Quit To Title.

# # Moving around

Minecraft is easy to use two hands — one on the mouse and one on the keyboard. Use the mouse to look around you and change your direction, sliding left and right to turn sideways, and forward and backward on the desk to look up and move. To, use the keys W and S for forward and backward, and A and D to take a sidestep left and right. These keys form a cluster on the keyboard, which makes it easy to switch between them.
