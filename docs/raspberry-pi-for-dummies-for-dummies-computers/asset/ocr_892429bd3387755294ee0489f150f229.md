# Using Samples

The programs you've made so far are an interesting way to explore computer
music, especially when the computer starts surprising with its random\compositions.

Sonic Pi can also use samples, which are snippets of music that you can manip-\ulate, such as by changing their speed or adding effects to them. Sonic Pi includes
a wide range of samples, and you can see a list of them by showing the Help pane
 then using the Sample button in the bottom left. They're especially useful for
adding drum loops to your music.

Here's one of our favorites:

```
sample :loop_industrial
```

Put that into a new buffer and click Run to hear it. You can speed up or slow
down by changing its rate. Here's how you make it play at half its normal
speed:

```
sample :loop_industrial, rate: 0.5
```
