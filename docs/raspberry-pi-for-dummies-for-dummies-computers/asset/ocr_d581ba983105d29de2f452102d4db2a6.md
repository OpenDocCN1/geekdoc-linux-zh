# Pedestrian Crossing

The dice project covered in the preceding section is all about displaying patterns.
The Pedestrian Crossing project is about time and sequences. It simulates a U.K.

the pedestrian's pressing a button. These crossings are also common in the United
States, but a little of the detail may be different from state to state, let's first go

green light is on for the traffic and all the other lights are off. There's a button at
crossing for the pedestrian to request a stop, and a traffic sensor is buried

ab busy road isn't stopped too many times by pedestrians or jokers, whereas a
