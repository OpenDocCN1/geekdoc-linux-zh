+   7. Click OK.

# Adding videos

To add to your library, follow these steps:

+   1. Click Movies from the menu on the left.

If you can't see it, it's probably off the top of the screen. Hover the mouse cursor over the menu and use the scroll wheel to bring the Movies option back again.

If you've already added some films, you'll see a list of them .. (two dots) at the top of list. In that case, click .. twice to go up a level and then choose Files. This will show you the folders you have added to your library already.
