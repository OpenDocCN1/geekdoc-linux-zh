# Chapter 14
Making Music with Sonic Pi

For much of the music we hear today, computers are at least important in the studio as microphones are, and they have been for many years. Sonic Pi, you can start composing your own computer music-programming your Raspberry Pi. It enables you to put together simple programs that play synthesizer melodies and sampled sounds, generating your own instrumental music.

Sam Aaron

Sonic Pi runs from the desktop environment (see Chapter 4) and is pre-installed in Raspbian.
