# Spreadsheets

In the accounting world, spreadsheets are king. These days, however, many home users have discovered the benefits of using spreadsheet software. From balancing checkbooks to keeping track of budgets, having all the numbers at your fingertips can come in handy.

The Microsoft Excel spreadsheet is the most popular package used in the Windows world. In the OpenOffice.org suite, the spreadsheet package is called Calc.

Although not a direct replacement for Excel, Calc can perform most of the functions of Excel. The following sections take a look at OpenOffice.org Calc so that you can get to work.
