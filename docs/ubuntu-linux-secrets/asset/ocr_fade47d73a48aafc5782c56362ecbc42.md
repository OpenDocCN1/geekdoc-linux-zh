Secret
From the Sounds tab you can also disable all sounds generated from the operating system, which can come in handy sometimes. Just remove the check from the Play Alerts and Sound Effects check box, and your desktop will be quiet!

# The Sound Applet

When Ubuntu detects a sound card in your workstation at installation time, it automatically places the Sound applet in your panel (see Chapter 4, "Exploring the Desktop"). The Sound applet appears as a speaker icon in the right side of the top panel. When you click it, the master volume control appears, as shown in Figure 11-3.
