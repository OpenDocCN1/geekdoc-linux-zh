# # Playing Devil's Advocate

Many experienced administrators feel that just taking preventive measures and hoping that nothing bad will ever happen is to court disaster. To get a real sense of security, they feel, a system should be put to real tests. In other words, why not attack your security infrastructure yourself before a miscreant does it? By posing as a potential attacker, you may be able to reveal potential vulnerabilities yourself before the *unfriendly* ones find you. Luckily, you do not have to work too hard to become a miscreant these days. Tools are available that can help you assume that role quite easily. This section discusses some of these commonly used tools.
