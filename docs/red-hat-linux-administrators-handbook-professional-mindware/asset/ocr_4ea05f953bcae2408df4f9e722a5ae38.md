# Note

![](bbox=[0.05, 0.7, 0.15, 0.8])

If you are planning to use an ISDN card to provide Internet connectivity for your server, I highly recommend that you get an ISDN router instead of an internal card. My experience with internal cards has made me come to this conclusion. I have tried a few name-brand ISDN cards that do not work very well. On top of the problems with dealing with yet another card (IRQ and I/O address resources are limited on a PC), the price difference between a internal card and an external ISDN router is not significant when you consider the fact that dedicated ISDN itself is a fairly expensive service in most parts of the United States.
