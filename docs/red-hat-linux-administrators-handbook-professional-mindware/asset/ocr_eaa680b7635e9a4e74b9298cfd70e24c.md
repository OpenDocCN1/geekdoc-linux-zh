# About the Author

Mohamed J. Kabir is the chief technology officer and a co-founder of Intevo, Inc. At work, he designed and developed Intevo Mail, an affordable, permission-based e-messaging service for organizations of all sizes. Kabir studied computer engineering at California State University, Sacramento, and is also the author of Red Hat Linux 7 Server, Apache Server Bible, and Apache Server Administrator’s Handbook and co-author of SuSE Linux Server. You can find more information about him at his public Web site, http://www.nitec.com. He can also be reached via e-mail at kabir@ nitec.com.
