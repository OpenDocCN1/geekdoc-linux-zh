# Chapter 5: Slipping and Sliding—Exploring the Internet, Linux Style

With Chapter 4 behind you, it's time to get serious about all this Internet business and start putting it to good use. Chapter 5 takes you on a tour of Ubuntu's suite of Internet applications, in particular the Firefox browser. You're introduced to Ubuntu's email and instant messaging tools before embarking on a crash course in social networking with Twitter and Ghibber.

# Chapter 6: Rounding Out the Bird—Downloading, Installing, and Updating Programs the Easy Way

The default Ubuntu installation includes most of the software you need, but there's still much available out there, free and waiting on the Internet. Chapter 6 teaches you how to easily download and install applications using the Ubuntu Software Center and how to broaden your software horizons using third-party repositories.

# Chapter 7: A Tiny Nest—File and Disc Handling in Ubuntu

From creating folders to copying files to browsing your system and network, Chapter 7 covers all file management. You'll learn how to work with USB storage devices, transfer files via Bluetooth, burn data CDs and DVDs, deal with CD-RW disks and multissession CDs, and create space-saving compressed archives of files.

# Chapter 8: Simple Kitten Ways—Getting to Know the Linux Terminal and Command Line ... and the Cool Things It Can Do

Many people stay away from Linux because they perceive it as a system in which everything still needs to be done by executing commands. Perception is a load of squashed avocados, as the saying goes. Still, a lot of stuff can be done via the command line-based Terminal, and Chapter 8 will tell you all about it as it tames your fears and piques your interest in commands. Really.

# Chapter 9: Dressing Up the Bird—Customizing the Look and Feel of Your System

Tired of looking at the same old desktop? Feeling nostalgic for the desktop in your previous operating system? Chapter 8 tells you how to beat the deja vu by changing the look and feel of your system to take control of Ubuntu's visual effects, Compiz.

# Chapter 10: Gutenberg—Setting Up and Using Your Printer and Scanner

Just about everyone with a computer has or needs a printer. Chapter 10 tells you exactly how to get your printer (or scanner) working with Ubuntu.

# Chapter 11: Polyglot Penguins—Linux Speaks Your Language

Need to jot a note in Urdu? Write a book in Korean? Send a letter in Chinese to your friend in Chengdu? Chapter 11 gives you what you need to read and write in just any language in the world.
