# ## RawTherape

RawTherapee is not some solution for what ills your troubled soul but rather handy application that allows you to manipulate RAW image files. RAW image files are images that have not been processed by the on-board software in your camera that converts the data it receives from the sensor into a format that your computer is able to deal with (usually JPEG files). For most people, JPEG works just fine, but many photographers want to have the best quality results after making adjustments to their images on their computers save their files in RAW image format, or RAW mode. While not all cameras have the option to save files in RAW mode, many advanced amateur and professional camera models do.\ Once you load RAW image files onto your computer, the thumbnails for those files will appear as black rectangles. That's essentially blobs of raw data that neither your computer on its own can make heads or tails of. In a sense, they are a digital equivalent of photo negatives.
