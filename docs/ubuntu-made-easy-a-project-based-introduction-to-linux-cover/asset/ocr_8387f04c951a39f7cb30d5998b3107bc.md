If you'd like to get out of the CD and return your computer to normal, click the power button at the top right of the screen (it looks like a circle between a power icon and a gear), **Shut Down**, and then choose **Restart** from the window that appears. After a little while, you will be asked to remove the CD from the drive and press ENTER. The computer will restart and return you to your normal Windows desktop soon. If you have no luck with the Restart button for some reason, it is safe to turn your computer off and then on again just by using the power button on the front of the machine.
