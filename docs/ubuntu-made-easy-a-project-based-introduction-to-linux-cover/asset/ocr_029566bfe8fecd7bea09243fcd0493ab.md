As the saying goes, there’s no place to begin like the beginning, so in this chapter we’ll do just that by introducing you to the world of Linux. We’ll then get you up to snuff on what this Ubuntu thing is all about, why you might want to install and use it, and what you will need in order to do. For those of you who already have joined the Linux world and have Ubuntu or other distribution installed on your machines, some of this may be old news, but a few bits of information may be of interest to you. For others who may be curious or are still unsure whether not to take the plunge, or those of you who have no idea what Linux even and have picked up this book to find out, this chapter will prove especially useful.
You probably have lots of questions as you make your way through the book, and like most people, you may also have a few doubts or worries. Hopefully by the time you finish, you will have your Linux system up and running; any doubts or worries you might have had will been dispelled; and your questions, for the most part, will be answered.
