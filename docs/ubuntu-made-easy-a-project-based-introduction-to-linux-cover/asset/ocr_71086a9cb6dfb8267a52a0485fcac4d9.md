After closing the Keyboard Preferences window, a keyboard layout indicator will appear in the upper-right corner of the top panel. The letters en will appear, indicating that you are currently using your English language keymap. If you click that indicator, you will toggle between any of the other layouts you activated. If you are not sure where the keys are located in your current keymap, you can get some help by right-clicking the indicator and selecting Show Current Layout in the drop-down menu, after which a map of the new layout will appear in a separate window, as shown in Figure 11-6. The keymap shown in that window will be hard to make out, so you will probably need to expand the window by dragging it by one of its bottom corners.
