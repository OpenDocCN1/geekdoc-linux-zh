# Managing Your Audio Device’s Audio Files with Rythmbox

As I've already mentioned, Rythmbox is set as the default application for
Phones, iPads, Android-powered devices, and, one way or another,
y other music player in Ubuntu. Once Rythmbox is up and running,
things work perfectly much as they do in iTunes.

+   - To view the contents of your iPod or other device, click the icon for the
device in the left pane of the window. The contents of the device will
appear in the right pane of the window.
- To copy a copy from your library to your device, just click Music in the
Library section of the left pane and then drag the artists, albums, or single
tracks from the right pane to your device’s device in the left pane.

If dragging files to device seems a bit tedious, it is possible to sync
to the Rythmbox music library. Just click the icon for your device
in the left pane of the Rythmbox window and then click the Sync with
Library above the main pane. A window, like that in Figure 15-5, will
appear. Check the items you want to sync in the top portion of the window
and then click Sync with the device. Rythmbox will begin the syncing pro-\cess, copying all the music and podcasts (if you chose that option)
to your device and showing the progress of the transfer in the bottom right
corner of the window.
