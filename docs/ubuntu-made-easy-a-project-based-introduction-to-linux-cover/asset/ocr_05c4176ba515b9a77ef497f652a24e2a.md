# About the Penguin

You may have been wondering about the penguin in the chapter title, so I might as explain that now. The penguin was chosen by Linux Torvalds as the mascot, and what has come to be thought of as the Linux penguin was designed by Larry Ewing and named Tux (see Figure 1-1). This explains not only the ornithological references and graphics throughout the book but also there are many penguin icons in Linux distributions and so programs that include penguin or Tux in their names, as Tux Racer, Tux Paint, and Penguins, combined with the fact that Linux is a revolutionary OS, explains why Linux users are sometimes referred to as Penguinistas. True, Ubuntu doesn't play the penguin as other distributions, but Linux is so the penguin lives in Ubuntu . . . . just a bit consciously.

The goal of this book is not to twist your arm and make you dump your current OS for Linux. If you are happy with what you've got, then stick with it. However, many people have switched to Linux for one reason or another. For many, as it may say, it is a matter of money.
