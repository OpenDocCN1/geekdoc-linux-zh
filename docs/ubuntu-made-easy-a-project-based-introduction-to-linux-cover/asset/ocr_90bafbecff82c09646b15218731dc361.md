To use SoundConverter, add the songs you want to convert to the main pane by clicking either **Add File** or **Add Folder** button. You can also drag audio files from your *Music folder* (or wherever you store them).\ Once you've chosen the files to convert, you need to choose which format to convert to. You can do this by selecting **Edit ► Preferences** and then there, making your choice in the Preferences window (Figure 15-9). While you're there, it's also a good idea to tell SoundConverter to place your converted files in a location other than the folder where the original files are stored. This prevents having to deal with duplicates in Linux audio playback applications that automatically scan your *Music folder*, such as Rhythbox. You might also want to check the **Create subfolders** box to keep things organized.

Once you have set things up and are ready to convert, click **Close** in the Preferences window and then click **Convert** in the main window. SoundConverter will begin doing its stuff.
