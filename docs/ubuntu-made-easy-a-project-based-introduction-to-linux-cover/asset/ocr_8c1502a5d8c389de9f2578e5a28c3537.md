Unfortunately, many guides to using the command line are written by hard-core command-line junkies, whose enthusiasm for what they see as a really good thing inadvertently makes what they write even more off-putting to the recent Linux immigrant or wannabe.

For your sake, I will try not to go with enthusiasm so as to scare you right back to Chapter 6 and the more comfortable world of the Ubuntu Software-Center. I will also try to keep things in perspective by teaching you, whenever possible, to use the command line as a complement or alternative to the graphical tools you have at your disposal, rather than presenting it as the sole way of going about things. Of course, I am not going to cover every possible angle in this regard—just enough to give you some exposure and experience and, ideally, make you feel at a little more at ease with the command line. Who knows? You might actually come to think of using the command line as . . . fun? Well, I won't get too carried away.
