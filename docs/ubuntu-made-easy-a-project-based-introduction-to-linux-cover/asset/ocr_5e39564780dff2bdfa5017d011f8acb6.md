# The Dash

The uppermost icon in the Launcher is often called the Ubuntu button because it has the Ubuntu logo on it (Figure 3-9), though it's also called the Dash button because it opens—the Dash. The Dash helps you find applications and files on your computer, and it can help you find things on the Internet. You may be thinking that the Dash sounds like Ubuntu's answer to the Windows Start button, and in some ways you would be right, though working with it is different and its abilities are greater.

![](bbox=[0.676, 0.107, 0.735, 0.159])

Figure 3-9: The Ubuntu button, aka the Dash button

Click the Dash button to get your first view of the Dash. You can also bring up the Dash by pressing the Windows key, also referred to as the super meta key, on your keyboard. The first time out, the Dash will be, in all its nearly transparent, empty (Figure 3-10). After you've used it a bit, the Dash will show your most recently used applications and files when it opens, for now the only things you will see are the search bar at the top and a series of white icons at the bottom. Clicking these icons takes you to the Dash's lenses.
