This is very cool and handy, let's say you're done with whatever you were doing and you're turning your machine over to your spouse or child. you might just want to close down your side of things for logging out. To do this, close the gear icon at the end right of the panel and select Log Out. A small dialog will appear asking if you are sure that you want to close all the programs and log out of the computer. Assuming you do, it will Log Out. If not, click Cancel. If you opt to log out, you will be returned to the login screen, and there you can select account to log into by clicking the account name and providing the password for that account.
