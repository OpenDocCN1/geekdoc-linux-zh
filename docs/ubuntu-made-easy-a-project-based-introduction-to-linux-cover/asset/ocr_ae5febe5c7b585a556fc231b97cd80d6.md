# Telling People You Don't Want to Chat

![](bbox=[0.683, 0.351, 0.89, 0.483])

Chatting is great, but very few people have the stamina (or spare time) to do it all day long. It can be annoying if people try to start conversations while you're busy. Unfortunately, there's an easy way to let everyone know you don't want to be disturbed. Open the Messaging menu. Your current status will be marked out by a small white circle at the top of menu. If you're connected to an IM network, the circle will be the green Available status, which means that other people can tell you're online. Click any one of the other items to choose a different status (Away or Busy, for example) from the menu (Figure 5-20). Depending on the status you choose, other people will be told that you're busy when they try to start a chat or won't be able to see that you're online at all. Peace and quiet, only a couple of clicks away.
