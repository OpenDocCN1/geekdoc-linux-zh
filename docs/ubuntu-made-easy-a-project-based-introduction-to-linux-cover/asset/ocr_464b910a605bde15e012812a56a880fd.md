feature that comes in handy when you are working with lengthy or otherwise complex documents.

The Navigator allows you to bounce back and forth easily between pages in a document or between elements therein, such as sections, links, and so on. Let say you have a document with lots of illustrations (like this chapter), and you want to jump directly from graphic to graphic. In this case you would double-click the word Graphics in the main pane of the Navigator and then click the buttons (the odd buttons to the left of the page number selector) to begin jumping.

If you are dealing with a document containing various headings, such as all of the chapters in this book, you can also use Navigator to switch among those levels. Say you've decided to add a new heading at the last minute to a document you've writing. You then need to drop all of the headings you had before a notch, with the former main heading becoming a subheading and so on. When double-click the word Headings, the text of all the headings you have listed in the document appears. You can then select a heading in list and click the Demote button (that one at the far right of the second row of buttons) to move it down a level.

# Gallery

To the right of the Navigator button is the Gallery button. By clicking this button, you cause the Gallery, a library of graphical elements for use in your documents or web pages, to appear in a separate pane at the top of your document window (see Figure 126). The elements within the Gallery range from various types of lines to buttons to colored three-dimensional doughnuts, and you can add items of your yours.
