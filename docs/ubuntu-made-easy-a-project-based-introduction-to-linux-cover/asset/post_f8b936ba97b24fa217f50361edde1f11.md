## Importing Digital Images

Although file manager for Linux can be bit spotty, for most users, here's what you need to do:

- 1. Plug your camera into one of your USB ports. Within seconds, a window like Figure 13-1 will appear. In that window, select Import in the drop-down menu and then click OK.
- 2. A file manager window will appear, revealing the contents of your camera. An icon for your camera (looking like a USB drive) will also appear over Devices in the side pane of that window. A message will appear at the top of the window telling you the device you connected contains digital images, along with a button that you can click to import the images.
- 3. Double-click through the folders on your camera until you find the photos you want to import.
- 4. Drag and drop, or copy and paste, the images from your camera to any logical spot in your Home folder.
- 5. When you're done transferring photos, you can click the eye icon next to the entry for your camera in the left pane of the file manager, or you can turn off your camera. The file manager that opened will automatically close, and your camera's entry will disappear from the side pane of any other file manager windows.

## Working with Digital Images in Shotwell

Shotwell not only imports images from your camera to your computer but also acts as a handy photo-organizing, browsing, viewing, and publishing tool, allowing you to easily send emails without fiddling around attachments. It also lets you easily publish to popular online sites such as Facebook, Flickr, and PicassaWeb.
