# # Concept and Approach

The book is intended as both a handy reference and a dynamic learning experience: You'll get some hands-on experience as you work through it. The text is organized by increasing skills, so that, as much as possible, you won't be asked to do something that you haven't already learned. In add-tion, you can put the various morsels of knowledge you acquire into practice through several exercises, or projects.

The projects in this book serve another purpose as well: Working through them, you will learn to configure and customize your Ubuntu system. By the time you finish this book, you should have a pretty mastery of Ubuntu.

These chapter descriptions should give you a better idea of what is in store:

**Chapter 1: Becoming a Pinguista—Welcome to the World of Linux**nWhat's Linux? What's Ubuntu? What's a distribution? Can I... ? Will me ... ? Chapter 1 holds the answers to these and many other questions you might have as it introduces you to the world of Linux and what it takes to get it up and running on your machine.

**Chapter 2: Wading and Diving—Running and (If You Like) Installing Ubuntu**nThe CD that comes with this book works as both a live CD and an install CD. Chapter 2 tells you how to run a live Ubuntu session from the CD and, assuming you take the Linux bug after doing so, how to install Ubuntu on your hard disk. You can install Ubuntu as your operating system or with Windows, and you get directions for both.

**Chapter 3: A Place to Call Home—Getting to Know the Desktop**nYou're probably already familiar with the concept of a desktop. Chapter 3 points out the differences between Ubuntu's Unity desktop and your previous operating system, and it teaches you a number of cool tricks you can use to customize the look and feel of things.

**Chapter 4: More Than Webbed Feet—Connecting to the Internet**nHave computer, cybertravel, would be the mantra of the Inter-net age, and that being the case, Chapter 4 is an indispensable part of your Ubuntu experience. In this chapter, you will learn how to connect to the Internet and set up wireless and mobile broadband connections. You'll also find tips for using a modem and some handy troubleshooting advice just in case you struggled to get connected.
