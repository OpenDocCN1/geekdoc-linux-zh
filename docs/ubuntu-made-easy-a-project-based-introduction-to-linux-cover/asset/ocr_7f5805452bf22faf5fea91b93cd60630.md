# PENGUINS AT WORK

Getting Down to Business in Linux

![](bbox=[0.173, 0.483, 0.355, 0.621])

Although computers are used for such diverse (and fun!) activities as programming, making music, creating art, and playing games, at some point or almost every one of us has to sit down in front of our machine and get some work done. That being the case, many users want to know what office-related applications are available for their system.

Fortunately, Linux can get down to business as well as the next OS. More importantly, there are so many applications freely available for you to try, you might find that you actually end up using far more office-related productivity apps than you would had to pay for everything up front. In this chapter, I'll walk you through the offerings that come bundled with Ubuntu and then point you to yet others you might want to consider adding to your collection.
