# # Shutting Down

Now that you know your desktop environment so well, you may feel like calling it day and shutting down your machine. To do so, just click the Quit button at the far-right corner of the top panel (it looks like a power button). In the menu that appears, there will be a number of choices in the lower half to choose from: Lock Screen, Log Out, Suspend, and Shut Down. Select Shut Down, and a small window will appear to ask you to confirm whether your decision or opt to restart (or cancel) instead (Figure 3-24). Click Shut Down, and the shutdown process will begin.

The actual shutdown will take a few seconds as the system closes its services. When it is all done, the system should power down your computer as well, in which case you are done. On a few machines, the system might not be able to power down the computer. You will know this is so in your case because all screen activity will come to an end. If you get to that point and nothing else happens for 15 seconds or so, then just power the machine manually by pressing the power button. It is completely safe to do so at that point.
