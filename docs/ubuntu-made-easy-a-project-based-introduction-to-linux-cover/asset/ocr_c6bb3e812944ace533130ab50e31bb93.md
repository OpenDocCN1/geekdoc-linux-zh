There are many threads on the forums that sifting through them would likely take a lifetime. Luckily, there's a pretty good search feature. Just click the Search link at the top of the page, type some search terms in the box that appears, and click Go to see what turns up.

# Replying to a Post

You can read any of the threads on the forums without a user account, but you do need to be logged in to reply to one of them. Click the Register link at the top of the page to create an account. You'll need to click through a couple of pages, entering your details as you go, registration should be quite straightforward. Click Complete Registration when you're finished and check your email. Once the confirmation email arrives, the first link that it contains is finish setting up your account. Log in using the form at the top right of the page.

Now that you're logged in, find the thread you want to reply to and click the New button at the top (or bottom) of the page. Type your message and click Submit Reply, and your post will be added to the end of the thread. If you realize that you made a mistake in your reply, you can go back to the thread and click the small EDIT button in the bottom-right corner of your post.
