If you are trying to connect to a wireless network in a public place (for example, at a conference center, university or airport or a train), you may need to perform some additional steps to finish setting up connection. These might include visiting a specific website in your web browser to register for the service or providing your MAC address to the network administrator. For these of first, open Firefox and type the address that the provider of the wireless connection should have given you. Hopefully, a page will load, and you will be asked to enter some details. If you're asked for your MAC address, you should instead click the Network icon and select Connection information from the menu that appears. Then find the Hardware Address (which will look similar to the one in Figure 4-6) and copy it and give it to whoever needs it. This is your MAC address.
