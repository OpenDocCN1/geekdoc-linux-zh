# Reading Data CDs and DVDs

Dealing with data CDs and DVDs in Ubuntu is quite simple, because everything is automatic. To read a CD or DVD with data on it, rather than music or video, place the disc in your drive, and a CD or DVD icon (they look the same) will automatically appear at the bottom of the Launcher and you can click once on the icon, after the disc's contents appear in Nautilus. Then you can copy files from the CD or DVD to your hard disk using standard drag-and-drop or copy-and-paste procedures.
When you want to remove the CD or DVD, just right-click the icon for that disc in the sidebar and select eject in the pop-up menu. The disc will be ejected automatically.
