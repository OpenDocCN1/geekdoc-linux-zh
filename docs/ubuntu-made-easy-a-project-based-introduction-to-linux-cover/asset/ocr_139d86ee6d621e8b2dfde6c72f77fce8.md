To create a playlist in Rhythmboxbox, select **Music > Playlist > New Playlist**. When the Playlist icon appears in the left pane of the Rhythmbox window, type a name for the list and press ENTER. Click the Music icon in the left pane to show the contents of your music library. Then add the songs you want by dragging them from the Library to the Playlist icon. The songs themselves will remain in the Library, so you aren't moving anything—just creating aliases.

You can also create *automatic playlists*, which are lists that automatically scan the Library for songs that match your criteria. For example, might choose to create a list for all the songs in your Library by a particular artist or of a specific genre. To do this, select **Music > Playlist > New Automatic Playlist**. A window will appear in which you can specify what the list is to contain. You can even specify how many songs you want in the list.

Once you've created your lists, you can play one song by clicking it once and then clicking the **Play** button near the top of the window (or by double-clicking the list).

# Ubuntu One Music Store via Rhythmbox

Similar to the iTunes store, Rhythmbox provides you with access to the Ubuntu One Music Store, which provides music by major artists from the 7digital catalog. The songs sold are free from the digital rights management (DRM) limitations found in many songs purchased from other stores. The Ubuntu One Music Store is also cloud enabled, whatever you is delivered to your Ubuntu cloud, from where you can download your purchases to your other devices. (An Ubuntu account is required, but you can get one, with 5GB of free storage, for free.)
