Once you've made your selections, click the Apply Changes button. You will be prompted for your password at this point, so provide what is required, and the Language Support tool will begin downloading and installing the support packages you specified. Once it is done, you will be notified and left with the Installed Languages window.

Once you have installed support for additional languages, you can choose to have your system open in a different language environment from the Language Support window by selecting the language you want to use when you next log in and dragging it to the top of the list (Figure 11-15). For languages with complex input systems, you can also select your input method of choice, such as IB, in the menu button next to the words Keyboard input method system. You can then close the window, log out, and log in again, after which your system will appear in the language you chose.
