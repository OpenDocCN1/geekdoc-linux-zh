> Figure 6-6: Finding commercial software in the Software Center

When you buy a commercial application in the Software Center, the purchase is recorded and kept with the details of your Ubuntu single sign-on account. If you ever need to reinstall Ubuntu, or accidentally uninstall an app that you bought, this means that all is not lost—you can easily reinstall any of your purchases. With the Software Center open, move your mouse to the very top of the screen to display the menu bar and click **Reinstall Previous Purchases**. If prompted for your single-on account details, enter them in the window provided and follow the instructions onscreen. You'll have your paid-for apps back in no time.

# Performing System Updates via Update Manager

A graphical frontend for APT that I'll cover in this chapter is Update Manager, which is used for updating your system. Updates are available quite readily and consist of newer versions of packages you have installed. Most of the time, a newer version will have been released because it fixes some security issue, but updates may also fix problems with software and introduce new features.

The Update Manager will pop up of its own when updates are available, so it's probably best to forget about it until it decides to appear. But just once, let's be a little more proactive.
