## The Complete Manual

The Raspberry Pi is one of the most exciting things to happen to computers in recent years. As an educational tool, this tiny PC has reignited interest in bare-metal computing in schools. As a platform for open-source software, it has also inspired millions of people to try Linux – many for the first time. Most exciting of all is the potential to incorporate the device into practical projects, as demonstrated by the tutorials in this newly revised edition of Raspberry Pi The Complete Manual. So grab your Pi and get creating!
