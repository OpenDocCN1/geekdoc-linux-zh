Where to get ROMs

Many older games have, after only, been abandoned. The original publishers are defunct and it's not clear who owns the rights. There are several sites which claim to have distribution from the original creators to distribute their games – but all of them should be clear that they are not giving a right to buy legal copies of or sell these games legally for commercial use. # Configuring

06  Right – you’re almost ready to play. Put the micro SD card into the Raspberry Pi Zero, hook up the controller USB cable and the HDMI cable. Finally, plug the Pi into the power. It should boot up automatically, and after a few seconds, you’ll be greeted with a configuration screen.

RetroPie should automatically detect any connected USB game pads and step you through setting up the buttons. Once you’re finished, you’ll be presented with a screen showing all the choices you made.
