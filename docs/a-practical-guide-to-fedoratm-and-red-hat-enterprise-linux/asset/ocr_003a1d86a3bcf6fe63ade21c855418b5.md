When enough free space exists, the Installation Options window offers you the choice of automatically partitioning the disk(s) or having you review and modify the disk partitions. If there are partitions in use by other operating systems that Anaconda can remove or shrink, it adds the choice of guiding you through the process of reclaiming that space. Select one of these radio buttons and click **Continue**.

If there is not enough space on the disks, Anaconda tells you how much space the new operating system needs based on your selection of software packages (page 66). It also summarizes how much free space exists and how much space you can reclaim from partitions in use by other operating systems. In this case it presents two buttons: **Custom partitioning** (next) and **Reclaim space** (page 77). The first of these buttons displays the Manual Partitioning screen and the second displays the Reclaim Disk Space screen.
