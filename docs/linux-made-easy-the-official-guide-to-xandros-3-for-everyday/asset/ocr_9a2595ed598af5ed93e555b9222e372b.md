You can also input or change your proxy settings at any other time via the Setup window:

+   Go to the **Launch** menu and select **Applications** \> **CrossOver** \> **Office Setup**.

+   In the Setup window, click the **Settings** tab.

+   Once in that tab, click the **Advanced** button.

+   When the Advanced Configuration Settings window appears (Figure 15), check **Use HTTP proxy**, and type in your settings in the two boxes below.

+   Click **OK** to close the Configuration window, and then click **OK** in the Setup window to seal the deal.
