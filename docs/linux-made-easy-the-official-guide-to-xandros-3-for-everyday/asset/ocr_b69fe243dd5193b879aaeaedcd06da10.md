Xandros Does Video

![](bbox=[0.1, 0.47, 0.35, 0.67])

Now that we've covered much of what Xandros can do in terms of audio, let's turn our attention to what is arguably the second most important of its talents in our CNN/MTV-era world: video. Xandros is quite capable in terms of video playback, allowing you to view video files you download from the Internet or from your digital movie camera or watch Internet video streams, video CDs (VCDs), and unencrypted DVDs. If you have a TV tuner card, it even lets you watch television right there on your computer screen.
