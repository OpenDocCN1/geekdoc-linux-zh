To bring about this remarkable transformation, right-click anywhere on the main XMMS program window, and from the pop-up menu select **Options > Skin Browser**.

This will open the *Skin selector* window (see Figure 14-17); scroll down until you find UltrafinaSEM. Once you see it, click it. Like magic, the transformation will be complete.
