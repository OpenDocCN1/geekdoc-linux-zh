There are a far greater number of games available from XN that do not require you to have an accelerated graphics card. These include famous clones of familiar games, such as *Tetris*, *Same Game*, the freeware classic from Japan; *Battleship*; and the German card game, *Lieutenant Skat*, which comes bundled with most other Linux distros. For my part, however, I will be focusing on two other games that may not be particularly familiar to you, if at all, but are loads of fun and classics in their own right: *Frozen-Bubble* and *Psol*.

# Frozen-Bubble

Frozen-Bubble (Figure 18-8) is a classic, and although it has been ported over for use in the Windows and Mac worlds, its roots and soul are pure Linux.

The objective of Frozen-Bubble is simple: shoot down all the bubbles in a screen in order to move on to the next. While this may sound so simple as to border on being boring, that is not the case. And best of all, you can live eternally in the world of Frozen-Bubble because you just cannot die; every screen you fail to clear the first time around will repeat over and over and over again until you get it right. Pretty cool (the life-eternal bit, at least), you have to admit.
