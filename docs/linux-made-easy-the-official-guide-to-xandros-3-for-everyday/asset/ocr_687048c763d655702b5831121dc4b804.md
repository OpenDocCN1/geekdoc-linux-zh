# Word Count

A common feature that is very easy to use, but not so easy to find, is *Word Count*. To take a tally of the number of words, characters, images, and lines in your document, just go to the **File** menu within your document and select **Properties**. When the Properties window for your document appears, click the **Statistics** tab, after which you will see all of the information clearly laid out before you, as in the example in Figure 17-5.
