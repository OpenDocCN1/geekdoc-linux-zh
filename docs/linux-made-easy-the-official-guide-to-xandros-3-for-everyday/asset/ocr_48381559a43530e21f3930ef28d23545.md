13. Now that your page is set up, it's time to print it out. Be sure to set your printer's settings to print at 600 dpi, and use a good-quality paper, preferably photo quality. Of course, if you are using a laser printer, your paper choice will matter much less. Once ready, print.

While the four images will probably look more or less the same on your screen, there should be a noticeable difference in your printed output. On the basis of that output, you will be able to see what a difference a few hundred ppi can make and thus have a better idea what scanner settings provide the best results on your printer.
