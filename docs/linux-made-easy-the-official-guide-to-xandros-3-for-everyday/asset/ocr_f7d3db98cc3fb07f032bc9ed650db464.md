# Finding Feeds for Live Bookmarks

Well this is all very cool, but you are probably wondering where you can find feeds that match your personal interests. A good place to start is a site called Feedster. By going to Feedster's Feedfinder page (http://feedfinder.feedster.com), you can type a keyword for the area you are interested in, and you will be presented with a list of results showing all of the available feeds that are relevant to your topic area.

To show you what to do then, let's use Chinese pop star Faye Wong as an example. If you want to search for someone or something else, feel free to make appropriate substitutions if you are going to follow along.

Anyway, here's what to do:

+   1. Go to http://feedfinder.feedster.com.
+   2. Type **Faye Wong** in the *Feedfinder* search box, and then click the **Find Feeds** button.
+   3. In the page of results that appears, click one of the links that sounds the most appropriate to you. When that page appears, there will be a small orange icon in the bottom-right corner of the window (Figure 13-10). The icon's appearance will vary according to the theme you are using.
