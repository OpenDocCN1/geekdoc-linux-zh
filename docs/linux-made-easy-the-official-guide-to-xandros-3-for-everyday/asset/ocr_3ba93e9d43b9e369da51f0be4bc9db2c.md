# ### Inserting Images into a Document

With all the images available to you via the Internet, your scanner, or your digital camera, it is hard to resist jazzing up your documents a bit by adding an image here and there. To insert an image into your document, just follow these steps:

+   1.  Click once wherever you want the image to appear in the document.
+   2.  Go to the **Insert** menu, and select **Graphics ▸ From File**.
+   3.  When the Insert Graphics window appears, check the box next to *Preview* (to make it easier to select images), and then navigate to and select the image you want to insert by clicking it once (Figure 17-10).
+   4.  Once you have selected your image, click **Open**, and the image will appear within your document.
