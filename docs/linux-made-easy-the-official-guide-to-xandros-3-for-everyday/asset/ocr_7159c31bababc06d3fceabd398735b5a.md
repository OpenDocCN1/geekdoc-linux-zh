+   12. You are now pretty much finished with the process of adding your first paste-on image, but to make things less confusing for you as you add additional images, it is a good idea to merge the paste-on image’s layer with that for its shadow, thus turning that particular pair of layers into one. To do this, go back to the Layers, Channels, Paths window and click the top layer listed in the main pane of that window, **Empty Layer**.

+   13. Now go to the **Layer** menu and select **Merge Down**.
+       If you now go back to the Layers, Channels, Paths window, you will see that you now have only two layers—*Background* and *Drop-Shadow*. Because each time you add a shadow for your additional paste-on images a new Drop-Shadow layer will be automatically created, it is best to rename this layer now, so as to avoid confusion later on. To do this, perform the following step:
+       a. Double-click the title area of the **Drop-Shadow** layer. The title area will then become a text-input box, in which you can type the new name for the layer. The name of the person in the paste-on photo would be appropriate. I’m calling mine *The Great Saw*. When finished, click anywhere else within the window, and the process will be complete.
