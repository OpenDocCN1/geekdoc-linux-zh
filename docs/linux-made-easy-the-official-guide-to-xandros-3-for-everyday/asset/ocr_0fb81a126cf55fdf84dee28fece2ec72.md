# **11A-4: Viewing Your Downloaded Images**

You will now be left with the main Digikam window, where you will *not* immediately see your images the first time around. To remedy this, click the drop-down button just below the menu bar that says *By Collection*, and in the drop-down menu that appears, select **By Folder**. Your new album will then appear in the left pane of the window under the *My Albums* heading. Click your new album once, and its contents will appear in the right pane of the window, as you can see in Figure 11-10.
