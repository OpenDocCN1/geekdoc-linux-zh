Figure 16-13: The top and bottom halves of the main GIMP window

The top half is a collection of the various drawing and selection tools available. The bottom half, on the other hand, shows the options available for each of those tools. This being the case, when you click a different tool in the top half of the window, the options in the bottom half change to reflect the options for the tool you’ve just selected.

The right GIMP window, like the left, also consists of two halves, each of which you can see in Figure 16-14.

The top half of the window shows the layers (a topic I’ll cover in greater detail later on in this project), channels, and paths for a selected image.

The contents of the window thus change when you click different open image windows.
