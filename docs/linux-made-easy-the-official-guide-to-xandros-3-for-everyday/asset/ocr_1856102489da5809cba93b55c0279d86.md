+   3. Two windows will then appear in rapid succession. The first window is explained in the next step. The second window asks you to place a blank disc in your drive. Do what that window says, and then once the blank disc has been swallowed into your machine and the little LED on the drive stops flashing, click **OK**. The burning process will then begin.

+   4. The remaining window (Figure 6-8) will indicate the progress of the burning process. The process itself consists of three steps, all of which are hands-off for you: preparing the track, recording the track, and fixing the disc. When complete, your disc will be automatically ejected from the drive, and you can click the **Done** button. You are now basically finished.
