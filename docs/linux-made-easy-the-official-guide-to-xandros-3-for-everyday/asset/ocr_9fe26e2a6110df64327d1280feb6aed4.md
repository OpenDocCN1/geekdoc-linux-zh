# Viewing Options

To view an image at a larger size, double-click it, and the image you chose will appear, slightly enlarged, in a separate window (Figure 11-11).

![](bbox=[0.2, 0.13, 0.7, 0.48])

Figure 11-11: Viewing your images at an enlarged size

To see the image at a yet greater size, either click the maximize button in the three-button cluster located at the top-right corner of the window—it’s the one in the middle—or just tug at one of the corners of the window with your mouse until it is the size you want. The image will then automatically enlarge. You can also easily see the other pictures in your album via this window by clicking the left or right arrow buttons just beneath the menu bar.

# Project 11B: Fine-Tuning Your Images

Digikam is fairly versatile little program that not only lets you download and display your images but also allows you to fine-tune them. With Digikam you can rotate, resize, crop, and sharpen your images; adjust their brightness and contrast; and reduce red-eye, although the flash units in most digital cameras today pretty much eliminate the need for this feature. Digikam also allows you to transform your color photos into black-and-white or, for a taste of faux nostalgia, sepia tones. All very cool, assuming you go in for that kind of stuff. If you do, most of these manipulation features can be found in the Fix, Transform, and Filter menus of Digikam’s enlarged-view windows that we discussed in Project 11A-4.
