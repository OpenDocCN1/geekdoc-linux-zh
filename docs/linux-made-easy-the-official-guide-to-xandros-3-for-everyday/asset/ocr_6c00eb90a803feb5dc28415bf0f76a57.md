It is also important to remember that the fish can push only objects that are supported, or will be supported, by other objects, or the other fish, upon completion of the push. This does not include pushing an object directly onto another fish, since this is not possible. It is possible, however, to push an object onto another object that is already supported by the other fish. Support of most objects can also be transferred from one fish to the other fish from beneath that object.
