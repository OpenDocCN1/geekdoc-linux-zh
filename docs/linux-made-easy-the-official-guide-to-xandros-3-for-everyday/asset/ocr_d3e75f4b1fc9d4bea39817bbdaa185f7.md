4. After that, click **Arcade** itself, and your window should look like mine in Figure 18-15.

Assuming everything looks pretty much as it should, you are ready to add a new menu item.

5. Go to the **File** menu and select **New Item**, and a small New Item window will appear.

6. In the text box in that window, type **Njam** (as in Figure 18-16), and then click **OK**. The *Name* box in the right panel of the Menu Editor window should now say *Njam*.

![](bbox=[0.2, 0.75, 0.55, 0.86])

*Figure 18-16: Naming a new menu item in the Menu Editor*

7. In the *Comment* box, type **A Pacman clone** (or just leave it blank if you prefer).

8. In the *Command* box, type `/home/yourusername/njam/njam -w`.

9. To start rounding things up, give your new launcher an icon by clicking the square box to the right of the *Name* and *Description* boxes.
