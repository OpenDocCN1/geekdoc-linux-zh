The following program is used in the next two examples.
write(*,'(' Blank '))
write(*,'('0Zero '))
write(*,'('+ Plus '))
write(*,'('1One ''))
end

With actual files.
castle% a.out | MyOutputFile asa < MyOutputFile | 1p

With only pipes.
castle% a.out | asa | 1p

Both of the above examples produce two pages of output.
Page 1:
Blank
ZeroPlus
Page 2:
One
