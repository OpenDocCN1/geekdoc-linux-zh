# Kerberos Version 5 Support (SUNWkr)

+   - kdestroy | Kerberos tickets. Moved from SUNWcsr in the Solaris 9 release.

+   - kinit | Kerberos login command. Moved from SUNWcsr in the Solaris 9 release.

+   - klist | List currently held Kerberos tickets. Moved from SUNWcsr in the Solaris 9 release.

+   - kpasswd | Change a user's Kerberos password. Moved from SUNWcsr in the Solaris 9 release.

+   - ktutil | Kerberos keytab maintenance command. Moved from SUNWcsr in the Solaris 9 release.
