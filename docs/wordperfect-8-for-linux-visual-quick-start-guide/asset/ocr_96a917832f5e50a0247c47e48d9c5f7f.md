# To add italics:

+   1. Select the text you want to format.
+   2. Click the Italic button on the Property Bar or press Ctrl+I on the keyboard.

or

+   A. Choose Font from the Format menu (Figure 1) or press F9 on the keyboard. The Font dialog box will open (Figure 2).
+   B. In the Appearance area, click the check box next to Italic (Figure 5).
+   C. Click OK to close the dialog box.

The text will be italicized (Figure 6).

or

+   1. As you type, click the Italic button on the Property Bar or press Ctrl+I on the keyboard.
+   2. Type the italic text.
+   3. Turn off the italic formatting by clicking the Italic button on the Property Bar or pressing Ctrl+I on the keyboard again.

The text will be italicized (Figure 6).

# Tip

To remove italic or bold formatting, select the text that is italicized or bold, then click the Italic or Bold button on the Property Bar.
