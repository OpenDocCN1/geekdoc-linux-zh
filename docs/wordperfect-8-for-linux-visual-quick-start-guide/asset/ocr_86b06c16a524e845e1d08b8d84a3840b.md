+   - To spell check a part of your document, position the insertion marker in the text you want to spell check (for instance, inside the word, sentence, or paragraph), then choose the part that you want to check from the Check menu in the Spell Checker dialog box (Figure 18).

+   - You can turn off spell checking for various options such as words with numbers, duplicate words, and irregular capitalization. To do so, choose the Options menu in the Spell Checker dialog box and deselect the items you don’t want spell checked (Figure 19).
