# To display the WordPerfect for Windows 6 or 7 Toolbar:

+   1. Right click on the WordPerfect 8 Toolbar. A QuickMenu will appear (Figure 13).
+   2. Choose either WordPerfect 7 or WordPerfect 6 from the QuickMenu. The toolbar you selected will appear at the top of the document window.

# Tip

When a toolbar is displayed, a tiny square appears next to the item on the QuickMenu.

# To hide the WordPerfect 8 Toolbar:

+   1. Right click on the WordPerfect 8 Toolbar. A QuickMenu will appear (Figure 14).
+   2. Choose WordPerfect 8 from the QuickMenu. The WordPerfect 8 Toolbar will disappear from the top of the document window.
