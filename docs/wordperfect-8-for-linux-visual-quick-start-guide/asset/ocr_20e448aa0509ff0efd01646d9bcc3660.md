+   Margins—the shadow cursor will snap to the left or right margins or to the center of the page between the margins. If you click in white space, the insertion point will snap to one of these positions.

+   Tabs—the shadow cursor will snap to the current tab settings. If you click in white space, the insertion point will snap to the closest tab. When you enter your text, it will continue from that position until the end of the line then wrap to the beginning of the next line.

+   Indent—the shadow cursor will snap to the current tab settings. If you click in white space, the insertion point will snap to the closest tab. When you enter text, it will continue from that position until the end of the line, then wrap to the indented position.

+   Spaces—the shadow cursor will snap to the closest space. If you click in white space, that's where the insertion point will appear.
