# Introducing the Cyclades-nQuirer with NetFlow™ support

The complex networks of today carry all different types of traffic, from customer information to viruses that can bring your network to a halt. Having visibility over your network traffic is essential for efficient network management and planning.

With the Cyclades-nQuirer, you can gather traffic data and convert it to NetFlow™ format, which allows you to consolidate and analyze your network in a higher-level format and achieve faster problem solving and increase network uptime. All this in compact form factor and at a fraction of the cost of comparable solutions.

With the Cyclades-nQuirer you will be able to look inside your network and understand it like you've never had before.
