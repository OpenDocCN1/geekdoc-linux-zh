Aspen Systems offers Beowulf clusters and high-performance computing (HPC) solutions with Myricom's Myrinet-2000 PCI-X cards: the low-cost M3F-PCIXD card, and the high-end M3F2-PCIXE card. Aspen Systems' customized Beowulf clusters and HPC solutions with the new E card offer 2 fiber ports per card for double the bandwidth, so now you can have up to 1GByte/s — perfectly balanced with the 1GByte/s PCI-X bus — for a double-speed and highly reliable connection.

Myricom's D and E card PCI-X interfaces were designed to work efficiently with GM-2 and MX Myrinet software support, supplied open-source by Myricom. GM-2 is the latest evolution of a message-passing system that is used in thousands of clusters worldwide. MX is a new message-passing system that is optimized for MPI, VI, and Sockets middleware.
