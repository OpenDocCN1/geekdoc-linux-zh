# Figure 8.24 Process Group Links

Figure 8.24 illustrates a process as the only member of a process group (upper diagram), and three processes in the same process group (lower diagram). The processes in the lower diagram that are not the process group leader obtain their PGID by linking to the PID structure of the process group leader.

Process groups are a subset of sessions; a session has one or more process groups associated with it. A session abstracts a process and the process’s control terminal and extends the abstraction to include process groups. All the process groups within a session have a common controlling terminal. Thus, all processes belong to a process group and are associated with the session. Sessions are abstracted by the session data structure, which the process links to through its p_sess pointer. As with process groups, sessions are inherited through the fork() code.
