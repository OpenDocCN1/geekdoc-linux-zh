# Figure 14.10 *UFS Idle Queue*

The `ufs_inactive()` routine attempts to free inodes with no pages in the page cache in preference to inodes that still have pages in the page cache. The goal—to keep as many file pages in the page cache as possible—is realized by the following procedure: inodes that have pages associated with them are placed at the tail of the idle queue, and inodes with no pages are placed at the head of the inode queue. When inodes are removed from the idle queue, they are removed from the tail of the queue, that is, the inodes without pages are taken first.
