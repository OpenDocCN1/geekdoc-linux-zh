Your first step in putting together a viable backup solution is to choose the backup media. It should be removable and stored in a safe place. It cannot be one of the disk drives in your system. Your selection of media should be either floppy, tape, ZIP media, or magneto-optical disk. Using copies as backup media can be unsuitable if a large amount of data should be backed up or unattended operation is an important issue. Mostly, SCSI or ATAPI tape is a suitable choice. The cost and availability may help you decide. If SCSI is your preferred choice, you have to choose between QIC, DAT, EXABY or DLT tapes of different capacities. The amount of data to be backed up and the cost of the backup device(s) and media may determine your decision. Some of the backup packages support libraries.

Choosing the right media may also depend on the backup tool you want to use and it supports your media.

Every IBM @server xSeries and Netfinity system has a built-in SCSI controller. We recommend you choose a SCSI device for your backup. You can choose between different technologies (QIC, DAT, EXABY, or DLT) depending on the amount of data to be stored and the speed required.
