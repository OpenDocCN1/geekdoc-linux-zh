5. To edit, click in the bottom pane in the place you want to change and start typing.

To delete characters, click to the right of the character you want to delete, and then press the Delete key as many times as you want to delete characters.

When you are finished, click the Save button and then click the Quit button. Or, if you have made a mistake and want to stop immediately without saving the file, Quit twice.

What happens if you have a really big file that can't fit into the bottom pane? The xedit provides a scroll bar so that you can move the data and down in the window, as shown in Figure 9-3. The easiest way to scroll is to drag the darkened pane of the scroll bar (known as the thumb), even though you are using your finger to move it up) and down. Drag, click and hold the left mouse button while moving the mouse cursor. You can see an example of this in Figure 9-3.
