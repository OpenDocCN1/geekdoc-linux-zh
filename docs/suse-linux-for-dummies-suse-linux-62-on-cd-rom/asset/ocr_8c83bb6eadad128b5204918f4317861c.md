# Part VI: The Part of Tens

A...For Dummies book just isn't complete without a Part of Tens. And that's Part VI. You find ten all-important resources in Chapter 17. In Chapter 18, we provide answers to the ten most bothersome questions people have after installing Linux.

# Part VII: Appendixes

Finally, the appendices. In Appendix A, we list all the hardware that we know of that's compatible with Linux. In Appendix B, you find out all you need to know about the Linux man pages. And in Appendix C, we tell you where in the book you get information on installing the CD-ROM.

# What You're Not to Read

Heck, you don't have to read any of the book if you don't want to, but why did you buy it? (Not that we're complaining.) Part I has background information. If you don't want it, don't read it. Also, text in sidebars is optional, although often helpful. If you're on the fast track to using Linux, you could skip the sidebars and the text with a technical stuff icon. But I suggest instead that you slow down a bit and enjoy the experience.
