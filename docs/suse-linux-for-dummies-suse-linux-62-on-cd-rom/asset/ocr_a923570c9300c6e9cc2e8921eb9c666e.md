# Setting Up Your Internet Connection

+   In This Chapter
- Finding an Internet service provider
- Connecting with your modem
- Making the connection between your modem and Internet service provider

One of the things to do these days is to surf the Internet. You connect to the Internet and tie up your phone line for hours, looking for obscure information and e-mail to people you barely know and never speak to the phone.

Now you, too, can go to a party and drop the casual phrase "I found this while surfing the Net this afternoon... (which will draw bored looks) ... on my Linux system." The mention of Linux will immediately show that you are truly a person of class and standing in the Internet community.

First, though, you have to find a good Internet service provider, hook up your phone to the computer (using a device called a modem), and feed a few simple commands to your Linux system (and the networking gods) to make the connection.

# The Search for an ISP

We categorize Internet service providers (ISPs) by those who specialize in Windows and Windows NT products and those who are good. By good, we mean ISPs who can also handle UNIX and Linux accounts.
