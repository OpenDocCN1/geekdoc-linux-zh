# Introduction

# Part I: Introducing Linux

In Part I, you find out about the birth of Linux and about the different types of Linux out there, including the one on the SuSE Linux For Dummies CD-ROM. You also get some tips on installing which one is right for you.

# Part II: Installing SuSE Linux

In Part II, you get your answer to the age-old question: How the heck do I install SuSE Linux? We give you detailed, step-by-step instructions. You also get lots of help installing X Window System, the graphical part of Linux. You may want a double espresso or a coffee Jolt soda (Twice the sugar! Twice the caffeine!) by your side for this.

# Part III: Using SuSE Linux

In Part III, you get the glorious particulars on actually doing something with Linux. We take you through the history of text editors and how to use them. You find out that not all shells are found on the ocean floor; Linux has one just for you. You can also take X Window System through its paces by moving, resizing, hiding, and closing windows.

# Part IV: Maintaining Your System

Linux isn't a high-maintenance operating system, but it does need some care (Chocolate helps too.) In Part IV, you find out how to manage the Linux file system, which is the biggest part of the care and feeding of Linux. You also get hints on boosting the performance of Linux. Vroom. Vroom.

# Part V: Going Online

Everyone is online these days, and Linux is no exception. In Part V, you find out how to contact an Internet service provider to get an account for accessing the Internet, how to set up your Linux system to interact with that account, and how to use the Netscape browser with that account to surf the Web.
