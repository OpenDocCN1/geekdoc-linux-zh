You use the scroll bar (a vertically spaced rectangle to the left of the window) to view all the data in the window. You can scroll down the entire list of commands, by placing your mouse pointer over the shaded portion of the scroll bar, pressing down the middle button of a three-button mouse or both buttons of a two-button mouse, and moving your mouse forward and back to make the scroll bar up and down.

In a few seconds, you see the manual page for the vi command, with scroll bars so that you can scroll back and forth through the text.
