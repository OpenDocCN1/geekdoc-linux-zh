# Network adapters

Make sure you have a good Ethernet if you want one. Most of the earlier cards were flaky and would make the system stop working from time to time. Most of the new cards are both 100 Mbit/second as well as the other 10 Mbit/second, they will work now in your current environment as well as in your lower environment

Supported cards are 3Com 3503, 3505, 3507, 3509/3509B (ISA), 3579 (EISA), 3Com Etherlink III Vortex Ethercards (3590, 3592, 3595, 3597) (3PCI), 3Com Etherlink XL Boomerang Ethercards (3c905) (PCI), 3Com Fast Etherlink Ethercards (3c15) (ISA), AMD LANCE (79960)/PCNet-ISA/PCI (AT1500, J2405A-NE1500/NE200), AT&T GIS Wave LAN, Allied Etheris AT170, Allied Etheris LA100PCI-T, Ansel Communications 43200 EISA, Apricot Xen-II/82596, Cabletron E212x, DEC DE425 (EISA), DEC DE434, DEC DE435 (PCI), DE450, DE455 (de4x5 driver), DEC DE450/DE4500-XEA (Tulip driver), DEC DEPECA and EtherWORKS, DEC EtherWorks 3, DEC QSaver’s (Tulip driver), Fujitsu FMI-198, 182, 183, HP MCLAN (27245 and 27252A), HP 10/100 PCI (J2577, J2573, 27248, J2585) (ISA/EISA/PCI), IC EtherTeam 16/62 ISA, Intel Etheris, IntelExpress Pro, KTI ET16/P-D2, ET16/P-DC ISA (works jumperless and with hardware-configuration options), NE2000/NE1000 (to be careful with clocks), Netgear FA-3107X (Tulip chip), New Media Ethernet, PureData PDC8028, PDI8023, SEEK 8005, SMC Ultra/EtherEZ (ISA), SMC 9000 series, SMC EtherPower 10/100 (Tulip driver), SMC EtherPower II (epic100.c driver), Schneider & Kohl G16, Western Digital WD80x3, Zenith Z-Note, IBM’s ThinkPad 300 built-in-adapter, and 312 Eterray (Tulip driver).
