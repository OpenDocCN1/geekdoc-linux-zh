You can use the up- and down-arrow keys on your keyboard to select a drop-down list from which you can select the necessary settings. You select a given option on the list by pressing Enter. After selecting a device and protocol, you need to Tab down to the Apply button and press Enter for your changes to take effect.

If you have a two-button mouse and want to emulate three buttons, click the Options tab and select Emulate Three Buttons. Do not forget to click the Apply button! If you have a three-button mouse, you can select the three-button mouse option in the main mouse configuration screen. Keep reading for details.

If you want to test your mouse settings, click the Test tab and place the mouse pointer on the diagram of the mouse. When you click the mouse buttons, the corresponding buttons on the diagram will turn black. Remember: if you are emulating three buttons, you need to click both the left and right buttons simultaneously to emulate the middle button. When you are satisfied with your mouse configuration, click the OK button. You will be brought back to the main mouse configuration screen. Now that you are finished with configuring the mouse, click the Next button in the bottom right hand of your screen.
