# In this part ...

Here you find out about the basics: files and commands, editors and shells, and windows. In Chapter 8, you take a journey through the SuSE Linux system, subdirectories, and the root directory along the way. Then you use commands to navigate through the Linux file system, changing, moving, creating, and deleting files as you go.

In Chapter 9, your journey takes you through the maze of text editing: searching, moving, adding, and deleting text. As the ever-conscientious tour guides, we present a few text editors to ease your tasks.

Any far-flung traveler has used an interpreter. In Linux, you use a command interpreter, or shell. Chapter 10 gives you a short history lesson in shells. And, to give you one decision to make, we offer a suggestion for which to use.

X Window System is the graphical portion of Linux. In Chapter 11, we show you how to use X (as it's known to its friends) to move, resize, hide, and close windows. Lots of programs run under X Window System, and in Chapter 12, we show you a few.
