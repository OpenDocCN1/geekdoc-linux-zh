# Sending E-Mail

To send an e-mail message, you have to know the recipient's e-mail address.
Just as a URL contains certain components, an e-mail address is made up of the person's e-mail name (often the same as his or her login name) and domain name. For example, to send an e-mail to the President of the United States, you address it to president@whitehouse.gov.

Your ISP should have given you an e-mail address, and you can ask your friends for their e-mail addresses. It's the height of irony when people first get on the Internet and can send e-mail, they invariably spend a lot of time calling their friends on the telephone to get their e-mail addresses.

With the Mail & Discussions window on the screen, click the New Msg icon. To the window like the one in Figure 16-14 appears.

Below the row of icons near the top of the window is the word To. To the right is a white space where — after you click in it — you can type the e-mail address of the person you are trying to reach.
