# # Resizing to your heart's content

Sometimes a window is a little too big or a little too small. Life would be much easier if you could just nudge that window into shape. To do just that, follow these steps:

+   1. Choose a window and give it focus.

+   2. Click the hyphen symbol.

The Window function menu appears.

+   3. Click Resize. Your cursor jumps to the bottom-right corner of the window and changes shape again.

+   4. Move the cursor up and to the left, just a little bit.

As you do this, you will notice that the window is changing shape. As you can see when you resize the window, it is divided into nine parts: four corners, four side sections, and one center section. The center section doesn't do anything in resizing. The four side sections allow resizing in a perpendicular direction from their side (that is, straight in or straight out). The four corners allow resizing in two directions at once; by grabbing a corner, you can make the window both wider and taller at the same time.

+   5. When the window is the right size and shape, click once with the left mouse button.
