Any frustrated youngsters, photographers, and choreographers out there love to be able to make their own CD-ROMs filled with music and pictures. Well, you can with Linux! Linux supports CD-writers, and a variety of software is available to instruct you on writing to-once (WO) CDs. Programs such as cdwrite and cdrecord can be used for writing to WO CDs and a graphical front-end called X-Road make the job even easier.
