# Getting E-Mail

Now that you can surf the Net, you probably probably want to tell people about your adventures. Well, Netscape can send and receive e-mail, too.

Go to the main window of Netscape and you see one of two menu items, Communicator or Window. Click either of these and then click the Messenger Mailbox option. You are taken to the Mail side of Netscape, as shown in Figure 16-12.

To get your mail, simply click the Get Msg icon. This tells Netscape to make contact with your mail server and see whether any e-mail messages are waiting for you. If you have no e-mail, text at the bottom of the screen tells you so. If you have e-mail, the subject and sender appear in the center of the screen and the e-mail message itself appears in the bottom of the screen, as shown in Figure 16-13.
