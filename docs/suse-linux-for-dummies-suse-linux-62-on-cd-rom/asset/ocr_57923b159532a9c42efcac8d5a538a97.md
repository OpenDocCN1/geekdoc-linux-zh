16-13: Receiving an e-mail message.

If you have multiple e-mail messages, you can see each one by clicking the Subject line in the middle window. You may reply to, forward, or delete the particular message by highlighting its Subject line and clicking the appropriate icon (Reply, Forward, or Delete). When you click the Reply icon, the address of the person you're replying to appears automatically in the To field of the reply. When you forward a message, a copy of the message is sent as an attachment to the address you specify.
