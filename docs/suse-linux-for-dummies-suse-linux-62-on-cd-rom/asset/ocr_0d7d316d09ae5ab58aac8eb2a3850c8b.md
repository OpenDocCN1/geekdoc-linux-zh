# ABOUT IDG BOOKS WORLDWIDE

Welcome to the world of IDG Books Worldwide.
IDG Books Worldwide, Inc., is a subsidiary of International Data Group, the world's largest publisher of computer-related information and the leading provider of information services on information technology. IDG was founded more than 30 years ago by Patrick J. McGowan and employs nearly 9,000 people worldwide. IDG has published more than 290 publications in 75 countries. More than 90 million people read one or more IDG publications each month.

Launched in 1990, IDG Books Worldwide is today to receive eight awards from the Computer Press Association in recognition of editorial excellence and three from *Computer''s First Annual Readers' Choice Awards*. Our best-selling *For Dummies!* series has been published in 33 languages. IDG offers online help to publish a book in the People's Republic of China. In record time, IDG Books Worldwide has become the first U.S. publisher to be the first choice for millions of readers around the world who want to learn how to manage their businesses.

Quality is simple. Every one of our books is designed to bring value and skill-building instructions to the reader. Our books are written by experts who understand and care about our readers. The knowledge base of our editorial staff comes from years of experience in publishing, education, and journalism — we use to produce books that carry us into the new millennium. In short, we care about books, we devote special attention to details such as design, interior design of use, and illustrations. And we use an efficient process of authoring, editing, and publishing books so we can spend more time ensuring quality and less time on the technicalities of making books

You can count on us to deliver high-quality books on topics you want to read about. At IDG Books Worldwide, we continue the IDG tradition of delivering quality for more than 30 years. You'll find no better book on a subject than one from IDG Books Worldwide.

IDG is the world's leading IT, market research and exposition company founded in 1964. In 1997, IDG reported revenues of $8.20 billion and has more than 90,000 employees worldwide. IDG offers the widest range of media options that reach buyers in 75 countries including print material, publishing, exhibitions and conferences, market research, education, and global marketing services. More than 90 million people read one or more of IDG's 290 magazines and newspapers, including IDG's global brands — *Computerworld*, *PC World*, and *InfoWorld*. IDG Books Worldwide is one of the fastest-growing computer book publishers in the world, with more than 700 titles in 36 languages. The *For Dummies!* series has more than 50 million copies in print. IDG operates a network of over 1,000 websites which comprises more than 223 Targeted Web Sites in 55 countries. International Data Group (IDG) is the world's largest technology research company, operating in over 40 countries with more than 400 research analysis worldwide. IDG World Expo is a producer of over 168 globally held conferences and exhibitions in 35 countries. IDG's subsidiaries include *Global Technology,*, ICE (International Computer Expo), *Agora*, DEMO, and Spotlight. IDG's subsidiary, ExecTrain, is the world's largest computer training company. IDG Marketing Services helps industry-leading companies build brand recognition by globally integrated marketing programs via IDG's print, online and exposition products worldwide. Further information about the company can be found at www.idg.com.**
