Most controls are labeled on Figure 12-6. Here's a little additional information:

+   - The Volume control boosts the bass slightly for you want low output (when your parents are home, the baby is asleep, or you have new batteries in your hearing aid).
- The Stereo Separation control is for cards that try to convince you that they have stereo separation capabilities.
- Each slider is made up of two columns, for the left channel and for the right channel. Place the cursor directly over one of the columns and drag to move the column up or down. Move the cursor between the two columns to move both at the same time.

This chapter highlights a few of the applications on the Suisse Linux For Dummies CD-ROM. Two other applications, for example, are gimp and xpaint. Note that applications may be graphical applications that use X Window System or character-cell applications that use the command line.

Please note that the current sound architecture in SuSE does not always work. Your card, or the configuration options described in this chapter, may cause a conflict in your system. If you are having trouble, consider joining the suse-linux mailing list and posting your question there. Often, Suisse users can provide you with an answer: To join the mailing list, follow the instructions on our Web at www.suse.com.
