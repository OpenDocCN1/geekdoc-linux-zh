+   2. Click K:Games:Poker.
Luckily, the program starts you off with a generous $100. To start the game, just click the Draw! button (see Figure 12-2). You will be dealt five cards. If you want to keep any of them, just click them and press the Draw! button again. Your earnings will be tallied as you go. If your luck is anything like ours, the game will be over soon. If you want to play it again, by clicking the File:New Game menu option. To end the game, click the File:Quit menu option.

+   3. Click K:Games:Patience.
If poker is not your style, perhaps another card game will tick your fancy. Note that this game is several games in one. You can select from a list of cards from the submenu under the Game:Choose New Game submenu. Our favorite is FreeCell, which is shown in Figure 12-3.
