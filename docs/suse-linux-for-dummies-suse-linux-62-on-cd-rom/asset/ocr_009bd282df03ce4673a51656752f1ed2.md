If you have followed along successfully, your sound card should now be functional.

If you find that you are having problems hearing anything, make sure that you have compatible (and working!) speakers plugged into your sound card output. If you do, then one of the following might be the reason you didn't hear the sound:

+   - You chose the wrong sound card.
- You entered the wrong parameters for the right sound card.
- Someone else's stereo is way too loud.

You can go back and revise the config file /etc/conf.modules and try other settings for your sound card.
