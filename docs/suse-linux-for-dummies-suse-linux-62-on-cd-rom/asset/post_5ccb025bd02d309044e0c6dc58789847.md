## UPS and Miscellaneous Devices

Many people have a little surge protector on the power to their computer system. Although it's better than nothing, surge protector offers only so much protection from surges and no protection from blacks. As you will come to know, Linux systems just hate their power turned off before a nice, orderly shutdown of services. And when the system comes back up, there is this long wait until it checks to see whether the file system is corrupted. An uninterruptible power supply (UPS) uses batteries to provide power to the computer system, allowing the computer system to shut down in an orderly if the power starts to fail. Some of the units are:
- APC SmartUPS
- APC-BackUps 400/600
- APC-SmartUPS 700/1400RM
- UPS with RS-232 monitoring port (generic package)
- MGE UPS
