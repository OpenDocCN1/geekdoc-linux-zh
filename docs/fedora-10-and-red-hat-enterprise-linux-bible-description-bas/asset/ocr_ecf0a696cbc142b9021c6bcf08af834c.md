# Ten cool things to do with Fedora 10 Linux

Just because Fedora is a serious operating system doesn't mean it can't be fun too. Here is a list of fun and useful things to do with Fedora.

+   1. Set Up a Personal Online Desktop: Use the GNOME Online Desktop to connect your friends, multimedia content, and online applications from Google, Facebook, Yahoo!, and others. Click right on the desktop to immediately get all your favorite content. (See Chapter 3)

+   2. Launch the new KDE 4 Plasma Desktop: The next generation KDE desktop lets you add widgets, called plasmons, to multiple places on your desktop. Find files, Web pages or other items quickly with new search tools. (See Chapter 3)

+   3. Play Commercial Audio/Video: Licensing restrictions keep many popular codecs from inclusion with Fedora. Using third-party repositories for Fedora, you get the codecs you need to play Windows Media (Audio, Video and MMS), MP3 audio, MPEG-2 and MPEG-4 video decoding, and others. (See Chapter 8)

+   4. Manage music collections: Linux Rhythmbox to gather, organize, and play music from your hard disk, CDs, or network file systems. You can select from thousands of free songs from Magnitude and Jamendo online services. (See Chapter 8)

+   5. Publish your ideas::Choose from dozens of publishing tools to create documents (OpenOffice.org Writer), presentations (OpenOffice.org Impress) hard-copy page layouts (Scribus), and vector graphics (Inkscape). Publish your work on paper or the Web. (See Chapter 6)

+   6. Share an Internet connection: Fedora can be set up as a router and a firewall. With a home or small office LAN set-up, you can use Fedora to share an Internet connection among multiple Linux, Windows, or Mac systems. Set up a firewall in Fedora to protect your LAN from intruders. (See Chapters 14, 15, and 16)

+   7. Play free games: As always, Fedora includes dozens of simple card games and board games. The Fedora repository includes a ton of games, including first-person shooter such as Doom, strategy games as fantasy Wesnoth, and online battle games such as BFlag. (See Chapter 7)

+   8. Run Applications: By adding the wine software packages, you can run many Windows applications from a Fedora desktop. (See Chapter 5)

+   9. Create an Internet server: Learn to configure a Web server (Chapter 21), FTP server (Chapter 20) and a mail server (Chapter 19). Then gather knowledge that to create your own Internet server (Chapter 25). Your server can run from your home Internet connection.

+   10. Share over the Internet: Share your personal videos, software, or content with friends over the Internet, using Bittorent swarm transfer software. While you're at it, instant message your thoughts using Pidgin (Ekika), and e-mail your party invitations (Evolution). (See Chapter 7.)
