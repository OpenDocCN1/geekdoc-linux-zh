# Part II: Using Fedora RHEL

+   - Change the current unit to a settler as follows: click the stack of units on the map and click one of the Settlers from the menu that appears.

+   - Begin building a city by clicking on Orders -> Build City. When prompted, type a name for the city and click OK. The window that appears shows information about the city. It starts with one happy city, represented by a single icon (more citizens will appear as the game progresses).

+   - The Food, Prod, and Trade lines reflect the productivity statistics for the city. The first number shows how much is being produced, the second (in parents) shows the surplus above needs to support the units. The Gold, Luxury, and Science lines indicate the city's trade output. Granny numbers show how much food is stored and the size of the food store. The pollution level begins at zero.

+   - The Units are not supported by a city (so nothing appears Supported Units). When Units require support, they will be assigned to cities, and they will draw on city resources. Units present appear under that heading.

+   - The map area consists of 21 squares to make up the city. The number indicates the size of the city. The number 211 reflects the production of food, manufacturing production, and trade, respectively. The number 210 shows where the city's citizen is working and the results of the work.

+   - The Phalanx line shows that the city can build a Phalanx and that it will take 20 production points to produce. Click Change to view other units the city could produce

+   - Close the city window by clicking Close.

# Exploring your world

To begin exploring, move the Settler.

+   1. Using the numeric keypad, press the 9 key three times to begin exploring. You can move the explorer to three times per turn. You begin to see more of the world.

+   2. When the next unit (the Settler) begins blinking, move one square in another direction. Click Turn Done. Information for the city will be updated.

+   3. Click the City to see the city. Notice that information about the city has been updated. In particular, you should see food storage increase. Close the city window.

+   4. Continue exploring and build a road. With the explorer flashing, press the numeric keypad to move it another three sections. When the Settler begins blinking, press r to build a road. A small R appears on the square to remind you that the Settler is building a road. Click Turn Done.
