NOTE: Although in my example I am creating an address book that is at the top of my directory structure, if you are in a large company chances that you will want a more complex directory structure. For example, instead of having one address book at top of your directory structure, you may create additional organizational units for countries, locations, or departments. Then, every single unit might have its own address book. You may also want to support multiple directories under each unit. For example, there may be a separate directory for keeping track of computer equipment or company vehicles.
