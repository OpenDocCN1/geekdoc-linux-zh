# Using more controls and actions

Now that you have some understanding of the controls and actions, the game can begin taking a lot of different directions. Here are a few things that might happen next and things you can do:

+   - After you take a tour, the computer gets a chance to play as well. As it plays, actions are reported to you. You can make decisions on what to do about those actions. Choose Game -> Message Options. The Message options window appears, containing a listing of different kinds of messages that can come from the server and how they will be presented to youn- As you explore, you will run into other explorers and eventually other civilizations. Continue exploring by selecting directions on your numeric keypad.
- Continue to move the Settler one at a time, after it has finished the road. (The Settler will blink again when it is available) Click Turn.
- At this point, you will see a message that your city has finished building Warriors. When buildings and units are complete, you usually check out what has happened. Click the message associated with the city, and then popup City. The city window appears, showing that you has additional population. The food storage may appear, but the new cities are working to increase the food and trade. You may see an additional warrior unit.
- A science advisory may also appear at this point to let you choose your city's research goals. Click Change and select Writing as your new research goal. You can then select a different long-term goal as well. Click when you are done.
- If your Warrior is now blinking, press the s key to assign sentry mode to the Warrior.

You should be familiar with some of the actions of Freecv at this point. To learn some basic strategies for the game, help Help -> Playing.

# Extreme Tuxracer

With Extreme Tuxracer (yum install extremuxracer), you guide the penguin (Linux mascot) down a snow-covered hill as fast as you can. Extreme Tuxracer (etracer command or Games -> Extreme Tuxracer) is an open source (GPL) version of TuxRacer, which was once free, but later made into a commercial game by Sunfire Studios.

To advance in Extreme Tuxracer, you need to complete courses in the allotted time while overcoming whatever obstacle is presented (gathering herring or negotiating flags). You move up to different courses and achieve higher-level cups. Figure 7-4 shows a screenshot of Extreme Tuxracer.
