12. Select that project name and click on the project administrative interface link.

13. Scroll to the bottom of the page and click on the List Administrator Password. Now you can configure the look, feel, and behavior of your mailing list. Important information you will want to consider includes:

+   - **Privacy options** — Do you want the list to be public or private (as in, just within your organization?) Who do you want to allow to see your member list?
- **Send filters** — Who can post messages to the list? Is the list moderated or unmoderated? Might the list just go in one direction, for example, as a way to contact customers?
- **Membership management** — Can you members see other member information? What are your policies for adding and removing members?
