Individual pages have entries in the chun k’s swap map. This map also contains a linked free list for its pages. The swap maps are pointed to by entries in the system swap table, **swaptab**. The swap table is filled in the order the swap devices were enabled but is searched according to the order implied by the priority table pointers. If two devices share a priority, their chun ks are searched in round-robin fashion.
