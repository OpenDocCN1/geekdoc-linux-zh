**GOCR**  
Command line OCR tool that outputs plain text from scanned images.  
DEB: gocr  
RPM: gocr  
WWW: http://jocr.sourceforge.net/

**gPhoto**  
Imports images from digital cameras.  
DEB: gphoto  
RPM: gphoto  
WWW: http://www.gphoto.org/

**Grunch**  
Merges partial scans together to form larger images.  
DEB: grunch  
WWW: http://www.radagast.org/~dplatt/

**Gsumi**  
Imports black-and-white images from pressure-sensitive input devices, such as drawing tablets.  
DEB: gsumi

**Pencam**  
Imports images from digital cameras that use the STV0680B-001 chip.  
DEB: pencam  
WWW: http://stv0680-usb.sourceforge.net/

**Streamer**  
Captures images from video4linux devices.  
DEB: streamer  
RPM: xawtv-streamer  
WWW: http://bytesex.org/xawtv/
