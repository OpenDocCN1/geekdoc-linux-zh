**Elm**

Menu-driven MUA that was popular in the early 1990s among experienced users—it has some interesting features, including ways to send mail in batch mode to many addresses at once, and comes with a tool to send memorandums as e-mail messages. Interest in Elm has waned considerably over the years, and most novices are advised to try Mutt instead.
