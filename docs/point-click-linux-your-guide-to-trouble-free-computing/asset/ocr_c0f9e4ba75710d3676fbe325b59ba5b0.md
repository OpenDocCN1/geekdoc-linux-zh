# Mozilla: Your Key to the Internet

Even though KDE has a built-in Web browser and email client that many Linux users love, SimplyMEPIS creator Warren Woodford and this book's author decided that Mozilla is both better-known and easier to use for people trying Linux for the first time. Therefore, it's the Internet tool we'll talk about in the Point & Click Linux! book and video. You'll probably want to experiment with KDE's KMail and Konqueror browser at some point. You may also want to try the new, Mozilla- designed Firefox Web browser and Thunderbird email client or, for email, calendar and general “organizing” functions, the highly regarded Evolution system.
