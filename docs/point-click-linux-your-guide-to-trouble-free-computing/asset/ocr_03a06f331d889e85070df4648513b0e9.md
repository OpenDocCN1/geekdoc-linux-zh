Mozilla settings work exactly the same as Netscape settings, and they're the same in Linux as they are in Windows or Mac OS, so any competent ISP or corporate help desk person will know what to do.

# Setting Up Mozilla Virus and Spam Filters

# One Person's Junk Is Another's Treasure

This is the secret of the Bayesian filter: It detects patterns in accord with your classification of emails as "junk" or "not junk."

If you keep tagging email with a particular string of words in it as junk, before long any email containing that string of words will go straight to the junk folder. If you go through the junk folder now and then—perhaps every few days at first—and reclassify some of the emails that got shunted there as "Not Junk," the filter won't classify future emails the considered similar as junk any longer. In other words, you train the filter according to your tastes instead of accepting someone else's definition of spam.
