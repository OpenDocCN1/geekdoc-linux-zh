# Point & Click Linux!

This is where we need some of the information we got from our ISP or network administrator. When given the "pop" or "imap" choice, if you don't know which one you have it's almost certainly "pop." The server name depends on how your provider has things set up. Just type in whatever they told you and you'll be fine. The same goes for the "Outgoing Server" space: Type in whatever your ISP or network admin told you should be there, and that's that. Next screen, please.

The name you type here is the one your ISP gave you, not your real name (unless they're the same). It could be something blah like "Miller123" or something colorful like "glowingsunflower" that you chose yourself. Either way, it goes here. Next:
