# **21: Digikam—Importing and Organizing Digital Photos**

We have one gallery set up, called “More Pictures,” so that’s where these photos will go. If we had more than one gallery, we’d make a choice in the “Download To Album:” line at the bottom of the window or use the “Add Gallery” button to create a new gallery.

![](bbox=[0.34, 0.09, 0.91, 0.14])

Now, if we return to the main Digikam program window and click on our album’s name, we have the downloaded pictures in front of us.

![](bbox=[0.34, 0.24, 0.91, 0.29])

We can move pictures from one album to another or reorder them within their original gallery with the usual KDE “drag and drop” actions. We can click on pictures and see them full-sized, use icons to “zoom in” and “zoom out” and there’s an icon to the right of the two magnifying glass “zoom” icons we can click to expand the Digikam window to fill our entire screen—and click again while in full-screen mode to shrink the Digikam window back to normal size.
