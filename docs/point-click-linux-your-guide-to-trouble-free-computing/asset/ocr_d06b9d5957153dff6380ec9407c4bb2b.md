# 17: Kopete: IM, IRC, and General Chat

After you have AIM—or another service—set up, when you start Kopete you won't get a Setup Wizard automatically; to enter information for another account you click "Settings" at the top of the little Kopete window that pops up when you start the program, then select "Configure Kopete" at the bottom of the little menu the click on "Settings" brought to your screen.

![](bbox=[0.48, 0.08, 0.95, 0.42])

From this screen, pick "Accounts" and you'll see the AIM account you already set up. Click "New" on the right side of this little screen to bring up the Account Wizard, then "Next" to show a list of services to which Kopete can connect you—exactly the same as when you set up the AIM account, and all of them except Internet Relay Chat (IRC) are set up the same way.
