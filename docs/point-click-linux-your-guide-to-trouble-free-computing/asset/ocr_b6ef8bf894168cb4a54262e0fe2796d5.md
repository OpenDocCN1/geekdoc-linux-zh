# “Which IRC Channels Should I Join?”

The only sane answer to this question is another question: “What are your interests?” There are tens of thousands of IRC channels. Some are centered on computers and software; almost every program included on your *SimplyMepis* CD has at least one dedicated IRC channel where you can go for help, and you can find IRC channels for almost any non-computer interest, from dating to sailing to your favorite video game or TV show.

An obvious first channel for you to join is **#mepis** on **irc.freenode.net**. It’s the MEPIS help channel. Another one you may want to check for general Linux help, also on **irc.freenode.net**, is **#linux**.

To find more channels, a great place to start is the web site **SearchIRC.com**. It doesn’t list every IRC chat room in the world—IRC channels come and go so fast that no one can keep up with the constant changes—but it lists thousands of the more popular ones and even has a little IRC utility built into the Web site you can use to check out a channel before you decide to add it to your Kopete list.
