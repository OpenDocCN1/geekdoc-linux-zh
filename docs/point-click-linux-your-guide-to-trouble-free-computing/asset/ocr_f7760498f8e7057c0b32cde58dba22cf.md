# Configuring Kopete's "Look and Feel"

This is not hard, and it's strictly optional. Select the "Settings" option from the top of your Kopete screen, then "Configure Kopete," and from the screen that gives you, either select "Appearance" or Behavior." Either one will give you sev- eral tabs to choose, like "General" or "Chat Window." These are areas where experimenting is safe, and even encouraged. At the worst, if you make something look strange, you can go back to the screen where you made the change and hit the "Defaults" button at the bottom, and everything will go back to the way it was before you started experimenting.
