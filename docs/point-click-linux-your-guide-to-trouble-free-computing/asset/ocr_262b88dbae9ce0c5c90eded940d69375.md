# Chess

The *SimplyMEPIS* CD contains only a basic chess-board. You'll need to supply your own two players. There's an excellent free GNUChess program available that will allow you to play against your computer, but it was too big to fit on a single CD along with all the other software we included. After you get a little practice with Linux it will be no trouble to download and install on your own.
