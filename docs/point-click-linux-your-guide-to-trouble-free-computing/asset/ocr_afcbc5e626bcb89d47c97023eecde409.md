Another advantage you have with Linux is that you can easily install new programs without worrying about spyware, adware or viruses. You can get over 1,000 MEPIS-certified programs directly from the MEPIS servers in return for a small annual subscription fee (or have them shipped to you on CD for a small additional charge). You'll also get free support for all of them through the MEPIS online forums, even if you are not a paid subscriber. If you need specialized software that isn't on the MEPIS servers, or you choose not to subscribe, you can download any of over 10,000 free programs from the worldwide network of volunteer-maintained Debian Project servers. These programs aren't guaranteed to work with MEPIS, but 90% of them will work just fine. It's just as simple to uninstall a program in Linux as it is to install it, so if you try one and it doesn't work out, it's no big deal.
