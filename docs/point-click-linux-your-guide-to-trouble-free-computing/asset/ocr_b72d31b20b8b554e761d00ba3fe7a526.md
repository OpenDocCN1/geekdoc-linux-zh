# Point & Click Linux!

**KNetLoad** is another icon that gives you text statistics when you click on it.

**XKill** is a last-ditch thing. Click on **XKill**, then click on a program window that's frozen up or otherwise not behaving right, and **POOF!** that program shuts down and disappears. The only program that may give you trouble after being killed this way (which, thankfully, it almost never needs) is Mozilla. If you **XKill** Mozilla, you may need to reboot your PC to get Mozilla working correctly again.
