# Card Games

# Patience (Solitaire)

This is your basic solitaire game. Reach it by clicking on your “K-Gear” icon, choosing “*Games*”, then “*Card Games*”, then “*Patience.*”

You can now play as long as you like. Or you can spend a few moments customizing the cards’ appearance by clicking “*Settings,*” then “*Switch Cards.*” From “*Settings*“ you’ll also see a “*Game Type*” choice that gives you 18 different one-player patience-type card games. If you run into one that’s unfamiliar to you, but still want to give it a try, the “*Game*” top menu choice has a “*Demo*” feature you can click to see how the game is played. While you’re learning a new game, you can click on the wand icon at the top to get a hint or two.
