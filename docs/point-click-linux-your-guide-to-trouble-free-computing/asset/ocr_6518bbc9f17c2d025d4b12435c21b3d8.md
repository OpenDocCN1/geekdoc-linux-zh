We do this by putting our cursor on the line between two of the letters, in this case "A" and "B," which makes it suddenly grow two little arrow tips, one pointed left and one pointed right. While holding our left mouse button, we move that line the direction we want it to go. In this case, since we want to make the "A" column wider, we drag that line to the right, probably to about where the line between "B" and "C" were when we started.
