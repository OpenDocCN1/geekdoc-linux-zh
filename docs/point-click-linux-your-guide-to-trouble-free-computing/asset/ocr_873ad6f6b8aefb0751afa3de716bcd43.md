# Chapter 2

Running the *SimplyMEPIS* CD

MEPIS runs as a “Live CD” without installation. On most PCs, running MEPIS Linux is as simple as putting the *SimplyMEPIS* CD in your CD drive and restarting your computer. After a little bit of whirring, you'll see a list of menu choices.
