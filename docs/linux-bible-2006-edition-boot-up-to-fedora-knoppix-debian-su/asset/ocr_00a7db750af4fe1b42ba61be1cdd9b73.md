# Beginning with Freciv

Check out the Freciv window before you start the game:

+   * Use the Help button for topical information on many different subjects that will be useful to you as you play. (You can find more help at the Freciv site.)

+   * The world (by default) is 80 × 50 squares, with 11 × 8 squares visible at a time.

+   * The active square contains an icon of the active view (flashing alternately with the square's terrain).

+   * Some squares contain special resources. Press and hold the mouse button for information on what special resources a square contains. (With a two-button mouse, hold the Ctrl and click the right mouse button) Try this few times to get a feel for the land around you. This action also identifies units on the terrain, as well as statistics for the unit.

+   * To see the world outside your 11 × 8 viewing area, click the scroll bars outside the map. At first, the part of the world you haven't explored yet will be black. Units as added, closer to those units will be visible. (Press C to return to the active part of your map.)

+   * An overview map is in the upper-left corner of the Freciv window. As the world becomes more civilized, this provides a way to get an overview of what is going on. Right-click a spot on the overview map to have your viewport centered there.
