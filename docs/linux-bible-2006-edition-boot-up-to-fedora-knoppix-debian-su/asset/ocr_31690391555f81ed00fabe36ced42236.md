# Acknowledgments

I consider anyone who has contributed to the open source community to be a contributor to the book you are holding. The backbone of any Linux distribution is formed by the organizations that produce the distributions, the major projects included in Linux, and the thousands of people who give their time and code to support Linux. So, thanks to you all!

As for direct contributors to the book, a small team of writers/reviewers went through and updated the content from Linux Bible 2005 Edition while I was off writing Linux Toys II. Their contributions gave me a head start so I could jump into this book with a good set of rewrites, suggestions, and constructive criticisms already in place. Primary contributors include Wayne Tucker (who originally wrote and then updated the chapters on Debian, LAMP servers, and mail servers) and Kurt Wall (who wrote the programming chapters and helped revise several others). Other valuable contributions came from technical editors Dilip Thomas, Bill von Hagen, and John Kennedy.

Thanks to the folks at Wiley for helping me press through the project. Debra Williams Cauley shepher the project through the book's early stages. Sara Shlaer did her great job of editing the book and managing its progress. Likewise, Nancy Rapoport produced hundreds of helpful comments as copy editor. Thanks to Margaret Haley Hutchison, Kimberly Valentini, and Maureen Maloney from Waterside Productions for contracting the book for me with Wiley.
