# Chapter 14 ♦ Running Slack Linux 401

Anything you can do with other Linux distributions, you can do with Slackware. It might just take a bit more manual work to get there. Slackware doesn't officially use a full-featured package management system, as as the Red Hat Package Manager (RPM) or Debian's files. however, some package management tools are compatible with Slackware's native package format: gzipped archives (I describe these tools later). In general, however, most users become adept at building and installing their applications (tar, make, and similar tools).

Slackware comes with a good set of libraries that will take care of dependency needs of most Linux applications. For video, audio, and some other types of applications, you may find yourself hunting around for libraries. The tools for satisfying package dependencies (such as yum and apt) that save you this trouble in other distributions are beginning to be developed or adapted to Slackware.

# The Slackware Creator

Patrick Volkding started Slackware in 1993 as a Linux distribution to use for himself and his friends. He was kind enough to answer some questions I had about Slackware, and I want to share his answers with you:

Patrick originally used a distribution called SLS Linux (named Soft Landing Linux, the company that made it). Why didn't he choose to SLS instead of starting his distribution?

> Patrick: I tried. By April of 1993 I had collected a huge list of bugs in SLS, along the fixes for most of them. Plenty of people to get these to Peter McDonald (SLS's author/maintainer) but the bugs in SLS (many of which were quite obvious) never seemed to get fixed.

Of course, I worked on my patched version of SLS with no plan to launch a lasting distribution. I figured I'd get it online and SLS would fix the issues, and that might just be that. SLS was a great distribution and isn't given enough credit for all the ideas that started there. Unfortunately it was Peter was busy working on inventing kernel modules that SLS sat online for a few months full of bugs and not getting any updates.
