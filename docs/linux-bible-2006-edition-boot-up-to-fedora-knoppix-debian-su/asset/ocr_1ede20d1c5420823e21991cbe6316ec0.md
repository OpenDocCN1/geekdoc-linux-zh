Depending on your distribution, you may need to get the vanilla kernel from kernel.org and boot that on your system running games with Cedega.

To get games to run in Linux, TransGaming incorporated Microsoft DirectX 8 features into Cedega. In fact, TransGaming implemented DirectX because subscribers to Cedega requested it. (A subscription has value, in part, because it lets you vote on games you would like to see TransGaming working on.

A full list of games supported by TransGaming, as well as indications of how popular they are and how well they work, is available from the TransGaming site (www.transgaming.org/games/). Today, hundreds of games are listed in that database.

You can use TransGaming's software to run Doom 3 out of the box. For news about other product milestones, check out the "Hot off the Press" link on the TransGaming home page.

With Cedega 4, TransGaming has included Point2Play 2.02. Point2Play provides a graphical window for installing, configuring, and testing Cedega on your computer.
