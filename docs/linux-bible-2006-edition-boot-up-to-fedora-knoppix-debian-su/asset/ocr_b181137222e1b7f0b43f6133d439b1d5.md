# Damn Small Linux

If you want your desktop distribution to fit in your wallet, Damn Small Linux is one of your best choices. Damn Small Linux is one of the first distributions based on KNOPPIX to fix on a bootable business (about 48MB currently).

Damn Small Linux is included on the CD that comes with this book. You can copy it and burn to CD as described in Appendix A.

With KNOPPIX inside, you have many of the features you get with KNOPPIX: hardware detection and booting to a desktop with network connectivity (provided you have an Ethernet connection with DHCP). Many features to Damn Small, however, are to let you get a workable desktop in a small medium (mini-CD) and low RAM. Figure 19-3 shows an example of the DSL desktop.
