# Linspike Hardware Requirements

While Linspike is top notch when it comes to ease of use, it won't be very easy if your hardware doesn't work with the software. Linspike systems follow the product's 'keep it simple' theme, and there is no discussion of RAID controllers or other such arcane features. Any version of Linux, the minimum requirements are pretty sparse, but you need to make sure your PC meets them:

+   - 800 MHz or faster processor — Any processor that crosses this performance threshold will work.

+   - 128MB of RAM — This will get you, but Linspike recommends splurge and install 256MB or more of RAM for optimum performance.

+   - Hard disk —Linspike requires a hard disk with 4GB of free space.

+   - Video — You need a color monitor capable of supporting a screen resolution of 1024×768. Some of the games and screen savers included with various packages or available from the CNR Warehouse require some kind of 3D graphics accelerator hardware

+   - Sound — You need a Linspike-compatible sound with speakers and/or headphones. Check your surround at www.linspike.com/compatibility/ before your installation.

+   - Modem — Your 56K, cable or DSL modem also needs to be Linspike-compatible. Before beginning your installation, check your equipment at www.linspike.com/compatibility/.

+   - Network card — Any Ethernet card is acceptable. While Linspike does not specifically recommend it, you would be wise to check the hardware list to make sure your Ethernet adapter is supported.

Most flavors of Linspike, the range of hardware supported is quite wide. If you find that your hardware is not on the supported devices list, check the hardware vendor's Web site for Debian drivers for your device. It is entirely possible that you can install the driver manually (if Linspike cannot configure it).

To make sure that your hardware will work or to confirm that it will not, select the diagnostics option when booting from the Linspike CD. Running diagnostics shows you any errors relating to the configuration or detection of your hardware.
