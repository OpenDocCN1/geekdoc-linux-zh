Here are some things to avoid when choosing a password:

+   * Do not use any variation of your login name or full name. Even if you use varied case, append or prepend numbers or punctuation, or type it backwards, this will be an easily guessed password.
* Do not use a dictionary word, even if you add numbers or punctuation to it.
* Do not use proper names of any kind.
* Do not use any contiguous line of letters or numbers on the keyboard (such as "qwerty" or "asdfg").

# Good Passwords

A good way to choose a strong password is to take the first letter from each word of an easily remembered sentence. The password can be even better by adding numbers, punctuation, and varied case. The sentence you choose have meaning only to you, and should not be publicly available (choosing a sentence on your personal Web page is a bad idea). Table 6-1 lists examples of strong passwords and the tricks used to remember them.

| Table 6-1
| Ideas for Remember It |
| Password | How to Remember It |
| Mr7cy! | My rusty car is 7 years old! |
| 2emBp1ib | 2 elephants BAD pets, 1 is better |
| ItMc7Gib | Is that MY coat? Give it back |

The passwords look like gibberish but are actually easy to remember. As you can see, I can place emphasis on words that stand for capital letters in the password. You type your password using the `passwd` command. The `passwd` command within a command shell, and it will enable you to change your password. First, it prompts you to enter your old password. To protect against someone "shoulder surfing" and learning your password, the password will not be displayed as you type.
