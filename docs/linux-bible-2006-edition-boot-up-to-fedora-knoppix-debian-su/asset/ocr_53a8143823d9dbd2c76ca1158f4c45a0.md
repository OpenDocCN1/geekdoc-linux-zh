# Understanding Gentoo

Performance and efficiency were the critical goals that led to the creation of the Gentoo Linux distribution. A dedication to the spirit of open source software (and to those drawn to it) has been vital to Gentoo's rapid and incredible growth.

In the years it has been in existence, Gentoo has grown from having one maintain—its creator, Daniel Robbins—to having more than 250 active developers. It boasts perhaps the strongest community among all Linux distributions. Gentoo users seem willing to contribute so freely because they feel that they get back what they give to Gentoo.
