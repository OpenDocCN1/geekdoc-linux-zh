Because most Linux distributions focus on the Intel/AMD (x86) platform, it's sometimes startling to realize that there's a major Linux distribution, with a passionate community of its, owning a strong presence in the world of PowerPCs. To go after PowerPC enthusiasts, Terra Soft Solutions tailors its Yellow Dog Linux to include the multimedia and ease-of-use features that Apple users expect.

This chapter aims at introducing you to Yellow Dog Linux and how it is moving to cater to those who love Apple hardware but are drawn to Linux.

Yellow Dog Linux is not included on the CD or DVD that comes with this book. You can purchase Yellow Dog Linux from Terra Soft Solutions (www.terrasoftsolutions.com/store) or download the four-CD installation set from a Yellow Dog Linux mirror (for a list of mirrors, see http://yellowdoglinux.com/resources/ftp_mirrors.html). See Appendix A for information on burning CDs.
