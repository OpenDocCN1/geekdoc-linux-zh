# Multimedia Bootables

Some Linuxes are tailored specifically to let you play movies, music, and images. Most let you play whatever you have on your hard disk or can point to the Internet. Many run in a small enough amount of memory to let you remove the DVD or CD containing Linux and insert your own content (such as a music CD or movie DVD) to play.

# MoviX²

With MoviX (http://movix.sourceforge.net), you run a multimedia player that disregards the operating systems (Windows, Linux, or otherwise) installed on your system. Because MoviX is small enough to run in your system memory, after it has booted you can insert the CD or DVD containing the content you want to run. With MoviX, you can play:

+   - **Videos**—You can play video from many different formats, DivXVID, MPEG 1 and 2, and MPEG 4. So that MoviX can be freely distributed, it does not include the capability to play most movies.

The libvdvcss library, to decrypt DVD movies (even if for playback), has been the subject of legal issues. Although this library is available on the Internet, no legitimate Linux systems in the U.S. distribute this library, and it may be illegal. You should research this issue if you plan to add libvdvcss MoviX or any other Linux distribution that includes MPlayer or xine media players.

+   - **Audio** files in AVI, MP3, Ogg Vorbis, and other formats can be played.

The MoviX player itself doesn't include any video, music, or images for you to play. Instead, let you choose the location of your content. Here are the possibilities, depending on what is available from your computer:

+   - **DVDs**—If you have a DVD drive on your computer, you can play supported content from them. (As previously stated, doesn't include commercial movies, by default).

+   - **VCDs and SVCD**—These are video formats that can be put on standard CDs.

+   - **Audio CDs**—Standard music CDs (including AVI, MP3, and other formats) can be played.

+   - **Hard disks**—Any supported content on the local hard disk can also be played from MoviX². Like KNOPPIX, MoviX detects hard disk partitions and then mounts them as you request files from those partitions. The mounts are read-only, by default, you can play your content without any risk of deleting or otherwise damaging it.
