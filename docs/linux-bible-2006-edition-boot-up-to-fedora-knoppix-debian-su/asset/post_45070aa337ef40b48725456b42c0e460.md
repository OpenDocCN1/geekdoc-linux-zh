## Downloading Digital Photos

New cameras are added frequently, so check the page if you do not see your camera. The following procedure describes how to download images from your digital camera:

1. Using a cable provided with your digital camera, connect your camera to the USB or COM port on your computer (I had better luck with the USB port).
2. Set your camera to Send and Receive mode.
3. From the main menu on your desktop, show the Digital Camera window appears.
4. Select Add Camera. The Select Camera window appears.
5. Click the down arrow to the Model box, select your camera, and click Detect.
6. Click Apply, and then click OK. Your camera should be listed in the window.
7. To begin downloading images from your camera, click the camera name that appears in the left column, and select the folder containing the images from your camera (which can take a while).
8. Select the images that interest you, and click Save Selected button to save the images. The Save Photos window lets you choose a directory to save them. You can rename the images or just use the names assigned by the camera.
9. Choose images you want to delete, and click Delete button.

## Using Your Camera as a Storage Device

Some digital cameras let you treat them like a storage device. By mounting a digital camera as a USB storage device, you can view, copy, delete, and move the pictures on your camera as you would a hard disk or CD (just at a lower speed).
