# 420 Part III ♦ Choosing and Installing a Linux Distribution

Many of the online Lin5 support options are available by clicking the Help Center icon (it looks like a life jacket) in the panel. The menu that appears not only connects you to official Lin5 online support and community forums, but also lets you view How-To tutorials and run demos of Lin5 software.

# Lin5 Forums and Information

If you should encounter any problems or simply want to ask some questions about Lin5, you can start by checking out the Lin5 support pages. By taking your Web browser to http://support.linsp.com, you can access user forums, FAQs, and (if you have a product login) personalized support.

While the FAQs and personalized support are very useful, you will get the most out of user forums. Most of the forums allow posts by registered users, but there are guest areas as well. These forums are frequented by other users and product developers are a great resource for getting future product information and providing feedback on how Lin5 works for you.

# Audio Assistant

What good new software be without a product tour peppered with a touch of evangelism? Lin5 offers both with the Audio Assistant. This animated tour of the Lin5 OS runs after your initial installation. The Assistant walks you through Click-N-Run, configuring your desktop, and a lot more.

> You can check out the online version of the Audio Assistant at http://media.lin5.com/howto/kiosk.swf.

Should you install Lin5 and bypass the Audio Assistant tutorial, have no fear. You can re-launch the tutorials by clicking the life jacket in the lower-left corner of your desktop (in the toolbar).

# Installing Lin5 Five-0

If you plan ahead, a Lin5 installation is one of the most straightforward installations. This includes Apple and Microsoft as well as other Linux distributions. There are very few configuration options and no disk partitioning options other than selecting an installation partition or wiping the entire hard. For maximum joy, make sure you have no data on the computer/partition/drive where Lin5 will be installed. The target location for the installation will be wiped out.

Make sure that you check the supported hardware list (www.linspire.com/windows_hwsw_compatibility.php) before you begin your installation to make sure there are no surprises there.
