# Working with TV, Video, and Digital Imaging

Getting TV cards

The first and biggest trick is to get a TV card or Webcam that is supported in Linux. Once you are getting video output from that device (typically available from `/dev/video0`), you can try out a couple of applications to begin using it.

This section explores the vtime program for watching television and the GnomeMeeting program for video conferencing.
