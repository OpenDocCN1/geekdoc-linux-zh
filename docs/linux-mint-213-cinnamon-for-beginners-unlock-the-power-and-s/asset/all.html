INTRODUCTION

Welcome to the Linux Mint 21.3 Cinnamon operating system tutorial!

If you have ever considered before, to give the Linux operating system a go, on your computer, you have been thinking about something very good and useful!

If you are someone, who has been using the Micro$oft Windows operating system, for some time, perhaps for years, and have decided to swap to Linux, you are going in the right direction. Why? Because using Linux on your computer, instead of Windows, has many benefits like:

●       Linux is a free of charge to download and use

●       Linux is a stable and secure operating system

●       You can do on Linux all those most common computer tasks like, browsing the Internet, watching YouTube videos, listening to music, playing games, watching your own videos, manipulating and browsing photos, typing and printing text documents, Email, and much more…

●       Linux Mint comes with more than 60 000 free applications, waiting for you to download them and use them on your computer.

●       Linux Mint is one of the most popular and most used Linux operating system in the world.

●       Linux is nearly free from viruses, so you will not be worrying about a virus on you computer, and you do not even need to install an antivirus application, on your computer. The author of this tutorial has been using Linux for many, many years, various Linux versions, and he never needed to install an antivirus application, on his computer, and during all those years, he never had any virus, on his Linux computers, as much as he is aware.

●       Linux Mint is based on the Ubuntu Linux, another most popular operating system in the world, which is used, in businesses and at home.

Linux Mint is easy to use, even if you are a beginner of Linux, especially, if you have already been using, the Micro$oft Windows operating system.

INSTALLATION PART 1

Making a bootable flash drive. You may be wondering, what is a bootable USB flash drive? Well, it is an USB empty disk, which needs to be with about 4 GB of empty space on it, which we will use, to download from the Internet Linux Mint 21.3 Cinnamon, and install that downloaded Linux Mint 21.3 Cinnamon file, on the USB flash drive, so that we can latter, plug that bootable USB flash drive with the downloaded Linux Mint 21.3 Cinnamon on it, and install Linux Mint 21.3 Cinnamon, from that USB flash drive, on the computer, which we have prepared already, to install Linux Mint 21.3 Cinnamon on that computer.

Do not worry, if you think it is difficult to do that! If you follow all the steps here, which I describe in this tutorial, then you will be able to download Linux Mint 21.3 Cinnamon and install it on your computer, in no time!

CONFIGURATION

●       Let us start the Linux Mint 21.3 Cinnamon configuration, by opening the System Settings window.

●       Click on the Main Menu icon, located at the bottom left side of the screen.

●       Once the Main Menu is opened, click on the Preferences category. Then, click on the System Settings icon, in the opened sub-menu.

●       Click and select on of the Desklets in the list.

●       Then, click on the down pointing arrow, on the right side of the window, to start downloading and installing that Desklet.

●       In the Backgrounds open window, click on the Images tab.

●       In the list of different backgrounds categories on the left side, click on one of the categories. Let us say, you click on the Linux Mint background category, which is on the top of the list on the left side of the window.

●       Then, from the backgrounds available, click on one of the backgrounds, to apply it to your desktop.

●       From the opened menu, click on the Customize option in the menu.

●       If you want the desktop icons, to be arranged automatically, click on the little white button on the right side of the window, in the Auto-arrange field, under Layout.

●       How do you want the icons on your desktop, to be arranged, vertically, or horizontally? To select the option you want, click on the drop down menu, in the Direction field, and then, chose Vertical or Horizontal.

POWER MANAGEMENT

●       In order for your computer to work efficiently, we have to configure the Power Management.

●       Click on the Power Management icon, in the open System Settings window.

●       Click on the Power tab, in System Settings window.

●       The first setting we are going to configure, is about turning the computer screen off, after a certain time of inactivity.

●       Under the Power Options, click on the drop down menu on the right side of the window, for the A/C power, in the field for the Turn off the screen when inactive for option.

●       From the drop down menu, chose in minutes, after how many minutes, you want the computer screen to be turned off, automatically, after a certain time of inactivity.

●       Now we will configure after how many minutes, the computer will go into the Suspend mode, automatically, when there is no activity on the computer.

●       Under Power Options, click on the drop down menu, in the field of the Suspend when inactive for option, and select the time in minutes, which must elapse, before the computer goes automatically, into the Suspend mode. That is still for the A/C power option.

●       In this step we will configure, what happens, every time you close, the laptop lid.

●       Still under the Power Options, click on the drop down menu, in the field for the When the lid is closed option, and select what the computer should do. That is still under the A/C power. You can chose the computer to Shut Down, Suspend, or Lock Screen, when the computer lid is closed.

●       Until now, we have been configuring the power settings for the computer, when the computer is working with the A/C power. But now, we will configure your laptop, and its Power Management setting, to use it, when your laptop is working only by using the battery power.

●       Under the On battery power setting, click on the drop down menu, in the Turn off the screen when inactive for field, and select the time in minutes, after which, the laptop screen will be turned off automatically, when there is no activity on the computer.

●       Now let us set the time of inactivity, after which your laptop will go into Suspend mode. To do that, in the Power Options, under the On battery power settings, click on the drop down menu, in the Suspend when inactive for field, and select what you want the laptop to do. Chose the time of inactivity, after which you want the laptop to go automatically into the Suspend mode. Or, if you prefer, you can chose the laptop to never go into the Suspend mode automatically.

●       And now, let us adjust what will happen with your laptop, each time you close the laptop lid.

●       In the Power Options, under the On the battery power, click on the drop down menu, in the When the lid is closed field, and choose what happens to the computer, if you want it to go into the Suspend mode, into Shut down immediately mode, or just in the Lock Screen mode.

●       Under the Extra options, click on the drop down menu, in the When the power button is pressed field, to set, what will happen, each time you press the power button, of your laptop. You can choose the Shut down immediately option, the Suspend mode, or just the Screen Lock option. Also, you can choose the Ask option, from the drop down menu, if you want to see a small window popup, and give you the option, of what you want to do about the laptop, and its various power options.

●       In this step, we are going to set what happens, each time the laptop battery becomes critically low.
