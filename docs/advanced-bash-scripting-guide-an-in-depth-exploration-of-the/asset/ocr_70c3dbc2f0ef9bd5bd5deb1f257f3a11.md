# 38.5. Credits

Community participation made this project possible. The author gracefully acknowledges that writing this book would have been unthinkable without help and feedback from all you out there.

Philippe Martin translated the first version (0.1) of this document into DocBook/SGML. Not on the job at a small French company as a software developer, he enjoys working on GNU/Linux documentation and software, reading literature, playing music, and of his peace of mind, making merry with friends. You may run across him somewhere in France or in the Basque Country, or you can call him at feloy@free.fr.

Philippe Martin also pointed out that positional parameters pass $9 are possible using [bracket] notation. (See Example 4-5).

Stephane Chazales sent a long list of corrections, additions, and examples. More than a contributor, he had, in effect, for a while taken on the role of co-editor for this document. Merci beaucoup!

Paulo Marcel Coelho Aragao offered many corrections, both major and minor, and contributed a number of helpful suggestions.

I would like to especially thank Patrick Callahan, Mike Novak, and Paul Domokos for catching bugs, pointing out ambiguities, and for suggesting clarifications and changes in the preliminary version (0.1) of this document. Their lively discussion of shell scripting and general documentation inspired me to try to make this document more readable.

I'm grateful to Jim Vanandt for pointing out errors and omissions in version 0.2 of this document. He also contributed an instructive example script.

Florian Weiner enlightened me on one of the fine points of testing scripts (see Example 7-6), and on other matters.
