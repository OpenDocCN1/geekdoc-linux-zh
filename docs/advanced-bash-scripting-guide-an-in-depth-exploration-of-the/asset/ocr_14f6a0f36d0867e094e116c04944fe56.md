In practice, all you really need to remember is following:

+   - My Dear Aunt Sally mantra (multiply, divide, add, subtract) for the familiar arithmetic operations.
- The compound logical operators, &&, -a, and -o have low precedence.
- The order of evaluation of equal-precedence operators is usually left-to-right.

Now, let's utilize your knowledge of operator precedence to analyze a couple of lines from the /etc/init.d/functions file, as found in the Fedora Core Linux distro.

while [-n "Sremaining" -a "Sretry" -gt 0 ]; do
