+   There is, however, a major problem with all: ANSI escape sequences are emphatically non-portable. What works on some terminals (or the console) may work differently, or not at all, on others. A "colored" script that looks stunning on the screen's author's machine may produce unreadable output on some else. This somewhat compromises the usefulness of coloring scripts, and possibly relegates the technique to the status of a gimmick. Colorized scripts are probably inappropriate in a commercial setting, i.e., your supervisor might disapprove.
