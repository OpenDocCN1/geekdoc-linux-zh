To encrypt, separate the plaintext message into digraphs (2-letter groups). If a group has two identical letters, delete the second, and form a new group. If there is a single letter left over at the end, insert a "null" character, typically an "X.",

THIS IS A TOP SECRET MESSAGE

TH HI IS AT OP SE CR EM ME SA GE

For each digraph, there are three possibilities.

+   1) Both letters will be on the same row of the key square:
For each letter, substitute the one immediately to the right, in that row.
If necessary, wrap around left to the beginning of the row.

+   2) Both letters will be in the same column of the key square:
For each letter, substitute the one immediately below it, in that row.
If necessary, wrap around the top of the column.

+   3) Both letters will form the corners of a rectangle within the key square:
For each letter, substitute the one on the other corner the rectangle
which lies on the same row.

To decrypt encrypted text, reverse the above procedure under cases #1
and #2 (move in opposite direction for substitution). Under case #3,
just take the remaining two corners of the rectangle.
