# GOING GREEK

Mathematical formulas often contain Greek letters, which you will find under **Tools** | **Symbols** or by clicking the rightmost omEGA symbol in the previously mentioned **Tools** toolbar. In the **Symbols** dialog you find a character (Figure 2) and click **Insert** followed by **Close**. Alternatively, you can combine the last two steps by double-clicking the character. The associated symbol ends up in the **Commands** pane with their names after a percent sign.

An uppercase letter is spelled out in capitals (e.g., %ALPHA, %BETA, %GAMMA); a lowercase character uses only lowercase letters (e.g., %psi, %phi, %theta). For a complete list of all Greek letters and markup equivalents, see the Math manual [1].

From the **Symbols** dialog, you can also change existing symbols or add new ones.
