# #### JAPAN - LIBREOFFICE ASIA CONFERENCE 2019

The *LibreOffice Asia Conference 2019* was held on May 25 and 26 in at Cybo- zu's Tokyo office. Attendees from sev- eral Asian countries were present – in- cluding Japan, Taiwan, and Indonesia – while some LibreOffice supporters made the trip over from Europe.

On the first day, Mark Hung from Tai- wan gave the opening keynote, talking about bugs, fixes, and success stories with LibreOffice in Asian languages. This was followed by talks in three tracks (one in Japanese and two in Eng- lish). Talks included: educational prac- tices in China, organizing translation sprints for local languages, running Li- breOffice in a factory, Collabora Online, and the new ODF toolkit from TDF.

On the second day, participants took part in a hackfest, business workshop, and certification reviews. Eric Sun, a TDF member and a certification inter- view candidate at this conference, won unanimous approvals from the commit- tee and became a certified migration professional and professional trainer.

![](bbox=[0.06, 0.42, 0.61, 0.61])

Asia has flourishing LibreOffice communities - they gathered together in Tokyo in 2019.

# #### NEPAL - LOCALIZATION EVENT

On September 21, 2019, Software Free- dom Day, the Nepalese LibreOffice com- munity organized a localization sprint at Kathmandu University. Sanjog Sigdel and Saroj Dhakal organized the event and reported back: "14 new volunteers signed up. We presented the guidelines for doing the translations, and together we translated around 376 text strings into the Nepali language in an hour."

![](bbox=[0.06, 0.65, 0.61, 0.91])

LibreOffice is available in over 100 languages, thanks to localization teams around the globe.
