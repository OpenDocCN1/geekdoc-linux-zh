# Getting involved with the LibreOffice community

# # Everyone Welcome

Join the LibreOffice community and help to improve the software. You don't need to be a programmer; there are lots of ways to dive in and build your skills! By Mike Saunders

LibreOffice is community-driven and developed software that is a project of the nonprofit organization, The Document Foundation (TDF). LibreOffice is developed by users who believe in the principles of free and open source software and in sharing their work with the world in nonrestrictive ways. At the core of these principles are the four essential freedoms and the tenets of TDF's Next Decade Manifesto [1] to which the community commits itself:

+   - *To eliminate the digital divide in society by giving everyone access to office productivity tools free of charge to enable them to participate as full citizens in the 21st century*
+   - *To support the preservation of mother tongues by encouraging people to translate, document, support, and promote our office productivity tools in their mother tongue*
+   - *To allowing users of office productivity software to retain the intellectual property in the documents they create by use of open document formats and open standards*
+   - *To an open and transparent peer-reviewed software development process where technical excellence is valued*

Similarly, the manifesto rejects:

+   - *The creeping domination of computer desktops by a single language, forcing all people to learn a foreign language before they can express themselves electronically*
+   - *The ownership of file formats by proprietary software companies – documents belong to their creators, not software vendors*
+   - *A closed software development process where errors can lie hidden and poor quality is accepted*

# ### ESSENTIAL FREEDOMS

The LibreOffice community believes that users should have the freedom to run, copy, distribute, study, change, and improve the software that it distributes. While TDF does offer no-cost downloads of the LibreOffice suite of programs, free software is first and foremost a matter of liberty, not price. LibreOffice supporters campaign for these freedoms because they believe that everyone deserves them.

Similarly, the community seeks to eliminate the digital divide and empower all as full citizens, support the preservation of mother tongues, and avoid proprietary software and format lock-in. The LibreOffice community works to attain its goals [2] by:

+   - Providing unfettered access to office productivity tools at no cost
