## kill

### 官方描述

向运行中的进程发送信号。

`kill [-signal_name | signal_number] process_ID...`

### 选项

```
[1] 913 Running buzz &

[2] - buzz&$

[3] + 915 Running buzz&
[1] - 913 Running buzz &
$ kill 0 # 消除所有的后台作业
[3] + Terminated buzz&
[1] - Terminated buzz &
$ jobs -l # 消失
$
```
