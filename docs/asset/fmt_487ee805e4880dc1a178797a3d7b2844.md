# Video games

Entertainment systems are used to drive video display and interact with players of various interests. Video games can be divided into arcade, home, and computer systems. A major difference between them is that a computer can be used for other applications such as word processing and internet interface while a game console is specifically for entertainment.

Arcade systems are typically built for a particular game or set of related games. From the choice of controls to the size and shape of the units, everything is made for gaming. The display is usually larger than that on a home computer, providing a greater sense of immersion. In home computers, the depth of field is limited. In consoles, multiple games are presented side by side in an arcade which allows the player to choose an even greater feeling of being 'in the game.
