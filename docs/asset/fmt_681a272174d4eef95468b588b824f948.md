+ ln P(k|n,it-1)
= \sum_{n=1}^{N} \sum_{k=1}^{K} P(k|n,it-1) [ln P(k|n,it) - ln P(k|n,it)]
+ \sum_{n=1}^{N} \sum_{k=1}^{K} P(k|n|it-1) [l(k|n|it) - l(k|n|it-1)]
