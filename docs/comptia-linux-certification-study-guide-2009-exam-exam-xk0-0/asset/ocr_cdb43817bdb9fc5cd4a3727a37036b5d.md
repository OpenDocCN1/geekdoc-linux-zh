# ## BENEFITS OF CERTIFICATION

If you are reading this book, perhaps you have looked around at your current situation and have chosen to make an improvement. Perhaps you are seeing Linux+ certification as a key credential to put on your resume to apply for a more challenging position in your organization or a different position in a more challenging area. Maybe you or your industry is seeing a change in the technology direction your company is taking or your industry is taking and you want to take advantage of any opportunities that may arise.
