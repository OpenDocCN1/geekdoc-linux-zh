a bind if you ever need a text editor on a UNIX system in which nothing else is available. Another reason for learning vi is that it is a very powerful and quick editor. After you have learned the various keystrokes, they become second nature. Not having to take your hands off the keyboard to use a mouse can greatly enhance productivity. Many people who initially start out hating vi learn to greatly appreciate its power after having worked with it for a while, so give it a shot.

Q Are there other editors available besides the ones you mentioned here?
A Yes. To see what is available, look through the Editors category in the ports collection. In particular, you might be interested in the emacs editor. Emacs is very powerful, but also very complex, so it is beyond the scope of this book.
