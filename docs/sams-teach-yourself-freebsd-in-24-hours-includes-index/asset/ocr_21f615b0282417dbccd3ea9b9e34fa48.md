# Workshop

This interactive section is designed to solidify and deepen your understanding of how TCP/IP settings work, through quiz questions and exercises which test your knowledge of the topic.

# Quiz

+      B. PCI

+   2. How much should you expect to pay for a good 10/100 PCI Ethernet card?
+      A. $30
+      B. $70
