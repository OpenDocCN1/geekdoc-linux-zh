# Customizing the Clock

The clock on the Menu bar can be customized by right-clicking on it and selecting Preferences. You can change the type of the clock from analog to digital or fuzzy. The fuzzy clock gives you an approximation of what time it is, and the accuracy can be controlled in the preference. Accuracy can range anywhere from “5 to 3,” to “Middle of week.”

You can also adjust the date and time by right-clicking on the clock and selecting this option from the menu. However, you will be asked for the root password in order to actually be able to set a new date and time.
