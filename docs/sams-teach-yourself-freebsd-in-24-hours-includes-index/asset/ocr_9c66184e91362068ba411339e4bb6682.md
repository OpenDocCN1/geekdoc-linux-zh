3. CIDR isn't the only technique for specifying subnet masks other than those aligned with the “Class” divisions—nor is it the most flexible. There’s another technique, called Variable Length Subnet Masks, or VLSM. This notation allows you to specify masks with as much specificity as you like; a good summary of VLSM, which is also worth reading in order to further your understanding of how masks in general work, can be found at http://www.lirmm.fr/~ajm/Docs/IP-VLSM.html.
