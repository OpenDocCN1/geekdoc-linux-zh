Before you can use it, of course, you need to tell KMail about your email account. To do this, click on Settings, Configuration. This will bring up the dialog box shown in Figure 20.13.

FIGURE 20.13
Configuring your email account information in KMail.

![](bbox=[0.38, 0.27, 0.78, 0.65])

In this screen, simply enter your email address, your name, and any signature information that you want to append on the end of your email.

Next, you need to tell KMail about your mail servers. To do this, click on the Network icon on the left side of the configuration dialog box. This will bring up the dialog box in Figure 20.14.
