At the time of this writing, components that are at or below the Local Bus Adapter (LBA) level are devoted to a partition. This means that the disks on which you load the software for two different Vxpars must be on two different LBAs. Let’s take a brief look at the LBAs and disks on a system in order to select the disks on which we’ll load software. If you need more detail than is covered here, please refer to the chapters that cover this topic in more detail. Also please keep in mind that the layout of systems varies depending on the model and that you need to become familiar with the layout of your system to the extent that you can determine the location of components in your system relative to the LBA level.

Let’s take a look at an L-Class system to view the System Bus Adapter (SBA) and multiple LBAs.

```
# ioscan -f | grep ba

# L-Class
```

This L-Class has only one SBA, as indicated by the leading 0 on all of the buses in the previous listing, and it has two SCSI buses on LBA 0.0. The internal disks are normally mirrored across these two different SCSI buses. There are then many additional LBAs.
