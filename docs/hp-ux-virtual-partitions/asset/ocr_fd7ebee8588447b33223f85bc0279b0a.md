# Virtual Partition Boot Process Overview

Booting partitions is similar to booting without partitions. The pri-
mary difference is the *Virtual Partition Monitor* - what I'll call **vpmon**
in this chapter. **vpmon** is loaded at the time of boot and is located in
*/stand/vpmon*.

The **vpmon** sits between the firmware and operating system on
your HP 9000. Figure 3-1 depicts the position of the **vpmon** relative
