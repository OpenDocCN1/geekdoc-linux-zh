The *Advanced Installation* menu lets you choose from among the menu tab areas with the ability of going back and forth among them until you are satisfied with your choices.

Across the top of the menu display are five tab areas: *Basic, Software, System, File System,* and *Advanced*. By pressing the tab key, each Tab area can be highlighted. To select the highlighted tab area, press the Enter/Return key. This will cause that tab area’s screen to be displayed.
