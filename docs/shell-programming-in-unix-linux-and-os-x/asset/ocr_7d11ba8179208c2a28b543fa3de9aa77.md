It's important to recognize that the shell is just a program. It has no special privileges on the system, meaning that anyone with sufficient expertise and enthusiasm can create their own shell. That's why so many variations or “flavors” of the shell exist today, including the older Bourne shell, developed by Stephen Bourne; the Korn shell, developed by David Korn; the “Bourne again shell,” mainly used on Linux systems; and the C shell, developed by Bill Joy. were all designed to serve specific purposes and have their own unique capabilities and personalities.
