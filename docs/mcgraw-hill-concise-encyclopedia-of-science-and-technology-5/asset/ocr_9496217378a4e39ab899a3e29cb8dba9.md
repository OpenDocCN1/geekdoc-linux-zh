Symbols commonly encountered in scientific writing are listed in **Table 7**. *Symbols following the ellipses and separated by commas are alternatives that are used only when there is some reason for not using the symbol given first. The meaning of abbreviated notations for nuclear reactions should be the following:

initial nuclide (incoming particle(s) or quanta, outgoing particle(s) or quanta) final nuclide

Some frequently encountered symbols for particles and quanta are as follows:

Some examples are:

+   1. $^{14}\text{N}(\alpha, p)^{17}\text{O}$
+   2. $^{59}\text{Co}(n, \gamma)^{60}\text{Co}$
+   3. $^{23}\text{Na}(y, 3n)^{20}\text{Na}$
+   4. $^{31}\text{P}(\gamma, pn)^{30}\text{Si}$

+   - neutron $n$ pion $\pi$
+   - proton $p$ muon $\mu$
+   - deuteron $d$ electron $e$
+   - triton $t$ neutrino $\nu$
+   - alpha particle $\alpha$ photon $\gamma$
