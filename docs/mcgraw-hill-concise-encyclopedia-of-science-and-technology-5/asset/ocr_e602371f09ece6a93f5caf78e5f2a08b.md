*a The graviton, with $J^a_c = 2$, has been omitted, since it plays no role in high-energy particle physics.

*b In units of the proton charge.

*c The gluon is a color SU$_2$ octet (8); each quark is a color triplet (3). These colored particles are confined constituents of hadrons; they do not appear as free particles.
