## Chemical reactions
- stoichiometric number of molecules (negative for reactants, positive for products) $\nu$
- standard equation of chemical reaction $\sum_{i} \nu_{i} B = 0$
- affinity ($- \sum_{i} \nu_{i} \mu_{i}$) of a reaction $\mathbf{A}$
- equilibrium constant $\mathbf{K}$
- equilibrium quotient or equilibrium product (of molarities) $Q$
- extent of reaction ($\Delta n_{B} = \sum_{i} \nu_{i} \Delta n_{i}$) $\xi$
- degree of reaction (e.g., degree of dissociation) $\alpha$
- rate constant $k$
- collision number (collisions per unit volume and unit time) $Z$
- rate constant corresponding to the rate $Z$ $k, f, s, J$

## Light
- Planck's constant $h$
- Planck's constant divided by 2π $\hbar$
- quantity of light $q$
- radiant power, flux of light (dQ/dt) $\Phi$
- illumination (d$\Phi$/dS) $E$
- luminance $L_B$
- luminous emittance $H$
- absorption factor (fraction of incident radiant power which is absorbed) $\alpha$
- reflection factor (fraction of incident radiant power which is reflected) $\rho$
- transmission factor (fraction of incident radiant power which is transmitted) $\tau = 1 - \alpha - \rho$
- transmittance ($T = \tau / (1 - \alpha)$) $T$
- absorption (extinction) coefficient ($\kappa/c = \log(1/T)$) $\kappa$
- absorbance (extinction) [$A = \log(1/T)$] $A \cdots E$
- absorptivity (specific absorbance) (decadic absorption or extinction coefficient) $a$
- molar absorptivity (molar decadic absorption or extinction coefficient) $[\kappa/c = \epsilon]$ $\epsilon$
- refraction index $n$
- refractivity $r$
- angle of optical rotation $\alpha$

## Mathematical logic

- *p*, *q*, *P*(*x*)
  Sentences, propositional functions, propositions
- ¬*p*, ¬*q*, non *p*, ¬*p*
  Negation, read “not *p*” or: ¬ “not equal”
- *p* ∨ *q*, *p* + *q*, *Apq*
  Disjunction, read “*p* or *q*,” “*p* and *q*,” or both
- *p* ∧ *q*, *p* · *q*, *p&q*, *Kpq*
  Conjunction, read “*p* and *q*”
- *p* → *q*, *p* ⊃ *q*, *p* = *q*, *Cpq*
  Implication, read “*p* implies *q*” or “if *p* then *q*”
- *p* ↔ *q*, *p* ≡ *q*, *p* = *q*, *Eqp*, *p* iff *q*
  Equivalence, read “*p* is equivalent to *q*” or “*p* if and only if *q*”
- *n.a.s.c.*
  Read “necessary and sufficient condition”
- ( ). [ ]. { } . . .
  Parentheses
- ∀ *x*, ∀ *x*
  Universal quantifier, read “for all” or “for every”
- ∃ *x*, ∃ *x*
  Existential quantifier, read “there is” or “there exists”
- ⊢
  Assertion sign (⊢ read “*g* follows from *p*”; ⊢ read “*p* is or follows from an axiom,” or “*p* is a tautology”)
- 0, 1
  Truth, falsity (values)
- Identity
  *a* = *a*, *a* ≠ *a*
  Definitional identity
- ∎
  “End of proof”; “QED”

## Set theory, relations, functions

- *X*, *Y*
  Sets
- *x* ∈ *X*
  *x* is a member of the set *X*
- *x* ∉ *X*
  *x* is not a member of *X*
- *A* ⊂ *X*, *A* ⊆ *X*
  Set *A* is contained in set *X*
- *A* ∄ *X*, *A* ∌ *X*
  *A* is not contained in *X*
- *X* ∪ *Y*, *X* + *Y*
  Union of sets *X* and *Y*
- *X* ∩ *Y*, *X* · *Y*
  Intersection of sets *X* and *Y*
- +, -, ∪
  Symmetric difference of sets *X* and *Y*
- ∪*X*ᵢ, ∩*X*ᵢ
  Union of all the sets *X*
- ∅, ∅
  Null set, empty set
- *X*′, *C*₂ *X*
  Complement of the set *X*
- *X* − *Y*, *X* *Y*
  Difference of sets *X* and *Y*
- *A*(*P*)(*x*), {*x*: *P*(*x*)}, {*x*: *P*(*x*)}
  The set of all *x* with the property *P*
- (*x*, *y*, *z*), (*x*, *y*, *z*)
  Ordered set of elements *x*, *y*, and *z*; to be distinguished from {*x*, *y*, *z*}, for example
- {*x*, *y*, *z*}
  Unordered set, the set whose elements are *x*, *y*, *z*, and no others
- {*a₁*, *a₂*, … , *a*ₖ}; {*b₁*, *b₂*, … , *b*ₖ*}, {*c₁*, *c₂*, … , *c*ₖ*}
  The set whose members are *aᵢ*, where *i* is any whole number from 1 to *n*
- {*a₁*, *a₂*, … }, {*b₁*, *b₂*, … , *b*ₖ*}, {*c₁*, *c₂*, … , *c*ₖ*}
  The set whose members are *aᵢ* where *i* is any positive whole number
- *X* × *Y*
  Cartesian product, set of all (*x*, *y*) such that *x* ∈ *X*, *y* ∈ *Y*
