The mineralized exoskeleton was composed of low magnesium calcite and a minor component of organic material. Most of the ventral exoskeleton, including the appendages, was unmineralized.

Most trilobites were benthic deposit feeders or scavengers, living on the sediment-water interface or shallow-burrowing just beneath it. Some were evidently carnivorous, equipped with sharp spines and processes projecting ventrally from their appendages. The morphology and broad geographic and environmental ranges of these groups suggest that they were active swimmers. Through their history, trilobites became adapted to all marine environments, from shallow high-energy shorefaces to deep-ocean, disaerobic habitats. [D.D.E., J.AdJ.]

# Trimerophytosida

Mid–Early-Devonian to Middle-Late-Middle-Devonian vascular plants at a higher evolutionary level than Rhyniopsida. Branching was profuse and varied, dichotomous, pseudo-dichotomous, helical to subopposite and almost whorled, and often trifurcate.
