
## Mathematical Notations and Symbols

- $A$: The set whose elements are $a_i$ where $i \in I$
- $x(y), R(x, y)$: Relation
- $\sim, \equiv, \neq$: Equivalence relations, for example, congruence
- $\gtrless, \triangleright, \triangleleft, \succ, \preceq$: Transitive relations, for example, numerical order
- $f: X \rightarrow Y, X \times Y, f: Y \leftarrow X$: Function, mapping, transformation
- $f^{-1}$: Inverse mapping
- $g \circ f$: Composite functions: $(g \circ f)(x) = g(f(x))$
- $f(X)$: Image of $X$ by $f$
- $f^{-1}(X)$: Inverse-image set, counter image
- $1 \to 1$, one-one: Read “one-to-one correspondence”
- $\triangle$: Diagram: the diagram is commutative in case $\phi \circ f = g \circ \psi$
- $\frac{Y}{X}, \frac{Y}{Z}$: Partial mapping, restriction of function $f$ to set $A$
- $f|_A$: Cardinal of the set $A$
- $X, \text{card } X, \#X$: Denumerable infinity
- $\aleph_0, \aleph_1$: Power of continuum
- $c, \mathfrak{c}, 2^{\aleph_0}$: Order type of the set of positive integers
- $\omega$: Integers
- $\mathbb{Z}$: Read “countable”
- a.e., $\mathrm{d}.\mathrm{p}.$: Almost everywhere, almost certainly
- ess sup: Essential supremum
- Space: Space of continuous functions
- $C^0, C^k([a, b], \mathbb{C})$: The class of functions having continuous $k$-th derivative (on $[a, b]$)
- $\ell_p, L_p$: Same as $C^1$
- $L_p, \ell_p$: Lipschitz space of functions
- $L^1, L^2, L^2[a, b]$: Space of functions having integrable absolute $p$-th power (on $[a, b]$)
- $L^\infty$: Same as $L^1$
- $C([a, b]), (C, p)$: Cesaro summability

## Special functions

- $\Gamma$: The integral part of $\pi$
- $\left[\begin{array}{c} n \\ k \end{array}\right]$: Binomial coefficient $n!/(n-k)!$
- $\left\{\begin{array}{l} n \\ k \end{array}\right\}$: Legendre symbol
- $!^x$: Exponential function
- $e^x$, exp $x$: Hyperbolic function
- $\sinh x$, $\cosh x$, $\tanh x$: Jacobi elliptic functions
- $\operatorname{sn} x$, $\operatorname{cn} x$, $\operatorname{dn} x$: Weierstrass elliptic function
- $\Gamma(x)$: Gamma function
- $J(x)$: Bessel function
- $\lambda_x(x)$: Characteristic function of the set $X$: $\lambda_X(x) = 1$ if case $x \in X$, otherwise $\lambda_X(x) = 0$
- $\Delta$: Signature: $\operatorname{sgn} x = 0$, while $\operatorname{sgn} x = x/|x|$ for $x \neq 0$
- $\delta(x)$: Dirac delta function

## Number, numerical functions

- 1, 4, 1.4, 1'4: Read “one and four-tenths”
- $[1](2)(10)100$: Read “from 1 to 20 in intervals of 1, and from 20 to 100 in intervals of 10”
- const: Constant
- $A \geqslant 0$: The number $A$ is nonnegative, or the matrix $A$ is positive definite, or the matrix $A$ has nonnegative entries
- $x \mid y$: Read “$x$ divides $y$”
- $x \equiv y \pmod{p}$: Read “$x$ congruent to $y \pmod{p}$”
- $a_0 + \frac{1}{a_1 + \frac{1}{a_2 + \cdots}}, \; a_0 + \frac{1}{a_1} + \frac{1}{a_2} + \cdots$: Continued fractions
- $[a, b]$: Closed interval
- $[a, b]$: Half-open interval (open at the right)
- $[a, b)$: Open interval
- $[a, \infty) \; [\infty, -\infty]$: Interval closed at the left, infinite to the right
- $(-\infty, \infty) \; [a, -\infty]$: Set of all real numbers
- $\max_X \{f(x), \max \{f(x) \mid x \in X\}$: Maximum of $f(x)$ when $x$ is in the set $X$
- $\min$: Minimum
- $\sup, \mathrm{I.u.b.}$: Supremum, least upper bound
- $\inf, \mathrm{g.l.b.}$: Infimum, greatest lower bound
- $\lim_{x \to a} f(x) = b, \lim_{x \to \infty} f(x) = b$: $b$ is the limit of $f(x)$ as $x$ approaches $a$
- $f(x) \to b$ as $x \to a$
- $\lim_{x \to \infty} f(x), \lim_{x \to \infty} f(x), f(a -)$: Limit of $f(x)$ as $x$ approaches $a$ from the left
- $\lim \sup$: Limit superior
- $\liminf$: Limit inferior
- $\mathrm{I.u.b.}$: Limit in the mean
- $\mathrm{L.L.}$
- $z = x + iy = re^{i\theta}, \; z = \xi + i\eta$: Complex variables
- $\bar{u} = u + iv = \bar{a}\bar{b}$: Complex conjugate
- $\Re$: Real part
- $\overline{\Im}$: Imaginary part
- $\arg$: Argument
- $\Delta(x, y), \; D(x, y)$: Jacobi, functional determinant
- $\Delta(x, y), \; \Delta'x, y)$: Jacobi, functional determinant
- $\int$: Integral (for example, Lebesgue integral of function $f$ over set $E$ with respect to measure $\mu$)
- $f(n) \to \log n$ as $n \to \infty$: $\log n$ approaches $\infty$ as $n \to \infty$
- $f(n) \to \log n$ as $n \to \infty$: $f(n) \log n$ approaches zero as $n \to \infty$
- $f(n) \to \log n$: $f(n) \log n$ approaches zero
- $f(n) \sim b, f(x) \uparrow b$: $f(x)$ increases, approaching the limit $b$
- $f(n) \sim b, f(x) \downarrow b$: $f(x)$ decreases, approaching the limit $b$

## Algebra, tensors, operators

- $+,-, \times, \cdot, ;, \tau, \tau$: Laws of composition in algebraic systems
- $\theta, 0$: Identity, unit, neutral element (of an additive system)
- $\theta, 1$: Identity, unit, neutral element (of a general algebraic system)
- $\theta$, $\theta$, $E, P$: Idempotent
- $\text{Hom}(M, N)$: Inverse of $x$
- $\text{Aut}(M, N)$: A group of all homomorphisms of $M$ into $N$
- $G/H$: Factor group, group of cosets
- $[x, k]$: Dimension of $K$ over $k$
- $\otimes$: Direct sum
- $\odot$: Tensor product, Kronecker product
- $\oslash$: Exterior product, Grassmann product
- $\boldsymbol{\otimes}, \boldsymbol{\odot}$: Vector
- $x \cdot y, \; \langle x, y \rangle, \; \langle x, y \rangle$: Inner product, scalar product, dot product
- $x \times y, \; [x, y], \; x \wedge y$: Outer product, vector product, cross product
- $\|x\|, \; [x], \; \|x\|, \; \|x\|_i$: Norm of the vector $x$
- $x$, $\|x\|$: The image of $x$ under the transformation $A$
- $\delta_i, \; A^i, \; \delta_{ij}$: Kronecker delta: $\delta_{ij} = 1$, while $\delta_{ij} = 0$ for $i \neq j$
- $A^T, A^*, A^{\dagger}$: Transpose of the matrix $A$
- $A^*, A^{H}$: Adjoint, Hermitian conjugate of $A$
- $\operatorname{tr} A, \operatorname{Sp} A$: Trace of the matrix $A$
- $\operatorname{det} A, \Delta(A)$: Determinant of the matrix $A$
- $\Delta x_i, \Delta x^i, \Delta f(x_i)$: Finite differences
- $[x_i, x_j]_i, \; [x_i, x_j]_j$: Divided differences
- $\nabla, \operatorname{grad} f$: Read “gradient of $f$”
- $\nabla \cdot v, \operatorname{div} v$: Read “divergence of $v$”
- $\nabla \times v, \operatorname{curl} v, \operatorname{rot} v$: Read “curl of $v$”
- $\nabla^2, \; \Delta, \; \Delta \mathbf{u}, \; \operatorname{grad}^2$: Laplacian
- $[X, Y]$: Poisson bracket, or commutator, or Lie product
- $\mathrm{GL}(n, R)$: Full linear group of degree $n$ over field $R$
- $\mathrm{O}(n, R)$: Full orthogonal group
- $\mathrm{SO}(n, R), \; \mathrm{O}(n, R)$: Special orthogonal group
