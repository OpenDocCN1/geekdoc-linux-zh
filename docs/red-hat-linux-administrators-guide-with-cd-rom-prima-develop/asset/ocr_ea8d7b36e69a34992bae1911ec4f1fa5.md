# TIP

For additional information about shells and the latest developments for each, consult the FAQ postings on the USENET newsgroups, such as comp.unix.shell, comp.unix.questions, news.answers, and comp.answers. These postings are updated monthly on the preceding newsgroups and can be referenced from any of the USENET archival sites such as http://www.deja.com/.
