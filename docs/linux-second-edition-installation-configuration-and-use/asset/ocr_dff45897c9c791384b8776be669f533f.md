# 2.1 Modem Converts Digital to Analog (and deversa)

Most telephone main lines are digital BUT the lines leading to your house (or business) are analog which means that they were designed to transmit a voltage wave which is an exact replica of the sound wave coming out of your mouth. Such a wave is called "analog". If viewed on an oscilloscope it looks like a sine wave of varying frequency and amplitude. A digital signal like a square wave might be a 0 bit; for most ports (used by external modems) 0-bit and +12v is a 1 bit (some +or -5v).
