# Configuring Your Hardware

When you read the release notes, click Next and you will be asked to configure your hardware (see Figure 1-30). The YaST installer and the YaST system configuration runs the same modules to configure hardware. For now you will configure the video card so that you can use X/KDE/GNOME.

YaST in SUSE 10 has changed how it detects your graphics capabilities. YaST will automatically sense what your current configuration is and will then allow you to change those individual settings.

# Configuring Your Monitor

To change your monitor configuration from what YaST detected, click on the monitor listed under "Graphics Cards." You will be presented with a list of available monitors from which you can choose (see Figure 1-31).
