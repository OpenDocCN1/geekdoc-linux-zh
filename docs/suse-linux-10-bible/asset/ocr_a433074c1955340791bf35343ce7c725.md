The filtering chains are extremely important to the operation of the filtering code because they determine whether or not a packet should be interpreted by the kernel.

The chains themselves represent the final destination of the packet:

+   **INPUT** — The packet is destined for the firewall itself.
** **OUTPUT** — The packet originated from the firewall.
** **FORWARD** — The packet is passing through the firewall (neither originates from nor is destined for the firewall).
