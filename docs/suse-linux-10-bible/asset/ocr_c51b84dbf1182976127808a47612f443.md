# Wrapping Up

DHCP is something that any administrator needs to know when it is no longer practical to assign static IP addresses to the hosts on your network. Thanks to the power and flexibility of YaST and the other administrative tools provided with SUSE Linux, DHCP can be quickly and easily configured to provide TCP/IP information to all of the hosts on your network, eliminating the need to do SABWA—system administration by walking around.
