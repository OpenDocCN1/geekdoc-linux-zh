The `d` command `d+w` can be used to remove a whole line, a word, part of a word, multi-ple lines, and multiple words.

To remove a word of text (text surrounded by space), move the cursor to the start of the word and press `d+w` sequentially. If you wanted to remove the part of a word, position the cursor at the character you want to remove the end of the word and use the `d+w` command.

> Tip It may be slightly confusing to put these commands into practice in your head, so we advise that you find a text file (or create one) of text and play around with the commands we tell about.

To remove a full line of text, press `d` sequentially. removes the whole line of text, until it finds the end of the line. It may be that you cannot see the entire text on the line if it is longer than your terminal display, be careful when you remove a line.

To remove all text from the cursor position to the end of the current line, press `d` and then `§` sequentially.
