# SUSE Linux in the Enterprise

Part V describes the place of SUSE Linux in the modern enterprise and covers the use of storage area networks (SANs). Configuration of the kernel is covered. A chapter is devoted to emulation and virtualization in SUSE Linux. Finally, the SUSE Linux OpenExchange Server, the Open Enterprise Server, and the Novell Linux Desktop are covered.
