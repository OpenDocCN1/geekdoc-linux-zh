# # Filesystem Benchmarks

Choosing the type of filesystem that you want to use on your system can be tricky. Throughout the earlier sections of this chapter, we have explored the capabilities of Linux filesystems and suggested the types of tasks that each is best suited to. However, nothing shows the performance of a filesystem better than benchmarks that you can run against, and simply compare the results. Various books and articles on Linux filesystems provide just this sort of comparison. Justin Piszcz, the author of one such article on Linux Gazette kindly gave us permission to reproduce the benchmark results from his article. Figure 3-2 shows some of the more general benchmarks that ran highlight some of the most important results. To see the full benchmark, you can view the article at http://linuxgazette.net/102/pisscz.html.
