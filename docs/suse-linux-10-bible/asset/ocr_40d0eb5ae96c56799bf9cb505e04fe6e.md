# Starting Your Installation

Inside your SUSE box you will find the SUSE manuals (which are considered
among the best Linux manuals available) and the media case.

The media case contains five CDs and two-sided DVDs. One of the install-
nVDs is installable, the other contains the SUSE Linux source code. The
installable DVD has two sides, one used to install SUSE on standard Pentium-
PCs, and the other containing an installable version of Linux for 64-bit systems.
Each side of the DVDs is labeled in extremely fine print around the center ring of
t DVD. Depending on the hardware in your computer system, installing from DVD
is the least-consuming installation method.

Insert the first CD or the bootable DVD in your system's optical drive. If you are
booting from DVD, make sure the side that you want to boot from is facing up
y your DVD drive.

Next, booting from the optical media drive on your computer to start the
installation routine. During the booting routine, you need to enter the BIOS and set
the order in which your system will probe attached devices by pressing a special key
media. You can enter your system's BIOS setup routines by pressing a F2, or F1 key—check your
system's boot screen for BIOS instructions, which are usually displayed at
t bottom of the screen. When you entered the BIOS setup screens, different
BIOS have different ways of configuring your system's boot sequence. You may find
the options you are looking for Startup Items, Boot Options, or under your
Advanced settings. Make sure that your CD or DVD drive is probed before your
floppy disk, drives, or network. Once, set the new settings, and your
machine will reboot.

At this point, your system should boot from the first SUSE CD or the DVD, and you
