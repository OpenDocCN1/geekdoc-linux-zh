# # A Typical Organization

We take Acme as an example of an organization that is quite large (more than 1,000 users). Acme is heterogeneous, as are most organizations, with multiple sites and a DR policy. After all, if Acme loses its data, it loses its business; it sells the best widgets in the world, and its customer lists, stock database, and distributors are all core to their business process.
