# ## 6.9 Automatic Partitioning

During a workstation-class installation, the Automatic Partitioning screen (Figure 6-6, Auto-matic Partitioning) will appear.

> **Please Note**
> 
> If the Automatic Partitioning screen does not appear, you may not have enough free disk space for this installation. You can either reboot your system and try to allocate more disk space, or you can choose to partition manually.
