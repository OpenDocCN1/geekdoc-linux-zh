If you are not sure how much memory your card has, consult the documentation accompa-nying your video card. Choosing more memory than is present in your card will not damage it, but may cause the XFree86 server to start incorrectly.

Next, choose a clockchip setting if your video card supports it (if it has one). The recom-mended choice, for most video cards, is No Clockchip Setting, since XFree86 can automati-cally detect the proper clockchip in most cases.
