If the CPU is the heart of a computer, the motherboard is its circulatory system. The motherboard functions as a means of connecting the CPU to all the other devices in the computer, either directly (as when the CPU accesses RAM plugged into the motherboard) or through another component (as when accessing a hard disk through a SCSI host adapter). The motherboard is generally the largest circuit board in a computer. It contains a slot or socket for the CPU, slots for memory, slots for expansion cards, connectors for hard disks, floppy disks, and external devices, and a power connector, among other features.
