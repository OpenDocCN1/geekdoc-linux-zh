Desktop computers are very useful tools, but they suffer from a major problem for some functions: They're too large. There are many situations in which you might want to work at a computer, but can't use a desktop computer because it's too bulky.

It's for situations like this that portable computers of various types have been developed. The most common types of portable computers are known as *notebooks* or *laptops*. These devices are large enough to host a standard-sized keyboard and a liquid crystal display (LCD) of 10–15 inches. They also usually have a hard disk, CD-ROM drive, and floppy drive.
