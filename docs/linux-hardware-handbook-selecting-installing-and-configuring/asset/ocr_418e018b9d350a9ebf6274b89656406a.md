# EISA Bus

The *extended ISA* (EISA) bus was developed by Compaq and other x86 PC vendors as an alternative to IBMâs MCA bus. Unlike MCA, EISA is backward compatible with ISA. You can plug an ISA card into an EISA slot, and it generally works. A 32-bit bus that runs at 8.33MHz, EISA is capable of maximum transfer rates of approximately 33MB/s. This speed is adequate even for many modern functions, although not all. Some SCSI host adapters, for instance, can transfer data at 40MB/s, 80MB/s, or higher; and some EIDE interfaces are capable of 66MB/s speeds.
