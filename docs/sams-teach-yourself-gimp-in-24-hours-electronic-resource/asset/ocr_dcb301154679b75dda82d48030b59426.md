# Q&A

+   I have an image surrounded by a colored background that I'd like to make transparent for a GIF. How can I get the color to be transparent?

+   Select the color or portion of the image you want to change and right-click, and choose Edit, Cut. Anything that is included in the selected area will become transparent.

+   Why make all those navigational buttons? Can't I just put everything on one big page?

+   You could, but a large size on one page would require a lot of scrolling to go through the entire page, and people won't scroll! As a rule of thumb, anything over to three screens worth on a page is considered bad design. Plus, the link buttons to other pages can help keep everything categorized for people who are looking for a specific thing.
