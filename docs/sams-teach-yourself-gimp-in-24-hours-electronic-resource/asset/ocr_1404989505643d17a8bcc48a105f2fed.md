You could leave it at this, but don't you want to see some light shining through your window?

Activate the original white background layer. Set the foreground color in the toolbox to white and the background color to black. Open the Gradient tool and choose either Shapeburst Angular or Radial for the Gradient option.

Place the cursor in the center of the image and drag to any edge. The light now brightens up the stained glass (see Figure 11.19).
