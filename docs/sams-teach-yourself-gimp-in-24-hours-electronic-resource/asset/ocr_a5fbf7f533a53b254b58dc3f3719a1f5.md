> You can change the rate of the blinking marquee (or ants) by going to File, Preferences, clicking the Interface tab, and changing the value in the Marching Ants speed text box. Also, you can toggle the marquee on off by right-clicking the image, and going to Select, Toggle.

When you make a selection, you will need to use the proper select tool for the job. With these six tools at your disposal, you will have the ability to set any shape, size, and color portion of an image for alterations. In this book, you will go over selections in greater detail, and learn how to utilize them to do new and interesting things to your images.

# Transformation Tools

A transformation is anything that alters the perspective of the image, whether it is by size, position, rotation, mirroring, or plain distortion.

You have a good image to begin with, but something just isn’t right with it. Perhaps you could position it a little better, rotate it, or get a closer look at it. That’s what the Transformation tools are for (see Figure 3.4). These tools are great at altering the perspective, size, position, ratio, or orientation of an image, as in Figure 3.5.
