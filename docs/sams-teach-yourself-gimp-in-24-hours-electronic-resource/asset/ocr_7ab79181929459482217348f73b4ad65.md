# The DB Browser

This plug-in keeps a browsable database of all available installed plug-ins, extensions, and internal functions in the GIMP. It can tell you the author, the version and date, and other such information. It can be very helpful when you're trying to determine what your GIMP installation has available, or at least seems to look like now and then just to get a glimpse into what makes GIMP tick (short of looking at source).

You can find DB Browser under Xtns, DB Browser. It might take a moment to initialize, but once you open it, you should see that what resembles is pictured in Figure 19.1.

You have two options: Here you can browse the database to you or use the Search by Name (refer to Figure 19.1) or Search by Blurb (see Figure 19.2) functions to find what you're looking for. Simply enter search by plug-in or function (for example, blur), and you should be able to find what you're looking for—as long as you do have the target plug-in installed and running.
