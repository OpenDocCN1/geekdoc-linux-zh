# IfsCompose

This truly remarkable plug-in lets you render fractals on-the-fly. Fractals are mathematical principles, composed of complex types of geometric shapes that usually exhibit the property of self-similarity (snowflakes are a good example of this). You can use this tool to create complex, realistic and unique patterns by manipulating three or more triangles in 2D space. (The initial interface is shown in Figure 20.8)

Your fractal starts out with three isosceles triangles. To create a unique pattern, all you need to do is manipulating these triangles with your mouse until it appears to your liking. You can rotate, stretch, and resize them to any proportions. It's really not that difficult, and you might be surprised at what you come up with. For example, many organic objects, such as trees, feathers, corals, mollusks, leaves, landscapes, and so forth can be closely approximated by fractal shapes. Using this tool, coupled with a little experimentation, can be useful in replicating these kinds of objects (see Figure 20.9).
