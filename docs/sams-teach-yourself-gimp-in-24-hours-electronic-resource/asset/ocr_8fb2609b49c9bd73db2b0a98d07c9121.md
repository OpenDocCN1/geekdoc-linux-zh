# Summary

By learning the basics of layering during this last hour, you have discovered the key to unlock one of the most important powers an image-editing program can possess.

The capability to combine and yet independently edit portions of images will unquestionably ensure that you gain a new and distinct form of mastery over your creations.

# Q&A

+   - How many layers can I have in a single image?

+   - Unlimited. The GIMP will allow you to add as many layers as you want. However, layering does require a good amount of memory from your computer, and the big-images, the more memory it takes. As long as you are fortunate enough to have a nice large amount of RAM, the sky is the limit!

+   - Can I view just one layer at a time?

+   - Yes. The eye symbol in the Layers & Channels dialog window enables quick-and-viewing of one or number of layers at a time. Just click on the Eye icon to add or remove a layer from view. By Shift-clicking on the eye, you can hide all but that layer.
