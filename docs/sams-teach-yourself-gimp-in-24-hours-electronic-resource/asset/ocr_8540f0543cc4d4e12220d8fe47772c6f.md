# NL Filter

The NL filter tries to combine the best of the Sharpen and Speckle filters. In fact, the plug-in is a combination of filters (see Figure 12.8).

The Alpha Trimmed Mean filter is a lot like the Speckle filter—it tries to remove image noise. It also works in much the same way as the Speckle filter. You can adjust the radius as you would in Duskle, and the Alpha variable in this case changes the behavior of the filter. A lower value will smooth pixels, as a blur filter, and a higher alpha will try to depict the image.

Optimal Estimation is another smoothing filter that is similar to Alpha Trimmed Mean. It works by determining the optimal range of color values within an area of surrounding pixels.

Edge Enhancement is similar to the Enhance filter—it tries to unblur an image by brightening and sharpening the edges (areas of contrasting color) in an image.
