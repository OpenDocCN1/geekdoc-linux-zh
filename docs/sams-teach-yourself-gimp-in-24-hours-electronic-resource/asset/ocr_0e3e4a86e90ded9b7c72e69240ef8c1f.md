# Fractal Explorer

Fractal Explorer is, nothing else, a great time-killer. This plug-in is just plain cool (see Figure 20.13). It lets you browse through a list of predefined fractal designs and draw them into your image. It's a quick-and-easy way to come up with some really intense designs and textures.
