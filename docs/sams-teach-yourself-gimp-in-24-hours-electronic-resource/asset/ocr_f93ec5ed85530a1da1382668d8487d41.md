# Get Started Now!

24 proven one-hour lessons

In just 24 sessions of one hour or less, you will be up and running with GIMP. Using a straightforward, step-by-step approach, each lesson builds on the previous one, allowing you to learn the essentials of GIMP from the ground up.

# Find your answer to...

+   - Installing and configuring GIMP
- Working with the GIMP interface
- Creating new art with the painting tools
- Using brushes and patterns
- Editing images and selections with transformation tools
- Adjusting color and working with transparencies and gradients
- Using layers, masks, and selections
- Applying filters and creating special effects

+   - Tips point out shortcuts and solutions
- Notes clarify concepts and procedures in a straightforward manner
- Cautions help you avoid common pitfalls
