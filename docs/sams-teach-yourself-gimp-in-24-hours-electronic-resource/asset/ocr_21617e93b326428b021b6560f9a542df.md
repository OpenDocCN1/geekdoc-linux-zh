To add a mask to an image containing only one layer, you must first add an alpha channel by going to the Layer menu and clicking Add Alpha Channel. When editing the layer mask, the black areas will appear as the checkered transparency. You can then add the mask as usual. You can add another layer and lower it under the original layer, and the mask will allow the lowered layer to show through the transparent areas.
