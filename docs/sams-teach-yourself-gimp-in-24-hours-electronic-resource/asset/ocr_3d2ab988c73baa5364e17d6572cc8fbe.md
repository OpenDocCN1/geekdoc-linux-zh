# Using Patterns

You will spend the second part of this hour concentrating on patterns. Through the composition of color, design, and special effects, you'll be able to create mind-blowing patterns with a minimum of effort.

Patterns can be useful in many graphics tasks. The capability of the Bucket Fill tool to perform pattern fills in selections can help you easily and quickly achieve a cool pattern looked to your graphics text, and GIMP's Seamless Tiling function enables your patterns to become beautifully tiled backgrounds.

Note that the GIMP comes with an abundance of preloaded patterns. To choose a pattern, left-click on the thumbnail image. The pattern name shows at the top of the pattern dialog window. Take a few minutes to click on the patterns in turn, and note that you hold down the mouse button, you can get a full size view of the chosen pattern, as in the brush dialog box.
