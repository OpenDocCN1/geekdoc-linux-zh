# Using Filters Artistically

Filters (and most filters you encounter in GIMP) are designed to add some abstraction to your image—sometimes in order to approximate a real life effect (such as painting and brush techniques, murals, and so forth), and sometimes to create something new and unique. All filters are subcategorized into different menus such as Artistic, Color, Combine, Distort, and so forth, depending on what they do specifically, but ultimately they are made with the same goal in—to abstract your images.

You will use these filters in the same way you activated the enhancing filters in the previous hour—only the desired effects of using filters has changed
