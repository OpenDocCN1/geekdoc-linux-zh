The mask options box contains three choices to start the mask with:

+   * White (Full Opacity)—By choosing white , you get a totally opaque mask. It completely covers your layer in white until it is edited.
* Black (Full Transparency)—This option creates the mask in black, which appears as totally transparent; you will be able to see your layer through it.
* Layer’s Alpha Channel—This means that the mask will be created out of the graysed version of alpha values in the layer.

For this example, I have chosen to create a white mask, which is also the default option. As you will see in Figure 10.3, a new thumbnail image indicating the mask appears in the Layer dialog window to the right of the layer thumbnail. Because I haven’t begun to edit the mask, it appears as solid white.
