# Net-Fu

Net-Fu is something that has interesting possibilities for the future of GIMP utility. It is an interface that lets GIMP accept commands from over a network. Net-Fu lets you work remotely within the GIMP and still come with some interesting effects. This can be useful if, for example, you are trying to create images remotely on a non-UNIX machine; truly Net-Fu is for the most part something that be fun to play with the developer's perspective. I mention it here to illustrate some of the things GIMP will likely be capable of as developers lend their support. In this point in time to make a proof of concept that a useful application. It will be exciting to see what kind of clever applications come up with in the near future. GIMP has a lot of room to expand into areas not previously considered.
