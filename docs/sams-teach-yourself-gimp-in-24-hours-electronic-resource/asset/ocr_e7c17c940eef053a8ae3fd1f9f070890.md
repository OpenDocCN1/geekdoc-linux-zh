+   Q After placing some text, I changed my mind about the color, but when I used the Bucket Fill tool to change to a different color, it didn't fill completely to the edges. Why not?

+   A More than likely, the Bucket Fill tool had a low fill threshold setting. Bring up the bucket fill options to raise the threshold.
