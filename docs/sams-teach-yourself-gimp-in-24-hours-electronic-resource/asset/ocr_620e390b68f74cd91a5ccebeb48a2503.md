The Placement tab has two options: Random or Evenly Distributed. A randomly painted image (randomly applied brush) has a more organic feel, whereas an evenly distributed one has a much cleaner, sterile feel.

By clicking the General tab, you will see options that will affect the texture of the painting itself. Edge Darken lets you create an effect similar to a photo edge mask by painting a border around your image. Edge Harden changes the width of your border; the border can be either a simple matching pattern or painted as a solid color. Paint Edges applies the effect all the way to the edge of the image, regardless of the presence of a border (see Figure 13.5).
