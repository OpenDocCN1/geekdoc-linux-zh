# Cutout Text

To give the appearance of having been cut out of an image (sort like a stencil sheet that you can spray paint through), you can utilize the Drop Shadow function, using a slightly different technique with it. Basically, you get the effect with an inverted drop shadow. This technique works best with text that is light to medium colored or filled with a pattern. The shadow would not show up in black or very dark text unless you use a lighter shadow color.

Open a new window. Bring up the Layer dialog box and add a new transparent layer. Activate the Text tool, insert your text, and anchor it in the transparent layer. Now, right-click, and select Layers. Alpha to Selection. Right-click again, and choose Select Invert. Now that your selection is inverted, you're ready to apply the drop shadow. Drop-click, and go to Script-Fu, shadows, drop shadow to bring up the shadow options. Keep the default options, but make sure that Allow window resizing is unchecked. Apply the shadow, unselect, and you have a cutout, as in Figure 14.10.
