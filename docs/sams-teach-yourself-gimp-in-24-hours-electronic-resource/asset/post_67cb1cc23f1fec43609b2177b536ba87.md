## What Is a Composite?

A composite is something that is composed or made up of separate parts or elements. When you refer to composites in terms of photographs and graphic images, basically you are talking about combining two or more images into one.

There are multitudes of ways you can go about combining images. By using layering, opacity, and blend mode techniques, you can virtually see through one image into another. With the use of masks and gradients, you can make one image appear to fade into another. By utilizing the selection tools, you can select portions of one image and place them into another, or you can create a collage effect by placing image selections adjacent to each.
