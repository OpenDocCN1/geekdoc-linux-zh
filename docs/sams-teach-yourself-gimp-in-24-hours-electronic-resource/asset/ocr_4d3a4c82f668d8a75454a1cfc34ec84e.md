# Summary

In this hour, you were introduced to the wonder that is Script-Fu, and looked at a few examples. There were no tutorials in this hour, but don't worry, the next hour will uncover some more Script-Fu secrets!

Because this is a brief primer, I did not cover all the scripts. There are simply too many out there already, and to attempt to do so would be an effort in futility. (And the GIMP is such a young program!) For every script I touched on, there are at least 10 more that are equal or greater utility and coolness factor. Each script has its own unique behavior and purpose, these can be best understood by direct contact with the scripts. Script-Fu encompasses a lot of different functions, and therefore can be used for many things that we probably haven't really thought of. We are living on the bleeding edge of development and experimentation, and I can think of no better environment for this in the graphical realm than the GIMP and Script-Fu.
