# Tutorial

Now that you've been through a few hours and have learned some of the basics, put them into practice and create a unique image while you're at it.

In this tutorial, you will make an image that resembles a photo cube.

To begin, you will need three images or photos close to the same size range. This might be a good chance to practice your scaling techniques. If you prefer, you can use one photo and put it on all three visible sides of your cube. Using three different but complimentary pictures makes a more interesting effect.
