Pick a foreground color that you would like to see blended with the image in the varied modes. I used a medium purple (R=128,G=0,B=128) for my illustrations. Use the Bucket Fill to fill the selected half of the image with your chosen color.

Because you are in the default Normal blend mode, the color will fill the selected lower area, as in Figure 9.3.
