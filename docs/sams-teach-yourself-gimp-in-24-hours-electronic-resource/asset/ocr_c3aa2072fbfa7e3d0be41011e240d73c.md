# FIGURE 17.9

In reality, this dog possesses big brown eyes, but the reflection of a flash bulb has hidden them.

![](bbox=[0.353, 0.116, 0.536, 0.263])

The first step to repair the damage here is to zoom in and use the Lasso to select the eyes. After selecting the first eye, hold the Shift key, which will allow you to then add the second eye as a selection also (see Figure 17.10).

# FIGURE 17.10

By using the Lasso to select the discolored eye areas, the color changes will not affect the rest of the image

![](bbox=[0.353, 0.376, 0.606, 0.533])

Because these eyes are reflecting so badly, there's more to than just change the hue to brown. Right-click and select Image, Colors, Hue-Saturation. In addition to getting the right hue, I also need to get rid of the reflect by greatly lowering the Lightness slider.

With these changes comes a vast improvement to the image, but because the area required such a major shift in brightness, the selections have now acquired jagged edges and do not blend well with the surrounding area.

To remedy this, with the eyes selected, right-click, Select, Grow, and set the grow to 2 pixels. This expands the selected areas just a little (see Figure 17.11).

Right-click and select Filters, Blur. By blurring the selections, the outer edges of the changed eyes will blend with the image, as in Figure 17.12.
