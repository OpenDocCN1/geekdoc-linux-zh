# Cubism

Cubism is another interesting painting effect, similar to GIMressionist, but much less complex. It creates a painted effect using gradient cubes applied at different angles (see Figure 13.12).

Cubism tries to emulate a different style of painting.

# Mosaic

Mosaic, from the Artistic menu, creates the illusion of an image painted onto a stained glass type of texture (see Figure 13.13). It creates a pattern of bordered polygons of varying-size and texture, and approximates the fill pattern of your original image.

FIGURE 13.13

Mosaics can make backdrops for various compositions. There are a lot of options, but they are pretty easy to figure out.
