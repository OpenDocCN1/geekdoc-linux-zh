# # Creating Sharp Images from Scratch

Sometimes you can get a really good graphic idea in your head, but you need to know the right techniques to make it happen onscreen. Your goal this hour is to cover some of the more common tricks to help you learn to create the look you want.

*Begin by looking at a couple of ways to create something from nothing.*

# # Simple and Complex Shapes

Forming shapes with the selection tools can be a quick-and-easy way to create images. In just a few steps, you can make a fairly complex object out of a simple shape.
