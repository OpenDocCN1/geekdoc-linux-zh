Now let's say you want to draw something simple, such as a star. All you need to do start with a clean slate and select the kind of shape you want to draw from the panel of choices in the Ops toolbar. Obviously, you would select the one that says Star. Now, if you click and drag over the preview window, you will see a star shape (refer to Figure 20.1). This is much easier than simply drawing by hand!

These tools on the left have many options as well. For example, you do not want to be stuck with a four-sided star but to draw a six-sided one. To take a look at all the available star options, all you need to double-click the Star button. This will bring up a dialog box with all available star shapes, as in Figure 20.2.

After you draw your shape, you might want to move and resize it to get it just so. If you look at your shape, you will notice hollow rectangles placed on the outline. These are your control points; they enable you to control the size and position of your object. By clicking on the Move button, and then repositioning your cursor over a control point, you can now move the shape anywhere you want. Likewise, using Mvpnt will move only one point at a time, resizing and reshaping your object. Using the Copy option will create an exact duplicate of the object, which you can drag into any position. The control point behavior is a different for each shape. For a star usually has three control points, one in the center for moving, and two others, each corresponding with the outer and inner points in the star.
