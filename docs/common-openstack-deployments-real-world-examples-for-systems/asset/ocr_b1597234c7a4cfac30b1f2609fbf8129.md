# ### Uses

We've explored a variety of uses for OpenStack already, from universities looking to give out instances to their researchers to car companies looking to handle their compute load and cloud storage to syncing companies offering automated data handling to their customers. Hopefully you've begun to see how components of OpenStack can fit into your own organization and have started making plans for how to use it.

Since this chapter brings together several of the pieces, you will finally get a view into what the manifests and configurations look like in a more complicated deployment.
