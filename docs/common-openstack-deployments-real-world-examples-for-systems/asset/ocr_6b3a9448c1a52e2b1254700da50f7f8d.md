# APPENDIX 7.5

160. (3) Application shall identify and describe in detail the specific health information being requested and the purpose of the request.

161. (1) If the applicant is an individual, the application shall be signed by the applicant. If the applicant is an agency, the application shall be signed by the head of the agency or a person designated by the head of the agency.

162. (2) The application shall state whether the applicant is requesting a copy of the health information or seeking access to the health information in a form other than a copy.

163. (3) The applicant shall submit a copy of the application to the Privacy Protection Officer within 30 days of submission to the agency. The agency shall confirm receipt of the copy to the applicant and the Privacy Protection Officer.

164. (4) If the applicant fails to submit the copy within the time limit, the agency may deny the application on the ground of noncompliance.

165. (5) In all cases, the agency shall provide the applicant with a written response within 30 days of receipt of the application.

+      - In the case of refusal, the response shall state the reasons for the refusal.

+      - In the case of granting, the response shall state when and how the applicant may obtain the information.

+      - In the case of delay, the response shall state the expected date of completion and the reasons for the delay.
