# # The Hacking Top 10

The term “needle in a haystack” seems to live vicariously in the minds and hearts of computer forensic investigators. Many times I have been asked by a customer “go find the bad stuff”, that’s almost a joke. I am sure you have heard something similar and thought to yourself, “ok, let me use my Jedi mind powers to Yoda my way into the hacker’s brain to find out what he was thinking six weeks ago!” Not bloody likely. There are simply too many ways to do many things with too many tools. So you will have to narrow your your focus and look for specific things, or you will never find what it is that you are not sure you are looking for. Clear as mid? Welcome to my world!

A practice that I have followed since making the jump from Ethical Hacker (yes, that is a real job) to Incident Response and Forensic analyst, is that I keep a keyword list of known hack tools. It sounds pretty basic, but I cannot tell you how effective this little practice has proven. I update the list at the conclusion of each case to include any new utilities or malware I have found or searched lately. It is a very handy practice and I highly recommend it. I have included a sample keyword list on the tools disk. It is by no means exhaustive, and is only meant to be a starter list. Use it to build your own custom keyword list, and search for those items in each case you work. I think you will be pleasantly surprised at the results.
