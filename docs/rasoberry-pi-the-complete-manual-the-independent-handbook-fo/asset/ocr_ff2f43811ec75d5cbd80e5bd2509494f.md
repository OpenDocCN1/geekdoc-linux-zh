# Raspberry Pi-controlled car build process

To demonstrate the build process, use two techniques and our Circuit Wizard data converter to make some circuit and control diagrams to help you understand the circuit. Diagrams are of the build can be downloaded with a couple of illustrated tutorials explaining a few more steps. The two diagrams below represent the main wiring and control flow.

# 1 Identify and remove old radio

+   **01**
+   * **Pick a car** - Choose a car that has a radio controlled motor and wheels but no onboard electronics. This car is a 1/10 scale off-road buggy.
+   * **Remove the electronics** - Remove the existing radio receiver and other electronics from the chassis.
+   * **Label the wires** - Before disconnecting anything, label the wires coming from the receiver and motor so you can connect them to the Raspberry Pi correctly later.

+   * **Power up** - Turn on the car to test the motor.

+   * **Servo control** - The servo motor controls the steering.

+   * **Pi-powered** - The Raspberry Pi powers the car.
