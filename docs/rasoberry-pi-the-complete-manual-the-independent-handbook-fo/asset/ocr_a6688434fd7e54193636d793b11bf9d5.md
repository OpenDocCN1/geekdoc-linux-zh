# The Complete Manual

The Raspberry Pi is one of the most exciting things to happen to computer technology in recent years. As an educational tool, this tiny PC has re-ignited interest in bare-metal computing in schools and homes all over the world. As a platform for open-source software, it has inspired millions of people to try Linux - many for the first time. Most exciting of all is the potential to incorporate the device into practical projects, as demonstrated by the tutorials in this new edition of Raspberry Pi The Complete Manual. Grab your Pi and start creating!
