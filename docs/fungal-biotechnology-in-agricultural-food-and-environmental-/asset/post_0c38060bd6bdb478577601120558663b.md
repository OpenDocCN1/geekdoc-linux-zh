## 2.1.2 Differential cDNA-AFLP screening

DNA-AFLP screening has been used to isolate response (HR)-specific genes in the Clade-subspecies aestivum and has led to the isolation of one putative and known resistance gene gene Cf-4 as well as new candidate resistance genes in wheat (De et al. 2002; Takeuchi et al. 2001). This method is an inexpensive way to identify differentially expressed genes with two different restriction enzymes and the amplification of the resulting products after ligation to adapters for these enzymes. The sizes of the resulting amplicons are measured by gel electrophoresis and resulting fragments can be excised and sequenced. Comparison of this method with differential display shown the cDNA-AFLP method to be superior (Jowers and Harper 1998). Using the cDNA-AFLP technique, we found a strong connection between the expression of genes involved in race-specific resistance and the wound response in tobacco cells.
