## REFERENCES

Molecular methods have been used successfully to address a number of problems in identification of pathogenic fungi. Not all of molecular identifications have been fully used, but this will probably improve the future. Molecular identification is increasing as the instruments become less expensive and the protocols less complex. The demand for rapid, accurate identification of plant pathogens is growing. In addition to the need for such methods in field and investigation, the threat of bioterror has added impetus to the need for rapid identification. The lack of a universally applicable technique that would make all fungi identification difficult unless morphological or physiological identification is available. New methods are being developed (Schaud et al. 2002) which hold great promise for identification of plant pathogens.
