The failure of T. atviride to induce ech42 gene may be due to complex interactions within regulatory circuits (Donzelli and Harman 2001); both ech42 nag required nitrogen starvation and the presence of chitin, whereas gluc7 was induced by nitrogen starvation alone. In the presence of ammonium (100 mM), both chitin-oligomers and chitin triggered CHIT42 and CHIT40 (chitobidase production); CHIT33 expressed in the presence of N-acetylcellulose and chitin-oligomers, with less effect. This suggests that the expression of cell wall degrading enzymes in Trichoderma is regulated by carbon and nitrogen interaction.
