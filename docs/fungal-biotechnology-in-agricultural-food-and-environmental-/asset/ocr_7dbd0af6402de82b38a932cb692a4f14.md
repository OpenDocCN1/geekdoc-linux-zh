Stochastity may be interpreted as an ecological advantage in hazardous environments. The complex stochastic and sensitive nature of community interactions is particularly evident within experimental systems involving more than two species, which demonstrate heterogeneity with the initial arrangement compared to binary-pairings (Sturrock et al 2002; White 1998). Agreement between based studies and those in natural strata is also likely to be influenced by environmental heterogeneity (Boddy 2000).
