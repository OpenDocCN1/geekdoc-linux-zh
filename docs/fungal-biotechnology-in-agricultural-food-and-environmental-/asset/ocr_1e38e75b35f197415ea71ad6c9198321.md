# ## 4.1 Lignin Peroxidase

# ### 4.1.1 Properties

Lignin peroxidase is a set of glycosylated isozymes with masses ranging from 3.2 to 4.0 and molecular masses from 30 to 43kDa, each containing 1 mole of protein (Ferrell et al. 1989; Gold and Alic 1993b). It possesses a high redox potential and a lower pH optimum than that of any other peroxidase (Camaro et al. 1997; Kirk and Hammel et al. 1986). Like other oxidases, LP is capable of oxidizing phenolic compounds through the generation of phenolic radicals.
