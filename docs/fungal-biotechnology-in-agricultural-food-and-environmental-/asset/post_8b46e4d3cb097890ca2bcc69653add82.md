It is generally believed that heavy metals inhibit the growth of mycorrhizal associations. Recent studies have shown the viability and activity of EM fungi in soils with different heavy metal concentrations. Richards and Söderström (1990) reported that the number of fruiting bodies and species decreased along a heavy metal gradient.
