# 3.1 Screening for Lignin Degradation by White Fungi

Lignin-degradation is commonly evaluated by measuring ¹⁴CO2 from ¹14C-labeled preparations, such as ¹14C-ring-labeled degradation (DH). The measurement of ¹⁴CO2 is the most sensitive and accurate method for testing ligninolytic activity (Ekinson et al. 1990). The evaluation of ¹⁴CO2 and the modification of lignin have been employed for determining ligninolytic activity in white rot fungi as well as for the elution of different types of wood constituents from lignin (Hansen et al. 1995; Costa et al. 2002; Edwards et al. 1996; Eggert et al. 1997; Hattaka 1984; Hattaka and Utsi-Rauva 1983; Hattaka 1988; Hochreiter et al. 1995; Seurmann et al. 1999; Slann et al. 1996; Yamaguchi and Hattaka 1998).
