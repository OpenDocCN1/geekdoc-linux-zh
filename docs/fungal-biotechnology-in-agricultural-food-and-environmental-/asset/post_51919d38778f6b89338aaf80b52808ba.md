The tools of biology have advocated a significant impact on control of fungal pathogens. The use of molecular markers to create genetic maps, identify, and clone plant genes for plant protection has had a spectacular beginning. While the availability of new types of markers such as cDNA-AFLPs and OMAs and the genomic and cDNA sequences of many pathogens and model plants has noted, progress in this area is anticipated in the near future. We hope that other genome-wide approaches will lead to an integrated view of how every gene contributes to the growth and development of defense.
