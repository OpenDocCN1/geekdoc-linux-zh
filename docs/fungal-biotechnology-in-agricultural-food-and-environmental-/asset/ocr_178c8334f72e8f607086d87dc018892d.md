Veratryl alcohol and Mn(III) complexes are of great importance in lignin degradation. It is shown that these low molecular weight species may be stable enough to diffuse into the 3-D structure and extract materials that are not accessible by direct oxidation by lignin peroxases, manganese peroxases, and oxidases (Bur and Hall 1994; Hayashi et al. 1986; 1997). Veratryl alcohol was not stable and short-lived to suit this purpose. However, Mn(III) complexes appear to be soluble for this purpose.
