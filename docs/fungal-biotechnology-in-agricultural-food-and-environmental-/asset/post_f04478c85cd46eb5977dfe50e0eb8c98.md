# 4.2 Maintenance and Preservation of Fungal Cultures

Pure culture of edible fungi

The pure culture of edible fungi is often maintained or preserved by several methods. The most common methods are (a) short-term storage on agar medium, which is used for a few weeks; (b) medium-term storage in the refrigerator, which is used for several months; and (c) long-term storage in liquid nitrogen, which can be used indefinitely. The choice of method depends on the purpose of the culture and the species of the fungus. For example, cultures that are needed for immediate use are kept in short-term storage, while cultures that are intended for long-term research are kept in liquid nitrogen.
