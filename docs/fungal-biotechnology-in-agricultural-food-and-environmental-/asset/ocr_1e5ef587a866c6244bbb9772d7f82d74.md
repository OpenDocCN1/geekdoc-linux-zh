# 6.2 Decolorization of CSB Using Pycnopus cinnabrus

The isolate of P. cinnabrus was examined for its ability to decolorize CSB on plates, initially then in shake flasks and subsequently in 2 and 101 packed-bed bioreactors. Every case the dye was decolorized at relatively high concentrations (500 mg/l).
