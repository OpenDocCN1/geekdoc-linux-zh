# # MONOCYCLIC HYDROCARBONS

The BITEX compounds (benzene, toluene, ethylbenzene, and the xylem isomers) are also types of liquid fuels (Ritchie et al. 2001). They are commonly used in industry as solvents and chemical feedstocks. Toluene and ethylbenzene support the growth of a few fungi, which can metabolize volatile compounds (Praneta-Boli 2011). Benzene and xylenes do not appear to serve as carbon or energy sources for fungi, however although reports of the fungal metabolism of benzene (Ghenlin and Cooleyn 2009) and one of their mineralization to CO2 (Yadav and Reddy 1993).

Toluene is utilized as a carbon and energy source by Cladiophora chrysosperum, Sclerodermaosporium [theomorph of P. bovidi], Cladiopophora sp., Exophiala sp., and Praoderma sp.; these fungi are used to clean up petroleum spills (Garcia-Peña et al. 2001; Penafeta-Boli et al. 1995). In high-nitrogen media, P. chrysosperum cometabolizes toluene to CO22 (Yadav and Reddy 1993).
