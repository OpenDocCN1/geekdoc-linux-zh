# 7.3 Screening for New Fungal Strains

Strain selection for explosive-degrading fungi is far from complete. The rather random screening approach used to date has shown the ability to degrade substrates like TNT across fungal genus (Schiebner 1997a,b). Most of the species have been from temperate regions. However, the tropical species have greater lignolytic capabilities as they are able to decompose lignin more widely than temperate species (1976). Many fungi with lignin degradation ability can partially degrade TNT and explosives by unknown metabolic routes. Understanding the enzymology associated with these reactions would be a priority. This would lead to a more rational screening approach.
