# 7.2 The Pilot Bioreactor

The fermentation was conducted in a nylon 20 aerated polyhene drum, with 2 cm³ (Scotchbrite) cubes (Lonergan et al. 1995 b). A reactor cycle of 25 days was used in this instance with the medium continually recycled at 36 ±1°C. P. cinabarrus colonized the colony. Veratryl alcohol (4 mM) was used as a inducing agent. The RBBR was added (500 mg/ml) to the medium on day 3 after inoculation. Daily samples were analyzed for glucose, nitrogen and loxidase activity. A sample was also taken 30 min after addition of the dye and is termed D3 + 30.

# 7.3 Decolorization in Bioreactor

The dye (395) was decolorized by 80% within 24h and almost completely removed within 48h after addition. The nitrogen of the reactor was removed in 7 days and the glucose in 12 days wherein more glucose was added to the reactor (Lonergan et al. 1995b). A small increase in nitrogen occurred through the end of the cycle to outliers.

Lacey activity was detected on day 2, peaked at a large 60 U/ml on day 8 and to a minimum on day 12. It then rose again towards the end of the cycle as the glucose was added.

In this series of experiments lacies were purified from 100 ml removed during the reactor operation on day 1, 3, D3 + 30, 20, 23, and 25 by migration to the method described (Schlipake et al. 2000). The profiles for all samples were similar to those of the lacey purification carried out on the Mono Q anion exchanger. The elution pattern for days 4 and 23 are shown in Figure 4. The anomaly in elution times (day 4, 18 min and day 23, 15 min) was explained.

On the electrophoresis of particular samples, under native conditions, significant laccase activity was observed in the enzyme through the gel from difference. Electrophoretic migration of proteins under nondissociating buffer conditions occur on the basis of size and charge (Hames 1990).
