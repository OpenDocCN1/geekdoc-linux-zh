# 7 CONCLUSIONS

This accompanying review (see chapter 57) show that the molecular biology of Trichoderma has made during the last decade, both as a theoretical and as prospective research. Gene manipulation in Trichoderma is now feasible, with more sophisticated approaches to study gene expression and function. In regulation, working models show that we have developed in our library on the induction of chitinases during mycoparasitism (Figure 1), can now be investigated and characterized in detail. A review of the current situation has been made mostly under laboratory conditions. Because of the redundancy of genes extracellular enzymes such as chitinases (Baek et al. 1999), the role of individual genes still needs to be assessed further focus may be related to colonization (rhizosphere competence) and competition with the respective hosts. The development of strategies to control the respective genes and their functional analysis will be the future of the field.
