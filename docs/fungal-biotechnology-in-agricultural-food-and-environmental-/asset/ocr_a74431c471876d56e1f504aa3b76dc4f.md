The co-metabolic reactions increase the electron density of the aromatic ring and facilitate electrophilic attack by lignin-degrading enzymes (Field et al. 1993). TNT degradation occurs by a series of co-metabolic synergistic reactions involving manganese peroxidase and lacase. The peroxidase enzymes are heme-containing glycoproteins that require hydrogen peroxide to catalyze single electron oxidations that generate free radicals. Hydrogen peroxide is generated by oxidase enzymes including oxygen, which is a better electron acceptor than phenol oxide, which uses molecular oxygen as its terminal electron acceptor (Fritsche et al. 2006).
