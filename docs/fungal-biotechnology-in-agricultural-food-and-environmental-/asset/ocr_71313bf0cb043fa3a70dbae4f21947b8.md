# CONCLUSIONS

A strain of cinnabinaris isolated decolorized an industrial waste as well as the disazo dye CSB and the vinyl sulfonyl RRBR. On purification of the phenoloxidase of the enzyme showed all the characteristics of a laccase The purified lacquer decolorized from the white-root fungus rapidly there appeared to be some change to size variation of lacquer during the reactor cycle. It may be hypothesized that the enzyme may have adsorbed lacquer molecules in a way that activated the protein. This protein could induce a conformational change, which may optimize the catalytic site of the enzyme in a way that it is easily accessible. As the amino group, there could be a reason occurring between a possibly free carboxyl group of an amino and lacquer of the dye. This would mean that such amino-containing molecules could behave like amino acid. Such modulation could explain the presence of an acidic band appearing closer to the cathode upon exposure to the dye.
