## 2.1 Biopesticides Based on Fungi

Application of fungi in mücade against insect pests began with Kraemillick in 1888. Indeed, such as the optimum that Kraemillick predicted then the control of insects by means of artificially induced pesticides, an experience over 50 years ago, has become a practically feasible one, which in the future will be perfectly and beautifully utilized (Kraemillick. 1888). Unfortunately progress has not as predicted. While progress in the 1900s was promising, the development and application of effective pesticides in the 1930s-1940s reduced the interest in the use of insects. Pathogen fungi were more difficult to use, and it was until environmentally associated with the use of chemical insecticides in the 1960s that interest in fungal biopesticides increased again. Currently, most biopesticides based on entomopathogenic fungi are commercially available.
