# 8 CONCLUSIONS

There is clearly plenty for new approaches to bioremediation of explosives-contaminated soils and sediments. There is increasing evidence that metabolically produced products of the degradation of explosive compounds such as TNT are not tightly bound to clay and organic matter as was found. Their presence in the ecosystem can be detected by assays, in terms of chemical metabolites, and various techniques have been used to fully exploit the capacity of fungi to minimize these compounds by naturally occurring pathways. This appears to be a matter of selection of strains and fungi that have been screened for their degrading capability. We need better understanding of metabolic pathways involved in the strains and the microbial ecology of these fungi when used in bioremediation. Further work is needed before large scale, field-based trials are undertaken that may create many challenges as they overcome.
