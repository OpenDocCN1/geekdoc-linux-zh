## 3.2 Competition

The AMI spores in soil are not known to compete for nutrients as resources are required for survival until root is established. Following entry, root competition for infection sites, host roots, and root space is more important. Competition between AMI and pathogens can be used for physical exclusion of pathogens (Davis and Mange 1980; Husseyn and Roccardi 1982; Smith 1988), if the pathogen is introduced after AMI. Simultaneous colonization of AMI and pathogen may not compete for space (Menge 1980) because of its slow growth rate compared with the pathogen. In some cases, others have noted that competition may not occur between AMI and other organisms (Sempalayan et al. 1995).
