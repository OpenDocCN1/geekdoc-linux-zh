More recent studies have attempted to examine and accommodate the complex and coordinated behavior exhibited by mycelium. A stochastic cellular automation for modeling the dynamics of two-species microcommunities revealed the significance of local and nonlocal interactions in generating the behavior of mycelial systems (Bown et al. 1999). Furthermore, the experimental system allowed for detailed spatial analysis of the communities revealed that interaction outcome depends on patch size, fine scale dynamics were highly dependent on nonlocal interactions (Bown et al.; Syrock et al. 2002). Furthermore, experimental studies indicate that fungal competition is more intense in smaller domains occupied by the same species, and in vitro where growth may be less than that growth (Holmer and Stahl 1953).
