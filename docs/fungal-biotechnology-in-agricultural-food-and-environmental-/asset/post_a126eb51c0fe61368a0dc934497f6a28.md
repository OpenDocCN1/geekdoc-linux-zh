## 7 BIOTECHEMICAL TESTS

A wide variety of methods have been used to quantify the fungal activity in stored grain. Citrate, ergosterol, adenine triphosphate, immunofluorescence, and DNA probes have been used (Fleurat-Lessard 2002; Mayan 1993). However, ergosterol is the most prevalent in spoilage fungi (*Aspergillus* and dermatycetes) and not found in insect pests; it has been extensively utilized as an indicator. The method was first described by Seitz et al. (1977) and can be performed relatively quickly and routinely using simple extraction and HPLC.
