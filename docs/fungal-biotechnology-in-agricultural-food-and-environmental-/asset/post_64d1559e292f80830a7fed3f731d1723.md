## 3 Targets Based on the Ribosomal RNA Gene Cluster

The ribosomal gene cluster occurs in fungi as a set of repeated units comprising three ribosomal RNA units, internal transcribes spacers (ITS) and intergenic spacers (IGS) (Figure 2). The DNA sequences within the units contain some extremely conserved sequences useful for the development of broad-spectrum amplification from mixed DNA samples. The resulting fragments can be further separated to species or genus specific profiles by other techniques, e.g., nested PCR, SSCP, hybridization, ELISA, RFLP analysis, or sequencing. The more variable regions can be used for genus- or species-specific detection approaches.

### 3.2.2 Trichothecenes

Trichothecenes are sesquiterpenoid mycotoxins produced by several fungal genera including Fusarium, Trichoderma, Acremonium, Myriam, Stachybotrys. Over 30 structurally related trichothecenes are known, which include T-2 diacetoxin, deoxivalenol, nivalenol and others the most important ones. The main producers of these toxins are found in Fusarium (e.g., F. fumatum, F. culmorum, F. graminee, F. solani, and F. sporotrichioides), Trichoderma, Alternaria and other species, and play a role in plant pathogenesis as virulence factors. Trichothecene biosynthesis genes were found to contain a 25kb gene cluster including at least 10 genes in F. sporotrichioides and F. gramineum (Brown et al. 2001).
