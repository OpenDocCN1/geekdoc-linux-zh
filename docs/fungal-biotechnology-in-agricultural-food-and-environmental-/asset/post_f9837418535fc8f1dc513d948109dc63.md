Not all molecules (usually protonated) in the positive mode, are detected better as negative ions, and gas chemistry is not like solvent chemistry, and many carboxylic acids are much more efficiently detected in gas phase by positive ESI. Negative ESI will give many acids and clusters, so it is better obtained for some classes of molecules in APCI than ESI.

The charge to mass ratio is detected using a analyzer, which is either: Quadrupole, time of flight, ion-trap (electric and magnetic) or an ion-cyclotron (IC). These are detected by scanning filters where only ions of one mass can pass at a time and nonscanning analyzers all entering the ions are detected (Table 4). Mass selectivity and resolution are the major factors for the identification of compounds whose sensitivity and speed are most of chromatographic importance.
