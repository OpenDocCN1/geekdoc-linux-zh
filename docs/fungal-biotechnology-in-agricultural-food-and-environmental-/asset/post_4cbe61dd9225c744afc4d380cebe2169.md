## 14 FERMENTED ME PRODUCTS (SAUSAGES)

The starter culture used in fermentation may have the bacteria, yeasts Debaryomyces chrysogenum and Candida fungi and N. poly. Yeast enhance color and improve aroma in sausages. Mould contributes to the fermentation and lowers the pH by lactic acid bacteria to preserve the fermented product and inhibit the growth of pathogenic microorganisms, and the production of nitric oxide due to activity of nitrate and reducing bacteria in the fermented polypeptide. ## 12 FERMENTED PRODUCTS

Fermented products, with their characteristic flavors, introduce variety to the South-East Asian diet.
a) Japanese katsuobushi: This is made from fish and gluason involved in the fermentation (Graitski 1973); (b) Cambodian Fish and Manis: This is made from a fermented product extracted from salted juice. Glutinous rice is also added to the fish (Padma and George 1999); (c) Vietnamese Nooc: This is a brown liquid produced by fermentation of marine fish or fresh water and placed in jars for several months. Bacteria and yeasts contribute to proteolysis and flavor. Enzymes from O. oryza can be used for reduction time to increase yield of nuo-mac (Richard 1959).

## 15 CONCLUSIONS

Fermented foods play a substantial role in people living in nations as the people of developing countries. Foods and drinks are found in supermarkets and with prices vary markedly; some products may be more expensive than others (Sarker 2002). Fermentation in the production of tasty foods and drinks well before the advent of modern biotechnology. Today some of these fermentation procedures have developed into lucrative industries. Some of the fermented products are white, reportedly rich in antioxidants. Many of the fermented products have been found to be aflatoxin-free. Nevertheless cases of poisoning after fermented food have been reported. It is essential that aflatfree raw materials and nutritious cultures be used for food safety.

Recent research on fermented food has focused in several areas including the effect of foods on health. The alpha, monas, contains the antioxidant dipeptides (Lee 2000). The effect of fermented dairy products and their mechanism of action have been reported (On-Go et al. 2001). A functional-component of soy sauce, 4-hydroxy-2(5)-5-ethyl-3-(2-methyl)CH2H is a potent antioxidant in milk (Nishimura). Also show beneficial health effects due to kombucha (tea fungus) ingestion in animal experiments and in humans what has been described (Greenwell et al. 2000).

Another area of research is the investigation of the chemical constituents and nutrient values of fermented foods.
