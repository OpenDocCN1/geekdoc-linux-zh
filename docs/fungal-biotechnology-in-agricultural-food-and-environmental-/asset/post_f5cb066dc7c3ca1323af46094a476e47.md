Plant breeding for resistance is practical when a large germplasm exists with differential resistance in the crop, such as in corn. However, genetic engineering for resistance may be essential for crops such as corn which seems to have resistance to aflatoxin contamination of seeds (Cody 1989). Extensive research has focused upon encoding mycotoxin-producing genes in bacterial species (Coflow et al. 1998) and its gene have been evaluated in vitro by resistance (Jacks et al. 1999) and in vivo enhancing fungal disease resistance in tobacco (Rajasekaran et al. 2001).
