# CHAPTER 14: Managing Email and Contacts with Novell Evolution

# Edit Contact Information

Edit the contact's information as needed on the tabs provided by the Contact Editor. After you have completed editing the information, click OK.

# Create Contact Lists

You can quickly create a contact list that serves as an alias or nickname for several contacts in the address book. For example, you may be working with a group of people who all need to receive email updates from you. You can create the contact list (which is saved as a separate card in the address book) and add the contacts to it. Sending an email to the contact list sends the email to all the contacts you included in the contact list.

# 1 Open Contact List
