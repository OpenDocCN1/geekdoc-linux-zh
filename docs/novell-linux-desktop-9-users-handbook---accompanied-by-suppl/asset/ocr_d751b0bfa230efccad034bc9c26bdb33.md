# Novell Evolution

All computer users, particularly those who use the PC extensively as a business tool, strive to stay organized and maintain a list of appointments, contacts, and emails. Novell Evolution is a groupware product that pulls together important business tools into one software package. Novell Evolution allows you to manage your email, calendar, contacts, and tasks. It also provides connectivity with network groupware servers as Novell GroupWise and Microsoft Exchange. Chapter 14, “Managing Email and Contacts with Novell Evolution,” and Chapter 15, “Staying Organized with Novell Evolution,” provide more detailed information on how to use Novell Evolution.

# KEY TERM

Groupware—Client communication software that allows collaboration with other users and is designed to connect to a communication server product.
