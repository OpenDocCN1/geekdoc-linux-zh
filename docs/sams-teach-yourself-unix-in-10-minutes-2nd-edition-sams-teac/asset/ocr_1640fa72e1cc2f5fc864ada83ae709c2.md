When Microsoft Windows and Apple Macintosh users are waiting for an operation to complete (such as the loading of an application), they are at least given a warning that their system is tied up. The KDE editor does not give you a warning, or an hourglass. You will need to be patient and wait for the editor to load. Don't assume your system is hung-up, unresponsive, or ignoring you.
