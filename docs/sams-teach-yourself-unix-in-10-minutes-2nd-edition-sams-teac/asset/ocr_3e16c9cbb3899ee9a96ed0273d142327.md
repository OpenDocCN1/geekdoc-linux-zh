# Graphical Logout

When logging out of your Unix system graphically, you will need to take more steps than when logging out using the text-based method. When logging out of a text-based session, you simply need to issue the command. Within a graphical environment, you need to manipulate the graphical environment to log out. The you see that indicates the main menu in which the logout process is initiated may vary depending on your Unix distribution, but in most cases, the first icon (sometimes represented as a K or the SuSE logo), which produces the Logout... command from menu. Once selected, you will be given an option to end your session, turn off your computer, restart your computer (warm start), or simply cancel your logout and go back to your current session.

> With No Mouse Don’t forget your keyboard shortcuts as the Tab key. Remember that not all systems come with a mouse, but all require a keyboard. This is determined by systems BIOS startup. You will always have a keyboard to work from, so you should really consider logging in and out of your Unix system without the use of your mouse as much as possible. It’s not common to find mice on Unix systems.
