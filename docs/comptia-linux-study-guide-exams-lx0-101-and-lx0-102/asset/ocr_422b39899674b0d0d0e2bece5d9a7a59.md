+   12. The traceroute command (option C) identifies the copies that lie between your own computer and a destination computer, with some very basic information about network packet time and reliability. Thus, traceroute can help track down the source of the described problem—perhaps a tracer to reaching all of the non-responsive systems has failed. The netstat and ifconfig utilities of both provide information about local network configuration options, but most likely won't be of help in diagnosing a problem that affects some sites. The ping utility (option B) may help you identify sites that have failed or beyond reach. You can use dig (option E) to obtain information on the mapping of hostnames to IP addresses, but it will help in resolving connectivity problems.
