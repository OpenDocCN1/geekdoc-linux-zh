| Option | Used with commands | Description |

| -y, --yes, or --assume-yes | All | Produces a "yes" response to any/yes prompt in installation scripts. |

> If you use APT to automatically upgrade all packages on your system, you're effectively giving control of your computer to the distribution maintainer. Although Debian or other distribution maintainers are unlikely to break into your computer in this way, an automatic update with minimal supervision on your part could easily break something on your system, particularly if you obtained packages from unusual sources in the past.
