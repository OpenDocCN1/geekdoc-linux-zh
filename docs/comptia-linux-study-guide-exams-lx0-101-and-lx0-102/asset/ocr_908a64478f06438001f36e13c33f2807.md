+   19. PostScript is the de facto standard for Unix and Linux programs, as specified in option B. Although they may have passwords, these are not account login passwords, option B suggests; those passwords are set on a per-account basis. Files do have associated groups, but these are in addition to individual ownership and they can't be used to make the file's owner, making option correct. Deleting a group does not delete all the accounts associated with the group, option D is incorrect. Groups are not fundamentally a cross-computer construct, contrary to option E.
