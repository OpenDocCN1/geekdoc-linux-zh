It's very important to reiterate that VMware doesn't care whether the physical drives on your system are SCSI or IDE. VMware's virtual SCSI controller can format SCSI-type drives and use SCSI-style access on your CD-ROM drive even if your hard drives and CD-ROM drive are on your PC's IDE controller. See Figure 5.21 for a sample diagram.

The reverse is true as well: You can configure real SCSI devices to appear as IDE devices to the virtual machine. See Figure 5.22 for the SCSI Configuration window.

By using virtual SCSI instead of virtual IDE, regardless of what the real disk is, you might get some notable speed gains in the virtual machine.
