5. You should enable CD-ROM access to the emulator. Click on the CD-ROM tab, shown in Figure 17.14. Ensure that CD-ROM enabled is checked, and move the CD-ROM drive (typically D:) to the Installed CDs list.

6. If you want the Mac emulation to have floppy access, click the Floppy tab and move A:\ to the Installed Floppies list.

7. Click the Screen tab to set the size of the MacOS desktop. Curiously, the DirectX full-screen and windowed modes work just fine in VMware, despite VMware's lack of DirectX support. Whenever possible, use a full-screen mode to get better video performance. See Figure 17.15 for a sample screen.

> Unless you configure Basilisk II to use the same color depth (8, 16, or 24-bit) as your XWindows display, you will get unpredictable results, especially if you ask for a DirectX display.

8. The last major tab to consider is the Memory tab, shown in Figure 17.16. As a rule of thumb, use at least 8MB for System 7.0 or 7.1, at least 16MB for System 7.5 or 7.6, and at least 32MB for System 8 or 8.1. Because your PC is VMware-ready, you should have at least this much on hand.

9. Also in the Memory tab, be sure to fill in the path to the Macintosh ROM you obtained.
