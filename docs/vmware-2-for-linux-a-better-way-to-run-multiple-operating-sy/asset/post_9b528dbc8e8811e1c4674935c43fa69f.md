## About the Author

Jason Compton has been an outspoken fan of virtual machines and emulators for a decade. A full-time freelance writer specializing in technology and business coverage, Jason regularly appears in a variety of publications, including Linux Magazine, the Chicago Tribune, Smart Business, CRM Magazine, and Mobile Computing. His writing career began in 1993, with an article for an online magazine in the days before the Web. His first published work? A column about emulators.

Jason is a graduate of Northwestern University.
