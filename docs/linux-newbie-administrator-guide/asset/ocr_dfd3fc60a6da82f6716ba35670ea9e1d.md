Star Office looks and acts very much like MS Office for Windows (this includes that it is big and slow) and may not be worth the trouble without at least 32 MB of physical memory. It also has a good file-level compatibility with MS Office (read and write MS Word, MS Excel and PowerPoint file formats).

Older versions of Star Office required registration after you obtain a "registration key". The registration key is based on your e-mail you give to Star Office Corp., so make sure you remember which e-mail you gave them and enter the name to the program when it pops the registration box, with the key. The latest versions (after StarOffice was purchased by Sun) does not anything like any more.

# 7.3 Applicware

Applicware is another complete office suite. It contains a word processor, spreadsheet, graphics, presentation, mail, html authoring, and a few more applications. Many say it's the best. I don't know because no free edition is available. See http://www.applux.com/appware/linux/index.html for more information. If you are really desperate for Applicware, you can download the demo at http://www.download.com do you really want it? # 7.4 AbiWord

AbiWord (http://www.abisource.com/) is a good light-weight wordprocessor. Worth trying for simple word processing needs. Although still incomplete, it is quite useful to me, e.g. it supports spelling-as-you-type without the overhead of WordPerfect.

# 7.5 Spreadsheet

Besides the spreadsheet applications in the above (StarOffice and Applicware), you may want to have a look at "xess": http://www.ais.com/linux_corner.html.

They also have a slightly scaled down she "xessile" version which can be downloaded from their site. We love the general design of xess (simple and almost self-explanatory). It seems have really good power for engineering applications and in our opinion it may be the best spreadsheet currently available on Linux.

You may want to keep your eye at "Gnumeric" (included with your RH6.0 distribution, part of the Gnome project). It is still relatively incomplete but it is already definitely usable. It is under heavy development and definitely has the potential to become really great in the near future--it already has a lot of built-in functions, but its printing is unreliable.
