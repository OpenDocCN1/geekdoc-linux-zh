There are a few text/document oriented file formats that are quite more standard than MS Word file format: ASCII, SGML, HTML, LaTeX, TEX, XML, PostScript, pdf, dvi ... and all of them have excellent support under Linux. The Word file format can also be read/written very well under Linux by Word Perfect, Star Office, Applizware, etc. to cover your today's needs.

The story is similar with other proprietary computing "standards". Linux, by its very nature, is based on true, published, and free standards because "open source" makes the full specifications available to everybody (competitors or not).
