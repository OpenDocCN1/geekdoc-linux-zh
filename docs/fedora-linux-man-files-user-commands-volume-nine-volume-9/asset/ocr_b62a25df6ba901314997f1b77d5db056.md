# DESCRIPTION

**rygel**

is a ho dia solution that allows you to easily share audio, video and pictures, and control of dia player on your ho network. In technical terms it is both a UPnP AV MediaServer and MediaRenderer implemented through a plug-in chanism. Interoperability with other devices in the market is achieved by conformance to very strict requirements of DLNA and on the fly conversion of dia to format that client devices are capable of handling.
