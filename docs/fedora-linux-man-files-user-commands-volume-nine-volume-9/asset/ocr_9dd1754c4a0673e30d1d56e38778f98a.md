## —show-graphs
Print graph nas (URIs) as they are seen in the input. This only has a aning for parsers that support graph nas such as the TRI G parser.

## —show-namespaces
Print namespaces as they are seen in the input.

## -t, —trace
Print URIs retrieved during parsing. Especially useful for monitoring what the guess and GRDDL parsers are doing.

## -w, —ignore-warnings
Ignore warnings, do not emit the ssages.

## -v, —version
Print the raptor version and exit.
