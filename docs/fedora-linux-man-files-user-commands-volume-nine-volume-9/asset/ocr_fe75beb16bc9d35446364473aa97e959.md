A validity interval may consist of a single ti, indicating that the certificate is valid beginning now and expiring at that ti, or may consist of two tis separated by a colon to indicate an explicit ti interval. The start ti may be specified as a date in `YYYYMMDD` format, a ti in `YYYYMMDDHHMMSS` format or a relative ti (to the current ti) consisting of a minus sign followed by a relative ti in the format described in the TIME FORMATS section of sshd_config5. The end ti may be specified as a `YYYYMMDD` date, a `YYYYMMDDHHMMSS` ti or a relative ti starting with a plus character.

For example: “+52w1d” (valid from now to 52 weeks and one day from now), “-4w:+4w” (valid from four weeks ago to four weeks from now), “20100101123000:20110101123000” (valid from 12:30 PM, January 1st, 2010 to 12:30 PM, January 1st, 2011), “-1d:20110101” (valid from yesterday to midnight, January 1st, 2011).
