# FORMAT OPTIONS

%at  
album title

%aa  
album artist

%aA  
album artist (lowercase)

%as  
album artist sortna

%aS  
album artist sortna (lowercase)

%ay  
album year

%ag  
album genre

%aG  
album genre (lowercase)

%an  
album disc nuer

%aN  
album disc nuer, zero padded

%st  
stream title

%tn  
track nuer (i.e 8)

%tN  
track nuer, zero padded (i.e 08)

%tt  
track title

%ta  
track artist

%tA  
track artist (lowercase)

%ts  
track artist sortna

%tS  
track artist sortna (lowercase)

%td  
track duration

%te  
track elapsed ti

Variables can be coined using quotes. For example "%tn %aaa %tt", will print the track nueroed by the artist and the title of the track.
