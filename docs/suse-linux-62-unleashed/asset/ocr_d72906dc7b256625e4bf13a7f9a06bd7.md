> Did you know?
Can I insert StarDraw drawings in another Star Office document?
You can move your drawings in the Gallery and drag them to the desired document via DragDrop.
To select an object, click with enough and drag 3D effects to it (You'll find the necessary tool in the toolbar). Open the Gallery and the Desktop to display the '3D' tool. Double-click an object in the Gallery wait a moment before the first click, then drag. Your object is now in the Gallery and you can drag it to a link or file document anywhere.

Cursor movement and functions such as cut and paste use fairly standard keystrokes. Ctrl+C performs a copy of a selection, while Ctrl+V performs a paste of the Clipboard contents. All of these functions are reminiscent of Microsoft Word, Excel, and the like, so users familiar with these products should have little problem adjusting to Star Office.

# Switching Between Applications

As mentioned, Star Office creates its own desktop environment and provides a taskbar. The taskbar icon lets the user move easily among the open documents; alternatively, you may use Ctrl+Tab to cycle through the open documents.

To create a new document, choose File, New, Document-Type. You can also click the desktop icon (a deask lamp) available on the taskbar to move back to your desktop, which has links to the individual applications.

# Importing and Exporting Microsoft Documents

Star Office provides an Auto Pilot that assists you with importing Microsoft Office documents. You may import Word, Excel, or PowerPoint files.

Choose File, then Auto Pilot, then Import.
