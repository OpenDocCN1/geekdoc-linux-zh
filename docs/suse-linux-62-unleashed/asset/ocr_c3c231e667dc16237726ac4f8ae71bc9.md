# Resizing the Quake II Window and Customizing
Controls

The default screen size of the Quake II window is 320 × 200 pixels. To enlarge the screen,
press Esc, scroll down to the video settings. Press the right or left cursor key to enlarge
the screen in increments from 320 × 200 to 640 × 480 or 800 × 600 pixels. When finished,
scroll down to the Apply menu item and press Enter. The screen will then resize.
