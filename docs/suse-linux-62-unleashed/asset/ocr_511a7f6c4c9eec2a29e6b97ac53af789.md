Enter a number to choose the corresponding card definition.
Press enter for the next page, q to continue configuration.

Your choices are to enter a number corresponding to your card (or a card recommended as a
choice by the readme file for your card under the /usr/X11R6/lib/X11/doc
directory), then press the Enter key to the next screen, or to press q to continue the
configuration. Note that if you press q, x86config uses Unknown for your graphics
device. On the other hand, if you pick a specific card, x86config reports with an
identifier, chipset, and selected server appropriate for your chipset (after choosing entry 97
in the database).
