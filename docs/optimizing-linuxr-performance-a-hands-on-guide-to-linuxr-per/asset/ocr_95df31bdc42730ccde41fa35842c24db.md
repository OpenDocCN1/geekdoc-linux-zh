The significant difference of this performance investigation from the GIMP investigation is that the times we are trying to reduce are on the order of milliseconds rather than seconds. When the times are so small, it can be difficult to make sure that the profiling data you are capturing is actually the result of the event that you are trying to measure rather than just the noise around trying to stop and start the profiling tools. However, this short time period also makes it practical to trace all aspects of the application for the interesting period of time.
