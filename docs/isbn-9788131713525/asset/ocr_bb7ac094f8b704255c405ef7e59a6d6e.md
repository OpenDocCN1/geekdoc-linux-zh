Supported sysfs buses:

Supported sysfs classes:
        block
        i2c-adapter

        input
        mem

You can see from this listing the variety of system information available from sysfs. Many utilities use this information to determine the characteristics of sys-tem devices or to enforce system policies, such as power management and hot-plug capability.
