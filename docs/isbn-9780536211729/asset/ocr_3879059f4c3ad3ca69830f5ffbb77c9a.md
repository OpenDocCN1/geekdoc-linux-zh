Many programs are secure only if properly configured, and it is common for SysAd-
mins to configure them improperly. Sometimes, it is a lack of training and understanding
the risks, while other times use of an insecure feature is deliberate, “I just gotta
have it.” A recent case in Apache is PHP capability. It has a recent history of
security problems. PHP security problems have been well publicized, and still some people
cannot to use it securely or find an alternative. Security and convenience often
are contradictory; frequently, a choice must be made.

Before deciding to deploy a service (i.e., changing what capabilities will be used
how the service will be deployed), research its security. Check its security history
and how it may be deployed securely. If it cannot be deployed securely, what are the
service alternatives? I still encounter people using FTP who don’t realize that sftp is
