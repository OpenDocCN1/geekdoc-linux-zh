# **14.11.1 Prepare Your Case for the SysAdmin**

Prepare a short summary of the evidence so that a quick inspection of the summary will implicate their system. Try to have a more complete set of logs because they probably will ask for this later. Review “Having the Cracker Crack Rocks” on page 513 for additional important procedures for handling the evidence. Failing to follow these procedures risks damaging the evidence and certainly your credibility, especially if you do contemplate legal action.

An effective demonstration of persistence is examined in the case study “Persistence with Recalcitrant SysAdmins Pays Off” on page 564.
