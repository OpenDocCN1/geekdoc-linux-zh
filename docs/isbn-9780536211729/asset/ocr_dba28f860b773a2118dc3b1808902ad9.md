# D.10.8 Incident Preparation & Response CD-ROM

The full title is *System Administrator Incident Preparation & Response (SAIPR) for Windows NT (for System Administrators)*. Although it is aimed at the NT administrator, some of the topics are general and applicable and of use to Linux SysAdmins. Topics include collecting information before an incident, recognizing unauthorized activity, policy and procedures, and investigation.

It was produced by the Department of Defense’s Computer Investigations Training Program (DCIPT).
