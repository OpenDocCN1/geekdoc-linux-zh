Several well-known ways that crackers take over a system from the keyboard are discussed here.⁶ Only some of the intrusions require access to the system's floppy or CD-ROM drives. Certainly, high-security systems should be kept in locked rooms but even in engineering labs that are considered reasonably secure, there are enough unfamiliar faces that someone could pop a rogue floppy into a system without being noticed. Certainly, if an intruder has time, they could simply take the cover off a box and steal the disk containing proprietary data. Most laptop disks are tiny and with a little practice can be removed in under a minute and hidden in a pocket. Keep in mind the next time you are on the road.
