You can set your time zone by selecting a physical location (Figure 3-21) or your time zone offset from Universal Time, Coordinated. An example selection is *America/Knox_TN* for Knoxville, Tennessee. A time zone offset for the same location would be *-5*, or UTC minus five hours.

In the graphical installation screen, you can click a specific city in your time zone via an interactive map. Cities are indicated with a yellow dot, which will change to a red X when selected. There is also a list of cities from which you can choose.

The user account called *root* has complete access to the entire system and is used for system maintenance and administration. Setting up a root account and password, as seen in Figure 3-22, is one of the most important steps during installation. The password should follow your password policy.
