# Video Card Support

Even though you are doing a text installation, you may want to run the X window system. The GUI desktop allows you to use the mouse to point-and-click the options you want. To use the GUI interface you must configure your video card.

Red Hat Linux will attempt to auto-select the video card on your system. If it does not find the correct card, you can select the Change option and then select your video card from a list, as shown in Figure 3–28.

![](bbox=[0.31, 0.56, 0.86, 0.86])

If your video card does not appear on the list, it may not be supported. If you have technical knowledge about your card, you can choose Unlisted Card and attempt to configure it by matching the card’s chipset. Most video cards are included in the list of supported cards and so you should not have any problems.
