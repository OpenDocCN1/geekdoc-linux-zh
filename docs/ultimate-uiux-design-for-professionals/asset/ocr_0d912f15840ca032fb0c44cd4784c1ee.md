# High-Fidelity Prototyping

Digital prototypes with high fidelity are intricate, refined models that closely resemble the final result. By thoroughly testing and validating their designs and user flows using these prototypes, UX teams can make sure that every part of the user experience is optimized.

High-fidelity prototypes provide a far more accurate representation of the final product than low-fidelity prototypes do. They offer a more authentic user experience because they are aesthetically pleasing and frequently include advanced functionalities. UX teams proceed to develop high-fidelity prototypes after they have a firm grasp of user and stakeholder demands. This phase is essential for testing intricate user processes and making last-minute changes.

Common types of high-fidelity prototypes include:

+   - Interactive prototypes
+   - Digital prototypes
+   - Coded prototypes
