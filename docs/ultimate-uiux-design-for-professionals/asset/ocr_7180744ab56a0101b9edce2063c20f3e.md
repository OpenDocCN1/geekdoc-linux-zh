# Effective Technique to Conduct Card Sorting

Let us quickly review the steps involved in doing card sorting:

+   1. First, list the elements on cards that you want users to organize, such as sections of your website or app. For in-person sessions, these can be physical cards, or they can be digital cards for online card sorting.
+   2. After that, ask participants to arrange the cards into groups according to how they feel. In the event of an open card sort, the players will name the groups themselves. They will arrange the cards into pre-made categories of your selection if it is closed.
