# Point of View

The term “point of view” in UX design refers to viewing the product or service from the viewpoint of the user. This method helps designers gain a thorough understanding of the needs of customers, objectives, and potential frustrations. Designers may produce more efficient and user-focused solutions that directly address user pain points by putting themselves in the user’s shoes. As an example, the designer would take into account the user’s feelings —such as excitement, confusion, or frustration — while they browsed, picked out, and purchased the products. User experiences that are more intuitive and fulfilling are produced by using an empathy-driven design approach.

In order to acquire clarity on the business goals and plans, it is imperative that you involve the stakeholders in the process of building a point of view.
