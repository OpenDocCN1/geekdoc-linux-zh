Think of a design tool that not only allows you to create attractive mockups but also involves every member of your team, no matter where they are in the world. That is Figma. You and your team can collaborate on a design in real-time with its features; there is no need to exchange files via email. It resembles an online whiteboard that is constantly updated. Also, Figma is the tool of choice for designers who wish to execute their concepts with accuracy and style because of its user-friendly interface, strong vector tools, and extensive plugin library.
