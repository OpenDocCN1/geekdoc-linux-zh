# User Persona

In this section, you will find an in-depth look into user personas.

A user persona is like creating a character in a story, but instead of a book, it is for designing products or services. It’s like painting a picture of your typical customer - you give them a name, age, hobbies, and goals. This makes them feel real, helping designers understand what they need and want. Creating personas helps teams make products that people will love because they are tailored to fit their wants and needs, just like a perfect outfit made just for you!
