# Effective Technique to Conduct Focus Groups

To conduct user experience research using focus groups, follow these steps:

+   1. Plan a schedule that allows time for recruiting, testing, analyzing, and incorporating results.
+   2. Gather your team and assign roles: moderator, note-taker, and discussion leader.
+   3. Determine the scope of your research: What questions will you ask? How deeply do you want to explore answers? This guides the number of participants and groups.
