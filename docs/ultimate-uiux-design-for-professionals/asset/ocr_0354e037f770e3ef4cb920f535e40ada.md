# UI Design Patterns for Mobile Apps

People spent most of their day interacting with their smartphones, with nearly 90% of that time devoted to using apps. These apps became vital in everyday life, assisting with tasks and social connections. In this digital world, app design has become essential. An engaging and user-friendly app attracted more users, while a confusing one pushed them away.
