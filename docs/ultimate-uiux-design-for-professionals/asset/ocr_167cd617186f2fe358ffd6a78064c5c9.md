# Principle of Disclosure

The goal of the Principle of Disclosure is to provide consumers with just enough information to help them comprehend what’s coming without going overboard. As consumers delve deeper, information is gradually revealed to them, enabling them to make wise decisions.

**For example,** an online course platform might offer a synopsis, instructor bio, and a few salient elements for each subject. Users can select whether to investigate further by reading student reviews or studying a complete syllabus after taking a quick look. Users are directed through the content with ease and feel less cognitive stress and better overall when information is revealed gradually.
