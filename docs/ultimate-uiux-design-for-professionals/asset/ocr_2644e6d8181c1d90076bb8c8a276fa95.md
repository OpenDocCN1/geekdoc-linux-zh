Hooray! You’ve reached the finish line of the design thinking process... or have you?

Design thinking is not a one-and-done deal — it is a journey that’s always evolving. It’s like a never-ending adventure because the process is fluid and keeps looping back on itself.

Here’s the scoop: you are designing for real people with real needs, not robots in a perfect world. Your users will always have challenges, and you’ll always need to find ways to help them out. That’s why we suggest you build a cycle of user research, problem-solving, idea brainstorming, picking solutions, and testing into your design routine. This way, you’re always in tune with what your users need and can keep improving your designs over time.
