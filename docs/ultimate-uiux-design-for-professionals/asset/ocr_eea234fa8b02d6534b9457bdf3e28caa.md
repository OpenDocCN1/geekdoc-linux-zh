# Video Sitemap

A specific kind of XML sitemap called a video sitemap gives search engines metadata about the videos on your website. The title, description, length, location (URL), thumbnail URL, and publishing date of the video are all included in this metadata. A video sitemap enhances your videos’ discoverability and indexing by providing search engines with this comprehensive information, which helps them comprehend the content and context of your films.
