It is their responsibility to make your experience as seamless and pleasurable as they can. Consider the time you spend using your preferred social media app. It all seems so obvious, don’t you think? It is because of UX design. It’s simple to locate what you’re searching for, and the buttons are where you would expect them to be. All of that is the result of a skilled UX designer. UX designers are concerned with having things function properly in addition to looking good.

To put it briefly, a UX designer’s responsibility is to develop digital experiences that are both efficient and easy to use. Making your life easier and more fun is the main goal of UX design, whether you’re shopping online, using an app, or visiting a website.
