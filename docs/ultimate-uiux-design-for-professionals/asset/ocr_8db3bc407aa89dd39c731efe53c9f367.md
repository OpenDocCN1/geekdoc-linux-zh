Mid-fidelity wireframes are used by designers to clearly illustrate the visual hierarchy and layout, which helps to communicate the significance of various elements without the visual clutter of complete design elements. These wireframes are usually made using programs like Sketch or Balsamiq, which guarantee a well-balanced and understandable depiction of the proposed design.
