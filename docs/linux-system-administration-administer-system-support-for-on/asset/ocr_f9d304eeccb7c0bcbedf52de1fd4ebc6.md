# ### DAILY JOURNAL

Write down the system-related events of the day (system-related only, please – keep your personal feelings for that cute coworker to yourself). A system journal enables you to keep track of trends in system failures, suspicious activity, and performance. It’s also a helpful resource if you turn in status reports on a regular basis. Your records can reflect the little details that might slip your mind when management is hammering you for answers after a system failure!
