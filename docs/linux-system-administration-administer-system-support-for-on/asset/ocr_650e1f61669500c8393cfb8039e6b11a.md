# # Examining USENET

Back before the Web, USENET was among the most popular forums for online discussion. USENET is divided into newsgroups such as `comp.os.linux` and `alt.shenanigans`. The newsgroups have a hierarchy (as does the UNIX file structure), and now have an archive.

However, on Slackware Linux there are several mediums to reading USENET. They include `trn`, `nn`, `tin`, `pine`, and `emacs` (yes, the last two are correct). The `trn` news reader will group your news articles by thread, which makes them much easier to read, as shown in Figure 12-9.
