+   3. Click the right arrow that appears to the left of the FileSystem folder; in the resulting tree view, locate the directory you want to browse.
+   
+   For example, to look at the `/etc` directory, click the right arrow next to the `etc` directory. Nautilus displays the subdirectories in `/etc` and changes the right arrow to a down arrow. X11 is one of the subdirectories in `/etc` that you view in the next step. Scroll down the contents of the left pane to locate the X11 folder.

+   4. To view the contents of the X11 subdirectory, click X11.
