# In this part . . .

This part is all about getting you started on your way to a lasting relationship with SUSE Linux. Before you can begin your SUSE Linux experience, I spend a chapter explaining what SUSE Linux is and what you can do with SUSE Linux (pretty much anything you can do with a PC that runs Windows).

To start using SUSE Linux, first you must install SUSE Linux on your PC’s hard drive. SUSE Linux installation is quite simple, and I explain everything in Chapter 2. In Chapter 3 of this part, I guide you through a quick tour of SUSE Linux, primarily focusing on the key steps: booting the system, logging in, using the KDE and GNOME desktops, and shutting down the system.

The final chapter in this part gives you an overview of all the applications in SUSE Linux—as a precursor to using these applications to get some productive work out of your SUSE Linux system.
