# In this part . . .

After you have installed SUSE Linux, this part helps you begin exploring and using SUSE Linux. First, I take you on a quick tour of the GUI desktops — KDE and GNOME. I explain the similar features of GNOME and KDE and then focus primarily on the KDE desktop, which is the default in SUSE. I devote a chapter to showing you how you can locate files and applications in your system.

If I am not mistaken, you probably want your Internet access as soon as possible. You can connect your SUSE Linux system to the Internet in several different ways — by using a dial-up modem or by cable or DSL. I explain the steps in the final chapter of this part.
