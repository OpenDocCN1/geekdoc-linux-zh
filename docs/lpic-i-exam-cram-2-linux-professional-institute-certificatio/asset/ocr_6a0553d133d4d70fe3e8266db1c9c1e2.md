# Testing Your Exam-Readiness

Some will wait until they have studied a long time to take practice exams, which is fine, but they're missing out on the continuous feedback that taking the chapter sample questions will give them. To get completely traumatized, before you read any of the load-bearing chapters about the exams, take the 101 and 102 sample exams just to see how you do.

I recommend keeping a couple of distributions running nearby, this book, and a Web browser for research—oh, and a cup of some caffeinated beverage to keep you sharp. Spend at least a couple of hours a day studying and trying the things you read. And don't slack off; it's easy to forget and not study for a while.
