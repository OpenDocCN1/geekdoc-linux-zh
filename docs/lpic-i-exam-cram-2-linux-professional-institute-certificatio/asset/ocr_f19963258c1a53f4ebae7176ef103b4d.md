# # Exam Scoring

The exams are scored on a scale from 200 to 890. Very few people I know have scored above a 700, and 890 (perfect) scores are almost unknown, with that level of intelligence and retention seemingly being incompatible with corporate existence. Seriously, many a darned good sysadmin has scored in the mid 600s, even if they did fantastic on the exam prep and in class. A pass is a pass. Take it and drive on to higher heights, I always tell them.
