This review allows you to change any answer of the exam again. You can navigate through the exam as you did in the first place; then it ends again with the review screen where you can choose a new criteria to review. After having finished the review, you can grade the exam by clicking the Grade Exam button. This leads you to the result page where you will find a statistic about how many questions were answered correctly, how many were wrong, and whether you passed or failed. To review the exam, you can click one of two buttons on the bottom of the screen:

+   - Review All—Displays the complete exam again. You will see the correct answers in green, the wrong answers in red, and your choice.

+   - Review Errors—Displays only the wrongly answered questions.

This review is a read-only review. You can't change any answer; you can just see which of your answers were correct and which were not. Every question provides a Show Answer button to read an explanation of which answer was correct. You can also click a Back to Grade Exam button to return to the result page where you will find an End Exam button to return to the main menu. Once there, you can start a new exam or quit the program.
