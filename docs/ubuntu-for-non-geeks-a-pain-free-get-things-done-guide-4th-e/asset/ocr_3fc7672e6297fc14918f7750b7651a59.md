# TUX ROCKS

Music à la Linux

![](bbox=[0.14, 0.48, 0.32, 0.68])

It’s now time to move on to the audio side of things. Yes, Linux does indeed rock, and in this chapter you will find out about those musical talents that your system possesses. You will learn how to rip CDs, create MP3 and Ogg Vorbis files (files that you can recognize by their .mp3 and .ogg filename extensions), add album cover art, change file tags, play music files, and burn files onto audio CDs that you can play in any CD player. You’ll also learn how to play a variety of audio streams. If you’re interested in learning how to work with your iPod, iPhone, or other digital media device, or how to convert audio files from one format to another, well, you’ll have to wait until Chapter 15.
