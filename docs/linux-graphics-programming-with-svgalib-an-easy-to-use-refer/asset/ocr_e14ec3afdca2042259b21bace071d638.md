# ## Chapter Summary

Chapters are designed to progress in an order of increasing skill level, and the majority rely on the knowledge presented in previous examples. However, you're welcome to skip forward if you're interested in a particular subject, and if you think you can handle it out of sequence. That said, here's a brief rundown of what you can expect to find in the pages ahead:

Chapter 1, *Getting Started*, introduces SVGAlib and covers the process of compilation and installation. You'll also learn what makes SVGAlib necessary, and how it connects with your programs.

Chapter 2, *Graphics Basics*, teaches you the general graphics theory that you'll need to know for the upcoming chapters. Topics discussed include screen modes and modern video hardware.

Chapter 3, *First Programs*, is designed to help you write and compile your first programs using SVGAlib. Common program layout, essential functions, basic drawing primitives, and compilation are all covered extensively.

Chapter 4, *Complex Shapes, Formulas, And Functions*, demonstrates how to develop and implement shape drawing functions that SVGAlib currently lacks. Circles are covered in detail, as are other useful geometric designs.

Chapter 5, *Color*, explains the theory behind color usage in the VGA card. Of course, numerous code examples are presented to help you learn about palettes and RGB color.

Chapter 6, *Shades, Fills, And Patterns*, covers special techniques such as screen fades, antialiasing, and providing graphics at boot time.

Chapter 7, *Fonts*, introduces you to the world of graphical text. Here you'll find information on creating your own fonts, as well as utilizing existing ones.

Chapter 8, *Fractals*, gives you the rundown on this esoteric art. You'll find plenty of theory, followed by actual examples.

Chapter 9, *Graphic Files*, explains how to easily read and display the images encoded in files such as GIFs and JPGs. You'll also learn how to make screen shots, and how to save the resultant data into a new file.

Chapter 10, *Basic Animation*, gets you started with moving shapes and images.

Chapter 11, *Polygons*, discusses that vital element of 3D images, the polygon. Triangles, as well as SVGAlib's threeDKit, are covered in detail.
