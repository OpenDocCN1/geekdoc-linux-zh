# Chipsets Supported By SVGAlib

The following chipsets, current as of January 1, 2000, are known to work with SVGAlib.

Be aware that many video card manufacturers, including big name brands, routinely use chipsets built by other companies. Therefore, it’s entirely possible that your graphics card is supported, even if you don’t see it listed in this appendix. At the very least, it might be able to use the VESA driver. Conversely, even though two companies might share a chipset, it doesn’t mean that the same driver will work with both cards. The two cards may differ enough that two separate drivers are required.

Finally, as stated previously, the new VESA driver will work with many cards that don’t have a driver of their own.

# ## Supported By SVGAlib

The following chipsets are definitely supported by SVGAlib:

+   - Standard VGA (Generic)
+   - 3Dfx—Voodoo Banshee and Voodoo3
+   - Alliance—ProMotion-AT24, ProMotion-AT25, and ProMotion-AT3D
+   - ARK Logic—ARK1000PV and ARK2000PV
+   - ATI—Mach32 and Mach64 (CT or newer only)
+   - Advance Logic—ALI2301
+   - Chips & Tech—64300, 65520, 65525, 65530, 65535, 65540, 65545, 65546, 65548, 65550, 65554, 65555, 68554, and 69000
