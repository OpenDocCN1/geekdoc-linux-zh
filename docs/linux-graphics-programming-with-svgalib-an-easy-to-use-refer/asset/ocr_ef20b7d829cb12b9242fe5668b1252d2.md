# *Texture-Mapped Polygons*

Expanding on the filled polygon concept, a *texture-mapped polygon* is created by rotating and scaling a bitmap drawing (the texture), which is then placed on a 3D polygonal image. This is the basis for most modern 3D games.

# *Raytracing*

In this technique, simulated beams of light are radiated from an illumination source outside the screen, from which point they sail forth to penetrate any objects in their path. As the rays bounce, shades of color are created. Special attention is given to the material of the objects being struck, taking into account any transparency or translucency. This is the most realistic of the four techniques, as shadows and shading are made possible. Unfortunately, it's also rather time consuming, and thus too processor-intensive to be used in real time applications. See Figure 12.2 for an example.
