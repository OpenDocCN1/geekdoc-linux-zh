# Random Fractal Forgery

Coined by Richard F. Voss of IBM's Thomas J. Watson Research Center, this term refers to the seemingly realistic depictions of natural objects that can be generated with simple algorithms. These algorithms are distinguished by their use of randomness and fractal self-similarity.
