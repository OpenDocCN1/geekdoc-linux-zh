# ## Recollections (Part 2)

Michael Weller explains how Matan Ziv-Av became the current maintainer:

> “I knew SVGAlib needed a complete redesign, but no one wanted to help me. Instead, people just yelled at me because I didn’t write a driver for their hardware. I got tired of this, and I also realized that I might stand in the way of future developments.”

> “I tried hard to get a new maintainer. At some point I finally convinced Matan. Mainly because he wrote so many new drivers and code (including the VESA driver) that I couldn’t follow his fast development cycles with new releases (due to lack of time).”
