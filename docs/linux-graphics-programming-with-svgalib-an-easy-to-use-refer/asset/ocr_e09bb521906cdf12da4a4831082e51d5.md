# Recollections

Michael Weller describes how he took over the duties of collecting patches and issuing new re-
leases:

> "Harm told [me that] he must be offline for personal reasons. I really don't know why. He left, and as I had already done a huge amount of coding for SVGAlib, he asked me to take over for a while."

> "Later (1–2 years) he got back to me and said he would be online again, and that he was very happy with what I had done up until then. I only had a few email contacts with him, and then he once more vanished from the Net."

# History And Changes

Tables F.1 through F.47 document the extensive changes made to SVGAlib throughout its lifespan. The data itself was taken from the doc/CHANGES file contained within the SVGAlib source package, and as such, may contain some grammatical errors. (Not all SVGAlib programmers are native English speakers. Given its international flavor, this shouldn't come as a surprise.)

In most instances, credit is given to the programmer who made the revision, but in some cases a programmer is marked as being "unknown." If you happen to be the "unknown soldier" in question, please write to maintainer@svgalib.org, and you will receive proper credit in future releases of SVGAlib.

This said, it can be reasonably assumed that Harm Haneumaeyer himself was responsible for many of the changes made prior to version 1.5 release.
