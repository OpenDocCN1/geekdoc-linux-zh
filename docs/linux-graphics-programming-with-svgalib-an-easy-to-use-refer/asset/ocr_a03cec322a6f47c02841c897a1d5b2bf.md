For me, this eventually led to design and development of computer games. In 1972, the
term “PC” was meaningless: There was no such thing as a Personal Computer and thank-
fully, the notion of “political correctness” had not yet reared its ugly head. But now, more
than 25 years later, the technology has advanced so far that it has become accessible to
many more people.

The book you hold in your hands can open a door into a new medium. Though it has been
around for more than 25 years, the medium of computer graphics is still very new. However,
there are a few things that you have to learn in order to be able to make inroads into this new
“frontier.” After all, you had to learn which end of a paintbrush to hold. And those of you
who drew those nice towns and farms after learning about how perspective works in art—
yes you—well, you had to learn about vanishing lines and such first too.

So, instead of sharpening your pastel pencils or mixing your paints, fire-up your compilers,
assemblers, and IDEs. Dig into this book, roll up your sleeves, and start coding. Hey, com-
pared to working in oils, the cleanup is a breeze.
