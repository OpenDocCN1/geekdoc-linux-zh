**F**ractals—if you’ve ever seen one, you’ll need no introduction. The mere name alone conjures up vivid imagery—fantastic, often intriguing forms that twist and flow, flower and bloom, as though dancing to the beat of a sensuous rhythm. Some liken fractals to the psychedelic imagery that was popular in the late 1960s and early 1970s. Others are satisfied to view them as mathematically generated pieces of art. Whichever the case, their appeal can’t be denied. See Figure 8.1 and decide for yourself. No matter how you categorize them—as cosmic seahorses spinning in infinity or as mundane numerical plots—I think you’ll find fractals interesting and memorable.
