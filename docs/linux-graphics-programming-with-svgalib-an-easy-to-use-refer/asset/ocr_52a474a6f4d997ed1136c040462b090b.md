# Resolution

This is your screen’s sharpness, or clarity. The more pixels a monitor displays, the better its resolution. To get a higher pixel count, and subsequently better resolution, you need to instruct your video card to enter a different screen mode.

# Screen Modes

Screen modes are great fun. By changing screen modes, you can change how many pixels are on the screen at one time. Generally, the more pixels you have, the better the quality of your image. There’s a price to pay, however, in that high pixel counts equal heavy memory usage in your video card.

Screen modes work by dividing your viewing area into a grid, or what’s often referred to as a matrix. The matrix can be compared to a Cartesian graph/coordinate system in mathematics, with an x-axis running horizontally, and a y-axis running vertically. The points in a matrix are expressed in standard (x,y) format, with point (0,0) being in the upper left corner, and subsequent points continuing down and to the right.

# Matrix

The grid of horizontal rows and vertical columns that make up a display screen. For example, a matrix of 640×480 means that you have 640 pixels horizontally and 480 pixels vertically.
