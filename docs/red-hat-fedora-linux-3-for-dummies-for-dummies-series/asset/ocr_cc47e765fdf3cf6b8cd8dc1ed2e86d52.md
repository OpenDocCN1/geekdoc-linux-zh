# In this part...

T his part is the area of every book where you find things that just didn't fit into the flow of the chapters: the fun and exciting *appendices*. This book has six of 'em.

Appendix A outlines the Fedora Core systems administration utilities. Appendix B shows how to figure out what stuff your computer is made of. Appendices C and D describe the Linux file system and how to use it. In Appendix E, you find out about RPM (the Red Hat Package Manager) and YUM (the Yellowdog Updater, Modifier). As a final bit of preparation, Appendix F describes the contents of this book's companion DVD.
