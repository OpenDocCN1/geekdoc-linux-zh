# Connecting your Internet gateway

After you configure your Internet gateway to forward network traffic from your private network to your Internet connection (refer to the preceding section), you need to make that connection. In this section, we describe how to use the Internet connections we introduce in Chapters 5 and 6. You build on those instructions to connect your entire network to the Internet through these connections.

![](bbox=[0.1, 0.84, 0.22, 0.92])

From a functional viewpoint, the type of Internet connection you use (telephone, DSL, or cable) doesn’t matter because all these Internet connections send and receive the same network traffic. (Practically speaking, of course, the higher throughput and lower latency of broadband make it more desirable than old telephone modems.) For this reason, we can treat as interchangeable the Internet connections you we describe in Chapters 5 and 6.
