# Introducing the Mighty MPlayer

Whenever a desperate need exists, Superman comes in to . . . er, the open-source movement comes in to save the day. Until recently, you couldn’t use any single Linux application to listen to and view most popular streaming formats. Now, MPlayer has burst on the scene and fills that gap.

MPlayer can play most popular (and many obscure) audio and video streaming formats. Although MPlayer is under intense development — it’s still technically in the beta phase while its development team fine-tunes its features — it’s quite usable, and we think you should consider using it. We do!
