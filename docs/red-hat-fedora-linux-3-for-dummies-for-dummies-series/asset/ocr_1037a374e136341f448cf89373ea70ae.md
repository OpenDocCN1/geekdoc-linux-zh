# Getting to Know OpenOffice

If you're familiar with Microsoft Office, you can find your way around OpenOffice. The look and feel are a little different, but the idea is the same. If you're a wild-eyed power-to-the-people person (who's also thrifty), you may find OpenOffice morally superior to Office because it's free and a part of open source. Or maybe you just want to get down to work. Either way, we briefly describe some of the most common OpenOffice functions here.
