After your computer receives information from someone who uses your program, the next step is to do something with the information. You can, for example, have your computer calculate a result.

Anytime that you want your computer to modify data, you must tell the computer to perform an *operation* by using one or more *operators* in your program. Linux recognizes the following three categories of operators (each with its own individual operators):

+   - Arithmetic
+   - Logical
+   - Comparison

In this chapter, I show you the operators that you can use in Linux and give you a tip or two on how to use them in your program.
