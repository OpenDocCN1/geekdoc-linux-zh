# **In this part . . .**

One of the tricks to writing large, complex programs is to divide the program into smaller programs known as subprograms and, within each subprogram, to group together repeating code into a function. Functions and subprograms perform one particular task of your program. Your job is to make your main program call the function and subprogram whenever you need them. You discover how to use this trick with your own programs in this part.
