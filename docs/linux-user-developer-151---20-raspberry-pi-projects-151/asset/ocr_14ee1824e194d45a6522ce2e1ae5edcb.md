the price – although generally a lot of free software does not tend to cost you money. ElementarOS is still (cost) free, as you have said, and any money spent is a donation to the devs so that they can continue with their work on a great distro that they do sink a lot of time into maintaining – time they could probably be using to get money elsewhere.
If they decided to sell ElementarOS too, then there is nothing stopping them. Licensing-wise, the open source software and such that comprises it is allowed to be sold without any form of repercussions either. That’s why Red Hat and SUSE are allowed to effectively sell their distros to enterprise, although it’s usually part of a service package and such, so not quite equivalent to a desktop product. Windows and OS X have GPL-licensed components, either they have made in-house or gotten from a third party, and they are perfectly legal to use.
Whether you choose to donate or pay is up to you though, and there are many alternatives out there that don’t throw it up in your face anyway.
