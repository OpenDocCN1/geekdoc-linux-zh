# Chapter 5

# Visible Surface Determination II:
Space-partitioning Techniques

This chapter continues the discussion of various VSD algorithms that was started in Chapter 4. Chapter 4 dealt with generally applicable VSD techniques. That chapter deals with space-partitioning techniques, which represent a “divide and conquer” approach to VSD. We partition, or divide, space into various regions, then perform VSD based on the regions. Of course, it is not all quite as simple as it sounds, which is why we take the time to look at these algorithms in detail in this chapter.

This chapter covers:

+   - Binary space partitioning trees
+   - Octrees
+   - Regular spatial partitioning
+   - Portals and cells
+   - Special effects with portals (shadows, mirrors)
+   - Overview of other VSD algorithms.

# Binary Space Partitioning Trees, Octrees, and Regular Spatial Partitioning

We mentioned in Chapter 4 the correctness and visibility aspects of VSD algorithms. The binary space partitioning (BSP) tree and octree algorithms are VSD algorithms which address both the correctness and efficiency issues of VSD.
