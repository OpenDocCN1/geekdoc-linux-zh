# Stack-Based Computation

Fundamentally, Calc operates with a stack of mathematical expressions. The typical flow of operations in Calc is to enter entries on the stack, then to perform operations on the entries on the stack. Some operations, such as negation, operate only on the topmost element of the stack. Other operations, such as addition, operate on the top two elements of the stack, removing these and replacing them with one new element, the result of the operation. This style of operation is also called *reverse Polish notation* or RPN; some desk calculators operate in this mode.
