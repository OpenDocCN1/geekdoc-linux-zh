Cybercriminals are increasingly discovering Linux and adapting malware previously designed for Windows systems. We take you inside the Linux version of a famous Windows ransomware tool.

By Thomas Boele

Since the beginning of the year, security researchers from Check Point Research (CPR) have been investigating the activities of a Chinese cyber espionage threat actor focused on Southeast Asia, Africa, and South America. The toolkit for this threat actor includes the DinodasRAI [1] cross-platform backdoor, also known as XDealer, which was previously observed in attacks by the Chinese group known as LuoYu.
