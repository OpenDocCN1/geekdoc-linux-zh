The data structures in the kernel to support signals in Linux are to be found in the task structure we discussed in Chapter 4 on Linux Processes.

Here are the most common elements of the task structure pertaining to signals:

+   - current->sig are the signal handlers.

+   - sigmask_lock is a per-thread spinlock,which protects the signal queue and atomicity of other signal operations.

+   - current->signal and current->blocked are a (currently 64-bit long but freely expandable) bitmask of pending and permanently blocked signals.

+   - sigqueue and sigqueue_tail are double-linked lists of pending signals. Linux has RT signals that can get queued as well. “Traditional” signals are internally mapped to RT signals.
