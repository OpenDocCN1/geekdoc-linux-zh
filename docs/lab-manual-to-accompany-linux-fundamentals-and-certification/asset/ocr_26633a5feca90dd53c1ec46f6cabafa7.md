## Directories

Create two directories within /home called groupA and groupB. Assign permissions to these directories that allow members of groupA full access to the /home/groupA directory and members of groupB full access to the /home/groupB directory. No other users should be able to read, write, or execute any files within the directories.

Outline the steps needed to accomplish the above tasks. Test that your permission schema works properly. Outline the tasks used to test permissions.
