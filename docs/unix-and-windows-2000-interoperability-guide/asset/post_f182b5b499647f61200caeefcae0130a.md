## 13.4 Conclusion

So, we end with a brief look at other interoperability tools. NAS will play an increasingly important role in mixed environments and will many server-based solutions. Managing X Window can be quite complex, but it offers a way to continue using UNIX-based systems even if you migrate your environment to Windows 2000. Inter-platform printing can take many forms, but the traditional method of attaching printers to a print server of some kind is easily assimilated in mixed environments. However, most sites have ways to print over the network to printers using JetDirect cards, regardless of the operating system in which the job arises.

Thus, we end this book. Certainly, we did not look at every aspect of UNIX and Windows 2000 interoperability in all its details. Such would have produced a large, unwieldy book. However, I think we achieved the objective of providing some practical advice to administrators managing the most common tasks they face in a mixed environment. I hope you found this book useful.
