Windows 2000 commands, like UNIX commands, take options (sometimes called flags or switches). Normally, these commands follow the DOS convention: Options are designated by forward slashes (/). However, the following TCP/IP utilities, reflecting their UNIX roots (for most of the commands, at least), have options designated by the minus sign (-):

+   - arp
- ftp
- finger
- hostname
- lpq
- lpr
- rcp
- reexec
- route
- ipconfig
- nbtstat
- netsh
- netstat
- nslookup
- ping
- rsh
- tftp
- tracert
