Many thanks go to the GNU Emacs and XEmacs development teams for creating and maintaining such versatile tools, as well as to the legions of Lisp hackers who have contributed so many useful extensions to the Emacs community.

# About the Author

Larry Ayers is a free-lance writer and Open Source advocate. He lives on a small farm in northeast Missouri with his family. When not writing, he does computer consulting in his community, builds musical instruments, and operates a small portable sawmill. Larry has written for the *Linux Gazette* and *Linux Journal*.
