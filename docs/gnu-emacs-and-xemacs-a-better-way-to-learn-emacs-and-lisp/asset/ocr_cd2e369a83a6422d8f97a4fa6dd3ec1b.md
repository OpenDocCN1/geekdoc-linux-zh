# Problems with Info

Disorientation can be a drawback when browsing a large Info manual. There is no indication of the manual’s *size* or of the relative size and importance of the constituent sections. It helps to conduct a quick survey of the entire manual before settling down to read sections of interest to you. Make brief exploratory excursions down a manual’s *various byways* so that you can develop a mental map, returning to the Top page often so as to keep your bearings.
