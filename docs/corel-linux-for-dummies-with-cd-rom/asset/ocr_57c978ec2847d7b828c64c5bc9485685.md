# Changing Your Color Scheme

Tired of the same old window colors? You can change the colors of all your window widgets, such as title bars, title text, general background, and window background and text. You can also select coordinated color schemes.

To change your window colors, choose Application Starter ↔ Control Center, double-click Desktop, and then choose Display to open the color settings in Figure 18-5.
