# # How many desktops did you say?

Having multiple desktops is a fabulous feature that alone would be worth the price of Linux, if it weren't free. The default four is a reasonable number of desktops, but you can have anywhere from two to eight:

+   1. Right-click a blank spot in the panel and then choose Configure.
+   2. Click the Desktops tab.
+   3. In the Visible list, choose the number of desktops you want, as shown in Figure 9-24, and then click OK.

# # Renaming your desktops

You may rename your desktops (Desktop 1, Desktop 2, and so on) to anything you choose — although I'm not sure how doing so would affect the quality of your computing life. Right-click a blank spot in the panel and choose Configure. Then click the Desktops tab, type the new names, and then click OK.
