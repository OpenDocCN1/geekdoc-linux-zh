A picture might be worth a thousand words, but can you imagine if we communicated only with pictures? I know about the prehistoric drawings on the walls of caves, but even cave folks spoke, didn't they? It's speech that makes us human, and writing is just scribble to represent the noises we make. If cave folks had writing, I'm sure we'd see “I love you, Muttonbrain” scrawled on the walls or maybe “Meet me at the roasting pit when the moon is in the walnut tree.”

Yes, there's no substitute for writing, so I expect you'll be doing some in Corel Linux.
