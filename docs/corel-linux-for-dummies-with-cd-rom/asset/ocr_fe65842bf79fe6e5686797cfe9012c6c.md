# Changing what happens as you move the mouse

To change what happens as your mouse moves around a bunch of windows, do the following:

+   1. Choose **Application Starter** (to the right of the Application Starter icon) **Control Center**.
+   
+   2. Double-click **Windows** and then choose **Properties**.
