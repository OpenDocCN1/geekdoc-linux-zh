# Throwing a Theme Party

You're invited to a theme party! To attend, you have to dress up your desktops in all kinds of crazy outfits. **Themes** are all-in-one packages with everything from custom colors and wallpapers to borders, icons, sounds, and more. Some theme party outfits are sedate and formal; others are totally wacky and wild!

![](bbox=[0.0, 0.53, 0.15, 0.61])

> Your theme outfits come wrapped in packages — compressed archives containing all the elements for that theme. The archive names are in the form of *Themename.tgz* or *Themename.tar.gz*.

Let's go party!

+   1. **Choose Application Starter Control Center.**
+   2. **Double-click Desktop** and then choose Theme Manager.
+   3. Select various themes in the list to preview them, as shown in Figure 10-12. Then select the one you want to apply.
+   4. Click the Contents tab and choose the various theme parts you want to apply, as shown in Figure 10-13. Click to remove or add a check mark.
+   5. **Click Apply** to wear your new desktop outfit.
