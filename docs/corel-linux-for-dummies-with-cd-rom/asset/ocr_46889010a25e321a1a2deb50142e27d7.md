# Changing Your Screen Fonts

You can change various fonts used in your desktop display, such as the font that appears in your window title bars. Here's how:

+   1. Choose **Application Starter · Control Center**.
+   2. Double-click **Desktop** and then choose **Fonts**.
+      - The settings in Figure 10-9 appear.
+   3. Select the **type of font** you want to change.
+   4. Select the **typeface, size, and other characteristics**.
+   5. Repeat Steps 3 and 4 for other fonts you want to change.
+   6. Click **OK** when you're finished.
