# Autohiding Your Panel and Taskbar

The panel is a fabulous tool, but it does take up quite a bit of your screen’s real estate. Want to have your cake and eat it too? Then autohide your panel to tuck it discreetly out of view. Then, whenever you need it, just point to where the panel is hidden to pop it back into view.
