+       The preview window shows what the scheme looks like.
+   4. In the widget color list, select various widgets to refine their colors.
+       Your selections appear in the preview window.
+   5. If you want to make your color contrasts higher or lower, move the Contrast slider.
+   6. If you want to save your custom colors to a new scheme, click the Add button to give your scheme a name, as shown in Figure 10-8.
+       You can't change or remove the system color schemes, but you can save them as custom schemes that you can then edit or remove.
+   7. Click OK to apply your changes.
