# Using the selection tools

The first six tools in the toolbox let you select and manipulate a specific part of your image. The boundary of your selection becomes a blinking dotted line, affectionately known as marching ants. Anything you do to the selected part of your image doesn’t affect the rest of the image. Click anywhere in the image to turn off the selection.
