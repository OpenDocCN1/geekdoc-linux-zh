# **Putting on the Pixels**

Perhaps no other setting affects your visual comfort more than the screen resolution you employ. Depending on your graphics card, you can adjust your screen resolution anywhere from the minimal 640 x 480 screen pixels to the 1280 x 1024 pixels (shown in Figure 19-5) or higher.

Chapter 10 gives you step-by-step instructions for changing your screen resolution.
