+   5. Select an icon text color — preferably one that contrasts with the background.  
+   6. If you want to show hidden files on your desktop, choose the Show Hidden Files on Desktop option.  
+   Hidden files are files and folders with names beginning with a period (.). These aren’t likely to be on your desktop anyway.  
+   7. Click OK to apply your changes.  
+   You see the results the next time you log in. To apply a new spacing, you’ll have to right-click on the background to arrange your icons.
