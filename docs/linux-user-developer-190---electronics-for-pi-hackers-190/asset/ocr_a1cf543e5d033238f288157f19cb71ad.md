Peter Buczkowski's 'Prosthetic Photographer' looks to the next level. His Prosthetic Photographer uses a digital camera for sensing and when it finds something, it hits you with an electric shock, forcing your body to trigger the camera's shutter and snap the image. Ouch... maybe.

What was the most challenging part of the project? Getting around the whole terminology of machine learning and neural networks, I have only just started learning the subject, but the computational power was also a challenge - retraining the network on a Raspberry Pi as a method, takes a lot of time. One challenge that I had was to create a method to use the project without any electrods - most projects would be impossible on a Raspberry Pi.
