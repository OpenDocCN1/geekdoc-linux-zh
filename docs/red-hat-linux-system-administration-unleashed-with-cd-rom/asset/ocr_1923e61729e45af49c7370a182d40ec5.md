# Caution

Always exclude the address that your mail transport agent uses when bouncing mail. This is usually mailer-daemon@your.domain.com or something similar. Failure to do this can result in mail loops between WREQ and sendmail.

The next two items to be configured are the distribution list for requests and the list of people who are able to work issues entered into WREQ (see Figure 30.5). The distribution list is usually an alias that includes members of your support staff and possibly others who are interested in seeing the number and types of requests that are going to your help desk.
