# The Verdict

Software whiteboards

When it’s your turn for taking notes, you typically find yourself at the table with a stack of paper and a pad of markers, or hunched over a notebook. Even with today’s technology, that’s not much of a change from the way we started doing decades ago, and that should prompt an important question: What if we stopped going in that direction? Instead of ever better tools to capture, share, and revise the notes we’ve written on our devices, what if we started focusing on whiteboards that allow us to write directly on a screen? Not just any screen—think of a large interactive display that’s wall-mounted and can be written on using special markers or a stylus. That’s the kind of environment where a team can gather and work together in the same room, but also where a student or instructor or doctor can write or draw on a large screen. It’s a powerful concept that has already been put into practice in a number of ways, from classroom setups to hospital rooms to boardrooms. In fact, there are several software applications that turn regular whiteboards into interactive ones, and there are dozens of software-only solutions that allow you to do the same things on a regular computer or tablet.
