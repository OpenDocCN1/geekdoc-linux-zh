# **ISSUE 299**
Aug 2021
Product No
**LXFD299**

In the magazine
Linux has been running under the hood of your PC, laptop or smartphone for quite some time now. It continues to evolve, taking developers on a tour of the most innovative technologies and the most useful tools. In this issue we explain why the two main families of distributions are hard-wired, and advise on the best practice in terms of the tools and techniques that will help you to make the most of them. This time with no major kernel changes, and no new releases either, it’s been a quiet month for the world of GNU/Linux. It’s also one of those months where we can take a break and just enjoy a few good stories, and take a deep breath before the storm of v5.3.

# **ISSUE 290**

Product No
**LXFD290**

In the magazine
With the return of Zynaptic in the 1.10 stable release, the Linux world has entered a new phase. We look at the key features of the GIMP editor, and the new versions of Firefox.
