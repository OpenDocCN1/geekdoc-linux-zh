# ## HACK YOUR
## GRAPHICS

Join pixel-cyzed **Joni Bidwell** for a guided tour across the world of graphics on Linux.

![](bbox=[0.3, 0.2, 0.95, 0.85])

+   Graphics has come a long way since the 1980s and with the rapid improvement in hardware and software in recent years there’s no doubt that Linux can compete with the best. It’s time to explore what’s available to you on your desktop or laptop. In this issue, Joni Bidwell is going to take you through the world of Linux graphics. Starting with the basics of how the system draws pixels to your screen and ending with tips for creating your own graphics, you’re going to get the lowdown on everything you need to be an expert.
