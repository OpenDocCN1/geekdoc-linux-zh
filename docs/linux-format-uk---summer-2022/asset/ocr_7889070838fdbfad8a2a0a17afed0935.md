# New world order!

It can be said today that Linux users are making quite a comeback. For the first time since its inception in 1991, it seems the world is finally warming up to it. The reason is simple: Linux is more secure and stable than its competitors. And the system that powers most of the web is finally getting the respect it deserves. While the Microsoft ecosystem still holds the lion’s share of the desktop market, Linux is gaining ground in data centres, mobile, and the cloud. The rise of ARM-based devices, such as the Raspberry Pi and various smartphones, is also playing a major role in the Linux renaissance.

And it’s not just about the server or mobile markets. There’s a growing movement of Linux users in the desktop space, with distros like Ubuntu, Fedora, and Arch gaining popularity among developers and enthusiasts. The open-source community is thriving, with new projects and innovations emerging all the time. From AI to the metaverse, Linux is at the heart of many of the most exciting technological advancements.

Meanwhile, on the enterprise front, Linux has become the go-to operating system for cloud computing and big data analytics. Companies like Google, Amazon, and Facebook all rely on Linux to power their infrastructure. And with the rise of containerisation and microservices, Linux is becoming more important than ever. Whether you’re a developer, sysadmin, or just a tech enthusiast, now is a good time to be a Linux user.

Speaking of the future, we can expect to see even more innovation in the Linux world. From quantum computing to the Internet of Things (IoT), Linux will play a key role in shaping the next generation of technology. So whether you’re a seasoned Linux user or just starting out, there’s never been a better time to dive into the world of open-source computing.
