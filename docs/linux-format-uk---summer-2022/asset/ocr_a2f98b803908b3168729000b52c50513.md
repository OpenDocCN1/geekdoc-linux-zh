W HILE MOST of the big-name distros like Debian, Fedora, Ubuntu or OpenSUSE may seem a bit similar at first, if you look closely you will find that there is a huge divide between them. For instance, Debian is not just Debian. There are other Debian-based distros out there, including those that ship with their own unique software and their own unique looks. In fact, there are quite a few of these Debian-based distros out there, including Mint, Xubuntu, Kubuntu and the like. However, one that stands out is MakuluLinux, which is based on Debian and comes with its own unique look and feel.

But it\'s not just about looks. MakuluLinux also comes with its own unique software, including a package manager called \`mak\` and a unique terminal called \`mshell\`. These are just a few of the many unique features that MakuluLinux has to offer.

So, if you\'re looking for a Debian-based distro that has its own unique look and feel, MakuluLinux is definitely worth a try. And if you\'re looking for a distro that comes with its own unique software, MakuluLinux is the way to go.

If you are familiar with a typical GUI desktop, you will be at home with MakuluLinux as the menu system looks and works just like that of other popular desktop environments such as Gnome or KDE. It uses the lightweight Openbox window manager, which is the same as used by the popular Linux Mint Xfce edition.

Makulu comes with over 350 packages to the latest version of 860,000. This is a huge number and means that the distribution comes with the latest versions of software, including Firefox, LibreOffice and GIMP, all installed and ready to use.

The distro itself is very easy to use and is a good choice for those who are new to Linux. It comes with a graphical installer and is very easy to install. The only thing you need to do is to choose a language and a keyboard layout and then follow the on-screen instructions.
