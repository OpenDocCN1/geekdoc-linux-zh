# Multimedia Applications

Fedora Core includes quite few multimedia applications — mostly multimedia audio players and CD players, but also applications for digital cameras and burning CD-ROMs. To play some other multimedia files (such as MPEG video), you have to download and install additional software in your Fedora Core system. Here's a sketch of a few typical multimedia tasks and the applications you can use to perform these tasks:

+   - **Using digital cameras:** Use the Digital Camera tool to download photos from your digital cameras in Fedora Core.
- **Playing audio CDs:** Use one of many audio CDs that can play with Fedora Core.
- **Playing sounds files:** Use Rhythmbox or XMMS, multimedia audio players (you have to download some additional software to play MP3 files with Rhythmbox or XMMS). You can also download other players from the Internet.
- **Burning a CD:** Use X-CDast to roast your CDs.

# Using a digital camera

Fedora comes with gtcam — a digital-camera application you can use to download photos from digital cameras. gtcam is a GUI front end of the gPhoto2 command-line application that works with many different makes and models of digital cameras.
