# About the Author

Naba Barkakati is an electrical engineer and a successful computer-book author who has experience in a wide variety of systems, ranging from MS-DOS and Windows to Linux. He bought his first personal computer — an IBM PC — in 1984 after graduating with a PhD in electrical engineering from the University of Maryland. While pursuing a full-time career in the emerging software market, he dreamed of writing instead of building a software empire like Microsoft. Over the past 15 years, Naba has written over 25 computer books on a number of topics ranging from programming with C++ to several bestselling titles, such as Turbo C++ Bible, Object-Oriented Programming in C++, Visual Studio++ Developer's Guide. His books have been translated into many languages, including Spanish, French, Polish, Italian, Chinese, Japanese, and Korean. Naba's most recent book is Linux Secrets, also published by Wiley. Naba lives in North Potomac, Maryland, with his wife, Leha, and their children.
