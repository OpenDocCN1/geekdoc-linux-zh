DSL and cable-modem service connect you to the Internet and also act as
your Internet Service Provider (ISP); in addition to improved speed, what
y're paying for is an IP address and your e-mail accounts. If you use a dial-
 modem to connect to the Internet, first you have to connect to the phone
line (for which you pay the phone company) and then select and pay a separa-
ISP — which gives you a phone number to dial and all the other neces-
sary goods (such as an IP address and e-mail accounts).
