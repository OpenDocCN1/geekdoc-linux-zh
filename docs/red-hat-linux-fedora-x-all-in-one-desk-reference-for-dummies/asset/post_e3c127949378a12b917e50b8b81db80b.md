
# Using Xianian Evolution 343

| Table 1-2 Shortcut Icons in Xianian Evolution |
| Name of Icon | What It Does |
| Mail | Switches to mail display where you can read and send mail. |
| Calendars | Opens your calendar where you can look up and add appointments. |
| Tasks | Shows your "to do" list where you can add new tasks and check what's due. |
| Contacts | Opens your contact list where you can add new contacts or look up someone from your current list. |

As the icons listed in Table 1-2 show, Xianian Evolution has all the necessary components of a PIM — e-mail, calendar, task list, and contacts.

Above the shortcut icons, Evolution displays folders that can be open by clicking. To read e-mail, click the Mail icon and then click the arrow to On This Computer. You see a number of folders containing mail messages.

To access your e-mail, click the Inbox icon. Evolution opens your Inbox as shown in Figure 1-4). If you turn the feature to automatically check for your mail every so, Evolution already prompted you for your mail password and downloaded your mail. The e-mail Inbox looks much like any other reader's inbox, such as the Outlook Inbox.

# Using Mozilla Mail

Mozilla Mail is the mail and newsreader that comes with Mozilla — Netscape Communicator. Mozilla is the open source successor to Netscape Web browser that also includes a mail and newsreader.

# Using Mozilla Mail 345

To use Mozilla Mail, start by running the Mozilla Web browser. Click the Mozilla icon (the earth lassoed by the mouse cord) on the panel, and the Mozilla Web window opens. Access to the Mozilla Mail and newsreader, choose Window>Mail and Newsgroups from the menu. Mozilla Mail runs, starts the Account Wizard (shown in Figure 1-6), and prompts you for information about your e-account.

Figure 1-6:
Provide your e-mail account information to Mozilla's Account Wizard.

![](img/d9230b719dc0efd80851f363d8c6f571_371_0.png)

Select the Email Account radio button and click Next. The Account Wizard takes you through the following steps:

- 1. Enter your identity information — your name and your full e-mail address as Joe@someplace.com — and then click Next.
- 2. Provide information about your ISP's mail server — the protocol type (POP or IMAP) as well as the incoming and outgoing server names — and click Next.
The incoming server is the POP or IMAP server, whereas the outgoing server is the one through which you send mail out (it's the SMTP server).
- 3. Enter the username that your ISP has given you; click Next.
- 4. Enter a name that you want to use to identify this account and click Next.
This name is just for Mozilla Mail, so you can pick anything you want such as "My home account."
The Account Wizard then displays a summary of the information entered.

# 5. Verify the information; if it's correct, click Finish. Otherwise click Back and fix the errors.

After you select the e-mail account, Mozilla Mail's main window appears and shows you the contents of your Inbox. A dialog box pops up and asks for your e-mail password. Mozilla Mail needs your password to download-your e-mail messages from your ISP. Enter your password and click OK.

Mozilla Mail downloads your messages and displays them in a familiar format. To read a message, click that message, and the full text appear in the lower window (as shown in Figure 1-7).

Figure 1-7: You can read and send e-mail messages and a whole lot more from Mozilla Mail.
