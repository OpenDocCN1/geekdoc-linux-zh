True, linux is monolithic, and I agree that micro-kernels are nicer. With a less argumentative subject, I’d probably have agreed with most of what you said. If I had made an OS that problems with a multithreading file system, I wouldn’t be so fast to condemn others: in fact, I’d do my damnedest to make others forget about the fiasco.
