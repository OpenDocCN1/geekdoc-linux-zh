# Metal Blob Solid

Metal Blob Solid, or "Blob Wars" as it is affectionately known by its aficionados, is an impressive original Linux arcade game. You install Metal Blob Solid by installing the blobwars package using your favorite package management software. After installing this package, an entry for it appears on the Games menu on Ubuntu systems.

After starting the game, you are the character Bob, a Blob freedom fighter who is fighting to free his Blob home world from aliens who have invaded it to assimilate the population and find four mysterious Crystals—Fire, Time, Space, and Reality (which is, coincidentally, the title of a song by the Moody Blues). When playing the game, your primary jobs are to rescue imprisoned compatriots, shoot aliens, and pick up treasures along the way. Figure 17-16 shows a game of Metal Blob Solid in progress.
