If you are happy with the new resolution, click "Keep Resolution." The Dialog will close, your screen will remain at the new resolution, and it will be the default screen resolution used-ever you log in to your Ubuntu system.

If the new resolution looks odd or is simply too small for your taste, click "Use Previous Resolution." You can then experiment with other resolutions, clicking Apply to test out any newly selected resolution until you find that you are happy with.
