# Setting System Sound Levels

The section of Chapter 5, “Using the GNOME Desktop,” entitled “Customizing Panel Contents” discusses how to add various apples to your GNOME desktop's panels. One of the more useful apples is preinstalled for you in the upper panel of your Ubuntu desktop is the Volume Control applet, which looks like a smaller speaker and is usually displayed in the notification area in the right portion of the top panel.

To customize your system's volume settings for the current session, right-click on this icon. slider displays on your desktop, below the icon for the Volume Control applet. To change your current volume settings, drag the slider up or down to the value that you prefer.
