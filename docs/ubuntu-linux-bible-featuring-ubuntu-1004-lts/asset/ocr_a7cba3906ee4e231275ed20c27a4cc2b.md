# Sample GIMP Tasks

As I acknowledged earlier, I am not a graphics artist. Many people who are serious artists have created extensive tutorials on using GIMP, and I'd be insulting them (and you) by either parroting those or by trying to pretend that you have major graphics skills. That said, I do GIMP regularly for a variety of common tasks that you may find useful, so I'll discuss some of those in the next sections. For tutorials on using GIMP to do serious graphics, see some of the URLs in the section “More Information about GIMP,” later in this chapter.
