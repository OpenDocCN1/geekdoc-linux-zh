Many users who are new to Ubuntu but are already familiar with the xterm application find that they prefer xterm GNOME Terminal. For complete details on all of the capabilities of the xterm application, see its man page—perhaps by starting xterm and executing the man xterm command in that.

As a generic Window System application rather than a GNOME application, you'll note in Figure 11-3 that an xterm window does not provide a menu or toolbar to provide easy access to configuration commands. Instead, you display xterm's configuration menus by holding down the Ctrl key on your keyboard and clicking the left, middle, or right mouse buttons on the background of the xterm window. Figure 11-5 shows the menu that you see when holding down the Ctrl key and left-clicking the background of an xterm window.
