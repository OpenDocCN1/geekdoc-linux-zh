# Other Office Software: Spreadsheets and Presentations

The previous chapter discussed how to create documents on your Ubuntu systems. Along with surfing the Web and sending receiving e-mail, writing letters, keeping To-Do lists, and working on your novel are probably the most common personal tasks that you'll find yourself doing on your new machine. Word processing is also a fairly critical business task because, although most companies live on e-mail at present, there's always the need for the occasional memo document, and so on.

This chapter discusses the commonly used and office-related software that are available for your Ubuntu Linux system, namely, spreadsheets and presentation software. While few people actually need to give presentations on their home computer or to their family (maybe “Next Month's Budget—What It Means to You”), spreadsheets are a crossover task that is often as important to home workers as it is to office workers. If you are working from home or using Ubuntu on a laptop for business reasons, you may need to create both spreadsheets and presentations, and Ubuntu makes this easy (well, as easy as it is on any computer system). Linux Desktop, OEM, or Text-Mode installations automatically install the OpenOffice Calc spreadsheet application as part of the OpenOffice.org suite, and the OpenOffice.org Impress presentation as part of the OpenOffice suite.
