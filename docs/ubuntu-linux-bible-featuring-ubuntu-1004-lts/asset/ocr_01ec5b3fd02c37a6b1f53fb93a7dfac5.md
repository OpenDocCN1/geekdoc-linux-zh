# Part I: Getting Started with Ubuntu

+   - case_howard_county_library.pdf: A case study of the adoption of Ubuntu by the Howard County Library
- case_KRUU.pdf: A case study of the adoption of Ubuntu by KRUU, a non-profit radio station
- case_oaklandUniversity.pdf: A case study of the adoption of Ubuntu by the University of Oakland
- case_oxford_archaeology.pdf: A case study of the adoption of Ubuntu by Oxford Archaeology, an English excavation and heritage management firm
- case_Skegness.pdf: A case study of the adoption of Ubuntu by the Skegness Grammar School
- case_ubuntu_johnhopkins_v2.pdf: A case study of the adoption of Ubuntu by Johns Hopkins University
- case_ubuntu_locatrix_v1.pdf: A case study of the adoption of Ubuntu by Locatrix Communications
- case_wellcome.pdf: A case study of the adoption of Ubuntu by the Wellcome Trust Sanger Institute
- fades_01_01_aesop.spx: A sample Speex audio file (an open source audio compression format) in Ogg container format. Double-clicking this item opens and plays it using the Totem video player.
- gimp-ubuntu-splash.xcf: A sample Ubuntu splash screen in the native source XCF format formed by the GIMP imagination program. Double-clicking this it in the GIMP graphics editor.
- kubuntu-leaflet.png: A sample Ubuntu document in the Portable Network Graphics (PNG) format. Double-clicking this item opens it in the GIMP graphics editor.
- logos: A folder containing sample Ubuntu and Kubuntu logos in PNG format. Double-clicking either of these items opens it in the GIMP graphics editor.
- 00-about-these-files.odt: A sample OpenOffice.org document that provides information about the files in the Examples folder. Double-clicking this item opens it in OpenOffice Writer.
- 00-about-ubuntu.rtf: A sample Russian document in Microsoft's Rich Text Format (RTF) markup. Double-clicking this item opens it in OpenOffice.
- 00-derivatives.doc: A sample document in Microsoft Office/Word document format that discusses derivatives of the Ubuntu distribution, as Edubuntu, Kubuntu, and Xubuntu. Double-clicking this opens it in OpenOffice Writer.
- 00-maxwell.odt: A sample OpenOffice.org document that shows the mathematical capabilities of OpenOffice.org Writer by discussing Maxwell's equations. Double-clicking this item opens it in OpenOffice Writer.
- 00-payment-schedule.ods: A sample OpenOffice.org spreadsheet that shows calculations and other capabilities. Double-clicking this item opens it in OpenOffice Calc.
