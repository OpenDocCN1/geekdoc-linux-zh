The panel on the left provides a hierarchical overview of how your bookmarks are currently organized, while the panel on the right provides a detailed view of the bookmarks in the folder that is currently selected. You can move bookmarks around by dragging and dropping them (left-clicking on the bookmark and dragging it to the new location) within either panel or even between the two. You can create new folders, new bookmarks, or new separators (the lines that appear in your bookmarks menu) by selecting the appropriate location in either panel and doing either of the:

+   - Select the appropriate icon from the top of the Library dialog.
- Right-click and select the appropriate command from the pop-up menu.

Any changes that you make are immediately reflected on the Firefox Bookmarks menu and Bookmark toolbar.
