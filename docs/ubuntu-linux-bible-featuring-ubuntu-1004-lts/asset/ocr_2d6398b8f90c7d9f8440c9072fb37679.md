# Introduction

# Tip

Tips generally are used to provide information that can make your work easier—special shortcuts or methods for doing something more easily than the norm. ■

# Note

Notes provide additional, ancillary information that is helpful, but somewhat outside of the current presentation of information. ■

# Caution

This information is important and is set off in a separate paragraph with a special icon. Cautions provide information about things to watch out for, whether they are simply inconvenient or potentially hazardous to your data or systems. ■
