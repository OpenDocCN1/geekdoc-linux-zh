Once you've entered whatever information you want to record about this contact, click OK to close the "Contact Information" dialog and return to the general Contacts view. Once you've defined one or more contacts, the Contacts view will look like Figure 7-28, with summary information about the currently selected contact displayed in the bottom half of the Contacts pane.

To see this summary information about any defined contact, select that contact from the Contacts list pane (above the summary information). You can scroll to right through this list to locate any existing contact. Once you've accumulated a large number of contacts, you may find it convenient to search for a contact by entering a portion of their name in the Search area above the contacts list and can also click the icon on the left of the Search box to search by e-mail address or by the contents of any field in the contact information.

To edit or view detailed information about any existing contact, double-click on the entry for contact to re-display the dialog, which enables you to correct or add information about that contact. If you were just checking information, click Cancel to close this dialog. If you have made changes, click OK to save the updated information.
