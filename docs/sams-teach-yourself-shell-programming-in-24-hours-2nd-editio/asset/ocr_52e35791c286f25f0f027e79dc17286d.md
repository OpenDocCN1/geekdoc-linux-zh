# Terms

**Anchoring** Anchoring a regular expression limits matches to lines that begin or end with the expression.

**Escaping** Preceding a meta-character with a \ is called escaping. An escaped meta-character is treated literally.

**Meta-Characters** Meta-characters are characters that have a special meaning inside a regular expression; they are expanded to match ordinary characters.

**Regular Expression** A regular expression is compact notation for describing sets of strings.
