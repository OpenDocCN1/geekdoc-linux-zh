The second exam is the A+ certification 220/902\. It contains the configuration and installation of different operating systems such as iOS, Windows, Android, Linux, and Apple OS x. Become acquainted with the objectives of Comp TIA A+ examination

As one awaits the CompTIA examination, it is important to be adequately prepared. This can only be achieved by going through as many practice questions as one can. To do that, use all available resources. As this is for preparation purposes, ensure that you apply the following:

*   It is important to note that you should not cram a specific answer to any question. Strive to understand the concept. Cramming is bad practice as in the exam room, if the question is twisted, you will get jittery and flunk the whole exam.

*   Read and understand each question, understand what each question entails as this will help you derive the best answer.

*   Attempt the questions but if you feel like you are a bit uncertain about your answers, you can always use secondary means to affirm your answers – use the internet (Google if you may, Bing if you can, or use yahoo answers)

*   After you have settled on an answer, ask yourself why you think it is the best response paying attention to the question at hand.

*   As the question has choices, eliminate those that look more far-fetched and inapplicable (least probable response) and by doing so you will have raised the probability of landing on the correct answer.

*   As this is just preparation for the exam, don’t just focus on the answer itself but do some extra reading on the areas around your solution. Don’t exclude the other choices in this, do greater research on them because the whole point is to gain more knowledge in readiness for your exam.

*   As you continue studying, identify your weak areas and work on them. Where possible, use mnemonics, draw tables and flow charts to aid in memorizing some of the harder concepts e.g. various ports, speeds etc.

*   Be dedicated. Dedication is key because it gives you the drive to study. Studying can be a tough job.

*   Find yourself a mentor. Find someone who is experienced to take you through the challenging bits in the course. The mentor can be an expert in the field. Some of the information may not be clear or may be vaguely articulated in a tutorial. However, if you find someone to explain the more difficult concepts, you will be in a better position to comprehend.

*   If you can execute a task practically, don’t hesitate to perform it because it will be easier to memorize the concept.

*   Set up a study timetable. Study when your mind is fresher. Stick to the study plan and you are good to go.

*   Study from different sources. As they say, don’t put all your eggs in one basket. Don’t rely on one source. Sometimes the same information is brought out in a different way by a different author which makes more sense to you.

*   Jolt down new concepts as you learn, this will help in retention of the concepts.

*   Do as many CompTIA A+ Practice Tests as possible. Don’t neglect a question, saying it’s too hard to be tested, these are the kind of questions that will catch you off guard. If you encounter a tough question, seek help. You can “Google” the concept or ask your friend who may have understood the concept being tested. Doing many practice questions helps you build your confidence and prepares you for the examination.

The type of questions one may encounter

There are three types of questions one will encounter:

1.  Application questions – these test on application of concepts learnt in situations that need a specific response.

1.  Multi-choice single answer questions – these are questions that require you to choose one correct answer from a bunch of choices.

1.  Multi-choice multiple answer – these are questions that require you to supply more than one correct answer among the choices. In case of such a question, if one chooses just one answer, the system will prompt the user to supply more than one answer.

In these questions, one is free to mark and leave a question for later in case the question is difficult. You may attempt these marked questions once you are done with the easier questions before time elapses.

Note that when you are given a scenario, make sure you answer relates to the scenario.

For example:

Osorio and sons’ company were experiencing numerous computer breakdowns. They are located in a dusty and hot area. What could be the likely causes of their computer breakdowns?

1.  Poor cooling systems.
2.  Moisture on the computer parts
3.  Dust clogs on the CPU vents
4.  Poor electrical connections

These could all be probable causes for the computer breakdowns but considering the question we can only have two possible answers A and C.

B and D are far-fetched hence eliminated with ease. Therefore, through eliminating the answers that are off, we end up with a 50% chance of finding the correct answer. As it is a multi-answer question, A and C will suit perfectly.
