# Virtual Team Services

On the team home page, you can access the virtual team services, as shown in the following:

![](bbox=[0.41, 0.18, 0.9, 0.43])

The following are services that are available only in a virtual team setting:

+   - **Discussion.** This service lets users in a team participate in online discussions and exchange messages.
+   - **Calendar.** This service lets you schedule events and appointments and share this information with team members.
