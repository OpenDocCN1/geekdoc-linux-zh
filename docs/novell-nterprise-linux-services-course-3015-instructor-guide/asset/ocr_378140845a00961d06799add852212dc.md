# Approve or Deny a Membership Request

Virtual Office notifies you when you are invited to join a virtual team. Unless you are assigned to the team, you have the option to accept or reject the invitation.

To accept or reject a membership request, do the following:

+   1. On the **Virtual Office** main page, open your team.

+   2. In the Team Membership column, select **Approve a Request**.
