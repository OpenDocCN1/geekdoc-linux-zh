# **1: How do you make decisions?**

**Answer:**

This is a great opportunity for you to wow your interviewer with your decisiveness, confidence, and organizational skills.

Make sure that you outline a process for decision-making, and that you stress the importance of weighing your options, as well as in trusting intuition. If you answer this question skillfully and with ease, your interviewer will trust in your capability as a worker.

# **2: What are the most difficult decisions for you to make?**

**Answer:**

Explain your relationship to decision-making, and a general synopsis of the process you take in making choices. If there is a particular type of decision that you often struggle with, such as those that involve other people, make sure to explain why that type of decision is tough for you, and how you are currently engaged in improving your skills.
