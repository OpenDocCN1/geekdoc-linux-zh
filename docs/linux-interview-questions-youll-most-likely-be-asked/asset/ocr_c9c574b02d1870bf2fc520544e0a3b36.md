# **24: Tell me about a time when an unforeseen problem arose. How did you handle it?**

**Answer:**

It's important that you are resourceful, and level-headed under pressure. An interviewer wants to see that you handle problems systematically, and that you can deal with change in an orderly process. Outline the situation clearly, including all solutions and results of the process you implemented.

# **25: Can you give me an example of a time when you were able to improve X objective at your previous job?**

**Answer:**

It's important here to focus on an improvement you made that created tangible results for your company. Increasing efficiency is certainly a very important element in business, but employers are also looking for concrete results such as increased sales or cut expenses.
