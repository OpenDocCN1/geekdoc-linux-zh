# Achieving compliance

As explained earlier, achieving compliance is a very complex task that may require a considerable investment of time and resources. However, there are some clear incentives that you can provide to senior management to justify an investment in compliance, as illustrated in the following:

+   * Avoid fines and penalties (including a temporary close of the business)

+   * Increase the markets in which your company can do businesses

+   * Reduce Cybersecurity risks

+   * Being compliant with regulations is a good selling point to attract new customers

+   * Increase the reputation of the company

+   * Improve the handling of customer data

+   * Increase the company presence on new markets
