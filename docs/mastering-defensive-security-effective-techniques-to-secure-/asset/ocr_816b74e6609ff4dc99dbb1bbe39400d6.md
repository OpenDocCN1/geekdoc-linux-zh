# Who creates the BIA?

The creation of the BIA is a joint effort in which each leader of the department or unit is in charge of assessing their organization and creates the BIA. However, there should be someone leading and coordinating the creation of them, as well as providing guidance and support in terms of how to create it. Depending on the company structure, normally, that task relies on the risk manager or the BCP manager. However, if this role is not present in your organization, I suggest you take over this role because, ultimately, you will be the one to benefit from its creation.
