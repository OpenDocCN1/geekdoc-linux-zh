As seen in the preceding screenshot, WSUS is a built-in server role. An interesting fact is that you can have as many WSUS servers as you want in your infrastructure, but at least one needs to connect to Microsoft Update to get available update information (this is called the upstream server).

> Tip
Keep in mind there are many other alternative patch management tools provided by third parties, such as SolarWinds Patch Manager, AutoPatcher, Kaseya VSA, ManageEngine Patch Connect Plus, Ivanti PatchLink, and more.
