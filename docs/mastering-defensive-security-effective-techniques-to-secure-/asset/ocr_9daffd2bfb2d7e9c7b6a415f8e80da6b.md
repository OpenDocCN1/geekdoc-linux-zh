# Design and management of cybersecurity policies and procedures

Cybersecurity policies are not just about creating a document based on a template and storing it forever in an unknown place.

In fact, cybersecurity policies and procedures are one of the most important assets you will have as they represent the baseline of the cybersecurity of your organization. Therefore, you must understand how to properly develop and manage those policies and procedures to ensure your defensive security strategy has a strong baseline.
