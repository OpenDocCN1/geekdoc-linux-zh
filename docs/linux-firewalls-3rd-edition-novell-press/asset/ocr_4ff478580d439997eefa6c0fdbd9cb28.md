# Praise for earlier editions of *Linux Firewalls*

![](bbox=[0.75, 0.03, 0.95, 0.21])

“The author’s approach was to start with the basic and then work up to the more advanced topics. The book contains everything you need to know to get a firewall up and running. This is a great approach for anyone regardless of his or her experience level.”

—Randy Flood, founder of RHCE2B.com

“In terms of content, this book is the most complete single reference I’ve used. This book covers ssh, makes recommendations for what should and should not be running on the firewall, shows how to use an intrusion detection system, and explains how to react to incidents. Nice and well rounded.”

—Ray Rallison,  
Research Assistant, Utah State University Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering;  
Sys Admin, USU Free Software and GNU/Linux Club

“This book provides detailed and practical coverage of iptables, rule optimization, and debugging.”

—Abhijit Menon-Sen, webmaster, linux.wiw.org

“*Linux Firewalls* is a great and current reference manual for setting up and administering firewalls. This is very important due to the increased concern and rise in need for secure networks. The author’s approach is great because he doesn’t dive in with a hard core approach to the topics, yet it isn’t a beginner’s book. This hits the middle of the road readers who will be the biggest users of a book like this.”

—William C. Mounts, Senior Linux Administrator, Caterpillar, Inc.
