# VI. Some remarks and suggestions on the new edition of the Corpus

The editors of the new edition have given us two very excellent volumes. The quality of the text and of the apparatus has been very carefully and conscientiously done and much effort and attention has been put into making the critical apparatus as helpful as possible. However, I would like to ask the editors of the new edition a few questions concerning the text itself, the apparatus and a few points concerning the layout of the new Corpus.

I. In my opinion, the apparatus should be much clearer and more complete. In the first place, it is very difficult to determine which reading belongs to which MS and whether a note has been given in the critical apparatus or not. In many cases a simple note has been given but in others it is not clear if it refers to the reading of the MS or to a previous edition.

II. In my opinion, the new edition of the Corpus should also contain an Index of Names and a Concordance of Proper Names. It would also be very useful to have an Index of Subjects and a Register of Words. Such an index would be a great help for all readers of the Corpus and it would also be a great help for the preparation of a critical apparatus.

III. I am sure that the editors of the new edition will give us a very good edition of the Corpus and that it will be a great help for all scholars working on the Greek novels. I am looking forward to the third volume of the new edition of the Corpus and I hope that it will be published soon.
