**Once we have successfully exploited the target system, like we did in Chapter 9, our job has just begun! We didn’t exploit the**

system just to get inside and send a greeting. We exploited the system for a purpose. That purpose is often called post exploitation in the hacking/penetration testing world. In the nonpenetration testing world,it’s called “getting the goodies.”
