In this case, we will be using OWASP ZAP to test the vulnerabilities of an online website designed to be attacked, webscantest.com. Enter the URL of the website where it indicates “URL to attack.” Then click the **Attack** below.

It will now begin its analysis of the website. First it will spider the site and then begin an active scan looking for vulnerabilities. This can take awhile. For very large websites, this may takes hours, so sit back and relax.
