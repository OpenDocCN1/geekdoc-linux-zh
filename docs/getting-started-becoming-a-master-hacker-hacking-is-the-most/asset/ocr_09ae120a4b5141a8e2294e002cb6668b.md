If you get an error when you’re installing Kali into your VirtualBox, it’s likely because you don’t have virtualization enabled within your systems BIOS. Each system and its BIOS will be slightly different, so check with your manufacturer or search online for solutions with your system and BIOS. In addition, on Windows systems, you will likely need to disable any competing virtualization software such as Hyper-V.

Again, an internet search for your system should guide you in doing so.
