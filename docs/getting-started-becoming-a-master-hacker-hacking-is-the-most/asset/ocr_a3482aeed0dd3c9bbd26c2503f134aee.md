**BeEF or Browser Exploitation Framework The Browser Exploitation Framework or BeEF, for short, enables you to take control of the target’s browser. It relies upon your ability to get the target to click on a malicious Javascript link, and once they do, you are inside their browser! Once inside, there is considerable mischief you can do, including: 1. Resetting or DoSing their router; 2. Social engineering them to give up their passwords; 3. Send their browser to other malicious web sites; 4. Social Engineer them to give you access to their webcam.**
