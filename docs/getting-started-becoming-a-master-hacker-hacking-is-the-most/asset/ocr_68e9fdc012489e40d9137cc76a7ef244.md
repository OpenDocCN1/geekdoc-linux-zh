Although Metasploit is a very powerful exploitation framework, just a **few keywords** can get you started hacking.

Let's take a look at some of those keyword commands.

The "use" command loads a module. So, for instance, if I wanted to load an exploit that took advantage of a specific vulnerability in Adobe Flash, I might “use”

**the exploit/windows/browser/adobe_flash_avm2** module (this is an exploit that takes advantage of one of the many vulnerabilities in the Adobe Flash plug-in).
