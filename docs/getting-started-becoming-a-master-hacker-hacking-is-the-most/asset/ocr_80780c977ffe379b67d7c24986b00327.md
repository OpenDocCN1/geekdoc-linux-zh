# Case Study of the NSA's EternalBlue

To demonstrate key principles throughout this book, I have focused upon a case study of the

NSA's EternalBlue exploit. This two year old exploit was stolen from the US espionage agency

in 2017 and it was responsible for wreaking havoc around the world. It was integrated into many

attacks including most famously the WannaCry, Petya, and NotPetya ransomware. In this book,

I use it as a case study in vulnerability assessment in Chapter 7, exploitation in Chapter 9,

sniffing for exploit analysis in Chapter 10, and Python in Chapter 16. I hope you find this

approach informative and enlightening.
