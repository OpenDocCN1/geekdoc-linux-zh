# Passwords are still the most common form of authentication used in our digital world. We use

passwords to log into our computers, our domain, our bank account, our Facebook account, and maybe even our phone. In recent years, security administrators have made a big effort to get people to use longer and more complex passwords. This makes password cracking more time consuming, but it can still be the hacker’s best entry point to an account or network.
