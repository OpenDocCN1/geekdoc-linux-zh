If the attacker can get the target to connect to their Evil Twin AP, then all the traffic will traverse the attacker’s computer. This enables the attacker to eavesdrop (listen) to the target’s traffic and even alter the messages.

Build our Evil Twin Let’s start building our Evil Twin. To do so, we need another tool from the aircrack-ng suite, airebase-ng. It converts our Wi-Fi adapter into an AP, broadcasting, and accepting client connections. We will also need two network interfaces. Here, I will be using my Alfa card as an AP and Ethernet connection (eth0) to connect to the Internet.
