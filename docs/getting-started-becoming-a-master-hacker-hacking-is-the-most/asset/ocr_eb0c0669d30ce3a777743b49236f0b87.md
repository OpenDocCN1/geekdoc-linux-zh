vulnerable to our attacks.

What we do NOT know include: (1) What UDP ports are running; (2) What operating system is running; (3) What actual services and versions are running on those ports.

Generally, UDP scans take much longer than TCP scans, as the mechanism that UDP uses for signaling a closed port is slightly different than TCP, and is more ambiguous. In my case, the TCP scan took 13.61

seconds, while the UDP scan took 1089.42 seconds, a factor of nearly 100x longer .

Be patient with UDP.
