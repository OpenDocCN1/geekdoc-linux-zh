# ## 8. Scripting

Without scripting skills, you will be relegated to using other hackers' tools. This limits your effectiveness.

Every day a new tool exists, its effectiveness diminishes as security administrators come up with defenses.

To develop your own unique tools, you will need to become proficient in at least one of the scripting languages, including the BASH shell, and at least one of Perl, Python, or Ruby.
