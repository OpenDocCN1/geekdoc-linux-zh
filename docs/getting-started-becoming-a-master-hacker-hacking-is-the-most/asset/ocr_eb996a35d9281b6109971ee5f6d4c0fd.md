# A Word about Wordlists Both online and offline password cracking require a list of words to try.

These wordlists don’t nesscarily include all words. Some may be combination of characters that are often used as passwords such as qwerty, asdfghjk, or 123456. Choosing the proper wordlist may become the most important decision you make in attempting to crack a password.

Kali has a large number of wordlists built into it. You can locate them by entering; kali > locate wordlists 120 | P a g e
