# A Word About Exploitation Success Hacking, or exploitation, is NOT simply choosing an exploit and “throwing it against a system” and...

Voila!...you are inside the system (although I wouldn’t blame you for believing that based upon the millions of YouTube videos that depict hacking that way). Hacking is a process; sometimes a tedious process. Even when you have selected the right exploit, it still doesn’t work.

If I depicted hacking as easy and always successful, I would be doing you a disservice. Often hacks fail.

You are trying to make a system do something it was NOT supposed to do. In some cases, you are breaking a service or system to gain access. This is very unlike the work of a system or network administrator who is trying to get the system to work as it was intended to work.

If you run into difficulty performing the hacks I display in this book, consider it a reflection of the real world. Hacking is not easy or always successful. As I point out in Chapter 2, two of the key qualities of hackers are persistence and creativity .
