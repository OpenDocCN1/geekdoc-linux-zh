**A Word About Key Size** In the world of cryptography, size does matter! In general, the larger the key, the more secure the encryption. This means that AES with a 256-bit key is stronger than AES with an 128-bit key and likely will be more difficult to crack. Within the same encryption algorithm , the larger the key, the stronger the encryption.

It does not necessarily mean that larger keys mean stronger encryption between encryption algorithms.
