where \( \psi = ((2)n3 + 256)(s + 4.56) \) and \( s = \min(64, (n1)(n2)) \).

# Function

The three-dimensional discrete Fourier transform of complex data in array X, with results going into array Y, is expressed as follows:

\[ y_{k_{1}, k_{2}, k_{3}} = \text{scale} \sum_{j_{1}=0}^{n_{1}-1} \sum_{j_{2}=0}^{n_{2}-1} \sum_{j_{3}=0}^{n_{3}-1} x_{j_{1}, j_{2}, j_{3}} W_{n_{1}}^{([\text{sign}] j_{1} k_{1})} W_{n_{2}}^{([\text{sign}] j_{2} k_{2})} W_{n_{3}}^{([\text{sign}] j_{3} k_{3})} \]

for:
\( k_{1} = 0, 1, \ldots, n_{1}-1 \) 
\( k_{2} = 0, 1, \ldots, n_{2}-1 \) 
\( k_{3} = 0, 1, \ldots, n_{3}-1 \)

where:

\[ \begin{aligned} W_{n_{1}} &= e^{-2\pi [\sqrt{-1}] / n_{1}} \\ W_{n_{2}} &= e^{-2\pi [\sqrt{-1}] / n_{2}} \\ W_{n_{3}} &= e^{-2\pi [\sqrt{-1}] / n_{3}} \end{aligned} \]

and where:
\( x_{j_{1}, j_{2}, j_{3}} \) are elements of array X.
\( y_{k_{1}, k_{2}, k_{3}} \) are elements of array Y.
Isign is + or – (determined by argument isign).
\( \text{scale} \) is a scalar value.

For \( \text{scale} = 1.0 \) and isign being positive, you obtain the discrete Fourier transform, a function of frequency. The inverse Fourier transform is obtained with \( \text{scale} = 1.0 / ((n1)(n2)(n3)) \) and isign being negative. See references [1], [4], [5], [21], and [22].
