
## Transpose of a General Matrix

For SGEMV, DGEMV, CGEMV and ZGEMV, the matrix-vector product for the transpose of a general matrix:
$y \leftarrow \beta y + \alpha A^T x$
is expressed as follows:

![](img/5b0f44f3be6048723819b26aed7f6f03_314_0.png)

For SGEMTX and DGEMTX, the matrix-vector product for the transpose of a real general matrix:
$y \leftarrow y + \alpha A^T x$
is expressed as follows:

![](img/5b0f44f3be6048723819b26aed7f6f03_314_1.png)

In these expressions:
- *y* is a vector of length *n*.
- *\alpha* is a scalar.
- *\beta* is a scalar.
- $A^T$ is the transpose of matrix *A*, where *A* is an *m* by *n* matrix.
- *x* is a vector of length *m*.

## Conjugate Transpose of a General Matrix

For CGEMV and ZGEMV, the matrix-vector product for the conjugate transpose of a general matrix:

![](img/5b0f44f3be6048723819b26aed7f6f03_314_2.png)

where:
- *y* is a vector of length *n*.
- *\alpha* is a scalar.
- *\beta* is a scalar.
- $A^{H}$ is the conjugate transpose of matrix *A*, where *A* is an *m* by *n* matrix.
- *x* is a vector of length *m*.

# SGEMV, DGEMV, CGEMV, ZGEMV, SGEMX, DGEMX, SGEMTX, and DGEMTX

## Error Conditions

**Resource Errors:** Unable to allocate internal work area (for SGEMV, DGEMV, CGEMV, and ZGEMV).

**Computational Errors:** None

**Input-Argument Errors:**
1. transa $\neq$ 'N', 'T', or 'C'
2. $m < 0$
3. $m > lda$
4. $n < 0$
5. $lda \le 0$
6. $incy = 0$

## Example 1

This example shows the computation for TRANSA equal to 'N', where the real general matrix A is used in the computation. Because lda is 10 and n is 3, array A must be declared as A(E1:E2,F1:F2), where E2-E1+1=10 and F2-F1+1 $\ge$ 3. In this example, array A is declared as A(1:10,0:2).

**Call Statement and Input:**

```fortran
      TRANSA M    N   ALPHA      A       LDA  X  INCX BETA  Y   INCY
             |      |    |      |       |      |     |      |     |
   CALL SGEMV('N',  4,   3,   1.0, A(1,0), 10,  X, 1,   1.0, Y,  2)
```

$A = \begin{bmatrix} 1.0 & 2.0 & 3.0 \\ 2.0 & 2.0 & 4.0 \\ 3.0 & 2.0 & 2.0 \\ 4.0 & 2.0 & 1.0 \\ \cdot & \cdot & \cdot \\ \cdot & \cdot & \cdot \\ \cdot & \cdot & \cdot \\ \cdot & \cdot & \cdot \\ \cdot & \cdot & \cdot \\ \cdot & \cdot & \cdot \end{bmatrix}$

$X = (3.0, 2.0, 1.0)$

$Y = (4.0, \ .\ , 5.0, \ .\ , 2.0, \ .\ , 3.0)$

**Output:**

```fortran
Y = (14.0,  . , 19.0,  . , 17.0,  . , 20.0)
```

## Example 4

```fortran
CALL CGEMV('T', 5, 3, ALPHA, A, 10, X, 1, BETA, Y, 1)

ALPHA = (1.0, 0.0)
```

