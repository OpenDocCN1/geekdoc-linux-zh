# Example 2

This example shows how to find the eigenvalues and eigenvectors of a real short-precision general matrix $A$ of order $3$, where:

+   - N AUX is equal to 2N.
+   - AUX contains 2N elements.
+   - DUMMY is used as a placeholder for argument select, which is not used in the computation.
+   - On output, $A$ has been overwritten.

Note: This matrix is used in Example 5.1 in referenced text [65].

**Call Statement and Input:**

```
```
             IOPT    A    LDA   W     Z      LDZ  SELECT     N   AUX   NAUX
CALL SGEEV( 1 , A , 3 , W, Z, 3 , DUMMY , 3 , AUX , 6  )
```
```

$$\mathbf{A} = \left[ \begin{array}{ccc} 33.0 & 16.0 & 72.0 \\ -24.0 & -10.0 & -57.0 \\ -8.0 & -4.0 & -17.0 \end{array} \right]$$

**Output:**

$$\mathbf{W} = \left[ \begin{array}{cc} (3.000022, & 0.000000) \\ (1.000019, & 0.000000) \\ (1.999961, & 0.000000) \end{array} \right]$$
