# **Convolutions and Correlations**

The convolution and correlation subroutines provide the choice of using Fourier methods or direct methods. The Fourier-method subroutines contain a high-performance mixed-radix capability. There are also several direct-method subroutines that provide decimated output.

# **Related Computations**

The related-computation subroutines consist of a group of computations that can be used in general signal processing applications. They are similar to those provided on the IBM 3638 Array Processor; however, the ESSL subroutines generally solve a wider range of problems.
