# 2. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Readers have questions. Thus, this FAQ section has been included for answering some of the most obvious questions from the reader’s point of view. All readers are requested to read through this section for finding answers to their personal questions.

After reading through this FAQ entirely, if the reader feels that he/she still has a "question" or two to ask, please feel free to drop a line at sahibshah_ghosh@linuxwaves.com.

+   1. *What is the aim/purpose of this Guide?*
+   2. *Why have you used "3" operating systems for this guide?*
+   3. *Did you yourself try out the steps mentioned in this guide? What are the configurations of the computer system on which you tested all the material mentioned in this guide?*
+   4. *All your installations are "CD-ROM" based installation types. What about "Ethernet", "FTP", "HTTP", "NFS" installation types?*
+   5. *Why would anyone ever need to run 3 operating systems on the same hard disk of a computer? I want to run 3 operating systems on 2 separate hard disks. What is the big deal with this guide anyway?*
+   6. *Why Red Hat? Why NOT Debian GNU/Linux, Slackware, Caldera, SuSE, TurboLinux and others?*
+   7. *Why FreeBSD, OpenBSD and NetBSD operating systems? Why not BSD/OS, Darwin, Irix, Sun Solaris, Digital UNIX, HP-UX, IBM–AIX and others?*
+   8. *Which kernel version have you used for GNU/Linux?*
+   9. *I installed 3 operating systems in my computer as you have illustrated in this guide. And then unfortunately, something went wrong somewhere.*
+   10. *You used the GNU GRUB for booting all the 3 operating systems. May I know the reason why? Why not use the popular Linux Loader (LILO) or FreeBSD or NT boot loader for this purpose?*
+   11. *I have older Red Hat, FreeBSD and OpenBSD distributions and/or releases. Will they work?*
+   12. *I would like to have access to good resources on Linux and FreeBSD. Could you suggest a few?*
+   13. *I have a few suggestions, new ideas and exciting comments. Can I send them to you?*
+   14. *I have "installation-specific" questions/queries or something "failed" or "this quite didn't work"? Can I send these reports to you?*
+   15. *Will you be updating this guide from time to time? Will "my" problems be listed here?*
+   16. *Does this guide have any newest version on the Internet?*
+   17. (For people living in and around Moscow, Russia) *I have a computer system and I would love to install 3 operating systems on them. Would you be kind enough to come to my place and do the installation and configuration?*
+   18. (For people of Russia) *We love Microsoft Windows, FreeBSD and OpenBSD operating systems. But "why" have you NOT used the common Russian GNU/Linux distributions? I mean "ASP Linux", "Black Cat Linux", "ALT ..."*
