I personally have networking and source-code hacking experience on FreeBSD and OpenBSD boxes. I do "not" have any experience working with Irix, Ultrix, Minix, HP-UX, IBM-AIX, Darwin and others. Thus, FreeBSD, OpenBSD and NetBSD have been chosen.
