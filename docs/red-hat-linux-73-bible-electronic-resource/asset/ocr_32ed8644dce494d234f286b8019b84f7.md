# ## Running GnomeMeeting

You can start GnomeMeeting from a Terminal window (type `gnomemeeting&`) or from the Gnome menu (Programs → Internet → GnomeMeeting). Figure 8-7 shows a reluctant author searching for friends on a video-conferencing (ILS) server.

![](bbox=[0.07, 0.36, 0.6, 0.64])

In the GnomeMeeting window that opens you can click on the book icon to open a search window (shown to the right in Figure 8-7). The search window enables you to connect to an ILS directory. By typing a name into the search filter, you can search for people you might be connected to that server by first name, last name, e-mail address, or location. Select a person from the list that appears and, if he or she accepts your call, you can begin video-conferencing.

Tabs beneath the video window let you adjust your audio levels and video appearance. The History tab shows a log of your activities.
