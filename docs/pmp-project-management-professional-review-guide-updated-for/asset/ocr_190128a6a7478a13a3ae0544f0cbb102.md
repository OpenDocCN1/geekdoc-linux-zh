# Inputs of the Identify Stakeholders Process

There are four inputs in the Identify Stakeholders process:

**Project Charter**  In this process, the project charter provides the list of stakeholders along with other individuals or organizations affected by the project.

**Procurement Documents**  When the project is procured, the list of key stakeholders and other relevant individuals or organizations may be obtained through the procurement documents.

**Enterprise Environmental Factors**  The following enterprise environmental factors are used in this process:

+   • Company culture
+   • Organizational structure
+   • Governmental or industry standards
+   • Local, regional, or global trends and practices

**Organizational Process Assets**  The following organizational process assets pertain to this process:

+   • Stakeholder register templates
+   • Lessons learned
+   • Stakeholder register from previous projects

# Tools and Techniques of the Identify Stakeholders Process

The tools and techniques of Identify Stakeholders are stakeholder analysis, expert judgment, and meetings.
