The Project Stakeholder Management Knowledge Area is concerned with identifying all of the stakeholders of the project. These processes also assess stakeholder needs, expectations, and involvement on the project. According to the *PMBOK® Guide*, stakeholder engagement and satisfaction should be managed as a key project objective.
