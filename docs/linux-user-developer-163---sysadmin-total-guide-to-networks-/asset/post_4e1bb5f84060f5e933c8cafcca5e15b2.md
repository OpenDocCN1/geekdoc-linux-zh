## INTERVIEW KEILA BANKS

### Undefinable Keila

Keila Banks, 14, was a speaker at this year's SCALE 14x and got a standing ovation at OSCON 2015. Meet the rising star of the open source world...

> So, tell us a little bit about yourself.
(Well, I just turned 14 four months ago, I've been speaking at conferences since I was about 11 years old... I have always been into computers, probably since I was three years old. I started getting into coding when I was nine - that's when I wrote my first website)

How did you start to get involved with conferences?
My dad was interested for SCALE, and he asked me if I wanted to go talk, and I said that sounded great. Then we went to another talk, and I ended up going to more and and places, and then I went to OSCON in kind of just meeting around my first keynote - then I was just giving small talks to SCALE groups of kids.

What was it like after that?
Well, I got a lot more connections to people: a lot people began to reach out to me instead of me having to reach out to them. MasterCard actually reached out to me from Africa and Caribbean! It was a business conference in Miami, and they invited me to there. That was a wonderful opportunity, and it opened up more opportunities for the future, reaching out to me.
