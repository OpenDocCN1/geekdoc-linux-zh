# Get into Linux

Have you ever been interested to attend your first conference or join a group? Well, a few interesting events are just around the corner. The Girls in Tech Catalyst Conference is being held in Phoenix, Arizona, on Oct 15-16 (https://girisintech.org/). With a focus on entrepreneurship and education, the event features speakers with workshops and promises excellent networking.

O'Reilly's Open Source Conference, OSCO, will be held on May 18-9 in Austin, and the event typically takes a deep dive into the latest technologies and tools and is known for its community-focused atmosphere.

The PyData community is always growing, so if you're interested in joining a charity workshop, you might find them on June 9. If you're interested in Linux Foundation (https://linuxfoundation.org/) and want to make sure you join the conversation on open source, search for a local chapter. Also, PyCon US is from March 17-21, 2023, but PyCon Europe is June 22-24 in North America and Oct 4-8 in Europe.
