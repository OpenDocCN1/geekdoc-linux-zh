Audio data can be recorded and played in WAV format with arecord and aplay. The command `arecord -t 10 -m mysong.wav` records a ten-second WAV file. View a complete list of arecord and aplay options with the `-h` option. Keep the correct mixer setting in mind when recording. The controls for these settings in alsamixer are ‘Capture’ and, depending on the recording source, ‘Line’ and ‘MIC’. The recording source must also be properly specified. The signal source is digitized by an analog to digital converter when recording. For 16-bit converters, the maximum amplitude is 65535. Any signal higher than that will be distorted, because it will be converted to a signal with an amplitude of 65535.
