# SUMMARY

We outlined the steps required to install DSL-Embedded on a USB pen drive, ways of modifying the default setup including the use of MyDSL extensions and custom boot options. We also reviewed how to use your pen drive on systems without a compatible BIOS.

We hope you find Damn Small Linux a valuable travel companion. It is much easier to carry around a pen drive than a laptop. So, next time you are on the road, try carrying your desktop in your pocket instead of in your briefcase.
