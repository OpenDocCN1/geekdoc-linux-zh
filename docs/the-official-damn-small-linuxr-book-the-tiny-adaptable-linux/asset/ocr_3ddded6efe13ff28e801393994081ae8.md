The Bargain BareBones is an example of a Mini-ITX-based thin client. Again, the design is very small, much more compact than a typical Mini-ITX computer. The BareBones was never intended to be a standalone work station, and it has no provision for a hard drive or a media drive. Although the unit comes without RAM, it accepts 184-pin DIMM socket DDR RAM up to 1GB.
