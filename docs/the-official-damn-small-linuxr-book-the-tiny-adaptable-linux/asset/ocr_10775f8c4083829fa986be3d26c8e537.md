Although underpowered next to the latest Mini-ITX boards, the Epia 5000 is a high-quality board, and this particular set up will last for years without any problem. I built this machine with only 256MB of RAM and this suited my needs more than adequately. I used the Mini as my daily computer for about a year without any problem. If I built it again, I would use a more powerful motherboard and at least 512MB of RAM simply so I could forget about RAM usage and just enjoy my silent computer.

When the DSL store started selling the Damn Small Machines, I finally retired the Mini in favor of the smaller yet more powerful thin client. I miss the look of the homemade case though, and I may build something similar again in the future.
