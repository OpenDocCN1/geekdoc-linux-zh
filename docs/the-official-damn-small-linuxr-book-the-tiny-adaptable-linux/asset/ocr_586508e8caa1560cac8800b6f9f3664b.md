# FIGURE 4-4 Choose to display icons (or not) and single- or double-click.

![](bbox=[0.12, 0.6, 0.42, 0.85])

The first panel of the DSL Icon Tool is obvious. Here is where you choose to display icons on the desktop or not. Click the button of your choice and then the OK button. If you choose to have icons, the second set of buttons is enabled and allows you to choose single- or double-click to enable the associated action of the icon.
