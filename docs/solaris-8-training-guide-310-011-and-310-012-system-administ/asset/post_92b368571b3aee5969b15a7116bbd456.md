## More books for Solaris Administrators

*Solaris 8 Security* has all the concepts and issues Solaris 8 administrators need to know to make and keep their Solaris 8 system secure. This book includes not only Solaris 8 security tools and features, but also covers topics such as cryptography and defenses against known attacks and vulnerabilities.

A great companion to the solarisguide.com web site, *Solaris 8 Essential Reference* assumes you are well-versed in general UNIX skills and simply need some pointers on how to get the most out of Solaris. This book provides clear and concise instructions on how to perform important administration and management tasks.

*Solaris System Management* teaches you how to make strategic decisions on how to provide an efficient, robust, and secure Solaris environment, with focus on using Solaris tools for innovative uses like the gathering and manipulation of management information. The goal of *Solaris System Management* is to arm system managers with sufficient knowledge to develop sound strategies for networks.

*Solaris 8 Training Guide (310-043): Network Administrator* helps certified Solaris system administrators seek a competitive advantage through achieving network certification. This book follows the successful *Training Guide* format, which delivers superior solutions in the form of lab examples, self-assessment opportunities, summary tables, and several effective learning tools—including ExamGear—that enhances the learning experience.

## Your Complete Solaris Certification Solution!

Solaris 8 Training Guide (310-011 and 310-012): System Administrator Certification is ideal for both new and experienced system administrators in need of insight into the Solaris 8 certification exams. This classroom-style Training Guide equips administrators with vital knowledge for success on exam day and provides information that serves as a daily system administration reference. This book prepares networking professionals by concentrating on such subjects as system setup and customization, security, software administration, script writing, and networking—all of which are applicable to the exam and the tasks involved in system administration.

Que has partnered with one of the most experienced authors and Solaris instructors to bring you the best information available to help you pass these exams and become Sun® Solaris-certified. Expert author Bill Calkins’s extensive compilation of real-world experience, including warnings about possible pitfalls, will bring all administrators within reach of Solaris 8 certification. Our books are double tech-edited to ensure accuracy and have been the study guide of choice for thousands of Solaris system administrators over the years.

## We make the most of your valuable study time by providing:

- **Lab and Step-by-Step Examples** that allow you to try out tasks you’ll have to perform on the exam
- **Multiple Self-Assessment Opportunities**, including a complete practice exam to evaluate your level of preparedness
- **Warnings** that alert you to the potential mistakes that can occur because of improper application of the technology
- **Tables** that summarize key command-line options and aid in retention
- **Structured Format and Pedagogy** that begins at system bootup and works through Solaris installation and customization tasks
- **Focus** on understanding all the essential procedures for administering systems
- **Tips** on troubleshooting the network by analyzing notes and pitfall warnings
