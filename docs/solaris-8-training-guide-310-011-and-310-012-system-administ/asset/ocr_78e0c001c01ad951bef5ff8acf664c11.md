Another nice feature of the Process Manager is the capability to display the ancestry of a process. When a UNIX process initiates one or more processes, we call them child processes, or children. Child and parent processes have the same user ID. To view the parent process and all the child processes that belong to it, highlight the process in the Process Manager window. Click Process from the toolbar at the top of the window, and select Show Ancestry, as shown in Figure 9.4.
