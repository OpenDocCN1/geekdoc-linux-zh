# Your Complete Solaris Certification Solution!

Solaris 8 Training Guide (310-011 and 310-012): System Administrator Certification is ideal for both new and experienced system administrators in need of insight into the Solaris 8 certification exams. This classroom-style Training Guide equips administrators with vital knowledge for success on exam day and provides information that serves as a daily system administration reference. This book prepares networking professionals by concentrating on such subjects as system setup and customization, security, software administration, script writing, and networking—all of which are applicable to the exam and the tasks involved in system administration.

Que has partnered with one of the most experienced authors and Solaris instructors to bring you the best information available to help you pass these exams and become Sun® Solaris-certified. Expert author Bill Calkins’s extensive compilation of real-world experience, including warnings about possible pitfalls, will bring all administrators within reach of Solaris 8 certification. Our books are double tech-edited to ensure accuracy and have been the study guide of choice for thousands of Solaris system administrators over the years.
