# The Linux Cookbook: Tips and Techniques for Everyday Use:

+   - First, open the extracted image in the GIMP (see section [Editing Images with the GIMP]).
+   - Then, click through the `Image` menu to the `Colors` submenu and then to the `Levels` submenu, and choose `Auto Levels`.
+   - Click `OK` in the `Levels` window to accept the changes.
