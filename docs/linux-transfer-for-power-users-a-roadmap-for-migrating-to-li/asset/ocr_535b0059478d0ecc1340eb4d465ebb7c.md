If you checked any of these options earlier, you’re opening your machine to unnecessary connections from the outside world. You’ll be much safer if you uncheck them now. Telnet and FTP are particularly insecure, because your passwords are sent to the other computer in plain text, which means that someone eavesdropping on your network (it’s easier than you think!) can easily pick them up.

# ## Summary

Security is a never-ending battle between the good guys and the bad guys. Unlike stock applications such as word-processing and drawing packages, you need to keep up on security due to its ever-changing landscape. Keep your machine updated (as described in Chapter 3, “Updating Fedora Core”), install and use a virus scanner, and pay attention to major new developments. The computer community is still in for a few turbulent years—it’s much like the Wild West of the American frontier—before things settle down. You need to stay alert.
