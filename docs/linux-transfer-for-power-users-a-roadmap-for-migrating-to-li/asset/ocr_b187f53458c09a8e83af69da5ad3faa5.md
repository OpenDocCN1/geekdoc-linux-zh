You’ll also find your removable storage devices under /mnt in the directory tree, as shown in Figure 5.

Some suggest that entries to mount shares to remote file systems (in other words, mapped network drives) should be placed in /mnt, but I disagree. I argue they go under /home/\<user\> in order to facilitate easier access to the data. I cover this in more detail in Appendix B, “Connecting to a Windows Network.”
