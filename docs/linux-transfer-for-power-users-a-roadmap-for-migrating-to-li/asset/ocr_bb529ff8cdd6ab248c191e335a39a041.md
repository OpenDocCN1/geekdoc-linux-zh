On the far left of Figure 6, the Main Menu icon (the red fedora) is used to display a menu that launches a variety of functions and applications. The panel is automatically populated during installation with some application launchers, such as the Mozilla browser and OpenOffice. Figure 6 shows the panel after I’ve customized it with the icons for the applications I use most often. I’ll discuss how to customize the panel later in this chapter.
