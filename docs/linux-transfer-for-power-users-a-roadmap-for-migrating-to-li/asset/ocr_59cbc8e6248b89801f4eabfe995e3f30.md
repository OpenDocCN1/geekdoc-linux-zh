Because you’ve allowed Kudzu to identify and configure the printer, the printer name will appear in the “Queue type” dialog. Your next step is to determine the queue type by using the “Select a queue type” box. The first choice, as shown in Figure 6, is “Locally-connected,” while the others all have to do with network printers, and are thus beyond the scope of this book. Whitpapers available on the Hentzeuerweke Web site address network printing with Linux in various incarnations.

Click Forward to open the “Printer model” dialog, shown in Figure 7. Oddly, the wizard requires you to select a printer’s manufacturer and model even if Kudzu has detected it. As a result, there’s really not much point going through Kudzua because you’ll need to identify your printer again during this print-queue setup.
