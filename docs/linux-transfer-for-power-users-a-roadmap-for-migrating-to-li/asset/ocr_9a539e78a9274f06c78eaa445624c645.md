# The Linux Documentation Project

The Linux Documentation Project (also known as TLDP) was started in the mid-’90s when a number of folks in the Linux community realized that the lack of good documentation was a serious hindrance to the increased acceptance of Linux.

The contributors at TLDP (well into the hundreds) have produced a huge amount of material over the years, and more is still being added on a regular basis. Visit them at http://www.tldp.org/ and be prepared to spend some time looking through the riches available. As a new power user, you may find that some of the help files are beyond you. Some authors assume a moderate level of Linux familiarity, and will say things like “then you can grep for the string in question.” Because you’re a new Linux user, that won’t be helpful now, but don’t be dismayed—you’ll be surprised at how quickly some of what seems to be Greek becomes understandable in short order.
