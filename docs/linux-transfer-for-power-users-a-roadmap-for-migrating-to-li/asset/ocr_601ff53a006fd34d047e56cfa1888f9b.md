Once you’ve selected your mode and type of image (and after you’ve put the image in the scanner¹), click the Scan button to start the scanning process. The progression of the main window is shown in Figure 28.

When the scan is complete, the Viewer window displays the scanned image, shown in Figure 29. Depending on the size of the image and the zoom selected, you may not be able to see all of the scanned image.

From there, you’re all set to work with the image as you need—manipulating and adjusting the settings if it didn’t come out quite like you wanted it, or saving the image and then manipulating it in a different program such as The GIMP.
