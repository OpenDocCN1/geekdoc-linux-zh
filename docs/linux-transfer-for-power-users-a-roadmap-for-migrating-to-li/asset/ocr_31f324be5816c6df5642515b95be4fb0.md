Now what? Herman and Igor both have their own home directories, under /home. But what we want is a separate directory, called /home/theboys, that all members of the “theboys” group have access to, just like Herman has access to /home/herman and Igor has access to /home/igor. But no one except members of the “theboys” group will have access to /home/theboys.

Here’s how to make this happen. Open a command window and enter the following commands, as shown in Figure 14 and in the following listing:

```
`$ su
Password:
# cd /home
# mkdir /home/theboys
# chown herman /home/theboys
# chgrp theboys /home/theboys
# chmod 770 /home/theboys
# chmod g+s /home/theboys`
```
