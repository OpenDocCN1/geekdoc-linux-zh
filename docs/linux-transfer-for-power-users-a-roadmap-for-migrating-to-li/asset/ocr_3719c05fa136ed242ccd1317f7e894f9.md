The toolbar on the side provides the ability to draw freehand or create predefined shapes such as rectangles, circles, and ellipses (all either filled or empty), and straight or diagonal lines. You can also use a spray can or a flood fill, and erase through an area that had been drawn on.

The color pickers on the side provide a fixed set of colors but also allow you to create a palette of custom colors; double-clicking one of the squares in the “Custom colors” picker opens the KDE standard Select Color dialog, shown in Figure 19.

The Preview box in the upper right corner above the “System colors” picker shows you a 1:1 display of what your icon will look like. You can change the background of the Preview box by clicking Settings \| Configure KiconEdit to provide a higher contrast if you like.

Finally, you can save your icon by clicking File \| Save As. The Save As dialog has the PNG filter already selected, but you can choose a different file format if you desire.

# ## Modifying images with The GIMP

The GIMP (The GNU Image Manipulation Program) is an application somewhat comparable to PC Paintbrush, Paint Shop Pro, and Photoshop. It provides a wide variety of image- and photograph-manipulation capabilities. For example, many of my authors use it on Linux to capture and modify screen shots for their books.

But The GIMP's capabilities go far beyond that; indeed, there are 900-page books that cover all of The GIMP's nooks and crannies. Some of its general image-manipulation capabilities (beyond screen-shot work) include a variety of tools, such as sharp and fuzzy drawing tools, airbrushes, fills, reverses, transparencies, and stamping; and selection tools such as freform, rectangular, elliptical, fuzzy, and intelligent scissors. You can rotate, scale, shear, flip, resize, resample, feather, and magnify images, and use a number of alternatives to crop them.

The GIMP also supports advanced scripting via Perl, Python, and others; you can use these scripts to access internal GIMP functions. In addition, a comprehensive plug-in system allows you to extend The GIMP with new functions, filters, file formats, and other additions.
