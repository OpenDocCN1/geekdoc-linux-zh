# ## Summary

One primary benefit of open-source software—and Linux in particular—is that you have choices. And these choices aren’t thinly masked options that are frequently trampled by the desired defaults of a specific manufacturer.

Dozens of e-mail clients and Web browsers are available for Linux, and they generally play well with each other. Your biggest challenge will be finding the time to evaluate all of them, should you feel the need.
