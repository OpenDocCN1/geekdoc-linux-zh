As a power user, the one list you will be most interested in is the “fedora-list” mailing list, although you’re certainly welcome to join others as you desire. This list covers subjects that users (as opposed to folks who do programming, administration, testing and quality assurance, or documentation) are interested in. You can ask questions and get help, and offer help to fellow Fedora Core users.
