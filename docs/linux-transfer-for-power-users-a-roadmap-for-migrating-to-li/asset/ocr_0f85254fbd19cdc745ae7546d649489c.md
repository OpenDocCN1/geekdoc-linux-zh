The downside of using Ogg Vorbis files is that the manufacturers of many portable audio players (such as the Apple iPOD and the Creative line of USB players) do not support OGG files—yet, at least.

I’ll use the Ogg Vorbis format in this chapter; if you choose to use a different open-source format, the concepts and processes are similar. If you choose to use a proprietary format, you’ll probably need to pay for a product because the owner of the format requires royalty payments.
