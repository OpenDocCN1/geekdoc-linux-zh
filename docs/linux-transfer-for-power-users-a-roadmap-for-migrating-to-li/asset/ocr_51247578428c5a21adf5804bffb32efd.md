# License Agreement

The License Agreement screen, although it looks like lots of other license screens, simply serves to confirm that Fedora Core is developed under the GPL, and to explain Red Hat’s role in the process. You’ll have to accept the agreement to continue, but you’re not signing your life away like you do with proprietary software license agreements.
