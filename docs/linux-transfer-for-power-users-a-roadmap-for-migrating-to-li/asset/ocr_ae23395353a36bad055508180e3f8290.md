Not as functionally rich as OpenOffice.org’s Calc or even KOffice’s KSpread, Gnurmic has nonetheless won a wide following for its no-nonsense, “just the important stuff” design. And Gnumeric’s development team has won kudos for responsiveness; when their implementation of important statistical functions was found to be buggy, the team fixed them within a few weeks; contrast that with Microsoft Excel, which has carried the same bugs through several major releases over half a decade. (See [http://www.csdassn.org/software\_reports/gnumeric.pdf](http://www.csdassn.org/software_reports/gnumeric.pdf) for details.)

As with other applications, one of the first things you’re going to want to do is set up some preferences. Select Format | Gnurmic to open the Gnurmic Preferences dialog (*Figure 23*).
