With today’s hard disks ranging from 40 GB to 120 GB or more, one would think that running out of disk space would be the last thing on one’s mind. However, one advantage of Linux is the ability to run on older machines that have small hard disks where disk space is at a premium. The KDiskFree monitor allows you to see at a glance how your disk space is faring. Open the monitor via Main Menu | System Tools | More System Tools | KDiskFree, and you’ll see the monitor as shown in **Figure 29**.

There are separate displays for each storage device attached to the system, including mounted drives. You can configure the update frequency and the mount commands by clicking Settings | Configure.
