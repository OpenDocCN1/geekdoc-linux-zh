After you click **Accept**, XSan e will attempt to locate the scanner attached to your computer, and will display a dialog telling you so, as shown in **Figure 18**.

If XSan e fails to locate or connect to a scanner, you'll get a message like the one shown in **Figure 19**. I mention this because it's important to click the Help button. Unlike a lot of error dialogs that purport to offer assistance (you know, with advice like "The software was unable to connect to the device. Check the documentation that came with the device for more information."), clicking the Help button here opens a dialog with a variety of suggestions that may prove significantly more helpful.
