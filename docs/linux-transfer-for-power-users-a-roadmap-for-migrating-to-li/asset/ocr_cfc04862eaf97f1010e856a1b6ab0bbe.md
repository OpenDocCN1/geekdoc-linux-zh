Once you’re done with the New Account Wizard, the main window of GnuCash opens—the one you’ll be working with most of the time. As you can see in Figure 28, it lists your accounts and the current balance.

From there, you’ll enter scheduled transactions through options under the Actions menu, and generate reports via options under the Reports menu. To work with the register for a particular account, select the account in the main screen, as shown in Figure 29.
