You’ll notice that a lot of items in the list are not users. The details of this are beyond the scope of the book, but the general idea is that individual processes (somewhat like programs) running on your computer can also be eligible to join a group so that they can have access to specific files or directories. In addition, this prevents a process from becoming a security hole if that service is somehow compromised.
