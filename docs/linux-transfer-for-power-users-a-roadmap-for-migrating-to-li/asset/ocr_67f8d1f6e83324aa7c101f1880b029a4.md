Some manufacturers are rather prolific in terms of models, and it can be a chore to scroll through the list. Try resizing the dialog so that it’s as tall as your screen, and it’ll be easier to find your printer model. Yes, this list should probably be presented as a treeview, with all the LaserJet models in one node, and so on. Maybe next release!

Then click Forward and you’ll see the Finish dialog, shown in Figure 9. Click the Finish button and the wizard will ask if you want to print a test page, as shown in Figure 10. It’s a good idea to do this now to make sure that all the connections are working properly. It’s a pain to troubleshoot an errant printer after the fact.

If the printer prints the page, you’ll get a chance to confirm that everything is working fine, as shown in Figure 10. Note that it can take a minute or two for the page to print, depending on your printer.
