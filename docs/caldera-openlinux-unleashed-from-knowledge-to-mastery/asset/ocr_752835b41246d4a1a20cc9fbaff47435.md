Before we go into the utilities section, there are two more features of KLyX that you have to know about. First is putting figures and pictures in your documents, and the second is putting foreign characters (mathematical characters are foreign to some of us) into the document.

# ### Pictures in KLyX

To attach a picture into your document, on the KLyX panel click on Insert, Figure.

In the dialog box that comes up, select whether you want encapsulated or inline PostScript.

If you select encapsulated, a little box is inserted into your document. Move your mouse over this little box and left-click on it.

This brings up the Edit Figure dialog panel (see Figure 28.7).
