# 21 Network Management and Debugging

Network management is the art and science of keeping a network healthy. It-generally includes the following tasks:

+   * Fault detection for networks, gateways, and critical servers
* Schemes for notifying an administrator of problems
* General network monitoring, to balance load and plan expansion
* Documentation and visualization of the network
* Administration of network devices from a central site

On a single network segment, it is generally not worthwhile to establish formal procedures for network management. Just test the network thoroughly after in-installation and check occasionally to be its load is not excessive. When it breaks, fix it.

As your network grows, management procedures should become more auto-mated. On a network consisting of several different subnets joined with switches or routers, you may want to start automating management tasks with shell scripts
