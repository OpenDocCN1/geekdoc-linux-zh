This configure/make/make install sequence is common to the majority of UNIX and Linux software and works as long as you have the development environment and any package-specific prerequisites installed. However, it's always a good idea to check the package's INSTALL or README file for specifications.

In this case, the --disable-ssl --disable-nls options to configure omit some wget features that depend on other libraries that haven't been installed. In real life, you'd probably want to install the prerequisites. Use configure --help to see all the configuration options. # 1.13 SYSTEM ADMINISTRATION UNDER DURESS

System administrators wear many hats. In the real world, they are often people with other jobs who have been asked to look after a few computers on the side. If this is your situation, treat carefully and be aware of how this scenario tends to play out over the long term.

The more experienced you become at system management, the more the community comes to depend on you. Networks invariably grow, and administrative tends to accumulate over time as your administration becomes more sophisticated and you add additional layers. You will soon find that the only person in your organization knows how to perform a variety of important tasks.

Once coworkers come to think of you as the local administrator, it is difficult to cultivate expertise from this role. That is not necessarily a bad thing, but you will know several people who have changed jobs to escape it. Many administrative tasks are intangible, you may also find that you're expected to be a full-time administrator and a full-time engineer, or analyst.

There is a common tendency for unwilling administrators to find off requests by adopting a sorry attitude and providing poor service.¹¹⁰ This approach usually backfires; it makes you look bad and creates additional problems.

Instead, considering detailed records of the time you spend on system administration. Your goal should be to keep the work at a manageable level and assemble evidence that you can use when you ask to be relieved of administrative duties. In many organizations, you will need to lobby the management from six months to a year to get yourself replaced, plan ahead.

On the other hand, you may find you enjoy system administration and that you prefer to do work. Employment prospects remain good. Unfortunately, your political problems will probably intensify. See Chapter 32, Management, Policy, and Politics, for a preview of the delights in store.

> ¹⁰ A tendency lovingly and sadistically documented in Simon Travaglia's Bastard Operator from Hell stories; see bofh.ntk.net for the archive (Look under BOFH).
