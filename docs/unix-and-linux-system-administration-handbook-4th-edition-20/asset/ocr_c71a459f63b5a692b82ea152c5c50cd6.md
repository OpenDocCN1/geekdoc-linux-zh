# CERT: a registered service mark of Carnegie Mellon University

In mid-2003, CERT partnered with the Department of Homeland Security’s National Security Division, NCSD, For better or worse, the merger altered the previous mailing list structure.

The combined organization, known as US-CERT, offers annual announcement lists, the most useful of which is the "Technical Cyber Security Alerts.\ Subscribe to any of the four lists at forms.us-cert.gov/mailists.

# SecurityFocus.com and the BugTrack mailing list

SecurityFocus.com specializes in security-related news and information. The news includes current articles on general issues and on specific problems; there is also an extensive technical library of useful papers, nearly sorted by topic.

SecurityFocus’s archive of security tools includes software for a variety of operating systems, along with blurbs and user ratings. It is the most comprehensive and detailed source of tools that we are aware of.

The BugTrack list is a moderated forum for the discussion of security vulnerabilities and their fixes. To subscribe, visit securityfocus.com/archive. Traffic on this list can be fairly heavy, and the signal-to-noise ratio is poor. A database of BugTrack vulnerability reports is also available from the web site.

# Schneier on Security

Bruce Schneier’s blog is a valuable and sometimes entertaining source of information about computer security and cryptography. Schneier is the author of the well-respected books Applied Cryptography and Secrets and Lies, among others. Information from the blog is also captured in the form of a monthly newsletter known as the Crypto-Gram. Learn more at schneier.com/crypto-gram.html.
