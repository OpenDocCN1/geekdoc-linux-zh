# 20.3 The SMTP PROTOCOL

The Simple Mail Transport Protocol (SMTP) and its extended version, SMTP,
have have been standardized in the RFC series (RFC5321) and are used for most mes-
hand-offs among the various pieces of the mail system:

+   - UA-to-MSA or -MTA as a message is injected into the mail system
- MSA-to-MTA as the message starts its delivery journey
- MTA- or MSA-to-antivirus or -antispam scanning programs
- MTA-to-MSA as a message is forwarded from one site to another
- MTA-to-DA as a message is delivered to the local message server

Because the format of messages and the transfer protocol are both standardized,
my MTA and your MTA do not have to be the same or even know each other’s
identity; they just have to both speak SMTP or ESMTP. Your various mail servers
run different MTAs and operate just fine.
