# Protect your backups

Another major problem is the rapid expansion of disk space that has resulted
from the ever-lower price of hard disks. You can no longer purchase a stock-top machine with less than 250GB of disk space. Why clean up your disks and
enforce quotas when you can just throw a little money to the problem and
more disk space? Unfortunately, it's all too easy for the on-line storage
to outstrip your ability to back it up.

Backup units are perfectly capable of dumping filesystems to multiple pieces of
media. However, if a dumps multiple tapes, an operator or tape library robot
m be present to change the media, and the media must be carefully labeled to
allow restores to be performed easily. Unless you have a good reason to create a
early large systems, doesn't do it.

If you can't fit your daily backups on one tape, you have several options:

+   * Buy a higher-capacity backup device.
* Buy a stacker or library and feed multiple pieces of media to one device.
* Change your dump sequence.
* Use multiple backup devices.

# Keep media off-site

First, baby steps: you should always have an off-line copy of your data. That is, a
protected copy that is not stored on a hard disk on the machine of origin.shots and RAID arrays are not substitutes for backups!

Most organizations also keep backups off-site so that a disaster such as a fire canndest both the original data and the backups. "Off-site" can be anything
from a safe deposit box at a bank to the President's or CEO's home. Companies
specialize in the secure storage of backup media guarantee a secure and cli-controlled environment for your archives. There are on-line (but off-site)
storage provider is reputable, bonded, and insured. There are businesses that specialize in safeguarding your backup.

The speed with which backup media are moved off-site should depend on how\often you need to restore files and on how much latency you can accept. Somenavoid making this decision by performing two dumps to different backup\devices, one that stays on-site and one that is moved immediately.
