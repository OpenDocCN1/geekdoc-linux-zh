# Ethernet topology

The Ethernet topology is a branching bus with no loops; a packet can travel be-
t hosts on the same network in one way. Three types of packets can
be exchanged on a segment: unicast, multicast, and broadcast. Broadcast packets are\addressed to only one host. Multicast packets are addressed to a group of hosts.
Broadcast packets are delivered to all hosts on a segment.
