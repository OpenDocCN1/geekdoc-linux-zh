Internet storage services start at 10€/GB/month and get more expensive as you add features. For example, some providers let you choose how many redundant copies of your data will be stored. Pay-per-use pricing allows you to pick the reliability that is appropriate for your data and budget.

Internet backups only work if your Internet connection is fast enough to transmit copies of your changes every night without bogging down “real” traffic. If your organization handles large amounts of data, it is unlikely that you can back it up across the Internet. But for smaller organizations, cloud backups can be an ideal solution since there is no up-front cost and no hardware to buy. Remember, any sensitive data that transmits the Internet or is stored in the cloud must be encrypted.
