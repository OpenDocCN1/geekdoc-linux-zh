# 21.9 NETWORK MANAGEMENT PROTOCOLS

Networks have grown rapidly in size and value over the last 20 years, and along with that growth has come the need for an efficient way to manage them. Commercial vendors and standards organizations have approached this challenge in many different ways. The most significant developments have been the introduction of standard device-management protocols and a glut of high-level products that exploit those.

Network management protocols standardize a way of probing a device to discover its configuration, health, and network connections. In addition, they allow some of this information to be modified so that network management can be standardized across different kinds of machinery and performed from a central location.
