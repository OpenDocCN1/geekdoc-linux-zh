
# About the Authors

For general comments and bug reports, please contact ulsah@book.admin.com. Because cause of the volume of email that this alias receives, we regret that we are unable to answer technical questions.

![](img/579efec8c67772f31ecf34844bbb71a2_1327_0.png)

Evi Nemeth (sailingevi@gmail.com) has retired from the Computer Science faculty at the University of Colorado. She is currently exploring the Pacific on her 40-foot sailboat named Wonderland. This is her last edition—it's impossible to keep up with the latest sysadmin toys when anchored in paradise with 30 baud packet radio email connection.

![](img/579efec8c67772f31ecf34844bbb71a2_1327_1.png)

Garth Snyder (garth@garsnyder.com) has worked at NEXT and Sun and holds a BS in Engineering from Swarthmore College and an MD and an MBA from the University of Rochester.

![](img/579efec8c67772f31ecf34844bbb71a2_1327_2.png)

Trent R. Hein (trent@atrust.com) is the co-founder of Applied Trust, a company that provides IT infrastructure consulting services. Trent holds a BS in Computer Science from the University of Colorado.

![](img/579efec8c67772f31ecf34844bbb71a2_1327_3.png)

Ben Whaley (ben@atrust.com) is the Director of Enterprise Architecture at Applied Trust, an IT consulting company based in Boulder, Colorado. Ben earned a BS in Computer Science from the University of Colorado in 2004.
