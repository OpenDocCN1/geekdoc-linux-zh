| 1. Use Syslog Daemon syslog-ng | In a Linux system, there are many logs that track various aspects of system operation. Many services log their activities to their own log files, and the level of detail can be set on a per-service basis. In addition, system logs in /var/log/ track system-level events.

logrotate is the utility to archive log files. |
