# Acknowledgments

I have now written my third book for Sytex, which, frankly, I never thought would happen. I thought writing Linux Network Servers 24seven, my first book for Sytex, would be a one-shot deal. But then came the opportunity to write a series of books all focused on Linux. As much as I love writing, I love writing about Linux even more. To add to the joy of this project, the project at Sytex would have been wonderful. Like the other books, this one has been written with the support of some excellent people.

I have been surprised by the consistent quality of the people I work with because the cast of characters has changed. It is perfectly normal for different books to have different editors, but fate has a bigger player in these changes than management. Guy Hart-Davis, the Associate Publisher who first listened to my proposals for this library, inherited a large home in England and went off to be “Lord of the Manor.” (Like everyone else at Sytex, I'm dying to go to visit him.) By great luck, Neil Ede took over as Associate Publisher for the Linux Library. Neil is the person who introduced me to Sytex. He was the first person to hear my ideas about the Linux Library and encourage me to propose them to Sytex. I couldn't have a better publisher than Neil.

Mauren Adams, who started as the Acquisition Editor for this series, has been promoted to Mom. She the project to give birth to Emma. Now instead of baby-sitting, she is sitting with a baby real. I'd call that a major promotion!

Tom Cretin, who took over as Acquisition Editor, is a career professional. Tom's no-nonsense style helps him deal with me and the other authors in the Craig Hunt Linux Library. Tom deserves special thanks for understanding that the technical quality and the production were the most important factor in creating this library.

The Production Editor for this book was Liz Burke—many thanks to Liz for her flexibility in working around my schedule. Suzanne Goraj was the Editor. I want to thank her for respecting my writing style while still doing a great job of improving my grammar. Randy Russell and Eric Gunnett were the Technical Editors. Their suggestions were very helpful in creating a more accurate book. Randy has a particularly fine eye for technical details.

I would like to thank all of the production people and artists for their work: Nike Nichols, Jennifer Campbell, Nancy Gutierrez, Nelson Kim, Yuri Rabinovich, Nanette Duffy, Nancy Rididough, Laurie O'Connell, and Andrea Fox.

I'd also like to thank Karen Rman of KJR Design in Washington, D.C. Karen is a professional photographer and designer. I can attest to the fact that she is one of the best. Only the best of photographers could make my mug look presentable for the cover of a book.

Twelve-hour days. No vacations. Not even weekends off. When the schedule gets tight and deadlines loom, I'm not the easiest person to live with. Kathy, thanks for living with me.
