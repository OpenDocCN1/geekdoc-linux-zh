The layout of the application is likely familiar to you. The **menu bar** contains all the familiar menus, and the **navigation toolbar** allows you to type in a URL, move back and forward between recently visited pages, reload a page, and stop a page from loading. The above screenshot also shows a **personal toolbar folder**, which contains shortcuts to your favorite websites – you can control the shortcuts on your personal toolbar folder by using the Manage Bookmarks option (which you’ll find under the Bookmarks menu):
