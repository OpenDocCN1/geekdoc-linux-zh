“ 1 – ” Indicates that you wish to extract from the first track to the end of the disc

# ### A GUI for CD Rippers and MP3 Encoders

To make your CD ripping and MP3 encoding activities easier, Red Hat Linux 9 includes the `grip` utility. You can fire up this GUI by selecting **Main Menu | Extras | Sound & Video | grip**. By default, it’s configured to use `cdparanoia` for extracting the audio files (as .wav files) and the `lame` executable for encoding the files into MP3 format.

As we’ve mentioned, the `lame` encoder is not included by default in Red Hat Linux 9, due to its decision to not support MP3 out of the box. However, you can download the `lame` encoder from [http://rpmfind.net/linux/freshrpms/redhat-8.0/lame](http://rpmfind.net/linux/freshrpms/redhat-8.0/lame), and you can get the `bladeenc` encoder from [http://bladeenc.mp3.no](http://bladeenc.mp3.no).

# #### Recording Files onto CD

The `cdrecord` utility is a command line utility for recording files onto writable CDs using an appropriate CD-Writer drive (CD-R or CD/RW). It is installed by default in Red Hat Linux 9, and its web site can be found at [http://www.fokus.gmd.de/research/cc/glone/employees/joerg.schilling/private/cdrecord.html](http://www.fokus.gmd.de/research/cc/glone/employees/joerg.schilling/private/cdrecord.html).
