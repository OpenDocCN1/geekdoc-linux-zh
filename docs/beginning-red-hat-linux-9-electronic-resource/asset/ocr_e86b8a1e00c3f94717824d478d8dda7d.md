Installing and configuring your Linux system is just the beginning. You should take some time to explore all of the resources presented in this chapter. Surf on over to www.osdn.com, search for your favorite applications, download and test them out. Eventually, you will get to a certain point where you want to implement your own open source software or modify one that exists to suit your needs. Then, you can decide on what programming language to use and how to develop an application. The bottom line is that you will be able to make your operating system run the way you want it to, instead of the other way around.
