An extent is a collection of contiguous Oracle blocks. When a segment is created, it will be assigned at least one extent called the initial extent. When a segment is dropped, its extents are released back to the tablespace for use by another segment. Figure 8.12 shows the relationship between the following table, called EMPLOYEE, its initial extent of 250KB, the tablespace called APPL_DATA, and its associated Datafiles, appl_data01.dbf and appl_data02.dbf:
