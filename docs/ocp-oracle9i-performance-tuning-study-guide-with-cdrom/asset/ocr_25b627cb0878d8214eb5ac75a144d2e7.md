The output shown in Figure 8.2 indicates that during the period between the two STATSPACK snapshots, the TEMP tablespace received the most read and write activity. Notice that unlike the output in VSFILESTAT, the STATSPACK output expresses the duration of the read and write activity in both seconds and milliseconds. This information is useful when determining where to physically locate Datafiles on the available disk drives, volume groups, and controllers.
