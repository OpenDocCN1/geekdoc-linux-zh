# WHY LITTLE BELGIUM HAS THE CENTER OF THE STAGE

Tonight our study concerns little Belgium, her people, and their part in this conflict. The reasons what they may, this little land stands in the center of the stage and holds the limelight. Once more David, armed with a sling, has gone up against ten Goliaths. It is an amazing spectacle, one of the smallest of the States, battling with the largest of the giants! Belgium has a standing army of 42,000 men, and Germany, with three reserves, perhaps 7,000,000 or 8,000,000. Without waiting for any assistance, this little Belgium band went against 2,000,000. It is as if a honey bee decided to attack an eagle come to loot its honeycomb. It is as if an antelope had turned against a lion. Belgium but 11,000 square miles of land, less than the States of Massachusetts, Rhode Island and Connecticut. Her population is 7,500,000, less than the single State of New York. Much of her soil is thin; her handicaps are heavy, but the industry of her people has turned the world into one vast flower and vegetable garden. The soil of Minnesota and the Dakotas is new soil, and yet our farmers average there but fifteen bushels of the wheat to the acre. Belgium's soil has been used for centuries, but it averages thirty—seven bushels of wheat to the acre. If we grow twenty-four bushels of barley on an acre of ground, Belgium grows fifty; she produces 300 bushels of potatoes, the Maine farmer harvests 90 bushels. Belgium's average population per square mile rises to 645 people. If Americans practised intensive farming; if the population of Texas was as dense as in Belgium—100,000,000 of the United States, Canada and Central America could all move to Texas, if our entire country was as densely populated as Belgium's, everybody in the world could live comfortably within the limits of our country.
