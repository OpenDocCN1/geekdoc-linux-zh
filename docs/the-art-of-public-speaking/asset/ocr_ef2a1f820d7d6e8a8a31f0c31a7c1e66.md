The speaker who pleads with real feeling for his own convictions will instill his feelings into his listeners. Sincerity, force, enthusiasm, and above all—these are the qualities that move multitudes and make appeals irresistible. They are of far greater importance than technical principles of delivery, grace of gesture, or polished enunciation—important as all these elements doubtless to be considered. Base your appeal on reason, but do not end in the basement—let the building rise, full of deep emotion and noble persuasion.

Oh, gentlemen, I am this day only the counsel of my client? No, no; I am the advocate of humanity—of yourselves—your homes—your wives—families—your little children. I am glad that this case exhibits such atrocity; unmarked as it by any mitigating feature, it may stop the rightful advance of this calamity; it will be met now, and marked with vengeance. If it be not, farewell to the virtues of your country; farewell to all confidence between man and man; farewell to unsuspecting reciprocal tenderness, without which marriage is but a consecrated curse. If oaths are to be violated, laws disregarded, friendship betrayed, humanity trampled, national and individual honor stained, and if a jury of fathers and husbands will give such discrepancy a passport to their homes, and wives, and daughters,—farewell to all that remains of Ireland! But I will not cast such a doubt upon the character of my country.
