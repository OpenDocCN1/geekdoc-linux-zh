19. Observe this passage from Newton's "Effective Speaking:"

"That man is a cynic. He sees goodness nowhere. He sneers at virtue,
sneers at love; to him the maiden trifling troth is an awful schemer,
and he sees in the mother's kiss nothing but an empty
conventionality."

Write, commit and deliver two similar passages based on your choice from
this list: (__a) "the egotist"; (__b) "the sensualist"; (__c) "the hypocrite";
(__d) "the timid man"; (__e) "the joker"; (__f) "the flirt"; (__g) "the
ungrateful woman"; (__h) "the mournful man." In both cases use the
principle of "Reference to Experience."
