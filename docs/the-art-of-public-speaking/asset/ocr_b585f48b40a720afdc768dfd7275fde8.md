8. Make a two--minute speech on any of the following general subjects, but you will find that your ideas will come more readily if you narrow your subject by taking some specific phase of it. For instance, instead of trying to speak on "Law" in general, take the proposition, "The Poor Man Cannot Afford to Prosecute;" instead of dwelling on "Leisure," show how modern speed is creating more leisure. In this way you may expand your subject list indefinitely.
