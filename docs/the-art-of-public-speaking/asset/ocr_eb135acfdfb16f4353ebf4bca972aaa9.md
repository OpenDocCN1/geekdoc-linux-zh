# CHAPTER XXVIII

Again, is hurtful to force the memory in hours of physical weakness or mental weariness. Health is the basis of the best mental action and the operation of memory is no exception.

Finally, do not become a slave to a system. Knowledge of a few simple facts of mind and memory will set you to work at the right end of the operation. Use these *principles*, whether included in a system or, but do not bind yourself to a method that tends to lay stress on the *way* to remember than on the development of memory itself. It is nothing short of ridiculous to memorize ten words in order to remember one fact.

The *Natural Laws of Memory Concentrated attention* at the time you wish to store the mind is the first step in memorizing—and the most important by far. You forgot the fourth of the list of articles your wife asked you to bring home chiefly because you allowed your attention to waver for an instant when she was telling you. Attention may not be concentrated attention. When a siphon is charged with gas it is sufficiently filled with the carbonic vapor to make its influence felt; a mind charged with an idea is charged to a degree sufficient to hold it. Too much charging will make the siphon burst; too much attention to trifles leads to insanity. Adequate attention, then, is the fundamental secret of remembering.

Generally we do not give a fact adequate attention when it does not seem important. Almost everyone has seen the seeds in an apple point, and has memorized the date of Washington's death. Most of us—perhaps wisely—forgot both. The little nick in the bark of a tree is healed and obliterated in a season, the gashes in the trees around Gettysburg are still apparent after fifty years. Impressions that are gathered lightly are soon obliterated. Only deep impressions can be recalled at all. Henry Ward Beecher said: "One intense hour will do more than dreamy years." To memorize ideas and words, concentrate on them until they are firmly fixed and deeply in your mind and accord to their true importance. LISTEN with the mind and you will remember.
