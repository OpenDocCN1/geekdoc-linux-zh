+   12. Read aloud the following with special attention to gesture:

His very throat was moral. You saw a good deal of it. You looked over a very low fence of white cravat (whereof no man had ever beheld the tie, for he fastened behind), and there lit, a valley between jutting heights of collar, serene and whiskerless before you. to say, on the part of Mr. Pecksniff,"There is no deception, ladies and gentlemen, all is peace, a holy calm pervades me." So did his hair, grizzled with an iron gray, which was all brushed off his forehead, and stood bolt upright, or slightly drooped in kindred action with his heavy eyelids. So did his person, which was soft and oily. In a word, even his plain black suit, and state of widow, and dangling double eye-glass, all tended to the same purpose, and cried aloud, "Behold the moral Pecksniff!"

> --CHARLES DICKENS, Martin Chuzzlewit.
