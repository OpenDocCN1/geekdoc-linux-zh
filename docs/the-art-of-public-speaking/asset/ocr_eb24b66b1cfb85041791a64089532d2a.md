INFLUENCING NARRATION

The art of narration is the art of writing in hooks and eyes. The principle consists of making the appropriate thought follow the appropriate thought, the proper fact the proper fact; in first preparing the mind for what is to come, and then letting it come.

--WALTER BAGEHOT, Literary Studies.
