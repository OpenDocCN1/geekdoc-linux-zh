1. In the following, speak the words "long, long while" very slowly; the rest of the sentence is spoken in moderately rapid tempo.

When you and I behind the Veil are past, Oh but the first, long long while the world shall last, Which of our coming and departure heeds, As the seven seas should heed a pebble cast.

Note: In the following selections the passages that should be given a fast tempo are in italics; those that should be given in a slow tempo are in small capitals. Practise these selections, and then others, changing from fast to slow tempo on different parts, carefully noting the effect.

2. No MIRABEAU, NAPOLEON, BURNS, CROMWELL, NO man ADEQUATE TO DO, ANYTHING but is first of all in RIGHT EARNEST about it—what I call_ A SINCERE_man. I should say_ SINCITY, a GREAT, DEEP, GENUINE SINCERITY, is the first CHARACTERISTIC of a man in any way HEROIC. The sincerity that calls A_itself sincere. Ah no. That is a very poor matter indeed—A SHALL, BRAGGART, CONSCIOUS _sincerity, oftentimes _SELF–CONCEIT _mainly. The_GREAT MAN'S SINCERITY is of a kind that he CANNOT SPEAK OF. Is NOT CONSCIOUS of.—THOMAS CARLYLE.

3. TRUE WORTH is in BEING—not SEEING—in doing each day that goes by SOME GOOD, not in DREAMING GREAT THINGS_to do and by. For however say in their_BLINDNESS, and in spite of the FOLLIES of YOUTH, there is nothing KINGLY as KINDNESS, and nothing so ROYAL as TRUTH.—Anonymous.
