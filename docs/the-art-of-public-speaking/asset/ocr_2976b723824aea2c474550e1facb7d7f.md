He cannot get the results of his work if he do better work or higher work or work longer; that is a dangerous thing, and in order to get every labor man free and every American equal to every other American, let the labor man ask what he is worth and get it—not let any capitalist say to him: "You shall work for me for half of what you are worth;" You shall work for the capitalist for half your worth." Be a man, be independent, and then shall the labor man find the road ever open from poverty to wealth. The other difficulty that the labor union has to consider, and this problem they have to solve themselves, is the kind of orators who come and talk to them about the oppressive rich. I can in my dreams recite the oration I have heard again again under under circumstances. My life has been with the labor man. I am a labor man myself. I have often, in their assemblies, heard the speech of the man who has been invited to address the labor union. The man gets up before the assembled company of honest labor man and he begins by saying: "Oh, you honest, industrious labor men, who have furnished all the capital of the world, who have built all the palaces and constructed all the railroads and covered the ocean with her steamships. Oh, you labor men! You are nothing but slaves; you are down in the dust by the capitalist who is gloating over you as he enjoys his beautiful estates and as he has his banks filled with gold, and every dollar he owns is coined out of the heart's blood of the honest labor man." Now, that is a lie, and you know it is a lie; and yet it is the kind of speech that are all the time hearing, representing the capitalists as wicked and the labor men so enslaved. Why, how wrong it is! Let the man who loves his flag and believes in American principles endeavor with all his soul to bring the capitalist and the labor man together until they stand side by side, and arm in arm, and for the common good of humanity.

He is an enemy to his country who sets capital against labor or labor against capital.
