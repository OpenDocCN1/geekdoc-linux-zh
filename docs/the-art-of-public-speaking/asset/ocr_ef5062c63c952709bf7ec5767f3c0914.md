"Clouds and darkness are around God's throne. The spirit of evil caused this war, but the Spirit of God may bring good out of it, just as the summer repairs the ravaged of winter. Meanwhile the heart bleeds for Belgium. For Brussels, the third most beautiful city in Europe! For Louvain, once with its libraries, cathedrals, paintings, missals, manuscripts—now a ruin. Alas! for the ruined harvests and the smoking villages! Alas, for the Cathedral that is a heap, and the library that is a ruin. When the angel of happiness was there stalk Famine and Death. Gone of Grotius! Perished the paintings of Rubens! Ruined is Louvain. Where the wheat waived, now the hillsides billowy with graves. But let us believe that God reigns. Perchance Belgium is slain like the Saviour, but militarism may die like Satan. Without shedding of innocent blood there is no remission of sins through tyranny and greed. There is no wine without the crushing of the grapes from the tree of life. So Liberty, God's dear child, will stand within the scene and comfort the desolate. Falling upon the great world's altar stairs, in this hour when wisdom is ignorance, and the strongest man clutches at dust and straw, let us believe with faith victorious over tears, that some time God will gather broken-hearted Belgium into His arms and her as a Father comforteth his well-beloved child.
