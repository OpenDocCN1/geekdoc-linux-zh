# CONTROL YOUR
CONNECTION

The Modem Lights
applet lets you connect
and disconnect by clicking
on the single green light
in the center. You can
also constantly monitor
the status of your modem.
■ To the left of the on/off
button, the upper display
shows data transmission
rates; the lower display
relates to data received.

![](bbox=[0.42, 0.1, 0.95, 0.26])

The Modem Lights applet

■ To the right of the
on/off button, the higher
figure shows the total
connection time. The
lower figure shows the
number of bytes read
per second.

■ You can customize the
applet by right-clicking
on it and choosing
Properties to open the
Settings dialog box. Here,
you can change a number
of the applet’s settings.
