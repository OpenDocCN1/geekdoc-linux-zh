# MULTIMEDIA

+   - To take full advantage of the multimedia programs, you will need to have installed and configured the appropriate hardware (i.e., CD-ROM player, CD-writer, sound card, and sound/video recording equipment).

+   - The internet is a good source for Linux software for recording, editing, and playing audio and video clips. However, the default applications supplied here provide a good starting point for your collection.
