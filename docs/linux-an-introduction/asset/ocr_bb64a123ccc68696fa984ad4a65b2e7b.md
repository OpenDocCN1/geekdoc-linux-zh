# FTP CLIENTS

+   * FTP (file transfer protocol) clients provide a means of logging on to a remote site from your PC and transferring files to or from that site – provided, of course, that you have the appropriate access privileges required by the site.

+   * Most anonymous FTP sites simply require your email address for permission to browse and download. This simple FTP client makes the process easy to grasp and execute.
