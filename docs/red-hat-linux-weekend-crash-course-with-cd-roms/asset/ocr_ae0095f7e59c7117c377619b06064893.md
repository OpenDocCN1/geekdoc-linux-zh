Red Hat Linux includes extensive built-in networking capabilities. In particular, Red Hat Linux supports TCP/IP (Transmission Control Protocol/Internet Protocol) networking over several physical interfaces such as Ethernet cards, serial ports, and parallel ports. (TCP/IP is a suite of networking protocols used for information exchange over the Internet. You do not need to know the details of TCP/IP to set up and use TCP/IP networking on your Red Hat Linux system.) You typically use an Ethernet network for your local area network (LAN). TCP/IP networking over the serial port enables you to connect to other networks by dialing out over a modem.

This session focuses on Ethernet LANs. You learn how to set up, configure, and monitor an Ethernet TCP/IP network. Much of what you learn also applies to TCP/IP networking over a dial-up modem, which is the focus of the next session.
