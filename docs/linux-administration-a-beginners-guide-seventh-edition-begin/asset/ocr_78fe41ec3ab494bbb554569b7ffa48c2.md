rsyslog Templates
As mentioned, ryslog relies on the use of templates, and the templates define the format of logged messages. The use of templates is what allows ryslog to produce log files that are formatted similarly to the traditional format that is produced by the older and traditional syslog. Templates that support the syslog log format are hard-coded in ryslog and are used by default.
A sample template that supports the use of the ryslog message format is here:

```
$template TraditionalFormat,"%timegenerated% %HOSTNAME% %syslogtag% %msg\n",<options>
```
