When choosing kernel options, take into consideration the specific hardware on your system and need for protocols and services. The following are some general guidelines that help you to choose kernel options:

+   - Under “Code Maturity Level options”, check “Prompt for development and/or incomplete code/drivers”. If you fail to check these options, certain essential services (most probably nfsd) may fail to work correctly.
+   - Optimize for your processor family, and turn the math emulation support off.
+   - Modularize all the possible hardware support.
+   - Turn off the ISDN and IrDA support for old CD-ROM drivers.
