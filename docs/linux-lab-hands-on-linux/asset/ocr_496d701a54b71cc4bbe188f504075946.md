# # ■ Configuring The Zone Files

Once we have define new zone in “named.conf”, we have to create forward and reverse zone file having same name as mentioned in named.conf.

By default, your zone files are located in the /var/named or /var/named/chroot/var/named or /etc/bind directories depending on your Linux distribution.

Each zone file contains a variety of records (SOA, NS, MX, A, and CNAME) that govern different areas of BIND (named).

Take a closer look at spit.ac.in.forward zone file in Figure 2:

                        604800     ; expire after 1 week
                        86400 )    ; minimum TTL of 1 day
