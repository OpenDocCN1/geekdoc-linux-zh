# LINUX

Learn with Linux for classes 1 to 8 is a computer science series for the primary and middle school, focusing on free and open-source software (FOSS). The curriculum for this course has been so designed that it conforms to the recommendations of the National Curriculum Framework 2005. Age-appropriate syllabi in each book links computer science education with real-life experiences. The aim of this series is to promote critical thinking and creativity amongst learners. The series is based on the Edubuntu distribution – a user-friendly, educational version of the Linux-based Ubuntu operating system.

# Key Features

+   ■ Makes appropriate use of screenshots to present concepts in a lucid and child-friendly manner.

+   ■ Think Tank within each chapter poses application-based questions, encouraging further exploration and thought on the concepts covered.

+   ■ Activity Sheet provides practical exercises integrating different subjects using the tools learned.

+   ■ Worksheet contains questions, both objective and descriptive, for revision.

+   ■ Teacher's Note at relevant places gives useful suggestions to teachers on teaching methodologies and directions on how to explore a topic further.

# About the Authors

Neeraj K. Bedhotiya is the principal of Mayor School, Ajmer, and has been one of the pioneers in introducing the use of FOSS in school education. He has been an educator for more than 20 years, including 11 years at The Doon School, Dehra Dun and a year at Marlborough College, UK.

Deepak Bhargava, currently the IT Co-ordinator at Mayor School, Ajmer, has nearly 10 years of teaching experience at the senior secondary level and has been instrumental in setting up the FOSS network at his school.

Gunjan Kotwari, faculty member of the computer science department at Mayor School, Ajmer, has a Masters in Information Technology, and has effectively implemented the switch to FOSS in her classes.
