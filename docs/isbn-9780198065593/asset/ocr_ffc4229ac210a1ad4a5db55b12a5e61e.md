# ## Mouse

![](bbox=[0.32, 0.13, 0.66, 0.32])

The device next to the keyboard is called a Mouse.

+   1. Moving the mouse causes the mouse pointer, I, to move on your screen.
+   2. Move the mouse up and down on the table top or the mouse pad. Notice that the pointer, I, also moves up or down on your screen.
+   3. Move the mouse to the left and to the right on the table top or the mouse pad. Notice that the pointer, I, also moves to the left or to the right on your screen.
+   4. Move the mouse so the pointer, I, is somewhere on the word “Help” that you just typed.
+   5. To click, put your right hand on the mouse. Put your index finger (the finger that you use to point) on the left button, press it and release the button.
+   6. Notice that the cursor (blinking vertical line) moves to where you click the mouse. Press the letter X. Letter x appears at the place where the blinking cursor was seen.
