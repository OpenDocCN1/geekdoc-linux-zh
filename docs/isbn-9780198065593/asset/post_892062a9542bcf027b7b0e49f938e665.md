## Preface

*Learn with Linux* for classes 1 to 8 is a computer science series for the primary and middle school, which focuses on free and open-source software (FOSS). FOSS refers to software that is liberally licensed under the GNU General Public License, granting users the right to study, change, and improve its design through free availability of its source code. Therefore, FOSS users have fundamental control and flexibility.

Our introduction to FOSS transpired at a training program attended by one of us (Neeraj K. Bedhotiya) in 2004 at IIM (A), which enthusiastically endorsed the use of FOSS in school education. Thereafter, we experimented with this option in our school (Mayoor School, Ajmer), and this series is an outcome of our experiences over the last five years.

The search for an appropriate open-source software led us to the Edubuntu distribution – a user-friendly, educational version of the Linux-based Ubuntu operating system. A comprehensive curriculum was then designed in conformity with the recommendations of the National Curriculum Framework 2005. The aim of this series is to promote critical thinking and creativity amongst learners, and age-appropriate syllabi in each book links computer science education with real-life experiences. Computer science is not treated as an isolated subject, rather a computer is projected as a tool allowing integrated learning and reinforcement of concepts from all core subjects.

### Key Features

1. Makes appropriate use of screenshots to present concepts in a lucid and child-friendly manner.
2. **Think Tank** within each chapter poses application-based questions, encouraging further exploration and thought on the concepts covered.
3. **Activity Sheet** provides practical exercises integrating different subjects using the tools learned.
4. **Worksheet** contains questions, both objective and descriptive, for revision.
5. **Teacher's Note** at relevant places gives useful suggestions to teachers on teaching methodologies and directions on how to explore a topic further.

We invite you to share your experiences with FOSS and this series with us. Your constructive feedback on improving the content and coverage of these books would be greatly welcomed.

## Introduction to Edubuntu

### Introduction to Linux

Linux is a free Unix-type operating system originally created by Linus Torvalds with the assistance of developers around the world.

Linux has an official mascot, Tux, the Linux penguin, which was selected by Linus Torvalds to represent the image he associates with the operating system.

![](img/9a5b9efff92da1263a1b04aa9d4ae262_8_0.png)

### Linux for the Young

“Ubuntu” is an ancient African word, meaning “humanity to others”. The Edubuntu Linux distribution brings the spirit of Ubuntu to schools, through its customized school environment.
