# ### Rain is falling

Rain is falling down,
tip, tap, tip, tap,
Children like rain and say,
tip, tap, tip, tap,
Rain is falling on my window pane,
Splish. splash, splish, splash,
Rain rain come again.
Children like to play in rain.
And they say. ‘Hurray’, ‘Hurray’.
Rain has made our day.
