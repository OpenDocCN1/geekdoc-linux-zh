# Shift key

![](bbox=[0.05, 0.15, 0.95, 0.2])

Pressing the [Shift] key along with a letter key makes the letters appear in capitals.

Type the word "Help" by doing the following:

+   1. Press and hold down the [Shift] key as you press the "H" key. Release the [Shift] key. (The letter "H" appears in capitals on the screen.)
+   2. Press the "E" key, then press "L", then press "P". (The word "Help" appears on the screen.)
