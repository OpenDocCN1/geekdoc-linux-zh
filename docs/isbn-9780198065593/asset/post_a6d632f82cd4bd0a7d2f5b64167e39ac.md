
## Introduction to Edubuntu

Edubuntu is a complete Linux-based operating system, freely available with community-based support.

The Edubuntu community is built on the following ideas enshrined in the Edubuntu Manifesto.

- Software should be available free of charge.
- Software tools should be usable by people in their local language and despite any disabilities.
- People should have the freedom to customize and alter their software in whatever way they see fit.

![](img/9a5b9efff92da1263a1b04aa9d4ae262_9_0.png)

### Edubuntu Desktop

These freedoms make Edubuntu fundamentally different from proprietary software: not only are the tools you need available free of charge, you, and thousands of developers around the world, have the right to modify and build upon your software until it works exactly the way you want it to.

## Fun with Math — TuxMath

### Tux, of Math Command or TuxMath

TuxMath is a simple arithmetic educational software starring Tux, the Linux penguin. Tux has to fire phasers at calculations that are falling down from the sky. The student has to type the right result and push [Spacebar/Enter] to destroy the calculation before it touches the ground. The math involved covers addition, subtraction, multiplication, and division. It is a great way for kids and adults to stretch their minds.

### Features of TuxMath

- Cute, animated graphics
- Sound effects and music
- Optional on-screen input, for children who can’t use a keyboard (great for touchscreens!)

### Objective of TuxMath

The objective of this software is to develop oral and mental math skills. In “Tux, of Math Command,” Commander Tux defends his cities from an attack of math equations. You can play the part of Commander Tux. Comets are hurtling towards your cities, and you must destroy them by solving their equations.

![](img/9a5b9efff92da1263a1b04aa9d4ae262_11_0.png)

### Gameplay Overview

Tux, the Linux penguin, stands at the controls of a laser defense system ready to defend his fellow penguins from falling comets. His companions are tucked away safely in igloos. Two igloos on each side of the defense station give Tux the task of defending a total of four other penguins.

Each incoming comet has a math problem attached. Entering the correct solution will cause the laser to destroy the comet. Entering an incorrect answer will result in a miss.

This simple gameplay is entertaining, which becomes more interesting when a comet hits the igloo. The first comet strike will partially melt the penguin's igloo.

![](img/9a5b9efff92da1263a1b04aa9d4ae262_12_0.png)

A second strike causes the igloo to melt completely, forcing the penguin to run away. No penguins are ever harmed; they simply walk away once their igloo is gone.

![](img/9a5b9efff92da1263a1b04aa9d4ae262_13_0.png)

Giving young players two tries before the penguin flees helps to prevent them from becoming frustrated while still providing challenge.

### How to Play

#### Destroying Comets

As the comets fall towards your cities, you must solve their equations.

To destroy a comet, do the following:
First, figure out the answer to the equation.
For example, "3 × 4" would be "12".

Second, type in the answer. As you type numbers on the keyboard, they will appear in the "LED"-style display at the bottom center of the screen.

Finally, press [Enter/Return/Spacebar].

The comet that has the number you entered as its answer will be shot down by Tux, the penguin.

Note:

1. Sometimes more than one comet will have the same answer. In this case, the comet closest to your cities will be destroyed first.
2. After typing [Enter/Return], the "LED"-style display will automatically reset to "000", so you can answer the next equation!

![](img/9a5b9efff92da1263a1b04aa9d4ae262_16_0.png)

#### Correcting your Answer

If you make a mistake, you can press [Backspace/Delete] and the "LED"-style display at the bottom center of the screen will reset to "000".

### Losing a City

If a comet crashes into one of your cities before you had the chance to answer its equation correctly, the city's shield will be destroyed. If the city is hit by another comet, it will be destroyed completely.

*Note:* The correct answer to the calculation is always displayed as the equation hits the ground.

Once you lose all of your cities, the game will end.

## OpenOffice.org Word Processor

### Word Processing

Word processing means using a computer to create, edit, and print documents. To perform word processing, you need a special program called a Word Processor.

### Advantages of a Word Processor

- You can make changes or edit without retyping the entire document.
- You can apply many formatting styles to the text in the document.
- You can include graphics in the document.
- You can save the document for future use.
- You can print the document.

OpenOffice.org Word Processor is a free and open source word processor software which is a component of the OpenOffice.org software package.

> **Teacher's Note**
>
> The teacher can give an introduction to the terms like document, edit, formatting, graphics, save, open, and print. She can open a previously created file, take a print and show it to the learners. She can explain the tasks that can be done using a Word Processor, like making shopping lists, writing letters, creating graphics, tables, check for spellings and grammar, perform calculations etc.
