# Title Screen

On this screen, you can either choose to play the game, change the gameplay options, view the credits, or quit.

Use the [Up] and [Down] arrow keys to select what you wish to do, and then press [Enter/Return]. Otherwise, use the mouse to click the menu item. Pressing [Escape] will quit the program.
