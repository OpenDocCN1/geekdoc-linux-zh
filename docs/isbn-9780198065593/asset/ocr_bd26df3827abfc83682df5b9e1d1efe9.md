# Educational Softwares in Edubuntu

Edubuntu offers a variety of educational programs. Some of them are as follows:

+   * **Fun with Math** -- TuxMath is an educational arcade game starring Tux, the Linux mascot! Based on the classic arcade game “Missile Command,” Tux must defend his cities by solving arithmetic problems.

+   * **Office Suite** -- OpenOffice.org is a complete office suite similar to the Microsoft Office suite. It features a word processor, as well as a spreadsheet, presentation, drawing, database, and mathematical formula applications. OpenOffice.org can open and convert most Microsoft Office documents and has support for many languages.
