I'm grateful to the entire team at Pearson for their help and feedback, but there are a few special thanks: Shelley Johnston loosed Andrew and I into writing this book, and we have had a great time. Without Shelley, this book would not be what it is. Damon Jordan worked hard to make sure that readers like you get the most value from our content. I think you will agree he did a great job!

On the technical side, we were blessed to have Dallas Releford scouring our every word, adding suggestions, making improvements, and ruthlessly correcting mistakes. Dallas is a supremely experienced Linux guru who eats, sleeps, and breathes Fedora. I hope I get to work with him again.

Our production editor was Megan Wade, and she turned our stilted, broken English into flowing prose. At the most basic level, she checks and corrects spelling, grammar, and style, but she went above and beyond the call of duty with this particular comment I got back during the author review process: “The title of The The's album is actually two words—Mind Bomb (I double-checked my copy of the album to make sure).” What author could ask for more?

Outside of Pearson, I owe a debt of gratitude to Nick Veitch at *Linux Format* magazine. It is because of him that I get paid to play around with Linux all day, which is my ideal job. I guess he also taught me a thing or two about writing, but I consider that a fair trade for all the lessons I have given him in *Unreal Tournament*!

My family's contribution to this work has been immense. My wife, Ildiko, managed to put up with months of me doing no housework, although I think that the minute I am finished with this it is back to the grindstone for me (why do you think these acknowledgements are so long?). Finally, none of this work would have been possible without the grace of God. Andrew and I have been blessed to be able to write this book, and I pray it blesses you even more.
