# Schedule Maintenance Tasks

An important part of your responsibilities as system administrator is to maintain a secure and uncluttered system. To do this effectively, actions must regularly take place regarding file system backups and purges and other operating system maintenance tasks. Regular updates must be made to software and hardware assets to preserve their currency and value. The security of your system is dependent on how well your users follow the security guidelines you establish, and how well you schedule the maintenance that you need to perform to keep the system and hardware working at peak levels.
