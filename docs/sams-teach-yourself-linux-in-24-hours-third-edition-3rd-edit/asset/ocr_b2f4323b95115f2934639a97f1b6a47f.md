# Q&A

Q Besides protecting the operating system with regular backups, passwords, and file permissions, is there anything else that can be done to keep the computer system safe?

A It is always a good idea to keep the computer in a clean environment and away from static electricity. Dirt and dust can be a computer's worst enemy. These particles build up on the electronic parts and can cause your computer to overheat or short out.

If someone who is unfamiliar with the system uses the computer, take time to properly train him so that he knows how to access his user account and where to save his files.

Another good idea is to plug your computer into an uninterruptible power supply (UPS). A UPS protects your computer during power surges and electrical storms, and protects your data by giving you time after a power outage to save your work and safely shut off the system.

Q Several times during the hour, it was mentioned that a system administrator is needed to do the job of maintaining the computer system. Please explain the job of the system administrator a bit more.

A The system administrator is responsible for keeping a computer system up and running. The job of the system administrator begins with planning the system and includes everything from setting up the computer and installing the operating system and application software, to keeping the system running, planning for upgrades, and restoring the system in case of a loss of equipment or data.
