# Q&A

Q What are some of the different ways people are using their Linux systems to help them manage their affairs and conduct their business?

A Right now, the most popular use of the Linux operating system is to run corporate networks and Internet servers. However, individuals are beginning to find new ways to put Linux to work for them. Many graphic artists are enjoying the availability of reasonably priced software tools. Find out what the artist community is doing with Linux at www.linuxartist.org. Musicians will find some interesting things going on with MIDI music at www.bright.net/~dlphip/linuxsound. Parents beware...your child might want to download the drumming software at this site. Scientists and engineers will find some tools of the trade at sai.kachinatech.com/index.shtml. If you often gaze at the stars, try some of the astronomy tools at linux.about.com/compute/linux/library/metalab/blmeta_science_astronomy.htm.

For those who need help keeping organized, there are many different personal information programs (such as calendars, diaries, and address books) from which to choose. Manage your finances with the Checkbook Balancer (CBB) available at cbb.surgeforge.net or GNUCash at www.gnucash.org.

Q What computer equipment definitely does not work with the Linux operating system?

A To put it briefly, Winmodems and Winprinters. These devices depend on operating system-specific drivers, which are developed for the Microsoft Windows operating system only.
