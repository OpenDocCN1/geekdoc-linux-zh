Screensavers activate after a period of no computer activity.

> If you are away from your computer, it might be possible for others to work in your user account if you don't log out of the account. To protect yourself, set a password for the screensaver by placing a check in the Require password checkbox. When the screensaver is active, the password for your user account is needed to disable the screensaver and return to the desktop.
