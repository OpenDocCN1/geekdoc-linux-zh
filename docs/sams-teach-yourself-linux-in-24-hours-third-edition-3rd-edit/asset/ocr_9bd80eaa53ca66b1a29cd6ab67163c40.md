# The Screensavers They Are A-Changin’

Screensavers are a great way to hide what’s on your desktop when you are aren’t working or are away from your desk. You’ll find all the available screensavers in the Screensaver configuration module. There are a number of settings (see the Screensaver configuration module in Figure A.6) that you can apply to any screensaver you select.

Even though you can specify an amount of time your system must be idle before the screensaver starts, you can make the screensaver appear at your command. Right-click on any corner in the sample display and select Save Screen. Now, when you leave the computer and want to start the screensaver, move the mouse pointer to the same corner on your screen. The screensaver starts immediately, without a delay.
