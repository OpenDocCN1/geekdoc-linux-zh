# Cycling Through Display Modes

After you have a working X server and the picture on your monitor looks good, you can easily change the screen resolution and color depth. Just press Ctrl+Alt and the plus (+) key on the number keypad at the far-right side of the keyboard.

Different distributions might also have a utility that performs this same function. In Linux-Mandrake, you can use the Xdrakres utility (see Figure 18.5) to change resolution and color depth. To find Xdrakres, open the DrakConf tool and click the Change X Resolution button.

# Q&A

Q The display isn't centered on the monitor correctly. A black line runs along one edge of the screen and part of the desktop is hidden on the other side. How can I adjust this?

A The first thing that you should do is adjust the position controls on the monitor. Most monitors have buttons on the front that enable you to change the size of the picture or to move the picture right, left, up, and down. If that doesn't work, try xvidtune. This program can move the display around on the screen. To use xvidtune, open a terminal emulator, type xvidtune, and press Enter. Read the warning message and click OK. Click the Auto button. You can then move the display up, down, left, or right. You can also make it wider, narrower, shorter, or taller.
