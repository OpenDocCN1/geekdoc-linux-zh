# What Is GNU/Linux?

Linux is a multiuser and multitasking computer operating system that is a free UNIX clone. What makes it GNU/Linux is the addition of the wealth of free utilities and software from Richard Stallman's Free Software Foundation and the GNU Project. These applications include text editors, utilities, tools, operating system upgrades, and much more. The valuable contributions of programmers from around the world are combined to produce quality applications for everything from a moon phase clock to professional audio/video creation and editing software. New multimedia players for everything are available to you for a free download. Office production tools such as word processors, graphics applications, and office suites are included in many Linux distributions or available for the download time.

# Why Teach Yourself Linux?

The Linux operating system and the associated bank of free software represent a truly inexpensive and effective alternative for the small business and home office. GNU/Linux offers an inexpensive networking solution to keep your enterprise together, strong applications to maintain the best work flow levels, and an easy configuration and adaptation to fit your needs exactly.
