# What’s on the Disc

The companion CD-ROM contains Linux-Mandrake 7.1 GPL Edition.

# Installing Linux-Mandrake from the CD-ROM

+   1. Insert the disc in the CD drive.
+   2. Restart your computer.
+   3. You may need to change your BIOS settings to boot from the CD-ROM. Typically, you enter your BIOS setup program with the F2 or DEL keys during the boot sequence.
+   4. Make your changes (if any) and exit the BIOS setup utility.
+   5. If your CD drive is capable of booting from CD-ROMs, you will boot into the Linux-Mandrake setup program.
+   6. Follow the onscreen prompts to complete the installation.

# Creating the Linux-Mandrake Boot Floppy from Within Windows

+   1. Format one 1.44MB floppy.
+   2. Double-click on My computer, then double-click on the icon representing your CD-ROM drive.
+   3. Double-click on the DOSUTILS folder.
+   4. Now, double-click on rawritewin.
+   5. Enter CDROM.IMG in the Image File prompt and make sure your boot floppy drive is chosen, typically A:
+   6. You may now click on the Write button.
+   7. Leave the boot floppy in your floppy drive and restart your computer.
+   8. You may need to change your BIOS settings to boot from the floppy drive. Typically, you enter your BIOS setup program with the F2 or DEL keys during the boot sequence.
+   9. Make your changes (if any) and exit the BIOS setup utility.
+   10. If your computer is set up properly, you will boot into the Linux-Mandrake setup program.
+   11. Follow the onscreen prompts to complete the installation.
