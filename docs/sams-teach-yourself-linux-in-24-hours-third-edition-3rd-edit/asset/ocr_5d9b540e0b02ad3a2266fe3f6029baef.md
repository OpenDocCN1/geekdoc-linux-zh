# Creating the Linux-Mandrake Boot Floppy from Within DOS or a DOS Prompt

+   1. Format one 1.44MB floppy.
+   2. Type
+      D:
+      and press the ENTER key. If your CD-ROM drive is not D:, substitute the drive letter in our example with the drive letter that corresponds to your system.
+   3. Type
+      cd \dosutils
+      and press the ENTER key.
+   4. Type
+      rawrite
+      and press the ENTER key.
+   5. When prompted to do so, type
+      cdrom.img
+      and press the ENTER key.
+   6. When prompted to do so, type in the drive letter of the disk you are going to prepare and press <ENTER>. Since you are going to be booting from this disk, it's typically A:
+   7. Leave the boot floppy in your floppy drive and restart your computer.
+   8. You may need to change your BIOS settings to boot from the floppy drive. Typically, you enter your BIOS setup program with the F2 or DEL keys during the boot sequence.
+   9. Make your changes (if any) and exit the BIOS setup utility.
+   10. If your computer is set up properly, you will boot into the Linux-Mandrake setup program.
+   11. Follow the onscreen prompts to complete the installation.
