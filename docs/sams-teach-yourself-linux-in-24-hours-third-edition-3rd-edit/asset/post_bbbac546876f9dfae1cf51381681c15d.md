## Introduction

Hello. Welcome to Sams Teach Yourself Linux in 24 Hours. We're sure that you have heard a lot of Linux hype, and we'll even bet that you've heard both success and failure stories from people who have already tried Linux. Don't you already have a computer running the Microsoft Windows or Apple Macintosh operating system? So, why should you subject yourself to all the hassle and effort to learn about an alternative computer operating system?

Well, every now and then a new idea comes along that offers an unbeatable opportunity to grab hold of a good thing and make it your own. Linux is a complete UNIX-like operating system. It's a whole laboratory where you can learn system administration, programming, application creation, and almost anything else there is to know about in the world of computers. GNU/Linux creates an environment that provides all the tools, documentation, and built-in help that you need to teach yourself some valuable skills and build on your existing knowledge of computers and networking.

### What Is GNU/Linux?

Linux is a multiuser and multitasking computer operating system that is a free UNIX clone. What makes it GNU/Linux is the addition of the wealth of free utilities and software from Richard Stallman's Free Software Foundation and the GNU Project. These applications include text editors, utilities, tools, operating system upgrades, and much more. The valuable contributions of programmers from around the world are combined to produce quality applications for everything from a moon phase clock to professional audio/video creation and editing software. New multimedia players for everything are available to you for a free download. Office production tools such as word processors, graphics applications, and office suites are included in many Linux distributions or available for the download time.

### Why Teach Yourself Linux?

The Linux operating system and the associated bank of free software represent a truly inexpensive and effective alternative for the small business and home office. GNU/Linux offers an inexpensive networking solution to keep your enterprise together, strong applications to maintain the best work flow levels, and an easy configuration and adaptation to fit your needs exactly.

What gives Linux an advantage over other operating systems? Let's look at a few examples:

- Linux can bring your old equipment back up to par to work with your new equipment, and extend its useful life. You will quickly recognize the potential for using Linux to revitalize and restore usefulness to obsolete or outdated equipment.
- Linux can do much more with the equipment you have. By allowing you full access to the power of your computer, Linux enables you to manage the operating system and run applications better and faster using just the equipment that you already have.
- When you install and configure Linux on your computer, you might never have to buy another piece of application software. Most people from the Macintosh Windows worlds are unaware of the mountain of free software that exists for the GNU/Linux user. There are myriad professional and scientific applications contained with the Linux distribution, including many that surpass their commercial equivalents in power and performance.
- Linux provides you with a readily adaptable TCP/IP server package, so establishing and administering your network—one especially tailored to serve your needs—is made simple.

### Why Use Linux-Mandrake to Learn About Linux

Remember the old days of DOS? How about the original IBM PC (back before hard drives) and when a Commodore 64 was hot stuff? Is there a CP/M machine stuck in a back closet that you keep 'for old times sake'? Perhaps your grandma told you stories about computers that didn't even have colored pictures on the screen? Yes folks, at one time you had to type in things on a black or green screen in cryptic little half words and initials to get your computer to do anything; no mouse, no GUI, no apple, nothing but a command line with a flashing cursor.

Things have changed since then of course, even for Linux.

Even though there are similarities between the different GNU/Linux distributions, it might become confusing when dealing with the individual distributions. We want to show you how to operate Linux, no matter which distribution you might choose. We chose Linux-Mandrake because it is less cumbersome and more user-friendly than most distributions. Linux-Mandrake offers several installation methods. You might elect to take one of the simple installation routes if you want to install a basic system and get up and running quickly. Or, you might want to dig in and get involved in the installation by selecting packages and preconfiguring your system during the installation process.

Linux-Mandrake also contains some powerful tools and utilities that make it easier to work with Linux.
