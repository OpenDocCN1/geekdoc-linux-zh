# ### System Requirements

Make sure that your computer meets the minimum system requirements listed in this section. If your computer doesn't match up to most of these requirements, you may have a problem using the contents of the media that come with this book.

# **For Linux or Windows Systems**

+   - PC with a Pentium-class processor running at 400 MHz or faster; for best performance, we recommend a Pentium 4 or AMD equivalent running at 700 MHz or better.
+   - At least 256 MB of total RAM installed on your computer; for best performance, we recommend at least 512 MB.
+   - Ethernet Network Interface Card (NIC) or modem with a speed of at least 28,800 bps
+   - A CD-ROM drive if you are testing CentOS with the Live CD or a DVD drive if you are installing from the DVD disk
+   - A VGA graphics card that is capable of at least 1024 × 768 resolution
+   - At least 3 GB of available disk space if installing CentOS
