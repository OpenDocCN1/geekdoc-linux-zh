# Mozilla Firefox

One of the most popular web browsers, and in fact the default web browser in Ubuntu, is Mozilla Firefox (see Figure 8.1). Built on a solid code base derived from the Mozilla Suite, Firefox offers a new lease on life to surfing the Internet. More than 200 million people have downloaded Firefox since its release in late 2004, and it has grabbed significant market share from Internet Explorer.

In Ubuntu you will find Firefox under the Applications, Internet menu at the top of your screen. An even simpler way to start Firefox is to click the small Firefox icon next to the Actions menu. Either way, you will open Firefox.
