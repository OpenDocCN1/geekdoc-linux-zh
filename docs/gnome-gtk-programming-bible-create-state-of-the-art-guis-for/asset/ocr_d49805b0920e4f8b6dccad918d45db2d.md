Each widget contained by the notebook is called a *page* because, similar to a book, all the other *pages* are hidden by the visible one. The one that is currently visible is called the *active page*.

There are a couple of size constraints. If the tabs are on the top or bottom, the notebook is at least as wide as the button. Likewise, if the tabs are on the left or right, the notebook is at least as tall as the buttons are. Also, the notebook's window is always large enough to display the largest widget it contains.
