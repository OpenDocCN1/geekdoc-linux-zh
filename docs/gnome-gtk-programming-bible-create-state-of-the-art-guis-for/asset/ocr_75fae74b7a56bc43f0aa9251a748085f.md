# # The Applet Menu

Selecting an applet with the right mouse button brings up the menu shown
in Figure 15-3.

![](bbox=[0.11, 0.235, 0.55, 0.28])

The default menu has two selections—the applet can be removed from the panel
or it can be moved to another location. You can add entries to the menu and assign
callbacks to them. The following applet adds two entries to its menu, resulting in
the menu shown in Figure 15-4.

```
```
1  /** menu_applet.c **/
2  #include <gnome.h>
3  #include <applet-widget.h>
4  
5  #define APPLETNAME "menu-applet"
6  #define VERSION "1.0"
7  
8  void changeCallback(AppletWidget *applet,GtkLabel *label);
9  void aboutCallback(AppletWidget *applet,GtkLabel *label);
10 
11 int main(int argc,char *argv[])
12 {
13 
14     GtkWidget *applet;
15     GtkWidget *label;
16 
17     applet_widget_init(APPLETNAME,VERSION,
18                     argc,argv,
19                     NULL,0,NULL);
20 
21     applet = applet_widget_new(APPLETNAME);
22 
23     label = gtk_label_new("Menu\nApplet");
24     gtk_widget_set_usize(label,48,48);
25     applet_widget_add(APPLET_WIDGET(applet),label);
26 
27     gtk_widget_show_all(applet);
28 
29     applet_widget_register_callback(
30                APPLET_WIDGET(applet),
31                "Change","Change",
32                (AppletCallbackFunc)changeCallback,label);
33     applet_widget_register_stock_callback(
34                APPLET_WIDGET(applet),
35                "about",GNOME_STOCK_MENU_ABOUT,
36                "About...",
37                (AppletCallbackFunc)aboutCallback,label);
