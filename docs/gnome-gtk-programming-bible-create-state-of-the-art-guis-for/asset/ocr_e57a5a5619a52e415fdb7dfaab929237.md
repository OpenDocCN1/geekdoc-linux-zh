There is something a little odd about drawing rectangles in X. Outlines of rectangles are one pixel wider and taller than filled-in rectangles. The top and left are in the same place, but the bottom and the right are extended by one pixel. If you want to draw an outline around a filled rectangle, you need to draw the outline last. If you draw the outline first, the filled rectangle covers the top and left portion of the outline, but it leaves the bottom and right portion visible.
