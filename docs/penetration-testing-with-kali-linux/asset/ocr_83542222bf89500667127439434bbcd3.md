# 5.1.1 Penetration Testing Deliverables

A penetration test or red team exercise is difficult to scope in advance. This is because the tester cannot consistently anticipate exactly what kind of machines or networks the client will want to tested.

> Even though the outcome of our assessment is often unpredictable, it is often recommended to define a detailed scope during the preliminary meetings with the customer. This process is especially verify when prioritizing business critical targets within large networks.

While the general execution plan for a penetration test will often follow a pattern maxim, "no plan survives first contact with the enemy"195. This means that any specific activities we might expect during the engagement might not actually happen, since the reality of the testing environment is almost certainly different than our initial ideas and hypotheses about. It is therefore difficult to report on penetration tests usingated forms. This is especially the case when the test is carried out with little discussion with the client, for example, if the client is looking to support their defending teams in some manner.
