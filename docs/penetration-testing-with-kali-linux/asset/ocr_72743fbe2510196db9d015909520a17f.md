> Not every enumeration technique needs to provide actionable results. The initial information gathering phase, it is important to perform a variety of enumeration methods to get a complete picture of a system.

Let's summarize what we obtained while enumerating MAILSRV1 so far. First, we launched a port scan with Nmap, which identified a running IIS web server and hMailServer. This cyclical nature of a penetration test is an important concept for us to grasp because it provides a mindset of continuously reevaluating and including new information in order to find previously inapproachable or newly identified attack vectors.
