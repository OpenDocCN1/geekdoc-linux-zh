# 25.2.5 Routers/NAT

Each of the Challenges has multiple subnets, with at least one external and internal subnetwork. For each Challenge, the internal subworks are not directly routable from the initial external network, but the external network is routable from all other networks.

You will need to use various techniques covered in the course to gain access to the internal networks. For example, you may need to exploit machines NATd behind firewalls, leveraging dual-hosted hosts or client-side exploits. Lengthy attacks as brute force or DOS/DDOS are highly discouraged as they will render the firewalls, with any additional networks connected to, inaccessible to you.

A number of machines in the lab have firewalls enabled and may not respond to ICMP echo requests. If an IP address does not respond to ICMP echo requests, this does not necessarily mean that the target machine is down or does not exist.

# 25.2.6 Passwords

Spending an amount of time cracking the root or administrator passwords of all machines in the lab is not required. If you have tried all of the available wordlists in Kali, and used information gathered throughout the labs, stop and consider a different attack vector. If you have significant cracking hardware, then feel free to continue to crack as many passwords as you can. With "regular" hardware, every intentional attack that relies on password-cracking should take less than 10 minutes with the right wordlist and parameters.
