# 4.7.5 Find a Community of Co-Learners

Let's take a moment to talk about and acknowledge the power of community.

There are numerous benefits to studying as part of a community of learners.192 not least of which is the opportunity to develop an entirely new set of skills. Group work is often used by educators as a way to encourage students to learn the social skills required in a collaborative environment. Even if one has to work as a sole-proprietor and not any co-workers, the tools learned when as part of a group can be immensely helpful to one's professional career.

In addition to social skills, there's a major benefit being responsible for explaining ideas to learners who might be struggling. This is at the core of the Feynman Technique that we reviewed earlier.
