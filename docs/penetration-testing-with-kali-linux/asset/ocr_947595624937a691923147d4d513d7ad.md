> Listing 659 - Display Meterpreter HTTPS non-staged payload

Listing 659 shows that index 10 is payload/linux/x64/meterpreter_reverse_https. Instead of a raw TCP connection, this payload uses HTTPS to establish the connection and communication between the infected target and our Kali machine. As the traffic is encrypted with SSL/TLS, defenders will only obtain information about HTTPS requests. Without further defensive techniques and technologies, they will be unlikely to detect the Meterpreter communication.

Let's select payload and display its options.

