# OffSec

Penetration Testing with Kali Linux attack included financial losses for specific Twitter users, data exposure for a number of high-profile accounts, and reputational damage to Twitter itself.

To understand potential prevention and mitigation, we need to understand how and why the attack occurred. The attack began with spearphishing and social engineering allowed attackers to obtain employee credentials and access to Twitter's internal network. This could have been prevented if employees were better equipped to recognize social engineering and spearphishing attacks. Additional protections that could have prevented or mitigated this attack include limiting access to sensitive internal tools using the principle of least privilege and increased monitoring for anomalous activity.

**Phishing.** Phishing is a general class of attack relative to spearphishing. Spearphishing attacks are targeted to specific individuals, phishing is usually done in broad sweeps. Phishing strategy is to send a malicious communication to many people as possible, increasing the likelihood of a victim clicking a link or doing something that would compromise security.

In September 2021, a subsidiary of Toyota acknowledged that they had fallen prey to a Business Email Compromise (BEC) phishing scam. The scam resulted in the transfer of ¥ 4 million (JPY), roughly to 37 million USD, to the attacker. This attack occurred because an employee was persuaded to change account information associated with a series of payments.

The United States Federal Bureau of Investigation (FBI) recommends these and other steps to prevent BEC:

+   - Verify the legitimacy of any request for payment, purchase or changes to account information or payment policies in person.
- If this is not possible, verify legitimacy over the phone.
- Be wary of requests that indicate urgency.
- Carefully inspect email addresses and URLs in email communications.
- Do not open email attachments from people that you do not know.
- Carefully inspect the email address of the sender before responding.

**Ransomware.** Ransomware is a type of malware that infects computer systems and locks a legitimate user from accessing it. Users are often caught by the attacker and asked for a ransom in order to unlock their machines or documents.

In May 2021, a ransomware incident occurred at Colonial Pipeline, a major American company. The attack lead to the disruption of fuel distribution for multiple days. This attack resulted in a loss of corporate data, the halting of fuel distribution, millions of dollars in ransomware payments, increased fuel prices, and fuel shortage fears.
