Nation States. Although international politics, cyber war, and digital intelligence are vast subjects and significantly beyond the scope of this Module, we recognize that some of the most proficient, resourceful, and well-funded operators of cyber attacks at the nation-state level within many countries across the globe.

Since 2009, North Korean actors, usually grouped under the name Lazarus,¹¹ have in a number of different attacks ranging from data theft (Sony, 2014), to ransomware (WannaCry, 2017) to financial targeting banks (Bangladesh Bank, 2016) and cryptrencies - notably, the 2022 Infinity attack. These attacks have resulted in loss and leak of corporate data, including proprietary data (Sony) and financial losses for companies that paid a ransom.

An insurance assurance firm NCC Group¹² suggests the following to prevent and mitigate attacks from the Lazarus group: network segmentation, patching and updating internet facing resources, ensuring the correct implementation of MFA, monitoring for anomalous user behavior(example: multiple, concurrent sessions from different locations), ensuring sufficient logging, and log analysis.
