# 5.1.5 Taking Screenshots

Screenshots are an important part of note-taking and technical reporting. A good screenshot can explain the issue being discussed at a glance and in more detail than a textual description. Screenshots are particularly useful to help present a technically complex or detail-heavy section of a report. As the saying, a picture is worth 1000 words. Conversely, a bad screenshot can obfuscate and draw attention away from what the issue is.

Screenshots are an important way to communicate the visual impact of a finding, and can be far more effective than more text. For it's more effective to show a screenshot of an alert popping up from an XSS payload than to write it in. However, it's more difficult to use a screenshot to describe exactly what we are happening when we use something like a buffer overflow payload.
