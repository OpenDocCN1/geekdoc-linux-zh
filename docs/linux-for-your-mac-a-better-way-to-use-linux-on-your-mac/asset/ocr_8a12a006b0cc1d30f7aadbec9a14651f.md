# Other Calculation Commands

You will find other calculation commands included with your Linux distribution. These include graphic calculators, such as *xcalc*, KDE’s *kscalc*, and the GNOME calculator, *gcalc*. Command-line calculators include the *units* program, the *dc* calculator, and the *bc* calculating language interpreter.

# Using teapot

Michael Haarndt’s Table Editor and Planner, or *teapot*, is another console, or text-based spreadsheet that you can use for simple or complex calculations. This editor features an easy-to-use menu system, but also offers a sophisticated, three-dimensional calculation space that you can use to perform complex monitoring, calculation or data hiding.

Cursor movement is similar to that in *sc*, except that Ctrl+b is used instead of Ctrl+h to move the cursor left, or backward along a row. Calculations can become somewhat complicated, as most functions may require a third cell reference.
