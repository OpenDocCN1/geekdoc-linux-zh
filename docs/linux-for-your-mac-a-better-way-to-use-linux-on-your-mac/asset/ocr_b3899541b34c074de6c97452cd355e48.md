Note that the above is just a representative list of Linux newsgroups (see this book's Appendix for a more complete list). To build your list of newsgroups for knode, click your new account in the main window, then click the Subscribe button. The knode client will ask if you want to download a list of newsgroups and descriptions from your ISP's server. If you click OK, knode will obtain the list (which could be nearly 2MB in size). When finished, you will see a list in a subscription dialog box as shown in Figure 10.17.

Click in the field named Filter: and type the phrase comp.os.linux. You will then see a list of Linux Usenet newsgroups, which appear in the window, as shown in Figure 10.18. knode's filtering is a handy way to subscribe or unsubscribe to newsgroups, and saves quite a bit of scrolling or guesswork when configuring a working newsreader. When finished, scroll through the resulting groups, then click to put a checkmark in each group's checkbox box you desire.

When finished, click the OK button, then click the OK button again. You will then see the main knode window. Click your account name, and your list of newsgroups will appear. To read a newsgroup, click its name in the list. The knode client will retrieve the group's message headers and display them in a list on the top right. To read a message, click its subject from the list of group messages. You can then read the message, as shown in Figure 10.19.
