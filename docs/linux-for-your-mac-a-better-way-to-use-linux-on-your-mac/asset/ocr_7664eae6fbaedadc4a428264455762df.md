If your connection requires a special login sequence, click the drop-down Authentication menu and choose a script-based or terminal-based login. Most modern ISPs will use the PAP (*Password Authentication Protocol*), though some may use the CHAP (*Challenge Handshake Authentication Protocol*). These protocols are used to negotiate and verify remote logins that wish to start a PPP session.
