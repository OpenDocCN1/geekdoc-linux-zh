You can choose one of 18 different color schemes to use for your sessions. When finished, click the Apply button. To change the background of your root display, use the Desktop’s Background menu item. Note that you must use KDE’s Background dialog box. The standard X11 xsetroot client, normally used with legacy window managers to change background colors or patterns, will not work, along with other fun animation clients such as xhihankan or xpenguins. This is because KDE takes over management of the desktop. In the kcontrol Background dialog box, shown in Figure 10.11, use the drop-down color palette to choose a background color. Note that a different background may be used with each active virtual desktop.

If you click on the Wallpaper tab, you can choose a background image, or wallpaper, to use in your root display. Wallpapers are found under the /usr/share/wallpapers directory, and are in JPEG format. If you use wallpaper, the Advanced tab offers additional color controls.

Theme management, or the ability to implement wholesale changes to a session’s appearance, first appeared for KDE as a neat hack, accomplished by running a separate client. Today you will find theme control in a separate module named Themes, found under the Look & Feel module. Click the Themes item in the kcontrol window to reveal separate controls for icons and style.
