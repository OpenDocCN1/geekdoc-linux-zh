Fortunately, every Linux distribution comes with two or more free spell-check programs. These include the spell filter command, the dictionary look command, and the interactive ispell program. They also come with one or more 45,000- and 100,000-word dictionaries, usually found under /usr/dict or /usr/share/dict directory with the filename words. The spell check ispell, can be found under the /usr/lib/ispell directory.

Commercial UNIX operating systems, such as those derived from AT&T's System V, have traditionally included the spell and look commands. As originally designed, both commands use the system dictionary words as the basis for comparing, cross-referencing and lookups. For example, to quickly look up the spelling of a word, use the look command, followed by a partial spelling of the desired word, like this:

```
```
$ look gira
giraffe
giraffes
