# Using helptool

The helptool client can be used to quickly search your system for helpful documents. Though not as glamorous or capable as SuSE’s, GNOME’s, or KDE’s built-in help, helptool is, however, helpful when attempting to find topical help on a variety of subjects. This client may be launched from the panel or the command line of an X11 terminal window like this:

```
# helptool s
```

After you press Enter, the helptool dialog box will appear. Start your search by clicking in Search for: field, then click the Search button. After some time, you may see results such as those shown in Figure 14.6.

This client searches along predetermined paths, looking for matches in documents under specific directories. If you click the Configure button, you can customize your searches along specific paths or through specific documents. When results are returned, double-click on a filename and the file will be displayed in a separate window.
