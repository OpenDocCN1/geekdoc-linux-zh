# About the Author

**B**ill Ball is the best-selling author of a dozen books about Linux. As an early Macintosh fan, his first Mac was an SE with 1MB RAM and a 20MB hard drive. He used a lab full of Macs to teach desktop publishing in a 1987 Dept. of Defense pilot project at the Defense Information School (now located in Ft. Meade, Md.), and was an Apple Federal Support Coordinator for a number of years in Washington, D.C. As a certified Quark XPress developer, he released two Quark XTensions (ASave and Stamp) as open source software in 1993. A fan of Linux since kernel 0.99, he first used Linux on Intel-based hardware, but has returned to the Mac and now uses Linux on an Apple G4 Cube and Titanium PowerBook. He is an active member of the Northern Virginia Linux Users Group, and lives in the Shirlington area of Arlington, Va.
