Give the dialup account a name and enter the phone number used to connect to your remote ISP. When finished, click the Next button. You will see a username and password entry window, as shown in Figure 9.10.

Type the your username and password given to you by your ISP. When finished, click the Next button. You will then be asked if you use a normal ISP or ATT Global Service. After that, click Next again. You will see an Accounts window. You can then click the Close button.

To connect, use the *xp3* command from the command line or the desktop's panel. Click on the new account connection to get connected.
