# Desktop Icons

KDE builds a set of desktop icons, partially based on your system's capabilities and previously installed hardware. For example, if KDE finds a CD-ROM with a configured mount point, you will see a CD-ROM drive icon on your root display. If you configure a printer, you may find an active printer icon, and documents may be dragged and released on the icon for printing. You will also find Web icons, representing handy URLs that will be displayed in a konqueror window if you have an active Internet connection.
