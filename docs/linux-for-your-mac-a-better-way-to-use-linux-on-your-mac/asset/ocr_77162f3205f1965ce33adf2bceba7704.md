This example shows the `efax` command running on the Apple TiBook. To send a fax, use the `fax` command and its `send` option, along the remote fax number and document name, like this:

```
# fax send 2025551212 mydocument.txt
```

The `fax` script takes care of dialing and converting your document into the proper format for transmission.

# Troubleshooting

Many experienced Linux users carefully research equipment purchases with Linux compatibility specifically in mind. However, if you have a computer that is not fully supported by Linux, do not worry. Linux on the PowerPC platform will mature and support for equipment will improve. The good news is that the majority of newer Power Macs out of the Apple stable are quite Linux compatible. This includes the G4 Cube, the latest Titanium PowerBooks, and the latest iBooks.

Your best bet in getting support for your equipment is to carefully monitor various PPC Linux-centric Web sites for details on how others using similar equipment have come up with solutions. As PPC Linux vendors improve distributions, support for newer machines will be added. Every new version of the Linux kernel brings additional support, and you are sure to find a number of computers on which all equipment works.

You should also use the various resources listed in the Appendix as starting points for more information. You can also get help from your local LUG (*Linux User Group*). Joining a LUG is the best way to learn more about Linux, gain exposure to new ideas, and talk with other users who may have similar equipment. The LUG experience is rewarding, and many LUGs sponsor talks, demonstrations, and Install Fests where new users can bring equipment and get Linux quickly installed and configured by experts.
