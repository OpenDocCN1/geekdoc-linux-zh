# Time and Service Configuration

The installer asks for your time zone, and whether or not your computer's hard-ware clock is set to Greenwich Mean Time. Use your keyboard or mouse to make a choice and press Enter to continue. You are asked to select various software and network services to start when Linux boots. If you are not sure, just highlight the OK button and press Enter. You can always control these services later on.

# System Security and Accounts

You can also choose a method of system security. If you're not sure, just use the OK button to continue.

![](bbox=[0.06, 0.33, 0.13, 0.43])

See Chapter 3, “Post-Installation Configuration,” for tips on setting up a printer. Chapter 6, “Using Text Editors,” has tips on how to print.

In the Account Creation section, you are asked to enter a password for the root user. This is extremely important. Enter the same password twice and do not for-get it! You are then asked for approval of the default authentication mechanism. Highlight the OK button and press Enter. Now you can create additional users for your system. You should always create a username for yourself!

The installer offers to configure your sound system. Click OK to continue.

You can create a bootstrap partition or boot directly from Open Firmware. Older Power Macs may be able to use BootX, while newer Power Macs must use yaboot.

# X Configuration

The installer probes your hardware and reports on the video card. You may also be offered the chance to define your monitor settings for horizontal and vertical resolu-tions, along with color depth settings. Press Enter to continue, and the XFree86 server is installed. After installation, you will be informed that you can choose to use the startx command to launch an X session rather than booting YDL, or boot di-rectly to a graphical session. If you are not sure about your settings, choose to boot to a text-based, not graphical, session.

See Chapter 7, “X11 and Window Managers,” for tips on configuring X for your system.

You have now installed Linux. Press Enter and reboot to Mac OS.
