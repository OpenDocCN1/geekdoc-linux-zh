One of the most sophisticated and challenging games for the console is GNUChess. This program plays a very strong game of chess and is difficult, if not impossible, for most chess players to beat. There are several versions included with every Linux distribution, but one of the most visual is the gnuchess version, shown in Figure 18.1.

The graphical counterpart client to the gnuchess family is xboard, a graphical interface with a colorful board in a variety of sizes. The client may be started from the command line of a terminal window, like this:

```
$ xboard &
```

After you press Enter, you will see a window, as shown in Figure 18.2.
