If you use X with a resolution of 800x600 or even 1024x768, you know that room for multiple windows and other screen real estate is at a premium! Getting the most out of your display can be an important issue. If you have a lot of open windows, your screen can quickly become cluttered. Try organizing your display or use KDE's window controls to send a client's window to a different virtual desktop. Using virtual desktops can help reduce cramped workspaces and organize your work.

Of course, there are several ways to organize your display. For example, you can double-click on a client's titlebar and its main window will roll up like a window shade, leaving a thin titlebar.
