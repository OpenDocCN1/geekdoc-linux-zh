# ## Customizing Your KDE Desktop

There are many different ways to customize your KDE desktop. KDE offers a variety of customization to the appearance of your root display, window controls, colors, and size. The main software tool used to control how KDE works and looks is the K Control Center, a client named `kcontrol`.

This client may be launched by clicking its icon on the desktop's panel, then click-ing the start menu, and finally selecting the Control Center menu item. You can also use the command line of a terminal window or the mini-command-line dialog box and typing:

```
kcontrol &
```
