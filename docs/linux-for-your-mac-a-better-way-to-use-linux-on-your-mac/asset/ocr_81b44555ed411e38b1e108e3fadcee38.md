# Miscellaneous fun Clients

Just because X is the world's premiere graphical networking software does not mean that it is all business. You find several clients for X that do not fall into the categories of a game, but are fun to use. The first is the `xroach` client. This client unleashes an infestation of pixel-driven roaches upon your desktop. When the lights go in the night, these animated roaches scurry around your desktop, and then quickly hide under any windows or icons, imitating the live insects.

You may not want to use the `-display` option to send an infestation to a fellow worker's remote display, but you may want to try the `-squish` option for a little target practice with your pointer, like this:

```
`$ xroach -squish -speed 1 &`
```

After you press Enter, 10 roaches will roam around your desktop. Click on a roach, and you will understand the `-squish` option.
