or choosing the newsgroup. Traditionally, newsgroup choices are kept in a file named `.newsrc` in your home directory. This file can look like this:

```
comp.linux.setup:
comp.os.linux.portable:
comp.os.linux.powerpc:
...
```

There are many different Linux newsgroups. See the Appendix for a more complete listing. You should know that not every ISP subscribes to all newsgroups. You may not find `alt.barny.die.die.die` available through your ISP's news server. In order to read Usenet news with Discussions, you must first download and then check those groups you desire to read.

To get started, again use the Preferences menu item, select Mail & Newsgroups, and then click the Newsgroups menu item. Click the Add button, and you will see a dialog box as shown in Figure 9.21.

Enter the name of your ISP's news server, and then click the OK button to exit the Preferences dialog box. When finished, right-click the name of your ISP's news server as it appears on the left-hand side of the Messenger window, and then select the Subscribe to Newsgroups menu item in the pop-up menu that appears. A subscription dialog box will appear, as shown in Figure 9.22.

A pop-up dialog box will appear and ask for your username and then password to the news server. After entering your information, Communicator will download a list of groups from your ISP. Click to select (subscribe to) your newsgroups, and then click the OK button when finished.
