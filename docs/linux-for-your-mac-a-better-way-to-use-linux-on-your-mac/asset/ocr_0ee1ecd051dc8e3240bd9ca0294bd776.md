The Messenger component is Communicator's electronic mail handler. In order to use Messenger, you must have a valid electronic mail account with a username and password, along with the name of your ISP's incoming and outgoing mail server(s).

To configure Messenger for sending and receiving electronic mail, click the Edit menu, select Preferences, and then click the Mail and Newsgroups menu item. Next, click the Identity menu item. You will see a dialog box, as shown in Figure 9.13.

Enter your name and e-mail address, such as jdoe@mydomain.com. If you would prefer that your recipients see a Reply-to: address, enter the address in the Reply to: address field.

+   Have a personal business card? You can create an electronic business card that will automatically be attached to each message by clicking the Edit Card button and clicking the Attach personal card button. These cards, known as vCards, are used by different programs on different operating systems. The cards are usually imported to address book components of e-mail programs or a PIM (Personal Information Manager) application.
