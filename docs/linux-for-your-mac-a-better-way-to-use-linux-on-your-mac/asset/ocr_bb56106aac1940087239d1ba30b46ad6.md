In this example, the -p command-line option is used to specify a printer. This is important, as you can have any number of printers attached to your system, or even print to a remote printer attached to another computer.

Printing works through the lpd printer daemon. This service, which starts when you boot Linux, monitors the /var/spool/lpd directory, and looks for documents that are waiting to be printed. When one is found, the lpd daemon starts a printing process, and sends the document to the designated printer (which has its own directory under /var/spool/lpd).
