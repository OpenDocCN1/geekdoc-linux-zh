# Using Newsreading Clients

Reading Usenet news can be a rewarding pastime and a great way to keep up on tips, tricks, and techniques on many different subjects from people all over the world. When you subscribe to a newsgroup but do not follow up with replies or post on new topics, you are *lurking*. If you post a stupid comment in the hopes of seeing a response, you have posted a *troll*. If someone vociferously lambastes your comments, you have been *flamed*. Exchanging written taunts and insults is a *flamefest*.
