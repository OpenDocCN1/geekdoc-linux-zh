After you press Enter, you will be asked if you want to create a new document, or open an existing document as shown in Figure 12.14.

If you select a blank document, you can create a new spreadsheet by entering data and you can also taking advantage of the kspreads' graphing features. Spreadsheet graphics can be built quickly by highlighting specific rows and columns of data, then clicking on kspreads' graphing icon in its button toolbar. The pointer will change to a crosshair, and you can then click and drag a rectangle or square across an empty area of your document. When you release your left mouse button, you will be taken through a series of dialog boxes asking you to select a graph type, or to enter axis labels. When finished, click the Finish button to see the result, which will be similar to that shown in Figure 12.15.
