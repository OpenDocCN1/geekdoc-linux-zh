After you press Enter, the main screen and floating toolbar will appear as shown in Figure 13.19.

Click the Disc button on vlc’s toolbar after inserting a DVD. You will then see a small dialog box, as shown in Figure 13.20.

Make sure the proper DVD device is selected in the dialog box, and then click OK. Play will then begin in the main screen. The vlc client has many different command-line options, including the ability to preset the video window and choose a type of display buffer. Best results will be obtained when you play DVDs during an X session using millions of colors (24- or 32bpp), and by using vlc’s framebuffer output, like this:

```
$ vlc -V fb
```
