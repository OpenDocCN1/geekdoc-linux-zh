Although the pkgadd and pkgrm commands do not log their output to a standard location, they do keep track of the product installed or removed. The pkgadd and pkgrm commands store information about a package that has been installed or removed in a software product database. By updating this database, the pkgadd and pkgrm commands keep a record of all software products installed on the system.
