M
ake sure that your computer meets the minimum system requirements shown in the following list. If your computer doesn’t match up to most of these requirements, you may have problems using the software and files on the DVD.

+   ♦ A PC with processor running at 400 MHz or faster for graphical installation
+   ♦ At least 192MB of total RAM installed on your computer for graphical installation (256MB recommended)
+   ♦ At least 520MB free space on your hard disk for a minimal installation; 5GB of free space recommended if you plan to install most packages so that you can try out everything covered in this book
+   ♦ A DVD-ROM drive (if you have only a CD-ROM drive, use the included coupon to order Fedora Core on CD-ROMs or download and burn your own CDs — see Book I, Chapter 5 for instructions)
+   ♦ A graphics card and a monitor capable of displaying at least 256 colors
+   ♦ A sound card
+   ♦ Ethernet network interface card (NIC) or modem with a speed of at least 56 Kbps

# DVD Installation Instructions

Installing Fedora Core from the DVD is easy as long as you can boot your PC from the DVD-ROM drive.
