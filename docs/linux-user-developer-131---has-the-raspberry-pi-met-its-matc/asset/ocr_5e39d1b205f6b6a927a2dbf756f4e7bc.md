# Distro directory

Want to find out what’s happening with the biggest distros? Check out our directory...

If you’re looking for all the very latest open source distros, applications, tools and tutorial files, you’ve come to the right place…

On this month’s disc we’ve got another four cutting-edge distros, and the best of them boot directly off the disc – just reboot your machine with the DVD in the drive.

Leading the pack is Elementary OS, one of my Editor’s Picks for this month’s disc and probably the most exciting new distro since Linux Mint made its debut in 2006. Elsewhere we have the beta of OpenELEC, the excellent HTCPC distro based on XBMC, Puppy 5.6; and Sparky Linux 3.0, which we’ve reviewed this issue.

Trying Elementary OS and Puppy Linux 5.6 is as easy as can be. Just drop the DVD in your drive, reboot your computer and select one or the other from the boot menu. If the menu doesn’t appear, go to your BIOS and ensure the DVD drive is set to load before your hard drive. Enjoy the disc!

# How to burn an ISO

Want to try a new distro ISO? Here’s how to get started…

![](bbox=[0.05, 0.81, 0.33, 0.91])

**STEP 1:** Grab a new ISO Copy the distro’s ISO from our cover disc to your hard drive: go to the Distros tab, click ISO and choose to save. Alternatively, find an ISO online to download. If you’re not sure if you need a 32-bit or 64-bit version, go with the former to be safe.

![](bbox=[0.35, 0.81, 0.64, 0.91])

**STEP 2:** Open or install Brasero Brasero is the most popular open source disc-burning utility. It’s not already installed on your distro. Locate it in your distro’s repository and install it. Then open the program and select ‘Burn image’ from the Projects tab on the left.

![](bbox=[0.66, 0.81, 0.96, 0.91])

**STEP 3:** Burn your disc image In the Image Burning Setup window, click the first box – marked ‘click here to select a disc image’ – and navigate to the ISO you just put on your hard disk. Once selected, ensure you’ve got a CD or DVD writable disc inserted and click ‘Create Image’.
