If you want to send a signal to all your processes except the sending process, use the `killall` command. Of course, if you are logged in as root, then the `killall` command will kill all processes except for the very basic processes that are needed to keep the system running. ADX signals are based on the SYSV implementation; however, some BSD signals have been mapped to their SYSV counterparts, and BSD signal system calls are available. When writing or porting programs that use BSD signals and calls, be aware that signals are not automatically reset after being caught. They must be specifically reset to the required behavior in the signal handler routine. You can also write into your shell scripts the capability to trap exit signals. You can **trap** any signal except for **kill -9**.
