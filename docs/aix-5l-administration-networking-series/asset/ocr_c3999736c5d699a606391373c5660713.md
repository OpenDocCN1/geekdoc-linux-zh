# Assign the Console

The next menu option enables you to assign or reassign the system console device as shown in the next illustration. Sure, it worked fine during installation when you had a terminal plugged into the serial port. Then you unplugged the tube without resetting console support. You don’t need to have a terminal plugged into a pSeries or RS / 6000 machine for operation, but you may want to direct console output to another device or file. You can do console assignments from the SMIT FastPath `smit chcons`.
