*Edge resistance factor for auto flip* - If you are using desktop areas you may choose to have the areas switch when your mouse goes past the edge of the screen in the direction of another area. If you have this feature on you may change the resistance the mouse has when it is moving past the edge by moving the slider to the number of seconds it takes to change to the next desktop.
