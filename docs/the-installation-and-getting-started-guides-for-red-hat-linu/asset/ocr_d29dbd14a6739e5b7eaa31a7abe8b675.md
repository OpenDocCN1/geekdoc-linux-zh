# Multimedia

The Multimedia Applets are a collection of applets that allow you to utilize multimedia on your system. You will find applets that control sound, video, and other multimedia.

# CD Player Applet

The CD Player Applet is a simple CD player that resides in the panel.

When you have an audio CD in your CD-ROM drive you may use the Play/Pause, Stop, Forward, Backward and Eject buttons to control your CD for playback. Above the buttons is a small display that shows the time remaining on the track and in between the Forward and Backward buttons is the track number.
