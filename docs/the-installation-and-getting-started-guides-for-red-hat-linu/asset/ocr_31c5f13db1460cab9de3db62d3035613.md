# The Week View

The Week View shows the current week with detailed descriptions of your appointments. If you would like to add an appointment any of the days in the week view you may right mouse click on the day and select the New appointment menu item from the pop-up menu. You may also use the week view to navigate to particular days in the Day View. Double-click on any day in the Week View and you will go to that day in the Day View.
