*Hybrid Cloud* is a trending architecture to combine a *Private Cloud* with a *Public Cloud*. Many reasons are behind this, such as reducing costs and flexibility. This chapter covered what is *Infrastructure as Service* is and detailed **OpenStack** as a *Cloud* solution.
