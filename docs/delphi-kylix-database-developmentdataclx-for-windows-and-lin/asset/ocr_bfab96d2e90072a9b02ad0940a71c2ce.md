procedure TfrmMain.cdsOrderCalcFields(DataSet: TDataSet);
begin
  DataSet.FieldByName('TotalPrice').AsFloat :=

Looking at the code, you see that there are only four methods. FormCreate creates the master dataset. It is important to understand that this creates the detail dataset(s) also. You seldom need to manipulate the detail dataset directly in code.

Similarly, btnSaveClick and btnLoadClick save and load the master dataset to and from disk, which takes care of saving and loading all detail datasets, as well.
