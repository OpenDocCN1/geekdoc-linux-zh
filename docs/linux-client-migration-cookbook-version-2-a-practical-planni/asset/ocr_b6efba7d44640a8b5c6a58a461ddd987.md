The effect of a good communications plan is to flatten the negative aspects of the acceptance curve in Figure 3-2. A communications plan coupled with proper training on the new desktop applications should minimize the number of users that fall into the rejection and opposition mode, cause users to start out with acceptance instead of dissatisfaction as the initial response, and lead to a quick transition into exploration and productive use.

Regarding the IT support staff, these same issues have even more importance. A strategic decision to switch the operating system and the way the clients are managed can cause the impression of disapproval of the work they have done so far. It could give the impression that their current skills are being devalued. It probably not be easy to convince an administrator of Windows systems that migrating to Linux is a good strategy unless you can convince them that the organization is prepared to make a significant investment in upgrading their skills as well.

Thus, very important areas to focus on prior to starting a pilot migration are:

+   - Develop a communications plan.
- Select a pilot group that is best suited for the task.
