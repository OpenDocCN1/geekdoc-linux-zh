## Introduction

Kali Linux is the world's most powerful and popular penetration testing platform, used by security professionals in a wide range of specializations, including penetration testing, forensics, reverse engineering, and vulnerability assessment. It is the culmination of refinement and the continuous evolution of the platform, from BackTrack to now, leveraging many features of Debian/Linux and the vibrant open source community. Kali Linux has not been built to be a simple collection of tools but rather a flexible framework that penetration testers, security enthusiasts, and amateurs can customize to fit their specific needs.

## Why This Book?

Kali Linux is not merely a collection of various security tools installed on a standard Debian base and preconfigured to get you up and running away. To get the most of Kali, it is important to have a thorough understanding of its powerful Debian/Linux underpinnings which support all great tools and learning how you can put them to your will. Although Kali is decidedly multi-purpose, it is primarily designed to aid in penetration testing. The objective of this book is not only to help you feel at home when you use Kali but to help improve your understanding and streamline your experience so that when you are engaged in a penetration test and time is of the essence, you won't worry about precious minutes to install software or enable a new service. In this book, we introduce you to Linux then we will dive deeper as we introduce you to the nuances specific to Kali so you know exactly what is under the hood.

## Is This Book for You?

If you are eager to dive into the intellectually rich and incredibly fascinating field of information security and have rightfully selected Kali Linux as a primary platform, then this book will help you in your journey. This book is written to help first-time Linux users, as well as current Kali users seeking to deepen their knowledge about the underpinnings of Kali, and those who have used Kali for years but are looking to formalize their learning, expand their use, and fill gaps. In addition, this book can serve as a roadmap, technical reference, and study guide for those pursuing the Kali Linux Professional certification.

## General Approach and Book Structure

This book has been designed so that you can put your hands on Kali right from the start. You don't have to read half of the book to get started. Every topic is covered in a very pragmatic manual, and the book is packed with samples and screenshots to help make the explanations more concrete.

In chapter 1, About Kali Linux (page 2), we define some basic terminology and explain the purpose of Kali Linux. In chapter 2, Getting Started with Kali Linux (page 14), we guide you step-by-step from the download of the ISO to getting Kali Linux running on your computer. Next comes chapter 3, Linux Fundamentals (page 48) which supplies the basic knowledge that you need to know about any Linux system, such as its architecture, installation process, file system hierarchy, permissions, and more.

At this point, you have using Kali Linux as a live system for others. With chapter 4, Installing Kali Linux (page 66) you will learn how to make a permanent Linux installation (on your hard disk) and with chapter 5, Configuring Kali Linux (page 104) how to tweak it to your liking. A regular Kali user, it is time to get familiar with the important resources available to all Kali users: chapter 6, Helping Yourself and Getting Help (page 124) which gives you the keys to deal with the unexpected problems that you will likely face.

With the basics well covered, the rest of the book dives into advanced topics: chapter 7, Securing and Monitoring Kali Linux (page 150) gives you tips to ensure that your Linux installation meets your security requirements. Next, chapter 8, Debian Package Management (page 170) explains how to leverage the full potential of the Debian packaging ecosystem. In chapter 9, Advanced Usage (page 222), you learn how to create a fully customized Kali Linux image. All topics are even more relevant when you deploy Kali Linux at scale in an enterprise documented in chapter 10, Kali Linux in the Enterprise (page 252).

The last chapter, chapter 11, "Introduction to Security Assessments" (page 280), makes the link between everything that you have learned in this book and the day-to-day work of security professionals.
