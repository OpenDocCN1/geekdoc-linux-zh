# ### Where to Go from Here

It's time to get started on your Linux adventure. Take out the DVD and install Linux — pick a distribution, any distribution (as long as it’s Debian, Fedora Core, Knoppix, MEPIS, SUSE, Ubuntu, or Xandros). Then, turn to a relevant chapter and let the fun begin. Use the Table of Contents and the Index to figure out where you want to go. Before you know it, you’ll become an expert at Linux!

I hope you enjoy consulting this book as much as I enjoyed writing it!
