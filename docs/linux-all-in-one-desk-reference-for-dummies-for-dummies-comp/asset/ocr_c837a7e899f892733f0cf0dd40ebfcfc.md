Clikcing Main Menu brings up the Main Menu, shown in Figure 1-12, that gives you access to most of the applications in Debian. Of course, the exact list of applications in various submenus depends on what you have installed, but Figure 1-12 gives you a view of a typical Main Menu in Debian. To browse the Main Menu, mouse over items that have a right arrow, and a submenu pops up with more items.

The Actions Menu is the other menu that you can launch from the panel. Through the Actions Menu, you can perform other tasks, such as run any application, search for files, take screenshots, and log out.
