Why doesn't **<3b>** reverse **<3e>** in steps h and i in Exercise 23? The "back-a-word" **<b, B>** and "end-of-word" **<e, E>** commands consider the word they are "in" to be the first word included in the movement. Step h in Exercise 23 positions the cursor on the "i" of "cursor". The command **<b>** moves the cursor back to the beginning of the word the cursor is currently in. So the first **<b>** positions the cursor on the letter "c"; the second **<b>** positions the cursor on the ")"; the third **<b>** positions the cursor on the "t" of "the"; and the fourth **<b>** moves the cursor back to its starting point before step h: the "c".
