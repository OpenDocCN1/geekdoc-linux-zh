The **SKIP** button appears alongside the Check-Up and the Review Questions. **SKIP** allows you to skip these quizzes. We urge you not to skip these quizzes the first time you go through the course. They will serve both to test you on what you have learned and to reinforce this learning. If at some point you use the course for a review of the material, skipping some of the quizzes may make sense.

# The Review Questions feature at the end of each module

Near the end of each module are the Review Questions. You see the second screen from one such quiz displayed below. The student's answer—your answer—is shown by the filled-in circle. Correct answers are shown in red. Your score is displayed on the bottom of the screen.

The button at the bottom of the screen labeled "See Review, Based on Your Incorrect Answers" is an important review tool. When you click on this button, the program will take you back to the screens that present the material you missed. If you don't want to see this review, click on the **CONTINUE** button to view the module Summary.
