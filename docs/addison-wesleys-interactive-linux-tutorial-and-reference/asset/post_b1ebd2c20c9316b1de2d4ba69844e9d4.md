# Commands to Remember

- cal: display a calendar for one month or one year
- date: display current date and time
- mail: send messages to people not logged in; send e-mail
- mesg n: prevent other users from sending you *write* messages
- mesg y: enable receipt of *write* messages
- more: prevent a long display from scrolling off the screen
- news: get system news
- who: find out who is currently logged in
- write: send messages to people currently logged in

# Linux Information and Communication Summary

- *cal* lets you display a calendar of a single month or an entire year.
- *news* will keep you informed about what is happening on the system.
- *date* gives you both the time and the date.
- *who* tells you who is logged in to the system.
- *write* lets you send messages directly to anyone currently logged in to the system.
- *mail* enables you to send messages. When your recipient is not logged in, she will be notified that she has mail when she logs in. **mail** also allows you to read messages waiting for you.
