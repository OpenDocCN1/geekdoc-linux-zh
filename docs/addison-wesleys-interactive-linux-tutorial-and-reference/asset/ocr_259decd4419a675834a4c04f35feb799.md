# Linux Information and Communication



# Introduction

Computer networks facilitate communication. (The Internet has certainly demonstrated this!) From its very beginnings, Linux has been an operating system designed for networking—providing communication among its users. You will use three of the commands in this section—**mail**, **who**, and **write**—to do just that: communicate with other system users.

**news** provides a quick way for you to get information that will allow you to keep up with developments on your system. **cal** and **date** provide handy information that will help you keep track of your world.
