In the first group of exercises, you will recreate at a Linux terminal what you did in the simulations in the *Linux Made Easy* CBT.

+   1. Change to your directory **practice**.
+   2. Open vi to create a new file named **nowtime**. Your screen will look like Figure 12-1.

+   3. Insert text with `<i>`.
+       a. You are going to enter the text in the paragraph below. The two small carets at the end of the first sentence are there to remind you that **vi** needs *two spaces* to mark the end of a sentence. Do not type the carets. Insert two spaces in their place instead.
+       b. Press `<i>` to put **vi** into input mode.
+           Note that the cursor does not change position when you press `<i>`.
+           Type:
+           *Now is the time for all good men to come to the aid of their country.*^^*Now is the time for all good women to come to the aid of their country, too.*
