There is more than one scenario for changing your shell. What we will do here is to change your login shell. We’ll assume that your current shell is bash and that you will change to the C-shell.

In Exercise 6, you listed the shells available on your system. Here is that screen again:

```
$ chsh -l
/bin/bash
/bin/csh
/bin/ksh
/bin/tcsh
/bin/sh
/bin/zsh
$ |

```

Figure 3-10 Listing of Available Shells

Remember that Figure 3-10 shows an example of a listing of available shells. What you see on your system in response to chsh -l may be different.

To change shells, you use chsh without a switch:

+   a. At the shell prompt, type chsh
+   b. Press <Enter>

```
$ chsh
Changing shell for david.
Password:

```
