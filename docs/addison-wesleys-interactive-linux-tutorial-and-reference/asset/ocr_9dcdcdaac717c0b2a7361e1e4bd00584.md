# **Input Mode Commands**

+   | i | Insert text at cursor position. |
+   | I | Insert text at beginning of line. |
+   | a | Insert text one character after cursor position. |
+   | A | Insert text at end of line. |
+   | o | Create a blank line below the current line. |
+   | O | Create a blank line above the current line. |

# **Move by Words**

+   | w | Small word forward |
+   | W | Big word forward |
+   | b | Small word back |
+   | B | Big word back |
+   | e | Forward to end of small word |
+   | E | Forward to end of big word |

# **Move by Sentence/Paragraph**

+   | ) | Sentence forward |
+   | ( | Sentence back |
+   | } | Paragraph forward |
+   | { | Paragraph back |

# **Change Screen Display**

+   | Ctrl-d | Half screen down |
+   | Ctrl-u | Half screen up |
+   | Ctrl-f | Full screen forward |
+   | Ctrl-b | Full screen back |
+   | Ctrl-e | Up one line |
+   | Ctrl-y | Down one line |
+   | z <Enter> | Moves line cursor is on to the top of the screen |
+   | z. | Moves line cursor is on to the middle of the screen |
+   | z- | Moves line cursor is on to the bottom of the screen |
