Racks come in many shapes and sizes, but the most common types are four-post and two-post racks (cabinet storage units are discussed in a later section of this chapter). Each type of rack requires different cable management methods and hardware mounts. The racks you choose and the way you fill them can determine how efficiently you run your data center. You have a number of options for mounting equipment within racks, managing cables of mounted equipment, and providing proper ventilation, power feeds, and switches for the equipment. Chapter 3, "Deploying Your Systems," offers good advice for organizing and mounting equipment (see Installing Hardware," in that chapter). The following sections discuss important factors for choosing and ordering the type of equipment racks and rack accessories that best accommodate your equipment, facility, and budget.

# Two-Post Racks

Two-post racks are simple and affordable, consisting of two posts supported by a base that typically is bolted to the floor for stability. Each post has prepunched screw holes running up the front and rear that are used to mount equipment directly onto the posts; you can install front-, rear-, or center-mount hardware.
