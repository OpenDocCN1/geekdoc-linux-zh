# Allocating Resources

An important part of an effective disaster recovery plan is the allocation of vital resources that will replace those made unavailable or inoperable during an emergency. These resources include the following:

+   * Replacement data center hardware
* A secondary data center site
* Operational staff

This part of the disaster recovery process is both complex and critical. Because the alternative data center, equipment, and staff must be capable of supporting your business's IT infrastructure and its operations until your primary data center is back up and running, you must carefully plan and budget to ensure its success. You can choose to plan and procure resources on your own, or you can hire a recovery services company to help you plan and implement your emergency operations. Each choice has advantages and disadvantages, and you will learn in the sections that follow.

# Planning for Emergency Hardware

After you've identified your company's critical business functions, you need to allocate hardware resources capable of supporting each function. Many companies choose to provide a backup DR (disaster recovery) system for every system they use in their regular production environment. This arrangement is ideal, but it could double your hardware budget for the next year. Most businesses do not have that kind of money, and so scale down a bit.
