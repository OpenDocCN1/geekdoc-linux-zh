# Using Other Technical Support Staff

Support engineers represent the next level of trained technical support personnel. In large organizations that offer multi-level technical support, support engineers work directly with the help desk staff and might work onsite at the help desk facility. Support engineers are often junior-level administrators who have a better understanding of all the technology at a site fits together. This support personnel have the ability to solve some of the more difficult problems faced by the help desk.

Senior administrators are the highest-trained support personnel in any organization. Senior administrators should have the technical knowledge to support the system and ample experience in diagnosing and fixing problems on the platforms they support. In multi-tiered support systems, senior administrators have access to hardware, configurations, and administrative accounts. These individuals also have all vendor contact information, so they can bring in outside technical support assistance to resolve problems they can't fix

# Offering Three-Tiered Technical Support

As you learned earlier, many companies need more elaborate technical support services than can be provided by a simple help desk. One common solution is to use a three-tiered support structure that offers advancing levels of technical support for resolving more complex issues. In most three-tiered support systems, the levels are as follows:

+   - Level 1: Help desk staff The front-line individuals who take the customer calls for tech support, answer those questions that can be dealt with immediately, and record and route remaining support issues to higher levels of technical support.

+   - Level 2: Support engineers When help desk staff are unable to handle a problem on their own, they can pass it on to level 2 support, usually support engineers. Support engineers do not have the access, experience, or technical know-how to fix every problem that flashes across their screens, sometimes the ball is passed to level 3 support.

+   - Level 3: Senior support administrators Most senior support administrators consider themselves the last line of defense. When all fail, senior administrators solve the problem. If the problems stem from product issues, senior administrators might bring in vendors to fix problems with their own products. In this case, the senior administrator calls the help desk of a vendor, and the support cycle starts all over at the vendor’s site.

The key to success of three-tiered support is communication. Customers see only the front-line level 1 support staff. An issue is passed to level 2 only if level 1 staff cannot solve the problem. Likewise, level 3 handles support problems only if level 2 pass it on. Eventually, as problems are solved, information is passed back down the communications chain to level 1 staff and the customer.
