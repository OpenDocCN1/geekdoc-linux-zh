# # Analyzing Hardware Requirements

When starting from scratch, chances are that you will spend the majority of your budget on hardware: servers, routers, disks, printers, and even cables. Consider this a luxury; you will only get to do it once every couple of years, depending upon your company's budget, so this is the time to choose wisely.

# # Choose Hardware Wisely

Choosing the right hardware is critical, for putting together a working system and maintaining your credibility. If you buy hardware that doesn't work as it should or that fails to scale well with your growing business, your choices reflect poorly on your decision-making skills.

Here are some considerations to make when choosing hardware for your system:

+   * **What kind of machines do you need?** Do you need database servers? Do you need application servers? Now is the time to put your knowledge of system classifications to use (see “Classifying Your Systems,” earlier in this chapter). Write down the types of machines you need.

+   * **What hardware will meet the needs of the software?** You've chosen software and operating systems, now what hardware do you need to support those choices? If you choose Solaris as your operating system, you'll need to buy Sun hardware (the x86 version is not widely used, and will not be available beyond Solaris 8). If your application requires 256MB of RAM and 20GB of disk space, make sure your servers meet or exceed requirements.

+   * **What hardware is required to support the technologies your system incorporates?** Hardware that looks cool may impair management in a walkthrough, but it will impress them when it comes to perform? Make sure the hardware does what you need it to do. Do your servers access to your storage area network (SAN)? Make sure you order appropriate fiber cables for them, and that you have slots to put them in. Do you need mirrored boot disks? Do you better make sure that there are two bays in your server and that your boot disks are identical? Think about all of these little things, and make sure you have all your bases covered.
