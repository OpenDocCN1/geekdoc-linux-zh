# Summary

The data center is the heart of any technology deployment. Whether you lease space at a hosting facility or build your own data center, putting together the right data center infrastructure is one of the most significant investments your department or company will make. Much as purchasing a new house, you should scrutinize every detail of the data center to make sure you know what you're getting for your money, and that it's the right home for your hardware. The key factors to consider in any data center are the environment (temperature, humidity, air quality, fire suppression, and noise), flooring, racks, cable management, power, consoles, and remote access.

You have a lot to consider when building a good data center, and all of those considerations deserve careful planning. But all of your up-front effort will pay off down the road; a properly designed infrastructure will provide your servers with years and years of comfort, saving you—the administrator—countless hours of worry over downtime.
