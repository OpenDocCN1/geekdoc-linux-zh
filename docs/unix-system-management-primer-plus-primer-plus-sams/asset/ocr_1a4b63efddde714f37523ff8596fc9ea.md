policies and agreements are necessary in any computing infrastructure to ensure that resources are used fairly, securely, and legally. Creating and enforcing policies establishes acceptable standards for the use of the organization's network and system resources, so that the system administrators and users have clear operational and legal guidelines within which to use the system. System administrators create different types of policies and agreements, but every organization requires policies that cover acceptable usage of the system, user rights and responsibilities, and security. In this chapter, you learn about each of these essential policy types; how to create, distribute, and enforce these policies; and some situations in which you might make use of established policies.

> Get Legal Clearance for Policies
Consult your organization's legal or human resources department before publishing any new IT policies. A legal review can save you numerous headaches if the policies are ever brought into question in a lawsuit.
