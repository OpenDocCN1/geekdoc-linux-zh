# Chapter 5 • SUPPORT ADMINISTRATION

The IT department us assist with providing the tools for this categorization by running through a potential problem analysis. Each group within the IT department should analyze the services they are responsible for, review problems reported to the past, and come up with a list of potential problems that customers are likely to encounter with their services. The help desk can then gather all of this information together in one place and come up with some general problem types and where to assign them. Problems cannot be immediately solved by the help desk should be reassigned to the appropriate group. See Table 5.1 for the results of a potential problem analysis.

As an organization grows, new groups are formed, and new products and services are deployed, the group assignments in your trouble ticket system can become out of date. When that happens, tickets can go to the wrong group and the problem resolution is delayed. You should review ticket assignments periodically, at least twice a year, to keep in sync with organizational changes. You should also monitor how tickets are currently assigned, and the rate at which they are reassigned to different groups. is more than 5%–10%, you may want to re-evaluate the current assignments sooner than later.
