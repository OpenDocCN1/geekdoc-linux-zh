# Summary

The first activity a system administrator should engage in is the planning process. You can't build a house without blueprints, and you can't build a reliable technology infrastructure without out detailed plans. This process begins by learning about your project's business goals and translating them into technical goals that you can use to drive your technology decisions. From there, you must make important choices about the specific components of your network, hardware, software, and operating systems, keeping in mind that each should be able to support your business goals and scale with your business's growth. The entire planning process, while long and arduous, will pay off the life of the project by forcing you to prepare ahead of time for many of the problems and issues that inevitably accompany any major project.
