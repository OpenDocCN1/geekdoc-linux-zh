# UNIX SYSTEM MANAGEMENT PRIMER PLUS

As you've learned in this section, you can plan and procure your own emergency equipment and site operations facility, or you can hire a recovery services company to handle that process for you. Both choices have advantages and disadvantages. The advantages to procuring your own hardware include the following:

+   - There is a fixed short-term capital cost.
- The hardware can meet your exact specifications.
- The hardware is guaranteed to be available during a disaster.
- You can test configurations and procedures in-house.

Here are some of the disadvantages of procuring your own hardware:

+   - You must pay for support contracts yourself.
- The cost of buying new hardware can double, as you must also purchase associated recovery hardware (assuming a one-to-one ratio of production to recovery hardware and using identical hardware).
- You are responsible for maintaining those systems while they are idle.
- The hardware, if idle, cannot be used for anything else.

On the other hand, the advantages to reserving hardware from a partner are:

+   - There is no need to purchase hardware yourself.
- The partner is responsible for support and maintenance.
- Your contract might allow for upgrades when next generation hardware is available.
- The partner can load and configure operating systems.
- The partner is responsible for transportation of hardware.

The disadvantages of reserving hardware from a partner include the following:

+   - If hardware needs to be delivered or reconfigured, you will have to wait for that to become usable.
- Your hardware may be part of a limited pool of shared resources. (This becomes a factor during regional disasters).
- There are recurring operational costs to keep the hardware reserved.

# Allocating Emergency Staff

Recovery procedures cannot take place on their own; you need the contributions of people responsible for each business function to keep an emergency operation functioning after a disaster. Because a disaster can happen at any time, you need to plan ahead by assigning responsibility for each function to one or more individuals and establishing emergency contact information and methods.
