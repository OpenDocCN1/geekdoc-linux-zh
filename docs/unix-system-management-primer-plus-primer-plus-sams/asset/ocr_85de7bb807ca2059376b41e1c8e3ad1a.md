One of the perks of being a system administrator is that part of your job involves the automation of your daily tasks. Efficient system administrators quickly learn how to make their systems do as much of their work as possible. Candidates for automation include processes such as file synchronization, configuration changes, and space management. In this chapter, you learn some practical and effective techniques for automating these and other tasks. You also learn how to use some of the most powerful tools available for automation over Unix system.

# Scheduling Tools

Most process automation is facilitated by one or more scheduling tools that determine when each process should run. All Unix distributions provide at least two tools: at and cron. You can schedule one-time jobs that occur in the future with at, while cron recurring jobs. Knowing how to use these utilities enables you to use the other automation mechanisms discussed in this chapter.
