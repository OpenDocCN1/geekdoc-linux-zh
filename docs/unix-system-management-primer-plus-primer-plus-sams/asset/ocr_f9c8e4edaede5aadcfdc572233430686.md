# CHAPTER 1

# PLANNING YOUR ARCHITECTURE

You will learn about the following in this chapter:
* How to define the scope of your project
* Techniques for classifying your system
* Useful methods for collecting specifications for your system's goals and solutions
* How to assess compatibility needs
* How to choose hardware and software for your system
* Important guidelines for evaluating support contracts

You have just been assigned the task of designing and deploying your company's next-generation IT architecture. Projects like this don't come along often, and you are understandably excited. As the euphoria wears off, you begin to realize the magnitude of the daunting task that lies before you: Construct a working, reliable technology infrastructure on which your company will depend for years to come...from scratch. Excitement quickly turns to panic as reality sets in. Where do you begin?\Every completed project owes its success to good planning. During this planning phase you lay out each step of the project that lies ahead. Constructing a good system architecture plan is just as important in a Unix environment as in a Microsoft shop, an academic VAX cluster, or any other environment. In fact, most of the issues covered in this chapter (and this section of the book) are universal to administrators of all operating systems, and the information you learn here can be helpful to you throughout your career.
