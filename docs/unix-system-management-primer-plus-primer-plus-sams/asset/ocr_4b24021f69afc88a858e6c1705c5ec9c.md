Common mistakes in cabinet placement and use placing servers on top of one another, blocking exhaust fans on machines that vent through the top, and placing rear vents too close to rear doors of racks, reducing the air flow out of the hardware and heating up the back of racks. You can prevent these problems from occurring by using hardware that vents out of the rear and ensuring at least 4 inches of clearance between the hardware and the rear door.

Fans mounted in the top of your cabinets can increase air flow around servers. Roof panels can be outlisted with fans that draw hot air out from the cabinet and pull fresh air into and through the cabinet (see Figure 2.4). This is especially useful in ambient air-cooled environments where minimal air flows throughout the cabinets.

# Cabinet Ventilation

Louvered doors can also help keep equipment cabinets cool. These doors have several horizontal perforations angled to pull fresh air into and up through the cabinet, and they're perfect for a cabinet with roof fans. However, if you have a raised floor with floor air conditioning, you may want solid doors so cold air makes it all the way up to the fans and doesn't escape through the louvers in the cabinet doors.
