# ABOUT THE AUTHOR

Jeff Horwitz has been a system administrator for the past eight years and is currently the Manager of Production Systems for TargetRx, Inc., a pharmaceutical market research company Prior to joining TargetRx, he worked for a national Internet service provider, a university, and a software company, giving him unique insights into each type of organization. He is an active member member of SAGE and Usenix, and he has written several widely used Perl modules. Jeff has a Bachelor of Science in Cellular and Molecular Biology from the University of Michigan and is a Sun Certified System Administrator.
