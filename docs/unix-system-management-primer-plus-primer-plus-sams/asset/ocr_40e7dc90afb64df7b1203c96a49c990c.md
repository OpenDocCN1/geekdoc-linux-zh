# Unix® System Management Primer Plus

Unix System Management Primer Plus offers a mature, complete tutorial for technology professionals who are serious about having a thorough understanding of the fundamentals of Unix system management.

This book introduces beginning system administrators to the most important elements of Unix system administration. With uncommon clarity it shows readers how to be a system administrator, teaching techniques that are usually learned on the job from more experienced co-workers or through trial and error. Real-world examples from the author's experiences are used throughout the book to demonstrate the practical applications of the concepts presented. Review questions reinforce the most important topics covered in each chapter.

With Unix System Management Primer Plus, you will learn the fundamentals of Unix system administration and how to apply them to real-world problems.

# Topics covered

+   - Choosing a data chart that meets your needs
- Monitoring critical services
- Ensuring high availability for your services
- Implementing disaster recovery procedures
- Implementing system security
- Communicating effectively with users

Jeff Horwitz has worked with Unix systems for more than eight years, both as a user and an administrator. He has managed a wide range of systems, from single-user workstations to highly tuned enterprise database servers. At the University of Michigan, he managed campus Unix systems for more than 65,000 users. At LaserLink.Net and Covad Communications, he managed more than 150 servers providing email, dial-up, and billing services to more than 800,000 users across the country. Currently he is the manager of production systems at TargetRx, Inc., where he maintains the entire infrastructure, having designed and built it from scratch. Horwitz holds a B.S. in cellular and molecular biology from the University of Michigan, is an active member of Usenix and SAGE, and is a Sun Certified System Administrator.
