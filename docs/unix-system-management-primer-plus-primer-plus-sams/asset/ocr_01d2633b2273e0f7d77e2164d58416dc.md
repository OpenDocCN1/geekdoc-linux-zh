> **Keep the Message of the Day Short**
Make that your messages of the day are kept short and to the point. Users tend to skip over messages of the day that take more than 5 or 10 seconds to read—they want to get started on their work as soon as they log in.

# Posting Messages to Newsgroups

Usenet newsgroups are threaded forums in which users can post messages to groups with a common interest. Although most newsgroups are publicly accessible and anyone on the Internet can post them, individual news servers can have newsgroups private to an organization. Users can check these private newsgroups for announcements about your services and even post questions and comments. Newsgroups are convenient for communicating with users when your systems are unavailable, because a user does not need to be logged into their systems to view them.

If you use newsgroups, be sure to post consistently. Much like a bulletin board, users must actively choose to look at or subscribe to a particular newsgroup; if users know that messages are posted often, they will be more likely to check for new messages.

# Posting Messages on the Web

Many organizations create their own intranets for posting internal information such as outage schedules and system status information. Most users access the Web on a regular basis, so it can be a very effective forum for posting information.

> **Keep Web Postings Current**
If you create a Web site for communicating information to your users, you must keep the pages current and up to date. Users eventually stop visiting a site that always contains the same stale information.

# Sending Messages via Email

Use email only to broadcast urgent messages to your users, especially when those are only occasional users of your systems. You can also use email if you need to personalize the messages—address confirmations and billing statements fall into this category.

> **Send Urgent Email Well Ahead of Time**
Send urgent announcements via email in advance of the event you are announcing. Users must actively check their email and are likely to miss a message announcing an urgent activity that is only minutes away. An office environment may require only a few hours lead time since your users are likely sitting in front of their computers all day, but when your users are remote, you may want to require an entire day of lead time to ensure that most of your users have the opportunity to read your message.
