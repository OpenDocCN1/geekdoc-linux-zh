# Check Usage on Dual-Processor Systems

Don't forget the CPU column in top reports total CPU usage across all of the processes. A process that is consuming 50% of CPU time on a dual-processor machine may not be as dormant as you might think—it actually may be consuming 100% of a single CPU's resources.
