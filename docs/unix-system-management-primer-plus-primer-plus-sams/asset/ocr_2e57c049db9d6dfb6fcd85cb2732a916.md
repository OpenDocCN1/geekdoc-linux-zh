# CHAPTER 4

# TESTING YOUR SYSTEMS

You will learn about the following in this chapter:

+   - Testing the functionality of your systems
- Verifying compatibility within your systems
- Testing the performance of your systems
- Regression testing
- Alpha and beta tests

Testing is a critical part of any deployment process. You cannot trust that an installation or configuration change just works—you need to see it for yourself before it is deployed in the real world. Your reputation (and potentially that of your organization) can be seriously damaged by deploying a system that fails in production; a rigorous testing process is your best guarantee against failures. Many administrators simply test the functionality of a system, making sure all the features are there and working as they should; but that kind of testing is insufficient. You need to verify that the system performs well in real life by testing factors such as its performance under heavy load and its compatibility with other systems in your environment; even gathering and reviewing end user feedback is an important part of the testing process. This chapter documents the various testing processes a system administrator is responsible for conducting and how you can use those tests in your own environment.

# The Testing Process

The word testing is a generic term with a number of meanings for system administrators, who are responsible for many types of systems that perform many different tasks. Administrators use a number of different types of testing to ensure the functionality and usability of any service or product they deploy. Administrators are responsible for scheduling and overseeing the tests, evaluating test results, and making potentially necessary adjustments to eliminate problems exposed in the testing process.
