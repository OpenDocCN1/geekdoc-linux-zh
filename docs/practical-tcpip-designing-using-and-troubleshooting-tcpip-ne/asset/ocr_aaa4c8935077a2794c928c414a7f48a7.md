- **tcpdump** shows more information in its summary lines, so you can get an overview of a whole networking conversation at a glance. With **ethereal** you have to drill down into each packet in turn to get a feel for what's happening.

**ethereal** is free and is available for both Linux and Windows. See Appendix 16 for more details on obtaining and installing it.
