# ## Introduction

Now you are familiar with the three fundamental network parameters – IP address, netmask, and default gateway router. This chapter tells you how to apply these practically to your own network in two ways. First, you’ll interpret the routing tables on your existing network, to understand and debug how the packets are moving in and out of your network. You will see that ranges specified using netmasks are used all over the place. Secondly, you’ll learn to add/modify/delete routes in order to divide your network into sub-nets for testing, or for performance, security, or other reasons.
