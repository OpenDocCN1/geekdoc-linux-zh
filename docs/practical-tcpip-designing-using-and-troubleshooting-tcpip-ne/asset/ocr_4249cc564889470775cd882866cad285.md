# ## Introduction

VPN encrypts traffic so that eavesdroppers and hackers can’t read it or modify it. It lets you secure links over the Internet, between sites, or from dial-up users accessing your internal network. We cover IPsec, which is the industry standard for VPN, and explain how it works and its various options, and outline how to set up and troubleshoot VPN links.
