Summary

# Introduction

Your firewall is only your first line of defense against hackers trying to enter your site from the Internet. You need finer-grained control over Web and e-mail and similar traffic: even if the Web or e-mail request is valid according to the protocol, it still might be an attempt to compromise your system. At best, it could be sending you junk e-mail. At worst, it could be an attempt to break into your Web server and take control of that machine, or a virus-laden e-mail message that could infect your whole network. Special proxy servers give you fine control over what traffic you allow and what traffic you block.
