# AND and OR operators in filters

A logical operator AND/OR groups all the branches that are connected to the base of the operator's box representation in the window. For example, Figure 18.10 means (SAP/ETYPE = ARP or IP) AND (INCLUDE DENNISNT...). The only AND that matters here is the left-most one; the other two have no effect because they contain at most one item. (There's a larger example, with both AND and OR in Module 18.9.)
