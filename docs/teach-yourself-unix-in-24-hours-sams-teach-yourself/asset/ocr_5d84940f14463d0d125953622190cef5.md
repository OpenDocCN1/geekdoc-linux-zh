# Using Netscape To See the World Wide Web

You may not end up on the Netscape home page. This is entirely dependent
on the configuration set up by the software provider. For example,
BSDI UNIX (a product for Intel Computers distributed by Berkeley
Systems Design, Inc.), the default home page is http://www.bsdi.com,
ther corporate home page. You can change your default starting page
easily from the Preferences section of the program.

JUST A MINUTE

When you reach this window, you can click on the text that is underlined in blue (these
hypertext on the Web page), or you can click on buttons displayed on the Web page
or buttons displayed by the browser itself to go to new locations.

A good place to start is the What's New page (see Figure 21.3). Click on the What's New?
in your browser to get a listing of new sites.
