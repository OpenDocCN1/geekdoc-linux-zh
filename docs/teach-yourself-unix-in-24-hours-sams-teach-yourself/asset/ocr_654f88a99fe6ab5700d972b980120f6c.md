# Summary

For awhile, you've known that there are other users on your computer system, and you've
learned how to find out what they're doing (with the w command). Now you know how
to communicate with them, too!

Here's a word of advice: It's frustrating and annoying to be peckered by unknown folk,
so I recommend that you begin by sending mail to yourself and to tell your friends on
the system. After some practice, you'll learn how net etiquette works and what is or isn't
appropriate for write or mail.
