## Task 22.6: All the News That’s Fit or Otherwise

No discussion of the Internet would be complete without a brief foray into the largest, most active, and most diverse forum in the world—the Usenet.
Imagine a bulletin board on wall. Imagine as people pass it, they glance at what’s there, and if they have something to add, they stick their note up, too. (And here’s the big thing), imagine that there are thousands of bulletin boards in this building, and with its own actually thousands of buildings throughout the world, each with its “identical” copy of the bulletin boards. Go it! Usenet

Usenet was created in 1979, when two graduate students at Duke University, Tom Trusc and Jim Ellis, hooked their terminal to another computer at the University of North Carolina. In 1980, there were two sites with Usenet. Today, by the very end of 1993, there are an estimated 120,000 sites on Usenet, representing over 4.2 million participants.
