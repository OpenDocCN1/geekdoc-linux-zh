JUST A MINUTE

![](bbox=[0.128, 0.791, 0.209, 0.814])

Why be paranoid? For a small UNIX system that will sit on your desk and won't have any other users, a high level of concern for security is, to be honest, unnecessary. As driving a car, though, it's never too early learn good habits. Any system that has dial-up access or direct-computer-network access—you might need to use such a system—is a likely target for delinquents who relish the intellectual challenge of breaking into an account and altering and destroying files and programs purely for amusement.
