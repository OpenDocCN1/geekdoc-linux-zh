# Task 22.3: Searching Databases with WAIS

The next step on your tour of Internet information resources is the Wide Information Server, or WAIS. The WAIS system is a collection of databases accessible through a single search or query, and it was developed as a joint research project of Apple Computer, Dow Jones News Service, and Thinking Machines, Inc. You can access WAIS by using telnet to connect to a system quake.think.com at Thinking Machines, Inc., in Boston.
