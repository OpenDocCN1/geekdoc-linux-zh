# Key Terms

**domain naming** Domain naming is the addressing scheme for hosts on the Internet. The domain name is the information after the hostname on the right side of the @ in an address. For example, `joe@mitt.cornell.edu` has a domain cornell.edu and a full name mutt.cornell.edu.

**gopherspace** The information space through which gopher travels while you're using the program.

**newsgroup** A Usenet group focused on a particular subject or topic of conversation.

**protocol** An agreed-upon language for transfer of information between two computers.

**quoted text** A portion of a previous article that is included in the current article to give context, particularly in disagreeing with or amplifying specific thoughts.
