# Key Terms

+   - **exclusion range** A set of characters that the pattern must not contain.
- **inclusion range** A range of characters that a pattern must include.
- **left rooted** Patterns that must occur at the beginning of the line.
- **regular expressions** A convenient notation for specifying complex patterns. Notable special characters are ^ to match the beginning of the line and $ to match the end of the line.
- **wildcards** Special characters that are interpreted by the UNIX shell or other programs to have meaning other than the letter itself. For example, * is a wildcard and creates a pattern that matches zero or more characters. Prefaced with a particular letter, X—X*—this shell pattern will match all beginning with X.
