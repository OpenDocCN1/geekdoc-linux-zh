## Task 21.3: Customizing Your Browser

Once you've been using Navigator for a while, you may want to change how it appears. Fortunately, Navigator is customizable, by using the Options menu.

After you have started Navigator, click on the Options button on the menu bar. At the top of the menu are five areas for customization. The one I'll look today is General Preferences.

There are five tabs under General Preferences that provide areas for customization: Appearance, Fonts, Applications, Helpers, and Images, as shown in Figure 21.16. Under Appearance, you can change your default home page. Because I liked that Duke Basketball page, I can enter it. By clicking OK, I make that default home page.
