# Task 7.4: Viewing the Few Lines with tail

The head program shows you the first 10 lines of the file you specify. What would you expect tail to do, then? I hope you guessed the right answer: it shows the 10 lines of file. Like head, tail also understands the same format for specifying the number of lines to view.
