3. Some processes are pesky and can resist the less powerful signals SIGTERM
SIGHUP. In UNIX, this is called "catching" a signal. In some processes, you need
to send catch signals to perform certain actions. When you need to use
what I call "The Big Guns," or SIGKILL, this referred to sometimes as the
terminate-extreme-prejudice command; the format is kill -9 processID, and
it's not for the heart of the faint!

I strongly recommend that you use kill to see if that really
does the job. If it doesn't, try SIGHUP, and if that also fails, use SIGKILL as a last
resort.
