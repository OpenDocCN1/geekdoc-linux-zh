# Internet E-Mail, Netnews, and IRC

You can see here a table of contents of the different databases available for searching through WAIS. Although they are all free of charge at this point, it is entirely possible that at some point in the future some of databases will have costs associated with them.

Type J and k to move up and down a scene at a time, respectively, and j and k to move up and down a single source. To add a database to a search, type . to select it. An asterisk will show up just before the name of the system that has the database selected. Type q to quit.
