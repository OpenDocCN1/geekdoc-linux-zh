# Advanced vi Tricks, Tools, and Techniques

associating an action with another result. For example, by plugging your computer into the correct wall socket, you could map your action of flipping the light switch on the wall with the result of having your computer turn on.)

![](bbox=[0.127, 0.222, 0.233, 0.298])

The use of the filename .exrc is a puzzling remnant of vi having been built on top of the ex editor. Why it couldn't be named .vi I don't know.

You can also save other things in your .exrc file, the :set number option if you're a nut out seeing line numbers. More interestingly, vi can be taught abbreviations so that each time you type the abbreviation, vi expands it. The format for defining abbreviations is :abbreviate abbreviation expanded-value. Finally, any line that begins with a double quote is considered a comment and is ignored.
