Different queue categories are managed by the kids_queue_type data structure; we can use register_queue_type() to register or unregister_queue_type() to unregister them. A special syntax was developed to keep the managing of the QoS mechanisms as simple as possible. Section 22.3.6 will introduce this syntax.
