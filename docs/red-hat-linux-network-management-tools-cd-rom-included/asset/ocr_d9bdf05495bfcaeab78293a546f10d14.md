+   - define a view-based access control model
+   - redefine some of the SNMP concepts and terms

SNMPv3 has enjoyed a certain level of success in the market- place at this point, but the protocols are still quite new. Major net- working hardware and software companies already support SNMPv3 or are working on it right now. However, SNMPv3 has yet to be deployed widely within the industry; this is perhaps just a matter of time. One of the major delays for adoption of this new protocol is the cost-to-benefit ratio. For those sites that require bet- ter security today, they can upgrade to the new protocols as ven- dors make their implementation available. However, it may not be justifiable for everyone to upgrade all existing equipment to sup- port SNMPv3. The most likely approach will be to migrate slowly over time as newer products that contain SNMPv3 support are deployed. Eventually, new products will replace the older ones, and deploying the new management protocols will simply be a matter of configuration.
