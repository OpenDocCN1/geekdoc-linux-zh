**Corollary 14.27** *Let $G$ be a graph and let $\ell = \min \{ \ell(D) \}$, where the minimum is taken over all orientations $D$ of $G$. Then*

$$ \chi(G) = 1 + \ell. $$

There is also a corollary to Corollary 14.27.

**Corollary 14.28** *Every orientation of a graph $G$ contains a directed path with at least $\chi(G)$ vertices.*
