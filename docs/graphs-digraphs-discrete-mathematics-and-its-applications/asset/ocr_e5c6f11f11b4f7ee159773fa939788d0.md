However, there exists a graph H with V(H) = {v₁, v₂, …, v₆} such that G - vᵢ ≅ H - vᵢ for 1 ≤ i ≤ 5, but G - v₆ ≇ H - v₆. Therefore, the graphs G - vᵢ (1 ≤ i ≤ 5) do not uniquely determine G. On the other hand, the graphs G - vᵢ (4 ≤ i ≤ 6) do uniquely determine G.
