Since $G$ is a complete 3-partite graph, it follows that $\chi(G) = 3$ and so neither bound in Theorem 14.9 is attained in this case. On the other hand, $\omega(G) = 3$ and so $\chi(G) = \omega(G)$.

For the graph $G$ of order 10 shown in Figure 14.2(a), we have $\alpha(G) = 2$ and $\omega(G) = 4$. By Corollary 14.3, $\chi(G) \geq 4$; while, according to Theorem 14.9, $5 \leq \chi(G) \leq 9$. However, the 5-coloring of $G$ in Figure 14.2(b) shows that $\chi(G) \leq 5$ and so $\chi(G) = 5$.
