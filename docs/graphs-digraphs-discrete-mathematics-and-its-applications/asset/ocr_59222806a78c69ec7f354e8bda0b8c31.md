# **Corollary 20.5** For every two positive integers $s$ and $t$,

$R(s, t) \le \binom{s+t-2}{s-1}$.

**Proof.** We proceed by induction on $n=s+t$. Since $R(1, t)=1$ and $R(2, t)=t$ for every positive integer $t$, it follows that $R(s, t) \le \binom{s+t-2}{s-1}$ when $n=s+t \le 5$. Thus, we may assume that $s \ge 3$ and $t \ge 3$ and so $n \ge 6$. Suppose that

$R(s', t') \le \binom{s'+t'-2}{s'-1}$

for $s'+t'=k-1$ where $k \ge 6$. We show for positive integers $s$ and $t$ with $s, t \ge 3$ and $k=s+t$ that

$R(s, t) \le \binom{s+t-2}{s-1}$.

By Theorem 20.4,

$\begin{eqnarray*} R(s, t) & \le & R(s-1, t)+R(s, t-1) \\ & \le & \binom{s+t-3}{s-2}+\binom{s+t-3}{s-1} = \binom{s+t-2}{s-1}, \end{eqnarray*}$

completing the proof. $\blacksquare$

As we have already noted, the bound given in Corollary 20.5 for $R(s, t)$ is exact if one of $s$ and $t$ is $1$ or $2$. The bound is also exact for $s=t=3$. By Corollary 20.5,

$R(3, t) \le \frac{t^2+t}{2}$.

An improved bound for $R(3, t)$ is now presented.

# **Theorem 20.6** For every integer $t \ge 3$,

$R(3, t) \le \frac{t^2+3}{2}$. $\quad(20.3)$

**Proof.** We proceed by induction on $t$. For $t=3$, $R(3, t)=6=(t^2+3)/2$, so that (20.3) holds if $t=3$. Assume that

$R(3, t-1) \le \frac{(t-1)^2+3}{2}$

for some integer $t \ge 4$ and consider $R(3, t)$. By Theorem 20.4,

$R(3, t) \le t+R(3, t-1)$. $\quad(20.4)$
