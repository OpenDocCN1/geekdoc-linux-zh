41.  A set $S$ of vertices of a graph $G$ is a **total dominating set** of $G$ if for every vertex $v$ of $G$, there is a vertex $u \in S$ such that $v \in N(u)$. The **total domination number** $\gamma_t(G)$ is the minimum cardinality of a total dominating set in $G$.

(a) Prove for every nontrivial connected graph $G$, $\gamma(G) \leq \gamma_t(G) \leq 2\gamma(G)$.

(b) Show that the bounds in (a) are sharp by showing that there are two infinite classes $\mathcal{C}_1$ and $\mathcal{C}_2$ of graphs such that (1) for each $H \in \mathcal{C}_1$, $\gamma(H) = \gamma_t(H)$ and (2) for each $F \in \mathcal{C}_2$, $\gamma_t(F) = 2\gamma(F)$.
