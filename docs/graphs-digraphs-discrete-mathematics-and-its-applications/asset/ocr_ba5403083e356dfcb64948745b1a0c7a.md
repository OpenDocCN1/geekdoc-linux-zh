# Planar Graphs

A graph *G* is called a **planar graph** if *G* can be drawn in the plane without any two of its edges crossing. Such a drawing is also called an **embedding of G in the plane**. In this case, the embedding is a **planar embedding**. A graph *G* that is already drawn in the plane in this manner is a **plane graph**. Certainly then, every plane graph is planar and every planar graph can be drawn as a plane graph. In particular, all five graphs of the Platonic solids are planar.
