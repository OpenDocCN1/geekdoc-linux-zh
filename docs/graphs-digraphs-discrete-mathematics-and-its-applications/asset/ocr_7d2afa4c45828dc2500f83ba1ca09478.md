# 14.2  Color-Critical Graphs

For every $k$-chromatic graph $G$ with $k \geq 2$ and every vertex $v$ of $G$, either $\chi(G-v)=k$ or $\chi(G-v)=k-1$. Furthermore, for every edge $e$ of $G$, either $\chi(G-e)=k$ or $\chi(G-e)=k-1$. In fact, if $\chi(G-e)=k-1$ and $u$ is a vertex incident with $e$, then $\chi(G-u)=k-1$.
