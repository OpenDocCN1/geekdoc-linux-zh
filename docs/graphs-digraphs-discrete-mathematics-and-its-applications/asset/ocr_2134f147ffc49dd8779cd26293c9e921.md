38. Give an example of a graphical sequence $s$ (where $G_s$ is the set of all graphs with degree sequence $s$) such that (1) the graph $G$ has $V(G) = G_s$, (2) two vertices $F$ and $H$ of $G$ are adjacent if $F$ can be transformed into $H$ by a single 2-switch, and (3) $G$ contains a triangle.

39. Let $s: d_1, d_2, \ldots, d_k$ be a graphical sequence with $\Delta = d_1 \ge d_2 \ge \cdots \ge d_n$. Show, for each integer $k$ with $1 \le k \le n$, that there exists a graph $G$ with $V(G) = \{v_1, v_2, \ldots, v_n\}$ where $\text{deg } v_i = d_i$ for $1 \le i \le n$ having the property that $v_k$ is adjacent to either (1) the vertices of $\{v_1, v_2, \ldots, v_{d_k}\}$ if $k > d_k$ or (2) the vertices of $\{v_1, v_2, \ldots, v_{d_k+1}\} - \{v_k\}$ if $1 \le k \le d_k$.
