and so $S_1$ and $S_5$ must meet at different times. The graph $G$ is shown in Figure 14.4(a). The answer to this question is $\chi(G)$. Here, $\omega(G) = 4$ and so $\chi(G) \geq 4$. +   2 - 3 pm: $S_2, S_1$ 		 3 - 4 pm: $S_3, S_5$
