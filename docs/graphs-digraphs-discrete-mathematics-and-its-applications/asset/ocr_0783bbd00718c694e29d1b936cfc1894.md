# Section 1.5 Bipartite Graphs

+   19. A bipartite graph G of order n has partite sets U and W where |U| = 10. Every vertex of U has degree 6. In W, there are four vertices of degree 2 and three vertices of degree 4. All other vertices of G have degree 8. What is n?

+   20. Show for each integer n ≥ 2 that there is exactly one bipartite graph of order n having size $\lceil n^2/4 \rceil$.

+   21. Prove for a 3-partite graph of order n = 3k and size m that m ≤ 3k^2.

+   22. Let G be a nonempty graph with the property that whenever uv ∉ E(G) and vw ∉ E(G), then uw ∉ E(G). Prove that G is a complete multipartite graph.

# Section 1.6. Operation on Graphs

+   23. Determine all bipartite graphs G such that $\overline{G}$ is bipartite.

+   24. Let G be a graph of odd order n = 2k + 1 ≥ 3 for some positive integer k. Prove that if the vertices of G have exactly the same degrees as the vertices of $\overline{G}$, then G has an odd number of vertices of degree k.

+   25. (a) Show that there are exactly two 4-regular graphs G of order 7.
+   (b) How many 6-regular graphs of order 9 are there?

+   26. Prove that there is no regular self-complementary graph of even order.
