## Packet tracing

Packet tracing a very powerful technique for network problem determination. Packet tracing helps us to determine the problem by monitoring the data on the network. It is common to packet trace on the local machine, on the destination machine, and on a network device such as the switch or router. In some cases it may be required to do packet tracing at more intermediate network levels depending on the complexity of the network setup. Packet tracing helps us to identify the problem layer, too.
