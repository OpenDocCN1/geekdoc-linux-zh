# Setting Evolution preferences

To really make Evolution your own, you can set preferences that are particular to you. Certain settings let you choose the information that appears on the Summary page when you start Evolution. Other settings let you choose how your e-mail is gathered and sorted.

# Changing Summary settings

You can change Summary settings by performing the following steps:

+   1. From the Evolution main window, select Tools → Settings.

+   2. Select Summary Preferences (left column). You can change the following Summary settings:

+   - **Mail** — Select which folders should appear on the Summary window. By default, only the Mail Summary link appears, but no specific folders are shown.

+   - **News Feeds** — Select from a variety of news feeds, to have headlines and links to news stories added to your Summary page. Click the News Feeds tab, choose the news headlines that interest you, and click Add to have them appear on the list. By default, news headlines will refresh every 10 minutes (600 seconds), and up to 10 headlines will appear for each feed (both values are configurable).
