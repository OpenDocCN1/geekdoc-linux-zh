+   1. ODROID: A series of single-board computers that offer higher performance than Raspberry Pi in some models.

+   3. Pine64: Produces a range of ARM-based devices, including single-board computers, laptops, and smartphones.

# Installing Debian on Other ARM Systems

The installation process for Debian on other ARM systems can vary depending on the specific hardware. Generally, the steps involve:

+   1. Downloading a Debian ARM image compatible with your device.
+   2. Writing the image to a bootable medium (usually an SD card or eMMC module).
+   3. Configuring the bootloader for your specific hardware.
+   4. Booting the device and completing the Debian installation process.

Always refer to the documentation for your specific ARM device for detailed installation instructions.
