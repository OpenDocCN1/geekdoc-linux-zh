For additional reading on the subject of IMAP, I would recommend Managing IMAP by Dianna Mullet and Kevin Mullet (O'Reilly & Associates, 2000).

In the case of POP, there isn't a heck of a lot more to know. RFC 1939, which documents the POP protocol, can be found at http://ietf.org/rfc/rfc1939.txt.
