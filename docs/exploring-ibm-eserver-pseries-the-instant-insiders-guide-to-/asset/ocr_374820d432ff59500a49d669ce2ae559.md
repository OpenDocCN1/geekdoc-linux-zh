# Ergonomics

No plan would be complete without addressing the human needs that directly affect the day-to-day productivity of computer users—ergonomics. pSeries systems have been designed with careful attention to ergonomics. Everything from the power switch to the length of the keyboard cable is scrutinized. Much effort is expended fine-tuning the ergonomics of most application programs. To get the most out of your pSeries system, such as the American Optometric Association (AOA) and the New York Committee for Occupational Safety and Health (NYCOSH), as well as IBM, have probed deeply into the relationship between the computer and the user. Items such as desks, chairs, lighting, noise, and the like are important to productivity.

# Comfort for the Eyes

Like any other part of the human body, the eyes can get tired as a result of intensive use. This fatigue, called eyestrain, is nothing new to people who read for many hours at a time. With the increase in computer use over recent years, we have seen an increase in eyestrain. Although strain is only a temporary condition, it can cause a user to feel tired and irritated, especially if he or she continues to work because of the pressures of deadlines.
