# Choices for Your Needs

Now, how do you select the right pSeries hardware and software for your needs? It gets complex with the multitude of models, options, peripherals, and software products from which to choose. Every situation and application is unique. It is a good practice to consult trained specialists, such as a local server professional, an IBM representative, or an IBM Business Partner (independent companies who have been trained and authorized to sell pSeries servers). Here are a few questions and concepts that you might keep in mind. There are some example configurations in Chapter 5 as well.

+   - Is there a critical component to the system? Could this be an application, specialized adapter, or communications facility that might force your decision in some other areas. For example, older systems may not support a new adapter card.

+   - Is availability an issue? How long can the system be down, if ever? This will drive a number of decisions regarding the system(s), disk, communications, and software. High Availability Cluster Multiprocessing (HACMP), discussed in Chapter 4, can be a lifesa in these situations.

+   - Will this system have a single primary user or will it be a server for multiple users?
