The primary benchmark for comparing on-line transaction process-

response time and system throughput and the overall workload that the system can handle. The TPC-C
unit of measure is expressed as transactions per minute (tpmC). TPC-
is intended to simulate a "real-world" order entry environment, with
m of read-only and update-interactive transactions. It relies on a rela-	ional database and uses client/server configurations.
