Further, the distributed computer system can act to consolidate communications. This is especially true at remote locations with many users, since it avoids the need for a large number of communications lines to support all of the remote users directly. Instead, the remote users are locally attached by LAN to the distributed system at their location, and they communicate to the central site through the distributed system using a single communications line. Although a large distributed computer network is more difficult to manage than a single computer system, pSeries systems and AIX5 are designed for such distributed environments. When the size of the network grows to hundreds of systems, possibly a heterogeneous mix of systems running other operating systems, the Tivoli management products provide additional network management tools to help ease the task. We will look at two of the types of distributed networks in which pSeries systems are used: pSeries distributed networks, and pSeries and zSeries distributed networks.
