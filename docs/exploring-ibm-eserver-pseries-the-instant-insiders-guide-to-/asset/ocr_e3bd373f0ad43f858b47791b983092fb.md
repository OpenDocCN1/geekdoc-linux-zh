# PCI 4-Channel Ultra3-SCSI RAID Adapter (#2498)

The PCI 4-Channel Ultra3-SCSI RAID Adapter (#2498) is a non-bootable high-performance Ultra3-SCSI RAID adapter providing RAID 0 (data striping), 1 (mirroring), and 5 (stripping with parity protection) capabilities that can address up to 60 Ultra3-SCSI disk drives on two internal and four external independent Ultra3-SCSI buses. Two of the external ports are shared with the internal parts, so ports may be used for internal attachment or for external attachment, but not for both. If this adapter is attached to the internal backplane of the pSeries server, its performance will be limited by the maximum speed supported on that backplane.

To increase the data writing performance, a 128 MB fast-write cache that utilizes nonvolatile RAM is provided as a standard, resident part of the adapter. During the unlikely event of a PCI 4-Channel Ultra3-SCSI RAID failure, a replacement adapter can be installed and the fast-write cache can be removed from the failing adapter and installed in the new adapter to maintain data integrity. The 128 MB fast-write cache can provide a significant improvement in data throughput and response time during certain write sequence operations compared to adapters without a fast-write cache.

# Integrated SCSI Controllers

Small Computer System Interface (SCSI) controllers are integrated onto the planars of all currently available pSeries and RS/6000 systems. Since these controllers do not consume an adapter slot, they provide significant value in terms of both function and PCI slot utilization. Based generally on the time at which the pSeries system was announced, both the number of integrated controllers and their SCSI function will vary.
