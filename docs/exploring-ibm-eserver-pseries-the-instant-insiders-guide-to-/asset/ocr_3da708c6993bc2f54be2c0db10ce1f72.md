# 5
npSeries and Your Environment

An important first step in bringing in a pSeries system or any computer into a business environment is planning. Largely depending on how well planning is done, introducing new computer resource(s) can be like pouring water or gasoline on a fire. Many readers will already have computer systems installed in their businesses and are considering the addition of a pSeries system to their computer arsenal. Others are considering a pSeries system as their first computer. In either case, the information in this chapter should help in understanding how to introduce pSeries systems into a particular environment.

The chapter starts by discussing the evaluation process critical to any automation project and then covers software selection, followed by a brief general discussion of differences among PCs, iSeries, and zSeries systems. Finally, some specific Series hardware configurations appropriate for example environments are offered.

The latter part of the chapter includes discussions on financial decisions, physical planning, education, technical support, ergonomics, security, and service. This chapter is by no means a complete guide to introducing pSeries systems into a business environment, but it will give you a starting point for developing your plan and will discuss some important issues. Perhaps you will find in this chapter some new considerations that are applicable to your situation.
