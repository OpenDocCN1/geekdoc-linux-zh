# Large Business—Atole Enterprises

Our hypothetical large business is Atole Enterprises. This multinational corporation is a manufacturer of aircraft and enjoys financial prowess worthy of its Fortune 500 membership. The many benefits afforded by computers are news to Atole Enterprises. They have been using computers in their day-to-day operations for many years. Atole has one large zSeries or S/390 system at every major Atole facility. These large computers are linked together in a computer network, enabling the electronic transfer of information all over the world. Atole has developed many custom application programs for zSeries and S/390 systems. We will look more closely at the hypothetical Atole site on the outskirts of Pensacola, Florida.

Atole—Pensacola is a large manufacturing and research facility. The company installed an Ethernet LAN several years ago and has a collection of workstations that run the UNIX operating system and participate in that network. These workstations support engineering, manufacturing, and research activities. There is also a large S390 system with custom application programs and many terminals and printers attached. This system provides inventory management, basic accounting, order entry, and other financial functions.

Although the inventory and order-entry systems have been performing quite well for Atole, recent expansion of Atole's product line has forced unforeseen upgrades to the S390. In addition, Atole's homegrown financial software is beginning to dull the company's competitive edge. Continuous "enhancements" through the years have caused any new features to take many employee-hours to implement. Facing an upgrade of the mainframe environment and replacement of the financial package, Atole looks for a way to solve both problems at once: Purchase a new system to run an up-to-date, off-the-shelf financial product. Maintaining the "rock-solid reliability" is paramount. Any new system must provide excellent reliability, communicate with the S390, be able to support, and meet the budget objectives for a new system.

Atole evaluated several financial products and selected PeopleSoft p690 system running the AIX 5L v5.2 operating system and also selected the IBM High Availability Cluster Multiprocessing software. With the large installed base of UNIX workstations at Atole, the support staff is comfortable that they can support the pSeries and implement the high-availability configuration that is planned. The dynamic partitioning capability of the p690 allows flexibility of adjusting system resources to the workloads of various applications. The Capacity Upgrade on Demand (CUoD) features help manage cost of ownership by allowing installation of hardware at acceptable cost, with the ability to activate it when the need arises to handle heavier workloads or to install new applications.

Figure 5 shows the dual pSeries 690 configuration. One p690 (12-way 1 GHz POWER4+) with four inactive CuOD processors will comfortably support the production users of the financial applications connected via Gigabit Ethernet LAN. The other p690 (12-way with four inactive processors) serves as the failover (backup for production) HACMP system and the development system. Atole decides to configure both systems the same, even though this is not required for normal workload on the development system. Atole decides that, in the event of a takeover, they want to maintain a similar response time that the users had while in normal production. The IBM 7315-C02 Hardware Management Console (HMC) will be attached to each system via the integrated DMC ports.

Mirrored SSA disks in an IBM 7133-D40 Serial Disk System will be accessed via dual Advanced SerialRAID SSA adapters to provide high reliability and performance. The development system will have separate storage in another IBM 7133-D40 Serial Disk System. Dual Ethernet adapters are used for communications between the two p690s in HACMP cluster. Each has an ESCON interface for accessing the order-entry and inventory data stored on the mainframe. IBM TotalStorage Enterprise Tape Subsystem 3590, attached to the development system, will be used to back up all systems.

The high-availability components of this environment include the:

- HACMP v4.5
- Dual pSeries 690 systems
- Dual Advanced SerialRAID SSA adapters
- Dual Gigabit adapters
- Mirrored disks
- Dual racks providing redundant power and cooling
