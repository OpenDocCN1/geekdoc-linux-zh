# Advanced SSA Fibre-Optic Extender (7133 Feature 8851)

This is a particularly interesting way to distribute SSA disks in a campus environment and consists of a pair of short cables with a standard SSA connector at one end and a dual-bayonet ST connector at the other. It includes the circuitry to convert the electrical SSA signals to optical signals. Two multimode optical fibers (not included) are required to replace a single copper cable.

The 8851 SSA Fibre-Optic Extender is designed for situations in which SSA disks need to be more than 25 meters, but not more than 10 km, apart. The 7133 Serial Disk System Models D40 and T40, SSA160 (160 MB instantaneous) data transfer is supported up to 10 km away, depending on the type of fiber-optic cable installed. For other SSA configurations the limitations are 2.4 km maximum distance and SSA80 (80 Mbps) data transfer rate.

# Communications Options

This section will provide a quick communications tutorial and then an overview of communications options available for pSeries systems. These options are either feature cards installed in an adapter slot or external controllers attached to the pSeries systems. With these communications options, pSeries systems can be attached to external devices or in a variety of network environments using local area network (LAN) or wide area network (WAN) technologies. Chapter 4 describes how these options can be used to allow pSeries systems to participate in various communications configurations.
