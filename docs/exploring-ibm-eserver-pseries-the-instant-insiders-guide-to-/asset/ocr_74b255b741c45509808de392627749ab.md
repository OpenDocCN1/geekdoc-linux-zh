HACMP/XD automatically responds to site and communication failures and provides for automatic site takeover. Tools are available for data resynchronization after an outage, configuration management, capacity planning, performance monitoring, and problem determination. The HACMP/XD feature of HACMP v5.1 is based on the technology introduced originally in IBM High Availability Geographic Cluster for AI (HAGEO). Although HAGEO has been replaced by the extended distance feature of HACMP v5.1, IBM Geographic Mirror for AI (GeoRM) is still available. For users who require a data mirroring solution without the fallover and resynchronization capabilities of HACMP/XD, GeoRM provides a lower-cost option.

We have seen that a wide array of communications hardware and software options are available to users of pSeries systems. When coupled with appropriate middleware, such as HACMP and the HACMP/XD feature, these options can be used to configure a more highly available computing environment.
