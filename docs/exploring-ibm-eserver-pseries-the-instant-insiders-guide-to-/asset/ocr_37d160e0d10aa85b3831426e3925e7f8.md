# What About iSeries Systems?

iSeries systems are IBM midrange computer system based on the OS/400® operating system, replacing the popular AS/400® computer family. iSeries computers are multiuser systems designed to meet the needs of business applications. The pSeries family, on the other hand, offers models suitable for either commercial or high-performance computing (HPC) environments, as described in Chapter 1. Because the iSeries family has no capability to be used for technical or HPC applications, the pSeries family is clearly the pick for situations where those applications prevail. In the general-purpose multiuser computer system arena, however, there is certainly room for confusion when deciding between the pSeries and the iSeries families of servers.
