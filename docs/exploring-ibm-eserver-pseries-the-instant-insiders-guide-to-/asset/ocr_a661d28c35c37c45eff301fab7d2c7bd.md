# IBM TotalStorage Tape Library 3582

The Ultrium Tape Library 3582 is an entry-level LTO Ultrium tape offering, designed for small to medium-sized companies. It features one or two Ultrium 2 tape drives and 23 cartridge slots plus an I/O cartridge slot. It has a barcode scanner for verifying and inventing cartridges. With high-capacity Ultrium 2 cartridges, the maximum storage capacity of the 3582 is 48 TB and 9.6 TB compressed, with data transfer rates of 35 Mbps and 70 Mbps compressed. It can be mounted in a 19-inch rack or used in a standalone configuration. The 3582 offers one or two host attachments—either Ultra3-SCSI or 2 Gbps Fibre Channel. It supports a wide range of IBM and non-IBM systems, including simultaneous attachment of heterogeneous servers.

# IBM TotalStorage Ultrium Tape Library 3583

The Ultrium Tape Library 3583 is scalable from one to six Ultrium 2 tape drives and comes in three models with capacities for 18 cartridges (3583-L18), 36 cartridges (3583-L36), and 72 cartridges (3583-L72). The Model L72 also offers a 12-cartridge I/O station. Like the 3592, the 3583 uses Ultrium 2 data cartridges, with a barcode scanner, offers Ultra3-SCSI and 2 Gbps Fibre Channel host attachment, and supports data transfer rates up to 70 Mbps. Maximum capacities range from 7.2 TB (3583-L18) to 28.8 TB (3583-L72).
