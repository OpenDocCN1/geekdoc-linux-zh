# IBMLink Support

No matter what computer system a business chooses, the system and its
users will require ongoing support. The users will have questions
that need answering, problems that must be addressed, software
updates that must be installed, and so forth. This support can be deliv-
er in many different ways. Personnel from the computer manuf-
turer, the software company, the business’s own staff, and so on are
gerally involved in supporting the computer system and its users.

In an effort to make this support more effective and convenient,
IBM offers IBMLink. Just as electronic mail and on-line business in-
formation streamline a business’s operation, IBMLink streamlines the sup-
port to the business. Access to IBMLink is available on the
companion Web site (www.maxpress.com) for this book. Once you have
logged onto the “Exploring pSeries” site, click on “Access Other IBM
eServer pSeries Resources,” “IBMLink” will be one of the selections in
that list. Many of the information resources on IBMLink are publicly
accessible, including the IBM sales manual, announcement letters, IBM
news, a publications catalog, lists of local dealers, and IBM education.
Others, such as product pricing and discounts and support services, are
available only to registered users with a user ID and password. Gener-
gally speaking, registered users will be either IBM customers or IBM
Business Partners.
