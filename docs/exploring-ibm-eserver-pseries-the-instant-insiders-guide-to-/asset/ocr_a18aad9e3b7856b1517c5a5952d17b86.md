# Fibre Channel Arbitrated Loop and Storage Area Networks

In the early 1990s, IBM helped pioneer a networking technology called Fibre Channel (FCS), which was used to connect systems together using optical fibers. These connections allowed high bandwidth (up to 100 Mbps) and long distances (up to 10 km) between hosts and other processors or storage systems. Costs were high, however, and FCS saw limited acceptance.
