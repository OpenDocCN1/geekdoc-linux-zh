For businesses such Bob's Gearbox Company, which has limited computer skills, the pSeries 615 offers some important serviceability advantages. The System attention light-emitting diode (LED) on the front of the system indicates if there is a potential or actual failure. There are other LEDs inside the system located near each replaceable unit to guide the component that needs attention, in a manner similar to that used in most copiers to update paper pans. All of the components of the p615 except the system planar and the operator panel can be replaced by the user, and the attention LEDs will help an untrained user, possibly with some telephone support from service personnel, to take corrective action.
