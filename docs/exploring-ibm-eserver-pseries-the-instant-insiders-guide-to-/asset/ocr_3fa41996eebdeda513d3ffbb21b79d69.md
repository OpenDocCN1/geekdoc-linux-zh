# Exide Uninterruptible Power Systems

IBM now offers a number of different Uninterruptible Power Systems (UPSs) under the IBM Machine Type 9910+01 for pSeries systems. These on-line UPSs provide high-quality power conditioning and battery backup. Double conversion technology, the UPS continuously creates clean power to protect the pSeries hardware and data, offering complete protection and isolation from all types of power problems such as surges, high-voltage spikes, switching transients, power sags, electrical line noise, frequency variations, brownouts, and blackouts. Some of the newer models feature advanced diagnostics and an informative LED display, as well as audible alarms. With user-replaceable, hot-swappable batteries, both the cost of hardware ownership and downtime are significantly reduced. Several different models with different Volt/Ampere (VA) capacities ranging from 500 to 6,000 VA are offered by IBM. These UPSs are covered under warranty by their original manufacturer.

# IBM Spaceball 3D Input Device

No, this is not a Mel Brooks spoof movie, but a real live input device for the serious engineer using an RS/6000 workstation and on pSeries 615 and pSeries 630 deskside models. The IBM Spaceball 3D Input Device (#8422) puts complete, intuitive 3D manipulation in the user's hands. With six degrees of freedom, versus two with a mouse or trackball, the Spaceball eliminates awkward commands and minimizes steps that tend to slow productivity and halt the creative process. The user is able to view a computer-generated model from any angle using natural fingertip pressure on the Spaceball to push, pull, and twist it, as if the 3D image were grasped in the user's hand. The six degrees of freedom are enabled by simultaneous translations and rotations about the X, Y, and Z axes. The Spaceball has handy programmable function keys and is supported by the SpaceWare 9.0 AI driver.
