The IBM 6400 printer family offers speeds of 500, 1,000, 1,500, or 2,000 lines per minute (lpm), depending on the printer model. Although the 6400 printer family is designed to minimize the noise level, high-speed impact printers create much noise than laser or inkjet printers. For business environments where very high volumes of multipart forms are printed and higher noise levels are acceptable, 6400 models are available in cabinets designed for easy forms access with less noise insulation.

There are also continuous forms printers that print with the laser or electrographic (EP) process like that used in office copiers. The 4370 IBM Infoprint 62, for example, can print 62 pages per minute (ppm) of 8.5-inch wide forms with graphics at resolutions of either 240 or 300 dots per inch (dpi). The Infoprint 62 is particularly useful in manufacturing and distribution applications in which high-speed label printing with barcodes is required.

# IBM Network Printers

IBM network printers are tabletop letter-quality printers, designed to be shared by workgroups via LAN attachment. Since they print individual sheets of paper (cut sheets), they cannot produce continuous forms. The IBM Infoprint 1000 family of workgroup or network printers offers a range of prices, capacities, features, and software support for a broad range of workgroup printing requirements. Infoprint 1000 printers provide resolutions up to 1200 x 1200 PELS per square inch, yielding excellent print quality. Attachment options range from Ethernet and Token-Ring to parallel, serial, or USB, with network support for all major operating system environments.
