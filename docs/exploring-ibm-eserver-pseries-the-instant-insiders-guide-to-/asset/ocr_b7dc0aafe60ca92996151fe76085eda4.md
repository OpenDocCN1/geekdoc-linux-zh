The p655 uses a single MCM with four POWER4 or POWER4+ chips (either Figure 1.20). The 4-way HPC MCMs (either 1.3 GHz POWER4 or 1.7 GHz POWER4+) use four chips with a single processor, while the 8-way MCMs (either 1.1 GHz POWER4 or 1.5 GHz POWER4+) use four chips with two processor cores, as depicted in Figure 1.20. Each MCM, whether 4-way or 8-way, has 128 MB of L3 cache. The 4-way MCMs are called HPC because the processor speed is higher and the L2/L3 caches are shared by half many processors, minimizing the number of times the processor must wait for data or instructions to be accessed from main memory.
