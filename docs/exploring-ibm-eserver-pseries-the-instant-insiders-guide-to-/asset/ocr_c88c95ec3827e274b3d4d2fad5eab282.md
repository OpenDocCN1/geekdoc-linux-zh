They concluded that they could increase the output of the Art Department personnel providing them with the ability to maintain a shared database of artwork and to enable collaboration among the employees in that department. Such a library of graphic images could be easily searched for current methods and could be modified and used more quickly than creating new images. To change an image, it could be recalled onto one of the personal workstations required. Collaboration software, which supports multiple users modifying the same file, will enable Art Department employees to share their work and will facilitate contributions from more than a single person to the artwork. These capabilities will increase the productivity of the desktop publishing capability already available at individual workstations, which should allow the Art Department to create most of its proposals in-house and reduce expenses for outside contractors. Compared with current methods, these tools will help increase quality, decrease the turn around, and reduce the costs associated with artwork.

The team also concluded that the simplest way to centralize the demographic information is to place the information in a database on a single computer system and use a LAN to provide all PC users with access to the information, ensuring that all are using the same current data for their marketing analysis. This will result in a more accurate market analysis and then more effective marketing efforts for clients. Similarly, an e-mail server application will be implemented to support all employees. As needs for individual departments are identified, applications can be installed.

After examining several database and messaging application programs, the IBM DB2 Universal Database (UDB) Workgroup Server Edition and the Lotus Notes/Domino 6 server program, along with the Lotus Notes 6 client packages for the employees' personal computers. The server selected is the pSeries630 Model 6EA, which offers plenty of processing power and storage capacity, and being a maximum 4-way SMP system, qualifies for workgroup pricing of the database software.
