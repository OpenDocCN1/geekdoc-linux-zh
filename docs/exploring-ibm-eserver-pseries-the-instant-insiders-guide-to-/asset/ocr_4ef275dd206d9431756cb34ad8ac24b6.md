On the p690, the minimum system configuration of active process-
s 12-way with 1.1 or 1.3 GHz MCMs. In other words, there must
be one fully active 8-way MCM installed before the first CUOD MCM
can be added. With the latest 1.5 or 1.7 GHz POWER4+ technology,
the minimum configuration allowed is 8-way. Two CUOD MCMs,
with each active and four standby processors can be installed. Third
and fourth MCMs (either CUOD or fully active) can be added.

The p670 has only two MCM positions. The minimum system conf-
ig of active processors with 1.1 GHz processors is 12-way (one
fully active 8-way MCM and one CUOD MCM with active process-
sors). With the 1.5 GHz POWER4+ processors, both MCMs may be
CUOD options, allowing an 8-way active and 8-way standby con-
figuration—just like the p690.

Since all processor cards on the p650 are 2-way, the minimum on
demand configuration is two cards with two active and two standby
1.45 GHz processors. Two more 2-processor cards (CUOD or ac-
tive) can be added. Note that CUOD is not available on the p650 with
1.2 GHz processors.
