Based on the requirements of the chosen application program and his small number of users, Bob selected a pSeries615 Model 6E3, shown in Figure 5.1, running the AIX 5l v5.2 operating system. The p6156E3 was selected for its low cost, more ample power, and expandability as Bob's business grows. Bob decides to order a second 36.4 GB drive to augment the standard 36.4 GB of storage that comes with the p615-6E3. The total storage of 72.8 GB will be sufficient to hold the operating system software, application programs, and associated data. The 1 GB of main memory, expandable all to 16 GB, will meet his needs for. Since the selected manufacturing and accounting applications use a client/server design, Bob selects personal computers as workstations, connected on an Ethernet LAN using one of the integrated Ethernet adapters that is standard with the p615-6E3. One PC is for the inventory clerk, one is for the purchasing agent, two are for the accounting department, one is for the production department, and one is for Bob so that he can get the management he needs to make intelligent decisions. Bob will select priced PCs because the users will primarily interacting with the applications running on the pSeries615 and he must control system costs. He will, of course, need an Ethernet adapter in each PC. As the future needs of his business grow, he can easily add more PCs on the LAN.

For now, Bob selects a printer attached to the parallel port of the p615-6E3. In the future, he can easily upgrade to a network printer attached to the Ethernet LAN. An IBM 4247 Multi-Form Printer is chosen to provide the various multipart form reports generated by the manufacturing application program at a rate of up to 700 characters per second. A 20/40 GB 4-mm Internal Tape Drive provides a way to back up data on the disk, ensuring that important business information will not accidentally be lost. This tape drive attaches to one of the standard internal Ultra3-SCSI ports.
