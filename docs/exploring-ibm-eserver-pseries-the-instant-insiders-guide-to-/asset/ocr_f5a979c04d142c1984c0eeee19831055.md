Both p630 models are expandable to four 1.2 GHz or 1.45 GHz processors, 32 GB of memory, four hot-swappable disk drive bays, two media bays, and six hot-plug PCI-X slots. In addition, the rack-mount p630-c4 offers DC power, NEBS Level 3 compliance for harsh environments, and further expansion with up to two IBM 7311-D20 I/O adapters running at a 1 GHz clock rate. Each 7311-D20 drawer provides 12 hot-swappable disk bays and seven hot-plug PCI-X I/O adapters. So, a fully configured p630-c4 with two I/O drawers can handle 20 I/O adapters for storage.

As shown in the p630 system diagram (Figure 1.10), there are two processor cards, each of which may have one or two processors on a single chip. The processor cards communicate with each other and memory over a Fabric Bus that provides a bandwidth of 11.6 Gbps. The GX bus provides an almost 4 Gbps interface to the I/O subsystem (PCI-X slots, internal controllers, service processor, and RIO-20 ports for attaching a 7311-D20 I/O drawer).

An important feature of the p630, particularly for the entry UNIX server market, is LPAR support. LPAR is a flexible and powerful configuration option for system administrators who need to configure multiple "virtual servers" to meet their computing requirements, as opposed to simply running applications on a 4-way SMP server.
