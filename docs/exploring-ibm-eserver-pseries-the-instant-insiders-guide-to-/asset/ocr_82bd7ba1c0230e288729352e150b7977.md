The P260/P275 display (#3628) (don't confuse this “P-series” display terminology with the pSeries family of UNIX servers) features Trinitron flat CRT and easy-to-use digital controls. It is available in sleek black.

The P260/P275 provides a front operator panel with controls such as the on/off switch, brightness, mode selection, and centering. A tilt/swivel stand is standard and allows the user to position the display for comfortable viewing. Antigl coating helps reduce any reflections on the face of the display. IBM’s displays are designed to meet energy and ergonomic guidelines. In particular, these displays do the following:

+   * Incorporate a power management function that reduces energy consumption and increases product life.

+   * Meet the Environmental Protection Agency’s requirements for the “Energy Star” program.

+   * Emit virtually no static electricity—meeting stringent Nordic MPR-II emission standards.

+   * Meet International Organization for Standardization (ISO) ergonomics recommendations and Federal Communication Commission (FCC) Class B requirements.
