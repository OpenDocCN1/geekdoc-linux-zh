# IBM @server pSeries—An Overview 57

For customers who must provide for rapidly increasing workloads, the Model 6M1 offers Capacity Upgrade on Demand. This allows the customer to begin with a minimum 4-way 750 MHz system and, for free, install a second 4-way processor option with two or four of the processors initially desired. When the need for additional processing power arises, an AIX 5 command is used to activate these inactive processors in increments of two. The AIX 5 Workload Manager (a standard feature of the AIX 5 operating system) is used to allocate resources to the newly activated processors. After activation, the customer will be invoiced for payment of the processors then in use. p650-6M1 CUoD offers an demand solution for activating processors quickly when required, the process just described is less flexible and responsive than the methods used for the latest demand offers on p650, p670, and p690 systems. The p660-6M1 CUoD has been superseded by the process described in “pSeries 690 Specifics” in this chapter.

Like the other p660 models, the p660-6M1 is NEBS Level 3 compliant, offering special features such as 48-volt DC power for telecommunications central office operations. The Model 6M1 delivers the performance, expandability, and reliability required for e-business, enterprise resource planning, business intelligence, and other database serving applications. For value and convenience of ordering, IBM offers several standard p660-6M1 Express Configurations.

# pSeries 670 Specifics

The pSeries 670 is a 4-, 8- and 16-way server offering 1.5 GHz POWER4+ and 1.1 GHz POWER processors. The electronics design and packaging of the p670 are identical to those of the high-end p690. As a server targeted for the upper end of midrange businesses, the p670 is more limited than the p690 in terms of processor speeds, memory capacity and scalability, and I/O expandability. In all other respects, it is equivalent. The p670 uses MCM packaging, offering the following processor options:

+   - 4-, 8-, and 16-way 1.1 GHz POWER4

+   - 4-, 8-, and 16-way 1.5 GHz POWER4+
