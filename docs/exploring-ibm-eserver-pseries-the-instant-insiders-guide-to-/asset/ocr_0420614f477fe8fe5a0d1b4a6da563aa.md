In other cases, the central location may already have a zSeries or System/390 computer system and may wish to distribute a small or medium-sized computer system or systems to remote locations or departments within the business to better meet user needs. In any case, distributed computer networks connecting of pSeries systems and zSeries systems are possible.


SNA communications is rapidly being replaced by TCP/IP networks (the previous examples use TCP/IP). In fact, IBM has withdrawn from marketing its long-time workhorse SNA 3745 and 3746 Communications Controllers. Nevertheless, Figure 4.3 shows an example pSeries configuration that can be used to communicate with a zSeries system in an existing SNA network. The 4-Port Multiprotocol Controller will communicate using the SDLC protocol used by the central zSeries. A modem allows the computers to communicate over leased telephone lines.
