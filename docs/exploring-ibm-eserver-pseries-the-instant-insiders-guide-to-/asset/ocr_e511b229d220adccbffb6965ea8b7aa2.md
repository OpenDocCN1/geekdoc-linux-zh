The slim, lightweight design provides flexibility in situations where space constraints are critical. The TFT monitor offers bright, clear images on a 15-inch viewable screen up to 1024 × 768 resolution. The viewable area is 304.1 mm × 228.1 mm. The Space Saver 2 keyboard is available in U.S. English, German, Italian, Spanish, U.K. English, and 15 other languages.

An important feature that allows the 7316-TF2 Flat Panel Console to be used to control several pSeries servers in a server farm is the VGA Switch (2 × 8) for Machine Type 7316 (feature #4200). This switch allows attachment of either one or two consoles to eight systems. If used in a tiered arrangement with multiple VGA switches, up to 64 total systems can be controlled from one or two consoles. The Switch (#4200) supports up to 1600 × 1200 resolution, and each attached pSeries server must have GXT135P graphics adapter.

One Switch (#4200), which is 1U or 1.75 inches high, can be mounted in the 7316-TF1 rack mounting tray. Additional VGA Switches (#4200) may be mounted horizontally in the rack or vertically in the side of the rack. To minimize cable clutter, there are composite CPU to VGA Switch Attachment Cables in lengths of 7, 12, and 20 feet. These cables provide in a single sheath cables for display, keyboard, and mouse.
