The FaST500 offers these FC-AL disks: 15K rpm (18.2 GB, 34 GB, and 73.4 GB) or 10K rpm (36.4 GB, 73.4 GB, and 146.8 GB). The FaST500 supports sixteen EXP700 expansion enclosures has a capacity of more than 32 TB of RAID storage. The EXP500 expansion enclosure is also supported. Designed for high-availability configurations, the FaST500 includes two hot-swappable controller units, redundant hot-plug power and cooling, and standard battery backup. Multiple minihubs on both the host and drive allow various ways to connect remote servers and the drives. Remote servers can be attached using Fibre Channel switches, and the drives can be configured in redundant loops. Long-wave and short-wave fiber-optic connections allow distances of up to 10 km (6.2 miles) to the host or switch.

The high-end FaST900 Storage Server (1742-90U) supports 2 Gbps fibre channel. The dual controllers support all RAID levels, and 2 GB of battery-backed mirrored cache is standard. The FaST900 offers up to 32 TB of storage and advanced functions such as dynamic volume expansion, 6 storage partitions, and replication functions as FlashCopy7 and Remote Mirroring. Its capacity, performance, availability features, and advanced functions make it ideal for multipatform SAN environments.

# IBM 2105 TotalStorage Enterprise Storage Server

The Enterprise Storage Server (ESS), shown in Figure 2.7, is the flagship of the IBM SAN storage family. With capacity up to 384 disks, it provides much as 55.9 TB of high-performance, RAID 5 and RAID 10 protected shared storage and is an ideal consolidation solution for multiple concurrently attached servers, such as pSeries servers and SP nodes. In addition, mainframes, non-IBM UNIX servers, and Intel-based servers can be used with an ESS server. The ESS system provides exceptional levels of performance and reliability.

The ESS can be partitioned among simultaneously attached AIX, iSeries, mainframe, and other UNIX servers, enabling users to easily manage and share storage resources.
