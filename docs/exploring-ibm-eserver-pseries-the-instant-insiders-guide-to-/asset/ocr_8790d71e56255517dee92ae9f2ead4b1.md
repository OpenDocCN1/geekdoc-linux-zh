The reason for the distinction between Models 6F1 and 6FO is that the maximum of four processors in the p620-6FO qualifies it for workgroup pricing for popular software programs, such as Oracle and IBM DB2 databases, making the total system cost more attractive for many users.

The rack-mount pSeries 630 Model 6C4 and desktop pSeries 630 Model E4 are 1-, 2-, and 4-way SMP servers with either 1.2 GHz or 1.45 GHz POWER4+ processors. The 1- and 2-way configurations provide attractive entry points for small businesses, with room to grow in processing power, memory, and I/O capacity. The p630-6C4 rack system meets NEBS Level 3 standards, which it is designed to withstand under rugged conditions of temperature, vibration, and the like. This versatile entry system also offers the flexibility of logical partitioning (LPAR—the acronym LPAR is used in this book to refer to "logical partitioning" as a technology or function to, which allows users to create four "virtual servers within a server." The p630-6E4 is positioned as a replacement for the pSeries 640, and the p630-6E4 will replace the RS/6000 Model 270.

The pSeries 640 is a rack-mountable server designed especially for high-density server farms in e-commerce and Telco applications. In addition, the 1- 4-way POWER3-II microprocessors used in the p640 provide the floating-point power needed for many HPC applications. The pSeries640 meets NEBS Level 3 standards, making it suitable for Telco and harsh environments.

The pSeries 650 is IBM's most popular midrange server. This 2-8-way rack-mount system offers 1.2 GHz or 1.45 GHz POWER4+ processors, LPAR support, and the capacity for attaching up to IBM 7311-D10 or 7311-D20 I/O expansion drawers. The p650, positioned as the POWER4-based replacement for the p660-6M1, is an excellent choice for business processes such as enterprise resource planning (ERP), supply chain management (SCM), customer relationship management (CRM) and business intelligence.

The cluster-optimized pSeries 655 fills a special niche in the pSeries family. It is a high-density configuration, offering 4-way 1.7 GHz or 8-way 1.5 GHz processors in a compact unit that occupies 4 EIA units (4U) in half of a 24-inch rack. This allows up to 128 processors in a single 42U 24-inch frame, making the p655 an excellent choice for clustered solutions operating under the control of a single AIX 5L server server.
