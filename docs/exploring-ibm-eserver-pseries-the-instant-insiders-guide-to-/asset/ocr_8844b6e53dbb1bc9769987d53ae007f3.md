After the warranty period, the customer becomes responsible for
maintenance of the system. IBM and others offer service agreements
that, for a fee, provide postwarranty on-site service just like that pro-
vided during the warranty, as well as less expensive options for other
than $7 x 24$ support. Various maintenance discounts are available, de-
pending on the level of systems management procedures implemented
in a specific situation.

If the system fails and there is no service contract, the user will have
to pay for parts and labor, which can quickly become extremely expen-
sive. For this reason, many businesses choose to keep their systems on
maintenance after warranty as a type of insurance policy.

Many factors affect the choice of computer system for a particular
use. These factors include application availability, total cost of owner-
ship, ergonomics, and the availability of qualified support providers.
Where a UNIX solution fits the bill, IBM eServer pSeries systems, the
AIX 5L operating system and associated software products, and the
support offerings available from IBM and IBM Business Partners, de-
liver solutions that merit careful consideration.
