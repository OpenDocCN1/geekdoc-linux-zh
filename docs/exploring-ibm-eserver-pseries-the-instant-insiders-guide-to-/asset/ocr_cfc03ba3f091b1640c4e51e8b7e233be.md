RIO-2 hubs interface only only to I/O drawers. The p650 supports both the IBM 7311-D10 and 7311-D20 I/O Drawers in up to four RIO-I/O loops. RIO-2 loops operate at a 1 GHz clock rate, and since they are bidirectional, they can support a peak bandwidth of 2 Gbps. If two or more I/O drawers are connected in series in a RIO-2 loop, the system will transfer data from each RIO-2 port to the near I/O drawer—increasing the peak bandwidth to 4 Gbps per loop.

Both the 7311-D10 and 7311-D20 I/O drawers are 4U19-inch rack units. However, the 7311-D10 provides six I/O slots and no disk bays in a half-drawer package, so two 7311-D10 drawers fit side-by-side in a rack. On the other hand, the 7311-D20 is a full-width drawer with 7 I/O slots and 12 optional disk drive bays. If additional storage is needed, the 7311-D20 is the best choice. If only I/O adapter slots are needed, then the 7311-D10 is the appropriate answer. The bottom line is that a fully configured p650 provides a peak I/O bandwidth of 16 Gbps and can accommodate up to 63 I/O adapters and 14.6 TB of storage—al of capacity.
