# User Education

The discussions in the chapter so far should assist you in selecting the appropriate software and hardware to fit your needs. However, no matter what computer hardware and software you select, you will need people to operate them. To maximize efficiency and morale, these people (or users) must be provided with education in the use of the computer system itself well as its software.

Proper education is critical to the success of any automation project. Inadequate education will prevent the productivity benefits afforded by moving a task to the computer. The goal of education is to make the users proficient at using the computer hardware and software and to make their interaction with the computer enjoyable. If using the computer is enjoyable, users will be more motivated. If the computer represents a frustrating struggle because of inadequate education, users will be less productive or perhaps will the computer altogether.

The education should cover the pSeries hardware, the AIX 5L operating system, and the application software that will be used. Both pSeries and AIX 5L have been designed with close attention to making them as easy to use as possible. The documentation and on-line education courses provide a self-guided tour through pSeries and AIX 5L basics. These offerings should provide adequate education for most users.

For programmers and IT personnel who will be responsible for installing and managing a pSeries system, more detailed on-line courses and many classroom training offerings are available from IBM and others. The amount of education needed will depend on the tasks performed performed the experience of the student with AIX 5L or another UNIX operating system. Again, the on-line education courses offered by IBM are a good starting point for learning the AIX 5L operating system. Although the systems administrator will probably need such formal AIX 5L operating system education, often the operating system can be all hidden from the users, minimizing their needs in this area.

Education in the use of the application program is usually the most important thing for the users. The content of this education depends on the particular application program selected. Typically, manuals provided with the application program will contain step-by-step tutorials designed to train the user.
