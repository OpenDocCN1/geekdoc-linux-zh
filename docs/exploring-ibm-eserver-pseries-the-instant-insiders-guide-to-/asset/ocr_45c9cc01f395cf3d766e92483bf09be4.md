We will describe a few of the choices. Figure 2.4 shows a typical FAStT rack enclosure, and Figure 2.5 is a table comparing the FAStT systems.

The 3552 TotalStorage FAST200 Storage Server (Figure 2.6) is a low-cost, Fibre Channel-attached storage system, offering 10 hotswapp drives drives per 3U rack drawer, expandable to 60 drives using five EXP500 disk expansion enclosures or 66 drives with four EXP700 expansion enclosures. Using the 146.8 GB disks, the FASt200 provides up to 9.6 TB of storage with integrated RAID 0, 1, 3, 5, and 10 capability. Both single- and dual-controller models are available.
