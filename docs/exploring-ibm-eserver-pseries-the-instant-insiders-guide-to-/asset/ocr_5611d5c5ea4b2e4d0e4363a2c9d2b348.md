# pSeries and the Token-Ring Network

The Token-Ring LAN technology, developed by IBM, is less pervasive in the industry than Ethernet. Although pSeries systems and AIX still support Token-Ring communications, it is not widely used. Figure 4.7 shows the basic structure of the Token-Ring network. The nodes of the network are arranged in a ring pattern, thus giving the network its name. Information is transferred over the network at either 4 or 16 million bits per second using the token-passing protocol.
