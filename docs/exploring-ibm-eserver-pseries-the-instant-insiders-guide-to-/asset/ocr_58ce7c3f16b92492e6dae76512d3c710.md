A communications line can be thought of as a telephone wire or wireless link between two or more computer systems across long distances, or as a cable between a computer and peripheral devices in the same room, or by using multiple types of communications lines, information and host access can be provided to users in many locations.
To be attached to a communications line, the pSeries system must first have the proper electrical interface. The term “interface” refers collectively to the connector, electrical voltage levels, connector pin functions, and so on that are provided for the attachment to a communications line. We will discuss options such as the EIA-232D, EIA-422A, MIL-STD-188-114, CCITT X.21, CCITT V.35, and Token-Ring interfaces. For our purposes, it is not necessary to understand exactly all these cryptic names mean. Simply know that different types of interfaces are necessary to support different types of communications.
In addition to the different interfaces, you must also know about communications protocols to conversant in computer communications. Just as there are different rules of grammar and punctuation in English, French, and other languages, there are different rules for types of computer communications. In computer communications, a set of rules calls a communications protocol. The protocols of most interest for our purposes are the async, bisync, SDLC, X.25, CSMA/CD (IEEE 802.3), and Token-Ring (IEEE 802.5). Each of these protocols has the same basic goal of moving information from one place to another efficiently and reliably. Each protocol has advantages and disadvantages, and the proper choice depends on system requirements in the area of transmission speed, cost, and compatibility with the other devices in the network. At all times, however, all devices using a given communications line must be using the same protocol.
The async protocol (short for asynchronous) is a low-speed, low-cost communications method very commonly used, but now used mainly for legacy devices such as ASCII terminals and hand-held devices such as barcode scanners or personal organizers. The async protocol is a good place to start in understanding the fundamentals of communications protocols.
