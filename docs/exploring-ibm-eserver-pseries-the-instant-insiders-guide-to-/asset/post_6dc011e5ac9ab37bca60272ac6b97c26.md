# Exploring IBM @server pSeries

Flexibility is increasingly important for customers and pSeries is making every effort to ensure

a wide range of infrastructure requirements. For example, we have integrated Linux into our pSeries ecosystem where it makes sense for customers. It's clear that Linux is an option that customers want available to, particularly at the entry and midrange levels, and pSeries has a number of Linux Ready Configurations for customers to integrate Linux using pSeries. In addition to native Linux on pSeries systems, our Dynamic Logical Partitioning (DLPAR) allows customers to utilize Linux, AIX 5 or both operating systems in any combination—all on one server. Customers have never had such extraordinary levels of flexibility and pSeries is only making it better.

Taking flexibility to the next level is what IBM's e-business on demand initiative is all about. IBM is connecting the future of how business gets done with technology in a way that is revolutionizing the entire concept of e-business. At its core, e-business on demand is fundamentally about taking organizational productivity to new levels of success. And pSeries technology plays a critical role in this new era of productivity.

In fact, many of the pSeries technology advantages developed by IBM over the years are perfectly suited for an on demand operating environment. It's in our DNA. We've been at this for a while and the pSeries heritage fits perfectly into an on demand era. IBM has a strong history in developing technologies that can breathe “on demand” life into customer enterprises in new and exciting ways.

Market dynamics are altering the IT approach customers must take to support their business in an era of demand computing. The pSeries platform is ready and waiting to implement with a host of new demand customers can implement immediately. And with a solid on demand roadmap in place, the pSeries offerings are well-positioned to represent cutting-edge “no compromises” on demand UNIX and Linux solutions.

I hope you enjoy this latest edition of Exploring IBM eServer pSeries and that it provides you deeper insights on how IBM technology is breaking new ground to provide even greater levels of customer value.

Adalio Sanchez, General Manager,
IBM eServer pSeries, IBM Corporation
