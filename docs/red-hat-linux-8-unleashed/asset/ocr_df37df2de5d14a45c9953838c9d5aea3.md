# Using XFree86

The valid range for horizontal sync and vertical sync should be documented in the manual of your monitor. If in doubt, check the monitor database /usr/X11RG6/lib/X11/doc/Monitors to see if your monitor is there.

Press Enter to continue, or ctrl-c to abort.

You press Enter to begin configuring your monitor. Each make and model of monitor or notebook LCD panel uses specific horizontal and vertical frequency ranges, which influence the minimum and maximum resolutions possible on the display. For desktop PC monitors, the frequencies should be documented in the monitor's manual. You might also be able to get the information from the monitor manufacturer's Web site. In order to use the highest resolution with X on your Red desktop, you select or enter the appropriate range in the following prompts:

You must indicate the horizontal sync range of your monitor. You can either select one of the predefined ranges below that correspond to industry-standard monitor types, or give a specific range.

It is VERY IMPORTANT that you do not specify a monitor type with a horizontal sync range that is beyond the capabilities of your monitor. If in doubt, give a conservative setting.

```
hsync in kHz; monitor type with characteristic modes
1 31.5; Standard VGA, 640x480 @ 60 Hz
2 31.5 - 35.1; Super VGA, 800x600 @ 56 Hz
3 31.5, 35.5; 8514 Compatible, 1024x768 @ 87 Hz interlaced, 800x600
4 31.5, 35.15, 35.5; Super VGA, 1024x768, 800x600 @ 56 Hz
5 31.5 - 37.9; Extended Super VGA, 800x600 @ 60 Hz, 640x480 @ 72 Hz
6 31.5 - 48.5; Non-Interleaved SVGA, 1024x768 @ 60 Hz, 800x600 @ 72 Hz
7 31.5 - 57.0; High Frequency SVGA, 1024x768 @ 70 Hz
8 31.5 - 64.3; Monitor that can do 1280x1024 @ 60 Hz
9 31.5 - 79.0; Monitor that can do 1280x1024 @ 74 Hz
10 31.5 - 82.0; Monitor that can do 1280x1024 @ 76 Hz
Enterter your own horizontal sync range
```

Enter your choice (1-11):

Next, you type a number that matches the range of frequencies that are available with your monitor and press Enter. You can enter specific values by typing 11 and pressing Enter.
