### Other Graphical CD Clients
Although KOnCD can rip audio tracks, Red Hat provides the GN Grip client to advance ripping to a fine art. Working as both a player and a ripper, it uses cdparanoia and ccda2wav to rip music tracks and access Internet databases for retrieving track information from disc servers. To launch Grip, click on its icon in the Sound & Video menu found in the Extras menu. Use the options in the Grip dialog box tabs to choose, and rip tracks (see Figure 26.7).
