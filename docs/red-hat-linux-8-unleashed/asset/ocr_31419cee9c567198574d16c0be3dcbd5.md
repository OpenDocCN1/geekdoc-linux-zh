# What Is Linux?

Linux is the core, or kernel, of a free operating system first developed and released to the world Linus Benedict Torvalds in 1991.—then a graduate student at the University of Helsinki, Finland, and now an engineer with the CPU design and fabrication company Transmeta, Inc.—fortunesately to distribute Linux under a free software license named the GNU General Public License (GPL).
