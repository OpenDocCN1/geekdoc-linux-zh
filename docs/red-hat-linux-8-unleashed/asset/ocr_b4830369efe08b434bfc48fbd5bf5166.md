# CHAPTER 20 News and Other Collaborative Communication

To begin newsreading with Mozilla, you launch Mozilla and select Mozilla, Edit; then you select the Preferences item from the drop-down menu. The window shown in Figure 20.6 appears. In the Category list, you can expand the Mail and Newsgroups item to display the list of configuration options. You should click OK when you're done.

Figure 20.6 Configuring Mozilla to read both mail and news.

Next, you click the Window menu selection on the Mozilla browser itself and chose Mail & Newsgroups. The first time you do this, the Account Wizard is displayed; from it, you can set up your email and news account. If you need to change the information, you can open the Edit menu in the Mail and News window and choose Mail & Newsgroup Account Settings. Also, if you click the mail or news accounts displayed in the left pane of the Mail and News window, clickable links appear in the right pane to allow you to check the settings or create a new account.

When you finishing this information, you can click OK, and arrive at the newsreader window, as shown in Figure 20.7. You can click the news server name in the left pane and select Subscribe to Newsgroups in the right pane to begin downloading a list of newsgroups from your news server. At any time, you can update the list of newsgroups by clicking the Refresh button in that window.
