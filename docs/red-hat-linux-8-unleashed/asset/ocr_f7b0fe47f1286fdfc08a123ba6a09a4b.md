Movies, music, animation, live video conferencing, and other multimedia functions have become essential capabilities for many home and business computing environments. Without question, multimedia has driven the growth of the Internet and the advances in computer hardware as much as the traditional services of mail, news, and other Internet-related services.

Red Hat Linux includes several hundred different programs you can use to perform feats of magic using audio and video, including graphics, video and audio formats used on, less capable operating systems.

This chapter provides an overview of some of the basic media-tools included with Red Hat Linux. You'll see how to create your own CDs, watch TV, rip audio CDs into the open source OGG audio format for playback, scan and manipulate graphics images, and perform other productive and enjoyable tasks.

> With the release of version 8.0, Red Hat has taken a step that has been controversial with many of its longtime users. Because of concerns of potential liability arising from copyright and patent issues in the United States, Red Hat has voluntarily removed any functionality that could violate the intellectual property rights of others or subject Red Hat to any penalties. That doesn't mean individual users would be violating the same rights, so we have provided resources to the reader as how some of that functionality can be restored. The choice is yours.
