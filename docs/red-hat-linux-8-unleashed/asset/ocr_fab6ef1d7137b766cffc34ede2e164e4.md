The Display Settings main screen is a dialog with tabs labeled Display and Advanced. You can use the options in the Display tab to select a resolution and color depth for an X session. The configuration options available in the Advanced tab are shown in Figure 6.2.

You can click the configure button in the Monitor section of the Advanced tab to open the Monitor Settings dialog, which is shown in Figure 6.3. You can use this dialog to configure a different monitor or change current monitor settings.

You can scroll through the list of monitors (from the MonitorDB database) and click one to select it and enter it in the Model Name text box in the right pane of the dialog. You can also enter new frequencies or probe the monitor for values to use. When you are finished, click the OK button to use the new settings or click the Cancel button to abort changes.

To configure the video card, you can click the Configure button in the Video Card section of the Display Settings dialog, the Video Card dialog appears, as shown in Figure 6.4.

You can scroll through the list of video cards. You can click one to select it and then click to select a new driver for your card. Alternatively, you can click the Probe button to probe your card for values to use. You can also manually enter video RAM values, and you can add or remove video chipset options. These options may be documented in an XFree86 driver manual page. When you are finished, click the OK button to finalize your selections and return to the Display Settings main screen.
