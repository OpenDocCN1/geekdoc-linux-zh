The QUERY: 1, ANSWER: 0 in the response means that e.root-servers didn’t know the answer to your question. It knows the authoritative servers for the com TLD, and it refers your query to them in the AUTHORITY section. (Not too long ago, the root-servers.net name servers were themselves authoritative for the com TLD, but additional delegations were introduced.)
