You use Gnumeric to perform financial calculations and to graph data, as shown in Figure 25.4. To launch Gnumeric, choose Extras, Office, Gnumeric. You can also launch the spreadsheet editor from the command line, like this:

```
$ gnumeric &
```

After you press Enter, the main Gnumeric window appears. You enter data in the spreadsheet by clicking a cell and typing in the text box. To create a graph, you click and drag over the spreadsheet cells to highlight the desired data, and then you click the Graph Wizard in Gnumeric's toolbar. Its graphing component launches, and you're guided through a series of dialogs to create a graph. When you're finished, you can click and drag a blank area of your spreadsheet, and the graph appears.

The MrProject application (see Figure 25.5) is for tracking the progress of projects, much like its win32 counterpart Microsoft Project. Although it's officially a part of the GNOME Office applications, Red Hat includes MrProject in the main Office menu as part of the Red Hat Suite. You click on the icon to launch the application. When the main window is displayed, you can start a new project or import an existing project. A user guide is available at http://mrproject.codefactory.se/user-guide.php.
