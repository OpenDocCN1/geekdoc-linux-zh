> NOTE
All seems unnecessarily complicated to fax users who are used to sending and receiving their
faxes on their own machine through a modem connected to that machine. The Linux (and
UNIX) approach is network focused. A single computer can be set up to send and receive faxes
for the entire network of hundreds of computers. None of the individual computers have a needor Efax; they just need a fax reader and a convenient way to send them to the central fax server.
