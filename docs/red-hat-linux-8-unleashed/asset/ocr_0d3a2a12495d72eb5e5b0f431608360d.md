# Hoyt Duff

Until I participated in writing this book, I had no idea of the amount of work that goes on behind the scenes by the editors involved. The popular notion is that editors are mean people intent on destroying the authors' creation. That makes for good party chatter, but the reality is far from stereotype. The editors associated with this book are delightful, professional folks who obviously enjoy what they do; I've learned a great deal about writing from them. Thank you Katie, Dave, George, Rhonda, Kitty, Mike, and all the staff at Sams Publishing.
