**FIGURE 19.4** You can launch and configure Evolution with just a few simple commands. The identity screen asks you to enter your information. Click Next to proceed.

![](bbox=[0.253, 0.427, 0.772, 0.693])

**FIGURE 19.5** The Receiving Email screen requests information from your ISP or your system administrator.

You must also choose between SMTP and Sendmail for sending your mail; enter your email address, and choose a time zone (very important for your calendar). Finally, you will see the opening Evolution Summary window in Figure 19.6.

Each choice shown as an icon in the left-hand pane in Figure 19.6 opens a different window when selected; each view has options that can be configured to suit your needs; just click on the Tools menu item from the top menu as shown in Figure 19.7.
