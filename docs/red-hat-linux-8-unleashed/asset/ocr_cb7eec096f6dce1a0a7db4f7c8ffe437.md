The Configure a Local Printer dialog box shown in Figure 12.3 presents a list of your Red Hat system’s printer devices. If your computer has a parallel port printer, you will see a /dev/lp0 device. If you have a USB printer attached to your computer, you will see a /dev/usb/1p0 device.

![](bbox=[0.283, 0.199, 0.687, 0.439])

FIGURE 12.3 Click to select the desired printer device you want to configure for your system.

In the example in Figure 12.3, a parallel-port printer device, /dev/lp0, is shown.
You can configure a printer for Red Hat even if it isn’t attached to your computer, or if its device is not listed. In this case, appropriate the appropriate device by clicking the Custom Device button and typing in the device name. Otherwise, double-click on the listed device that you want to configure, and the Select a Printer dialog appears, as shown in Figure 12.4.

![](bbox=[0.283, 0.643, 0.687, 0.883])
