# Mail List Management with Mailman

![](bbox=[0.187, 0.085, 0.812, 0.477])

Figure 20.10 The mailman admin page for a mail list named Rhl8.0.

Configuration Options and Other Administrative Activities, shown in Figure 20.11, are clickable links that permit access to the full range of administrative options available for Mail.

Anyone who wants to subscribe to the list can browse to the page displayed at http://localhost.localdomain/mailman/, fill in his name and email address, and then pick a password. The person will receive an email notification of membership when he or she is approved.

> NOTE Christopher Kolar has assembled "User Documentation," a "Quick Reference Guide," and a "Manager’s Guide" for managing mail lists; these guides are available for free at http://staff.isa/~ckolar/mailman/. They are expertly written and are indispensable resources. "Site Administrator Documentation" is available at http://www.list.org/admins.html. This is where you find out how to use the scripts in /var/mailman/bin that are of most use in scripts you would write to automate many administrative tasks; it’s another can’t-live-without-it resource.
