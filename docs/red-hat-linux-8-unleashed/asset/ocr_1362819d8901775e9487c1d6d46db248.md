# Using XFree86

If you can't find your card in the database, there's nothing to worry about.
You should only choose a database entry that is exactly the same model as your card; choosing one that looks similar is just a bad idea (e.g. a GemStone Snail 64 may be as different from a GemStone Snail 64+ in terms of hardware as can be.

Do you want to look at the card database?

You type a and press Enter to view the XFree video card database, and then you can select your system's card from the database:

Enter a number to choose the corresponding card definition.
Press enter for the next page, q to continue configuration.

You press the Enter key to go through the file, and then you type the number corresponding to your video card and press Enter. If you don't know what video card is installed in your computer, you can consult your PC's original accompanying documentation, look for specifications on the manufacturer's Web site, or physically inspect the card or motherboard if support is built in.

For example, after you enter a number as 422, xf86config reports this:
