# About the Technical Reviewer

Dave Taylor is a popular writer, teacher, and speaker focused on business and technology issues. The founder of The Internet Mall and iTrack.com, has been involved with UNIX and the Internet since 1980, having created the popular Elm Mail System and Embot mail autoresresponder. A prolific author, his most recent books include the best-sellers Learning Unix for Mac OS X, Sams Teach Yourself in 24 Hours, Creating Cool HTML 4.0 Web Pages, Dynamic HTML Weekend Crash Course, and the e\Auction Insider.

Previous positions include research scientist at HP Laboratories and senior reviews editor of SunWorld magazine. He has contributed software to the original 4.4 release of Berkeley UNIX (BSD), and his programs are found in all versions of Linux and other popular UNIX variants.

Dave has a bachelor’s degree in computer science, a master’s degree in educational computing, and he is working on an MBA. He teaches business and technology-related courses both in the physical world through the University of Colorado at Boulder, and online with the University of Phoenix. His email address has been the same for more than 15 years: taylor@intuitive.com.

When not typing maddly on the keyboard of his Mac, Dave’s either relaxing with his wife, Linda, and two wonderful kids, Ashley and Gareth, or out bicycling around Boulder.
