The process of changing a user's image is simple. Just perform the following steps to change it in no time:

+   1. Open the Start screen and click your user picture, as shown in Figure 5-2.

![](bbox=[0.18, 0.17, 0.87, 0.58])

**Figure 5-2:** Click your user picture to access your account settings.

+   2. Click Change Account Picture.
3. Click the Browse button.
4. Navigate the browser until you find an image you want to use and click it.
5. Select Choose Image.

Your user image on the logon screen is now changed; you have also updated the image used on the Start screen.
