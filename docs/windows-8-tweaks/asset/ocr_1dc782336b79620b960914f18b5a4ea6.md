To get started, head over to [tweaks.com/647410](http://tweaks.com/647410) to get a free evaluation copy. After it is installed, follow these steps to customize your system icons:

+   1. After you have installed Microangelo On Display, right-click any system icon, such as the hard drive icon in Computer, and click Appearance.
This brings up the Appearance window shown in Figure 7-7.
2. Under the Display section, switch the option to Custom.
3. Browse and select your replacement icon and click OK.
4. Click Apply to save and see your changes and then click OK.

If you do not like your new icon and want to change it back to the default, right-click the icon again and this time select System Default under the Display section and click OK.

Now that you've finished customizing the look of your desktop and system icons, you can customize the desktop.
