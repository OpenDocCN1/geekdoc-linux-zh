# Get under the hood
and create your own Windows 8

New from the ground up, Windows 8 has revolutionized Microsoft’s operating system.
Now, whether you love Windows 8 or hate it, this no-nonsense book gives you the tools
to customize it exactly to your liking. Boost speed, turbocharge performance, unlock
hidden settings, banish features that drive you crazy, lock down security, and make
Windows 8 your own.

+   * Customize the lock screen and navigation
* Make the traditional desktop touch-friendly
* Turn your PC into a DVR
* Speed up system boot and logon
* Increase network and online speed
* Protect your computer with BitLocker drive encryption
* Disable Internet Explorer features that put privacy at risk

Steve Sinchak runs Advanced PC Media LLC, a web development firm that operates
Tweaks.com and other websites geared towards computer enthusiasts. He also works
as a systems engineer for a financial services company, and has received nine Microsoft
Most Valuable Professional (MVP) awards for his contributions to the Windows community.
