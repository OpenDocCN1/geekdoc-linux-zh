The difference between good and bad cookies is how they are issued. If you go to Tweaks.com, the web server issues your browser a first-party cookie. That cookie contains harmless user session data so the web server knows whether you are logged in. The cookies that have the potential to be abused are called third-party cookies because they are not issued by the web server you are visiting, but rather by a third-party. These cookies are common among online advertising programs such as Google AdSense. The privacy concern that can make them bad cookies depends on what the advertising company does with them.
