# Conventions Used in This Book

This book uses two notification icons—Tip and Caution—that point out important information. Here's what the two types of icons look like:

**TIP** Tips provide small, helpful hints and related tweaks.

**CAUTION** Cautions alert you to possible hazards that can result from the tweak.

Code, commands, and executables within the text appear in a *monospace font*, whereas content you type appears in **bold**.
