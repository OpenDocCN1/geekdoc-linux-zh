To get your cursors set perfectly for your theme file, follow these steps:

+   1. Open the Start screen, type main.cpl, and press Enter to open Mouse Properties.

+   2. Click the Pointers tab.

+   3. You have two options to customize the cursors. You can use the drop-down Scheme box to change all the pointers simultaneously to different styles by selecting a different cursor scheme from the list, as shown in Figure 8-3. When you select the different schemes, all the cursors change automatically. Alternatively, if you do not like the cursor schemes, you can individually select a cursor from the Customize list by scrolling through the list and selecting the cursor you want to change. Then click the Browse button to change it.

+   4. When you have finished customizing your cursors, just click OK, and you are finished.

Now you are ready to move on to customizing the visual style that the theme will use.
