You have a choice between three different standard icon sizes on the desktop. Figure 7-3 shows the three icon sizes: small (classic), medium, and large.

You can change the icon size by simply right-clicking the desktop, clicking View, and then clicking the icon size you prefer.

If you are a power user, there is a secret way to shrink or enlarge the icons with a much wider range of sizes, but you must have an external mouse with a scroll wheel. Just hold down the Ctrl key on your keyboard and scroll up or down while on the desktop. You can create ridiculously large icons with this method, as shown in Figure 7-4.
