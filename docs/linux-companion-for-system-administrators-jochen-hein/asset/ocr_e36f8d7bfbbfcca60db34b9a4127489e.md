+   - LINUX can, without great expense, turn a PC into a workstation, an terminal or a flexible and powerful server.

+   - LINUX support is equally provided by distributors and developers, and even by other users. In fact, any interested party can offer commercial support, so the user has freedom of choice between many providers.

+   - LINUX is well adapted to common PC hardware, many drivers are available and the system is comparatively fast.

+   - There are already many applications in the areas of program development, office packages, Internet servers and clients, as well as emulators of other systems such as Windows or IBC2. And there is also a growing market for commercial applications.

+   - The system is well documented via electronic manuals (in /usr/doc or /usr/share/doc), or man pages, and there are many books on the subject. If the documentation leaves a question unanswered, you can always look for a solution in the source code itself, if the worst comes to the worst.

+   - LINUX is available at a low price due to the free nature of LINUX and the stiff competition between the distributors. This is often a major selling point, but personally I consider the freedom to choose an operating system much more valuable.

+   This list is certainly not complete and could be extended, with different users emphasizing different aspects. There is no system yet that would satisfy every possible user.
