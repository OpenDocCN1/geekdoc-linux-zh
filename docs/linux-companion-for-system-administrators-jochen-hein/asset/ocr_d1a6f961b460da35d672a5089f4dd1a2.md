Washburn, K., and Evans, J. (1994). TCP/IP: Running a Successful Network. Addison-Wesley

An excellent introduction to the setup and operation of a TCP/IP network. The basics are explained in an understandable manner, taking into account the problems of a continuously growing and developing network. Another point in its favour is the presentation of protocols and data exchanged by the programs.
