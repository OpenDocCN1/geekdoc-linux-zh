A special mention should be given to the effort that has gone into providing a powerful and flexible desktop. Three such projects are GNUStep, GNOME, and KDE. Furthermore, a series of applications have become available, as StarOffice, ApplicWare, and Adabas. All in all, LINUX is beginning to leave behind its secretive status as an insider's tool for hackers and programmers and is increasingly becoming a general use system.

The second edition contains many small improvements to practically all subjects, but also major changes to current areas, in particular the desktops and network functions.
