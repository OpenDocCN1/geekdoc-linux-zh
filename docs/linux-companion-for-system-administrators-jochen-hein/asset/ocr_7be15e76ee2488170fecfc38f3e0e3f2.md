# 1.2.3 Caldera Open Linux

Based on the LST distribution, Caldera presents Caldera Open Linux (COL). Caldera also intends to provide a stable LINUX basis for other software enterprises. This basis is intended to induce other companies to port their programs to LINUX. Caldera distributes, for example, a port of WordPerfect and CoreDraw. With this, Caldera wants to make LINUX usable in commercial environments; this is backed by an offer of support.

On the Caldera Solutions CD, several commercial programs (for example the Adabas database) are distributed. These can be unlocked by means of a license key.

The installation is simple and well-devised, many packages are included, and the system is immediately usable. If you are interested in commercial packages, Caldera is a good choice.

# 1.2.4 Debian (GNU/Linux) distribution

The Debian distribution was initially developed by Ian Murdock. In the course of time, many other developers became involved with Debian. This made the Debian distribution a worldwide project that was also supported by the Free Software Foundation (FSF). The FSF considered this distribution a ‘test case’ for their own Hurd operating system.
