**Listing 24.2** Display of all tcp connections in the network.

The next example shows only the connections with the login port. If you
compare the output of Listing 24.3 with that of Listing 24.2, you will find fewer\packets. Beyond the limitations to ports, you can also make selections by
computers (host), networks (net), or gateways (gateway). You can also limit
tisplay to showing incoming or outgoing packets (dst or src) and individual
protocols (udp, tcp, or icmp).

```
(Linux):# tcpdump -n -q -t port login
```
