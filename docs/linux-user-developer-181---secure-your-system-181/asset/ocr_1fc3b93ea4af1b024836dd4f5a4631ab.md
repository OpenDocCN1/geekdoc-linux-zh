Welcome to the UK and North America's favourite Linux and FOSS magazine.
Last quarter, 36% of all malware was targeting Linux (WatchGuard Threat Report Q1 2017).
This was mostly IoT devices, but that's hardly surprising given the number of products hitting the market that can't be patched post-release and are sitting there waiting to be hacked. This free ride needs to end. These companies need to invest in the open source software they use or be named and shamed out of business.
While the IoT situation is lamentable, on the desktop side we've got you covered with our feature on securing your system (p18): we go into everything from disabling unused services to security auditing and automating system monitoring to sandboxing.
We also take a fresh (sorry) look at Linux Mint development (p28) and an interview with its founder (p12). For the tinkerers, we've a report from MakeFest in Liverpool as well as a packed Pi section (from p69) and for developers there's a comprehensive guide to getting the most out of AWS. As well as all our regular pickings, including a group test on virtualisers (p81), we've kick-started a Linux Essentials series for newcomers (p36) and on p44 we've dabbled in recreational maths. It's a packed issue so tuck in!
Chris Thornett, Editor
