# MintBox machines

A close partnership between Compulab and the Linux Mint team has produced a line of miniature PCs that come ready to use with Mint pre-installed. Based on Compulab's fitel mini-PCs, three different Mintbox variants are available – the high-end Intel i5-powered MintBox 2, the mid-range AMD A10-powered MintBox Mini Pro and the lower-end AMD A4-based MintBox Mini. Although sold at a relatively high price, the machines have been well received due to their pocketable size, completely fanless running, good performance and – of course – excellent hardware compatibility with Linux Mint.
