To resize a window, click and drag its edge or corner or use the scroll bars to move your view of the window's contents about. You can click the up arrow on the vertical scroll bar to move up a line or the down arrow to move down a line; click the scroll bar itself to move up or down a screen, and click and drag the slider (elevator) in the scroll bar to move the window up or down as far as you wish. The horizontal scroll bar performs these same actions in the right and left directions.
