# KaOS 7/10 Manjaro 8/10 Netrunner Rolling 7/10

KDE has been noted for its configurability. This can be a curse as well as a blessing. KDE 4 in particular dispersed options all over the window, and also with a meaningless window glow enabled by KDE Plasma 5 has a default that offers plenty of options to manipulate.

The principle Manjaro edition is the Xfce one. It's not going to resonate with people wanting desktop bliss, since Xfce hasn't changed in nearly three years. It's functional, but far ugly and gentle on the eyes. Compared to Plasma, scalers aren't working at all, but this edition is not as useful as KDE edition.

Netrunner's Plasma setup features the fullscreen application dashboard, which like Gnome's can also search. The large fonts look old, indeed. We much prefer Manjaro's hybrid launcher, and we might not like the clean leading menu used by KAOS.

Being a KDE user after all, we expected KDE to look up a lot different from KAOS, and it certainly did. A menubar on the right-hand side of the screen, and Dolphin with its navigation panel. You can probably get used to it, but it's just a bit of fresh experience. Call us old (Old is better than new), but we were much happier with the panel at the bottom, and the file manager panel on the left. We heartily approve of the Powerline terminal stylish, though.

KAOS Manjaro favours exist for those desktops, and community favours for others. Traditionalists with a shade of control (bars) and the ease and configuration of appearance/window manager settings. There are several icon and GTK themes to choose. Quantum theme manager can jazz up Qt apps, but that is not as useful in the KDE edition.

The Computer and Network icons on the desktop seem a bit-Windows, or are largely ignored/unresponsive. The Alphabet theme for versions of Plasma (5.14) and Qt (5.11) give some transparency effects. Minor annoyance about the layout of the desktop and the new wallpapers developers are included. The Breeze theme for a GTK counterparty, such applications will conform to the desktop fiery. No desktop are by default. Good.
