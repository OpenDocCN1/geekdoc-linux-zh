You can drag and drop all your accessed email folders into the box on the top-left of the application's interface. This creates a list of frequently accessed folders, you can then navigate the left sidebar for these folders.

Unless you're comfortable with announcing to all your peers that the Mailspring has been sent using immediate attention, Note the signature is not quite the same as 'Sent from my iPhone',

Despite opting the choice of layouts such as Wide View, Vertical View and Folder Pane, the best appearance is of the Classic View, which presents a three-panel interface.

All your emails are grouped by day/month/year. Messages are collapsed into the respective day/month/year, and you can double-click to get a list of messages. KMail does the threaded view better than the other tools, using line space to denote features. The search at the top can be used to perform search operations, but if you want more functionality, you can click Tools>Find Messages.

Like Claws and Thunderbird, Mailspring supports editing themes. Click Edit-Options to choose your six options theme.

When confronted with an unfamiliar email in one language, you can right-click the entry and then click "Search for 'word'. You can change the language settings in the message dialog.

Unlike the other tools, Mailspring has a four-pane layout. The left sidebar is the same as all the other options. To your right is the selected message. In the fourth pane you get all details about the email, which performs you the names of the people in the conversation. At the bottom of this right sidebar is a list of all messages exchanged with the same person.

When searching messages, it will show in a new tab, with filters to select the different messages based on sender, recipient or whether messages have an attachment.

For messages that require attention, click the Create-Do button on the menu bar. You can then fill in all the details to create a reminder

When composing a message, you click Options>Request Receipt if you want delivery confirmation. You can configure this for all outgoing messages by clicking Menu>Preferences>Preferences>Advanced. Then click Return Receipts.

# Customising with add-ons

Building up on the top of install isn't enough ...

Mailspring's default installation is the total of what's on offer. You're provided with a few more features if you opt to subscribe to the Pro version, at a cost of $8 per month ($6), but it still doesn't give as many features as you can get for free tools.

Thunderbird offers the choice of dozens to change the appearance, in addition to specific extensions to tweak the menu entries and other changes to the interface. You also get functionality such as the Automatic Dictionary. This extension allows communicating with a particular person or group and automatically switches the spell-checker. The add-ons page provides a list of available extensions such as Colours, Filters, Filters and News reading, Security and Tags. You can also scroll through the most popular list on the left.

Claws is the straightforward email client, and you must resort to its plug-ins if you want to add an aggregator. One aggregator is the NCPIR plugin, which allows you to read email from Acer and Asus. For example, you can also opt from one of several themes to change the appearance of the interface.

Evolution's default interface has several plug-ins installed. In fact, it has many new email features, such as new mail notification is also provided through a plug-in.

A lot of functionality in KMail is driven when you use plug-ins. Click Settings>Configuration KMail and click Plug-ins for a list. You might be especially interested in the ad-blocker plugin.
