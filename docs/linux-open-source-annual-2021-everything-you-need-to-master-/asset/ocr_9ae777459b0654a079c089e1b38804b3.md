# Office suites
Verdict

The choice of Roundup subjects over the past several issues has left us to conclude that one might not cater to all of needs. This is true for image and video editors, and office suites aren't either. If you can spare the resources, you might find yourself preferring presentations of one suite's application, while relying on other spreadsheets.

With the exception of the polish finisher, these office suites also disappointed and improved in our view. WPS Office is a highly capable suite, and we optimistically advise others to give it a chance, but the lack of ODF support, with no wonder if it will ever be available, is a stated flaw for this suite. The last of all is that it is fast and responsive, and fun enough for Linux also.

Calligraphy, despite being highly capable, is also a major disappointment – but cheap for its interface. If you can master its esoteric workflow, and don't mind finding your way trial and error, there's nothing wrong with it. But if you plan on running Calligraphy on a small, 14-inch laptop, you'll barely have room for your dishes and you couldn't make the poppies

While OnlyOffice and LibreOffice support the primary requirements of documents. OnlyOffice fares far better at rendering documents. Unlike the other suites, however, it doesn't feature a database or drawing application, offering only a spreadsheet, word processor and presentation application. The office edition provides all that you need except for the collaboration with the cloud variants. OnlyOffice performs well with the limited number of options offered, and isnt as robust as LibreOffice. If you don't care about collaboration, then we would definitely advise you to give it a try.

There's very little speedy Google Docs and LibreOffice, for the fact that the first needs an internet connection to function, while the latter is a desktop solution.

# ALSO-CONSIDER

There aren't any other open source suites to consider for the ones already featured in this Roundup. If none of our solutions work for you, check out the Apache OpenOffice suite. LibreOffice is based on it, but whereas the latter continues to innovate, OpenOffice has stagnated in recent years. Besides SoftMaker Office 2018, FreeOffice is another freeware suite, but as with WPS and OnlyOffice, it only features a spreadsheet, word processor and presentation tool.

If you're looking for a cloud-based alternative, there are a couple of others besides Google Docs, including the proprietary Zoho Office suite and the host-on-one office suite Tiki. The AGPL3 licensed Feng Office Editor is also a viable option. If you notice of these catch, you can grab individual applications, such as Gnumeric or Pymip for spreadsheets. Abword and Sozi can be similarly used for word processing or creating presentations, respectively.
