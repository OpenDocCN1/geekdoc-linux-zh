# OPEN SOCIAL NETWORKS

Tired of Facebook knowing better than he knows himself, Jonny Bidwell goes in search of a more open and less creepy open social network.

Arguments about whether having a rich, and carefully curated, online persona (which may not be a particularly good reflection of who we are in meatspace) is a healthy thing that we can use, save for another day, can we all agree that if we're going to put our media and ramblings online, then things would be a whole lot better the sites that them should be open source, and have clear policies about how that data is used.

Better yet, we should be able to host our own instances of these services. This isn't

far fetched as it seems. Decentralised and federated architectures enable different instances to connect with one another, so an account on each platform is all that's necessary. Unlike the old days of following many across MySpace, Bebo and Geocities, as at one guy who hosted his own site and wrote lamenting the monetisation of the web, and the cost of using 'free' closed platforms.

Maybe his message would be heard but for our being distracted by all cat photos, 'influencers' and the rise of meme culture on all those proprietary social networks...
