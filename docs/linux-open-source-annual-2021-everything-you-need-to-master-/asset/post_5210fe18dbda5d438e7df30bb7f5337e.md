You can drag and drop all your accessed email folders into the box on the top-left of the application's interface. This creates a list of frequently accessed folders; you can then navigate the left sidebar for these folders.

Unless you're comfortable with announcing to all your peers that the Mailspring has been sent using immediate attention, note the signature is not quite the same as 'Sent from my iPhone'.

Despite opting the choice of layouts such as Wide View, Vertical View and Folder Pane, the best appearance is of the Classic View, which presents a three-panel interface.

All your emails are grouped by day/month/year. Messages are collapsed into the respective day/month/year, and you can double-click to get a list of messages. KMail does the threaded view better than the other tools, using line space to denote features. The search at the top can be used to perform search operations, but if you want more functionality, you can click Tools > Find Messages.

Like Claws and Thunderbird, Mailspring supports editing themes. Click Edit-Options to choose your six options theme.

When confronted with an unfamiliar email in one language, you can right-click the entry and then click "Search for 'word'". You can change the language settings in the message dialog.

Unlike the other tools, Mailspring has a four-pane layout. The left sidebar is the same as all the other options. To your right is the selected message. In the fourth pane you get all details about the email, which performs you the names of the people in the conversation. At the bottom of this right sidebar is a list of all messages exchanged with the same person.

When searching messages, it will show in a new tab, with filters to select the different messages based on sender, recipient or whether messages have an attachment.

For messages that require attention, click the Create-Do button on the menu bar. You can then fill in all the details to create a reminder.

When composing a message, you click Options > Request Receipt if you want delivery confirmation. You can configure this for all outgoing messages by clicking Menu > Preferences > Preferences > Advanced. Then click Return Receipts.

## Customising with add-ons

Building up on the top of install isn't enough ...

Mailspring's default installation is the total of what's on offer. You're provided with a few more features if you opt to subscribe to the Pro version, at a cost of $8 per month ($6), but it still doesn't give as many features as you can get for free tools.

Thunderbird offers the choice of dozens to change the appearance, in addition to specific extensions to tweak the menu entries and other changes to the interface. You also get functionality such as the Automatic Dictionary. This extension allows communicating with a particular person or group and automatically switches the spell-checker. The add-ons page provides a list of available extensions such as Colours, Filters, and News reading, Security and Tags. You can also scroll through the most popular list on the left.

Claws is the straightforward email client, and you must resort to its plug-ins if you want to add an aggregator. One aggregator is the NCPIR plugin, which allows you to read email from Acer and Asus. For example, you can also opt from one of several themes to change the appearance of the interface.

Evolution's default interface has several plug-ins installed. In fact, it has many new email features, such as new mail notification is also provided through a plug-in.

A lot of functionality in KMail is driven when you use plug-ins. Click Settings > Configuration > KMail and click Plug-ins for a list. You might be especially interested in the ad-blocker plugin.

# FOSS
# Protecting against the hazards of the internet.

The applications featured in this Roundup provide different tools and software to safeguard your privacy of communications and the safety of your machines.

In addition to default settings, many other methods can protect you from viruses and ad-plugins.

You may have heard of using BOGoth or MailAssassin to detect and block spam messages with Evolution, KMail, and Claws. The core package includes several useful plug-ins like Blogfather, if you've installed it, or if you use anti-virus software like Bitdefender, you must install the anti-mail-plugins.

KMail's anti-spam and anti-virus widgets help you configure the respect tools. You may already have the tools installed to handle viruses, such as ClAV. If no tools are found, the wizard will merely inform you. We do not recommend any tools. Some users have reported problems with the wizard detecting anti-virus tools. Still, KMail's performance is considerably slower, as each message is scanned for viruses.

## Other features
Will it make your breakfast?

With the web browser, the applications are one of the most recognizable components of the desktop. What we started as a basic way to communicate with your contacts has expanded to vast feature set. Not all users would want their email client to set reminders or use as calendar. But for those who use it daily, it's probably best to use just one, rather than several.

Evolution offers free Personal Information Manager (PIM), calendar, address book and task manager. You can get the same features with Thunderbird in the KDE environment, with other well-known components such as Contacts and KOrganizer.

If you speak a language other than English and receive emails, you'll appreciate Mailspring's ability to translate emails written in English, Russian, simplified Chinese, French, and German. You can also adjust your language preference and various message settings. It's especially tailored for touch devices and configurable gestures as you swipe left or right.

Unlike the other tools, Thunderbird can also connect with social network accounts. It keeps record of all your messages with your contacts. The advantage of its multi-channel chat support is that you can use the search feature to go through past conversations and emails exchanged with your contacts. Would you ever need to perform a search on the web, you can do so from the bar within the application, instead of switching over a web browser, and can even search the search engine you wish. Claws doesn't provide any features out of the box but you can get all the same functionality with the use of it. It can easily be used as a news aggregator, manage appointments, and more. Evolution and Thunderbird are more resource hungry than the others. To consider if you use these applications for more than just mail. The use of anti-spam and anti-virus plug-ins affect performance.
