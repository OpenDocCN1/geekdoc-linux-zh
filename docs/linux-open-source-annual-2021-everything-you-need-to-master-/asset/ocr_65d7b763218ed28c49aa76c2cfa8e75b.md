# NEXT-GEN DISTROS

Step right – LXF's greatest showman Jonni Bidwell is here to unveil the finest Linux distros around!

ast your mind back 15 years, to pre-credit crunch optimism of the mid 2000s. We were wiped by Windows Vista, and this new Ubuntu operating system was claiming that it could displace Windows. Despite being free, you needed to have contributed to yourself or subscribe to an arcane journal such as Linux Format to understand that crappy brown hue was a good desktop color.

Well, Windows may not have been wiped out by Ubuntu. But Linux has been developed in layers and bounds. It's

usable than ever and key players take it much more seriously now. Thanks to Valve and Vulkan, we can now play thousands of games. More companies than ever are shipping Linux on consumer hardware. Your wireless desktop works perfectly with it.

Best-looking array of distros to choose from. Ubuntu has always been a great place to start, but it's not for everyone.s taste. Here, we pick the best and since Making Linux is very easy difficult, we've got a section on usable distros, including long-time favourite for beginners: Linux Mint.

Next, we'll explore some of the best-looking distros out there that will give you a truly modern desktop. Elementary OS is making Linux simple, powerful, and dare leveraging the latest in open source. We say – it's at least a bit like Mac OS. Finally, we look at some new technologies. You might not want these (or if it seems the number of changes about Wayland, Systemd et al.), or you might not be able to use your favourite software with them easily, but these will shape desktop Linux in the years to come.
