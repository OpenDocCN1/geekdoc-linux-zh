# Licensing

# Examining the small print.

It will all our software is free. This choice was made partly because the highly respected Adobe Illustrator and CoreDRAW aren't available on Linux, but also to illustrate the fact that you can compare performance to these least commercial packages without spending hundreds pounds. Though, there are differences between our packages.

First of all, not all open source, which probably not not an issue for more technically minded users but will be an issue for more quality conscious users more than the others. Inkscape and LibreOffice are not open source. In order to use any of the three you will have to create an account with the publisher. In the case of Google Drawings, it's just a free Google account which you might already have.

The situation with Gravity might be a concern to potential users, as it is no longer free like Corel, and while the basic version of Gravity Designer is free to use, it has been joined by Gravity Designer Pro (which you get a 14-day free trial when you sign up) that costs £75/99 per year.
