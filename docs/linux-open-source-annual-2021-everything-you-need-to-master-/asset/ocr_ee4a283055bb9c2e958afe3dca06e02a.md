# Protecting against the
hazards of the internet.

The applications featured in this Roundup provide different tools and software to safeguard your privacy of communications and the safety of your machines.
In addition to default settings, many other methods can protect you from viruses and ad-plugins.
You may have heard of using BOGoth or MailAssassin to detect and block spam messages with Evolution, KMail, and Claws. The core package includes several useful plug-ins like Blogfather, if you've installed it, or if you use anti-virus software like Bitdefender, you must install the anti-mail-plugins.
KMail's anti-spam and anti-virus widgets help you configure the respect tools. You may already have the tools installed to handle viruses, such as ClAV. If no tools are found, the wizard will merely inform you. We do not recommend any tools. Some users have reported problems with the wizard detecting anti-virus tools. Still, KMail's performance is considerably slower, as each message is scanned for viruses.

# Other features
Will it make your breakfast?

With the web browser, the applications are one of the most recognizable components of the desktop.
What we started as a basic way to communicate with your contacts has expanded to vast feature set. Not all users would want their email client to set reminders or use up as a calendar. But for those who use it daily, it's probably best to use just one, rather than several.
Evolution offers free Personal Information Manager (PIM), a calendar, address book and a task manager. You can get the same features with Thunderbird in the KDE environment, with other well-known KDE components such as Contacts and KOrganizer.
If you speak a language other than English and receive emails, you'll appreciate Mailspring's ability to translate emails written in English, Russian, simplified Chinese, French, and German. You can also adjust your language-preference and various message settings. It's especially tailored for touch devices and configurable gestures as you swipe left or right.
Unlike the other tools, Thunderbird can also connect with social network accounts. It keeps record of all your messages with your contacts. The advantage of its multi-channel chat support is that you can use the search feature to go through past conversations and emails exchanged with your contacts.
