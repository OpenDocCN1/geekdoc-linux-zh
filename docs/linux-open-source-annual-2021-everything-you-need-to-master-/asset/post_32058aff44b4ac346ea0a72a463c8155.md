# Arch-based distros

For some users, having a purely GTK or Qt system will be a boon, if it restricts the number of applications that are available them. For those people Anteros, and in particular KaOS, may have some appeal. Others will be happy that cross-toolkit them is better standing than one way and not installing toolkits.

In many ways KaOS should be offering no KDE packages, as it hits the Arch repos soon. Of course, KDE is the premier distro for showing off its features, and great Plasma to maybe think there's no point competing with that. The Blue Systems sponsored Netiling is a futile effort, but it feels like trying to win over. Manjaro does a great job with its flagship XFCE. It shows that this desktop is capable of looking good as well as it is considered on resources. It's become popular over the past years because of its Ubuntu base, but it's a fine distro that gives people exactly what they want. When X 4.14 is released and people become tired of making jokes about that, this will surely become even more as it grows. The Gnome and KDE flavours are great too. If you prefer the latest desktop distros, then you admire Manjaro's approach in focusing on Xfce.
