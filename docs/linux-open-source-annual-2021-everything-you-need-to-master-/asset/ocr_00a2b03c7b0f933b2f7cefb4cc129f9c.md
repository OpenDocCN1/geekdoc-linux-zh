# # Collaboration
What are your options when you need to share the workload?

A major factor for the popularity of office suites is that they enable multiple users to collaborate and work on the same document simultaneously. While office suites are catching up, each has a unique feature that sets it apart from others, such as Google Docs.
 Docs offers real-time, character-by-character collaboration on Google Docs, Google Slides and Google Drawings. When multiple people are working on the same document at the same time, they can see changes made by others. These collaboration options work in conjunction with the sharing options that makes it possible to set access levels for files, so you can control who can edit and see your pages. You can invite others to collaborate with a group of people with a single user.
LibreOffice allows simultaneous access for many users. Each user adds their name to the LibreOffice Data page making it easy to track the contributions made by the different users.

# # Presentation
A good slide-show takes more than just a collection of bullet points.

As is with word processor applications, Calibre's presentation Stage also takes some getting defining the different formats. Some animations and transitions are relegated to a separate docker, on the right of the screen. Adding shapes is simply confined to the Added shapes.
Google Slides offers the choice of how to add pictures, shapes, charts and text to slides, and configure transitions and effects for each. It is also possible to choose different colours and themes to create dynamic slides. Also on offer are here a large number of add-ons that can enhance your presentation. For instance, you can insert free HD photos to your slides using the LibreOffice add-on. The add-ons are split into different categories such as Education, Business Tools and Productivity.
LibreOffice allows you to choose from various background designs. You can insert shapes, charts, text and pictures. The slides has a built-in photo editor. It can be used to manipulate the background image you imported into a slide.
