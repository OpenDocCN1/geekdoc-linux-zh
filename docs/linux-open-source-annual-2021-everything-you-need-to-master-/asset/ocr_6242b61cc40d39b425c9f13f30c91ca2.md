# Rescue distros

Linux is pretty resilient, but when things get out of hand, Shashank Sharma uses one of these specialised distros to rescue an installation.

HOW WE TESTED...

Our parameters for testing haven't really changed from the previous Roundup in LXF209 we're still looking for the distros with the wildest collection of tools that I'll help us with all kinds of repair and rescue operations.

However, that's not the only parameter we're interested in and it is not even the most important one. Instead of the number of tools, the effectiveness of the complete distro gets more weighing in our books. Distros that help you wade through the multitude of tools and home in the right place for your job are rated higher than distros that only claim to be the largest number of rescues.

Since some utilities interact directly with distros and the critical data they hold, they can do more than just repair. For a specialised task such as this, documentation plays a very crucial role.
