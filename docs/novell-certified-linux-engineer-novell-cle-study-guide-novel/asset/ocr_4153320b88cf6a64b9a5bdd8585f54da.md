**NOTE**

SMTP stands for *Simple Mail Transfer Protocol*. The SMTP protocol supports both sending and receiving email messages; however, its receiving capabilities are very limited. Therefore, it is usually deployed in conjunction with IMAP and/or POP3. SMTP is used to send mail, whereas IMAP or POP3 is used to receive mail. SMTP runs on IP port 25.
