# ## Lab Exercise 11.1:
## Configuring Virtual Office

In this lab, you're going to configure Virtual Office on your server. Then you configure a virtual team and invite users to join it.

> **WARNING**
> 
> Because Virtual Office is dependent on all the other NNLS components, you must complete all exercises in this book up to this point before attempting this lab exercise. If you haven't completed the preceding exercises, go back and complete them before starting this exercise.

Complete the following steps:

+   1.  Configure the Virtual Office SMTP server parameters by following these steps:
+       a.  Open a browser window and navigate to `https://your_server_IP_address/nps/iManager.html`.
+       b.  Authenticate to iManager as **admin** with a password of **novell**.
+       c.  In the left frame, scroll down to the **Virtual Office** role.
+       d.  Select the **Environment Administration** task.
+       e.  Verify that **Enable team creation** is set to **On**.
+       f.  On the **SMTP address** line, click **Edit**.
+       g.  In the **SMTP address** field, enter your server's IP address. This will configure Virtual Office to send notification messages through your NetMail server.
+       h.  In the **Username** field, enter **admin**. This will configure notifications to be sent to your admin user's email account.
+       i.  In the **Password** fields, enter **novell**.
+       j.  Click **OK**.
+       k.  Click **Save**.
+       l.  Click **OK**.

+   2.  Configure your company info field by completing the following steps:
+       a.  In iManager, select the **Services Administration** task.
+       b.  Verify that **Home Page Company Info** is enabled.
