+   f. In the **Driver Store** field, browse to and select the **CLEDriverStore.IFCLE** object you created previously. 
+   g. Ensure that **Start print manager after creation** is marked; then click **OK**. 
+   h. When prompted that the iPrint Manager was created, click **OK**. 
+   6. Create a **Printer Agent** for your printer by completing the following steps: 
+   a. Under the **iPrint role**, select the **Create Printer** task. 
+   b. In the **Printer name** field, enter **CLE-PA**. 
+   c. In the **Container name** field, enter **IFCLE**. 
+   d. In the **Manager name** field, enter **CLEManager.IFCLE**. 
+   e. In the **DNS Name** or **IP Address** field, enter the IP address of your printer. 
+   f. In the **Location** field, enter a description of the physical location of the printer. 
+   g. In the **Description** field, enter a description of the printer. 
+   h. Click **Next**. 
+   i. In the drop-down list for the version of Windows running on your workstation, select your printer driver from the **Driver Store** object. 
+   j. Click **Next**. 
+   k. When prompted that the Printer Agent has been created, click **OK**. 
+   7. Configure your iPrint service to support user workstations by completing the following steps: 
+   a. On your server, open a terminal session. 
+   b. Change to the root user account by entering `su –` and supplying a password of **novell**. 
+   c. At the shell prompt, enter `vi /var/opt/novell/http/htdocs/ippdocs/iprint.ini`. 
+   d. Scroll down to the **AllowUserPrinters** line. 
+   e. Press **Ins**. 
+   f. Change the value of the **AllowUserPrinters** parameter to **1**. 
+   g. Press **Esc**. 
+   h. Enter `:exit`.
