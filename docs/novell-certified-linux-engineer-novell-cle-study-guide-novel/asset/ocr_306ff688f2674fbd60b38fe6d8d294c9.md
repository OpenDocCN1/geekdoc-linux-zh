NNLS includes three web-based administration utilities:

+   - **iManager 2.0.2** — iManager is the primary administration tool for Novell eDirectory. It's used to manage users, manage eDirectory services such as DirXML, and back up the tree. iManager uses role-based administration. This allows you to create a management role, assign tasks to it, and then associate it with specific users. This automatically grants them the rights they need in the tree to complete the tasks you've assigned to them.

+   - **iMonitor** — iMonitor is an advanced eDirectory troubleshooting and maintenance tool. You can use it to check the overall health of your eDirectory database and repair any problems you may discover.

+   - **NetMail WebAdmin 4.0** — WebAdmin is a web-based utility used to implement and manage the NetMail 3.5 service in NNLS.
