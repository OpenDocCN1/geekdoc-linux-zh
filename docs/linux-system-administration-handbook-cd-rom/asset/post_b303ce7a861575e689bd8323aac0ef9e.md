
## 4 Linux Allocated Devices 369

255 = /dev/ttysf 64th pseudo-tty slave

Pseudo-tty's are named as follows:
* Masters are "pty", slaves are "tty";
* the fourth line is one of p, q, r, s indicating the 1st, 2nd, 3rd, 4th series of 16 pseudo-ttys, and
* the fifth line is one of 0123456789abcdef indicating the position within the series.
