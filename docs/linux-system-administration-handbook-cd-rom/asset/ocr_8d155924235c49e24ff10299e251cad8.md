36 = /dev/fd?CompaQ Compaq 2880 drive; obsolete?

NOTE: The letter in the device name (d, q or u) signifies the type of drive: 5.25" Double Density (d), 5.25" Quantum Density (q), 5.25" High Density (h) or 3.5" (any model, u). The use of the capital letters D, H and E for the 3.5" models have been deprecated, since the drive type is insignificant for these devices.
