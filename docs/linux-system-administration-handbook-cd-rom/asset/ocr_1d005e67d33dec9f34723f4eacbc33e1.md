# 16.8 Amusements and Games

xpat

For those of you who want just a simple game, xpat may be for you. It’s a collection of solitaire games (including the classic “solitaire” and “freeceelle” games that are popular on other operating systems).

# Quake/Quake II

It’s not hard to ignore these popular games (and their predecessor—Doom and Doom II). Quake and Quake II are available for Linux and provide all three display types for playing: X11, SVGAlib, and 3Dfx. The X11 interface brings Quake up in a window and allows you to play with other applications at the same time.
