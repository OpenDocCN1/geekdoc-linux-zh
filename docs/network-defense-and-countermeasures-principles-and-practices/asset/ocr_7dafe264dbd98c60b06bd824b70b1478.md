Frankly speaking, if you are going to do vulnerability scans, you will be well-served to at least consider Nessus. They have a seven-day trial version you can use to see if it suits your needs.

# NetStat Live

One of the most popular protocol monitors is NetStat, which ships free with Microsoft Windows. A version of this, NetStat Live (NSL) is freely available on the Internet from a variety of sites, such as www.analogx.com/download/network/nsl.htm. This product is an easy-to-use TCP/IP protocol monitor that can be used to see the exact throughput on both incoming and outgoing data whether you are using a modern, cable modem, DSL, or a local network. It allows you to see the speed at which your data goes from your computer to another computer on the Internet. It tells you how many other computers your data must go to get to its destination. NSL also graphs the CPU usage of a system. This can be especially useful, for example, if you are experiencing slowed connection speeds. It can identify whether your computer or your Internet connection is the reason for the slowdown.
