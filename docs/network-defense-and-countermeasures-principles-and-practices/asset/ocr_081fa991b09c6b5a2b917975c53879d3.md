# Anti-Spyware Policies

With all aspects of computer security, appropriate policies must be in order to protect your system against spyware and adware. Many of these policies are the same as the policies that protectct your system from Trojan horses and virus infections.

+   - Never download any attachment unless you are completely certain it is safe. This means that unless you specifically requested an attachment, or at least expected one, and that attachment matches what you expected (i.e., named appropriately, right format, etc.) do not download it.
- Make sure your browser is configured to block cookies, or at most to allow cookies for only a very limited time. Cookies store information from a particular website, but any website you visit can read any cookie on your machine.
- Your browser should be configured to block scripts that run without the user's awareness.
- Some browsers (e.g., Chrome and Mozilla) also offer pop-up blocking. Pop-up ads are often a vehicle for adware.
- Never download any application, browser skin, screen saver, or utility from the Internet unless you are completely certain of its safety.
- Block Java applets, or at least require that the user manually approve them before loading. This will stop RedSheriff and many other spyware utilities.
