Another interesting feature of Spy Sweeper is the various shields it provides, shown in Figure 10-7. These shields can prevent changes to your Internet Explorer home page, favorites, Windows startup, programs in memory, and more. Most spyware and adware programs will attempt to alter one or more of these items. These shields require your direct approval before any change can be made.
