> In Practice
When considering any tool that might be considered a "hacking tool" you should first check to see if it violates your company's policy to have such a tool. Secondly, it is probably not a wise idea to travel internationally with tools in your possession. Laws vary from country to country and cannot guarantee what the reaction will be if a particular country's authorities discover such tools on your laptop, portable media, or otherwise in your possession.
