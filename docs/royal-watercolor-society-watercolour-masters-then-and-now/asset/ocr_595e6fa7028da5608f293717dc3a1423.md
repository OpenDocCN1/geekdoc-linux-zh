I build up the composition using distinct areas of colour, working to establish a sense of atmosphere and balance. Contrasting busy and calm components I make adjustments, guided by a concern for pictorial effect rather than realistic transcription. In *Round Table by the Window*, rounded and rectilinear elements are repeated throughout the composition. The shapes of the table and window are echoed in the fruit and chequered mat; the details in the artwork on the wall and the domed architecture beyond.
