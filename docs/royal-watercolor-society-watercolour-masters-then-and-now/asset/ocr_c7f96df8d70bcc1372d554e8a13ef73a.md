In this beautifully illustrated book, you can learn how today's masters of watercolour respond to the work of their predecessors. Experience, with them, the vast variety of inspiration, both technical and artistic, that can be drawn from looking at the work of past masters and enjoy some of the most accomplished paintings in this medium.
