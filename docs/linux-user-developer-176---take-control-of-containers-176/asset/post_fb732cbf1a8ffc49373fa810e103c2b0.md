![](img/6866071e033820549b214c239744aaa0_5_0.png)

#### Docker

Docker containers wrap a piece of software in a complete filesystem that contains everything needed to run: code, runtime, system tools, system libraries – anything that can be installed on a server. Find out more about how you can use Docker and take advantage of its complete containerisation package in our complete guide on p18.

![](img/6866071e033820549b214c239744aaa0_5_1.png)

#### rkt by CoreOS

rkt is the next-generation container manager for Linux clusters. Designed for security, simplicity, and compatibility within modern cluster architectures, rkt discovers, verifies, fetches, and executes application containers with pluggable isolation. This means that you can containerise individual apps and everything that's needed to run them.

![](img/6866071e033820549b214c239744aaa0_5_2.png)

#### Docker Toolbox

Containers aren't limited to the Linux side of your system! The Docker Toolbox is an installer to quickly and easily install and set up a Docker environment on your computer. This is the Windows version. Using the Docker Toolbox, you'll be able to set up specialist containerisation distros and environments to run on a Windows machine.

### KDE unveils lightweight Linux laptop

**Move over Macbook, there’s a new king in town**

For many looking to get their Linux fix, choices primarily revolve around which desktop PC you’re going to buy. Laptops pre-loaded with Linux at their core are few and far between, and unless you managed to get your hands on Dell’s recent model, many contain a myriad of shortcomings that have often caused users a lot of frustration and annoyance, rather than enjoyment. However, the latest pre-loaded Linux laptop to make its debut could just be the game-changer we’ve been waiting for.

Ever-popular KDE, famed for its fantastic graphical desktop environment, is launching its very own laptop. To do this, it’s partnering with the Spain-based Slimbook for the official launch of the KDE Slimbook. In the past, KDE has been the desktop environment of choice for a vast majority of UNIX workstations, providing one of the most accessible ways to get started with everything that Linux offers. Slimbook, on the other hand, won’t be overly known to those outside of Spain, but it has previously built and sold entry-level laptops with varying levels of success.

> “The latest pre-loaded Linux laptop could just be the game-changer we’ve been waiting for.”

The word budget won’t apply to the KDE Slimbook, however, as it’s being marketed as a mid to high-level desktop PC replacement. Early models allow for two variants to be sold, with a choice of a 2.3GHz Intel Core i5 or 2.5GHz i7 model available for users. Other noticeable specifications include 4GB of RAM, a 13.3-inch 1080p display, 120GB SSD and dual USB 3.0 ports. On paper, there are pleasing specifications, and at just 0.71 inches thick, it’s going to rival the same shape and design of leading laptops currently on the market.

While Slimbook is taking care of the hardware side of things, KDE is in its element on the software side. The new KDE Slimbook ships with the popular KDE Neon distribution. While it’ll be tailored for new users, thanks to its Ubuntu base, there’ll be plenty of scope for advanced users to customise the system to their exact needs.

Perhaps the best thing about using KDE Neon on the Slimbook is having access to the KDE Plasma desktop environment, which has proven to be a big success over the years. It will contain a wide range of KDE-based apps, and it won’t skimp on the usual personalisation tools associated with the desktop environment. Although details on the matter are still scarce, it’s believed that KDE will provide all the necessary tools to switch environments if KDE Neon simply doesn’t suit your needs.

What won’t be as pleasing to the ears of potential users will be the pricetag. Prices for the KDE Slimbook will be starting at around £700/$770 for the i5 model, with it then increasing closer to the £820/$910 mark for the faster i7 version. Pre-orders are currently being taken over at kde.slimbook.es, with the first units expected to ship towards the end of March, but if pre-orders take off, expect some delays.
