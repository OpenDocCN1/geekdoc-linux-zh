# Maintaining Templates

Templates are an incredibly powerful tool—one critical to the VM life cycle. It is, however, important to keep templates up to date. It's a level-of-effort deal; it's easier to maintain one template than it is to patch or update many VMs postdeployment. Some companies choose to update their templates quarterly and rely on their management tools to fill that gap. We find this foolish: regardless of how good an enterprise tool is, why cycles on a fresh guest deployment when it is easier to just maintain the source template? Whatever the decisions, just remember: PowerCLI is here to assist. With that in mind, there are a few common issues, but one universal pain point with templates.

No matter what you're doing, the first step is always to convert a template into a VM and the last is to convert back to a template. You might or not need to power on the VM in the middle of the process. You will find that you constantly account for the state of the VM during any update. To assist in this trivial task, you can use Wait-VMQuest function (see Listing 7.15).
