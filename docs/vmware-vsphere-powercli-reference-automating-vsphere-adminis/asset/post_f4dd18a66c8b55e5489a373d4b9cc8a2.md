
# ABOUT THE AUTHORS

Matt Born likes quick, efficient things. Automation? Great. Elegant automation with PowerShell? Even better, he says. Matt began his automation career with Perl and the like in the late 1990s. After many languages across several years, he came to enjoy PowerShell with v1.0 and the VMware VI Toolkit. A couple of years later, in 2009, Matt and his friend AC started the http://vnuggets.blog, which is now a hearty sort of mainly virtualization-focused PowerShell nuggets, where the posts also focus on keeping things as fast as can be. After a few more years, Matt earned the VMware vExpert designation thanks to these blogging efforts and to the VMware Technology Network (VMTN) forum participating. He has vExpert status for four years so far, each year from 2012 to present (2015). Matt continues to find joy in making things faster, stronger, and better. Follow Matt on Twitter (@mtbornen).

Luc Dekens spends many moons in the mainframe world as a system programmer. While the companies he worked for worked Unix and Windows boxes on, it was a natural evolution for him to expand into those areas. A couple of years ago, Luc was impressed by a new scripting tool, Monad, that Microsoft was bringing to market. When the organization Luc works for was expanding their virtualization platform, he stumbled upon a product called VI Toolkit. It was ideal for automating many administrative tasks. Luc was admitted to the early beta program and started contributing to the ever-growing PowerShell community. After attending VMworld 2009 in San Francisco, he had a session with Hal Rotten and started a blog (http://lucd.info).

Brian Graf has in many different roles in IT for more than 10 years. He has always had a passion for technology and learning. For the past four years, Brian has focused mainly on virtualization and automation. Brian is a multi-vExpert and is currently VCAP5-DCA and DCD certified. Brian graduated with a Masters of Information Systems from the University of Utah. He enjoys taking trips and spending time with his wife and kids. You can follow Brian on Twitter at https://twitter.com/vBrianGraf or on his blog at http://www.vtagion.com.

# ABOUT THE AUTHORS

Jonathan Medd is a Senior Consultant with Xtravirt in the UK. He shares PowerShell and other automation content via his blog, http://jonathanmedd.net, and also co-hosts the Get-Scripting PowerShell podcast, which provides information on how to learn PowerShell and what's going in the PowerShell world—you can find it at http://get-scripting.blogspot.com. In April 2010, Jonathan was awarded as a Microsoft Most Valuable Professional (MVP) and in 2011 the status of VMwareExpert. He has been re-awarded with each of those community awards in every year. You can follow him on Twitter at http://twitter.com/jonathanmedd.

Glenn Sizemer has just built about every position one could hold in Enterprise IT—from cable dog to enterprise architect. Throughout it has been a passion. He started scripting early in his IT career and mastered VBScript by the time PowerShell first shipped. He was an early adopter and supporter of PowerShell, and the desire to automate all the things propelled him higher into the solution stack. Today Glenn is a FlexPod Reference Architect at NetApp, where he builds cloud integrated turnkey architectures for use by customers of all sizes. Outside of work, Glenn is the proud father of two beautiful children, an avid automation evangelist, and a hater of negativity.

Andrew Sullivan has worked in the technology industry for nearly 15 years, with a rich history of database development and administration, DevOps experience, virtualization and storage architecture, and automation evangelism. Andrew started as a Linux administrator fluent in Perl and Python many years ago, but has since learned the error of his ways and now favors PowerShell whenever possible. He blogs frequently at http://practical-admin.com, pontifying about PowerShell for virtualization and storage automation, vReal for storage systems, and occasionally some actual wisdom. Andrew is the co-host of the NetApp Tech ONTAP podcast, focusing on the NetApp storage ecosystem, and is a regular presenter at VMUGs, Docker Meetups, and other community events.
