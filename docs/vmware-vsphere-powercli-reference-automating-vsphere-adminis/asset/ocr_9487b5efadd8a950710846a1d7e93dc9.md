# vApp Templates

vApp templates are the vApps that are found in your catalogs. In large vCloud
Director environments, there may be dozens of catalogs with hundreds of tem-
plates. You can use the Get-CIVAppTemplate cmdlet to retrieve a report of all the tem-
plates that exist at several different levels within your environment. You can retrieve
all vApp templates that are visible to your account by entering the cmdlet itself:

```
Get-CIVAppTemplate
```
