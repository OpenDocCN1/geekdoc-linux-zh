# Before You Begin

Linux can be a large and intimidating topic if studied in a haphazard way. So, as you begin your study of Linux, keep in mind that each chapter in this book builds on the preceding one. To ensure that you gain a solid understanding of core Linux concepts, read the chapters in consecutive order. You should also participate in a local Linux Users Group (LUG) and explore the Internet for websites, FAQs, HOWTOs, and newsgroups that will expand your knowledge of Linux.
