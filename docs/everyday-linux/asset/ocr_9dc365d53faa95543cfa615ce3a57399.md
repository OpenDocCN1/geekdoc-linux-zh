# Star Office Auto Pilots

You can get a good start to using Star Office by using the auto pilots. These will guide you through creating a document, webpage or other project. To access the auto pilots, click "file" and then "auto pilot." Another menu will allow you to choose document, webpage, spreadsheet, etc. As an example, we will choose to create an agenda from the auto pilot.

![](bbox=[0.04, 0.29, 0.93, 0.52])

**Figure 11-7**
Using Auto Pilot to Create an Agenda

The auto pilot begins by asking you some basic layout questions about your agenda. When you have answered them, click "next."

Auto pilot will continue asking you questions about your agenda. Keep clicking "next" until you have all the information you want in your agenda. When you are through, click "create." Your agenda will be created and opened in Star Office. You can then fill in additional information and finally save your document.

# Working with Star Office Templates

Templates are preformatted documents. Star Office provides you with a large selection of templates to work with. Templates are also used by Star's auto pilots to help you create documents and other projects. To view the various templates there are to choose from, click "new" and then "from template."
