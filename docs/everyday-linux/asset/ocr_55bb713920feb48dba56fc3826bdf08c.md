When you choose an image to be inserted, it will appear on your document with small blocks around the edges.

You can place your mouse over the blocks and drag them to resize the image. When you have positioned your image as you want it, just click outside the image area to permanently place it. If you change your mind later, just click on the image again, the blocks will reappear and you can reposition or resize the image.

# ## Selecting a Printer and Printing a Document

Before you print any documents you must first select a printer for WordPerfect 8 to use. Click "file" and then "print".

When the print window pops up, choose "select" near the top right of the window. The "select printer" dialog box will pop up.

The dialog box will list whatever printers are connected to your system (our screenshot doesn't list printers because there were none connected). Choose a printer from this box and then click ok.
