You can use templates to help create many types of reports and presentations.
Click “file,” “new,” and “from template.”

![](bbox=[0.04, 0.25, 0.96, 0.61])

**Figure 14-6**
*Starting a New Presentation.*

On the left side of the box choose “presentations.” Be sure not to get this confused with “preipitation layouts.” From the right-side box, choose “technical report.” You can click the “more” button to see a preview and get a basic description. When you are done, press “ok.”

The first page of your report will come up on the screen and you can add the title, choosing font, size, and color. You will notice across the bottom of the page there are additional pages to the report.

They are entitled things like “definition of subject,” “status,” and other pages to the report. All you need to do is click on each section and then fill in the information it suggests that you use there. You can add charts and images to your report as needed by clicking “insert” and then choosing “object,” “chart,” or whatever you want to insert.
