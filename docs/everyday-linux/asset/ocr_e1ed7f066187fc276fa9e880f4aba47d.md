In the last three years, the open-source community has made enormous strides in simplifying installation and normal housekeeping and presenting it through graphical user interface—to the point where it's really quite a bit easier over time to maintain a Linux box than a Windows machine, whether you're an expert techie or not.

The real problem is that most of the ordinary people who could benefit from Linux don't know how easy it is. And that's the problem Ethan Metsger and Kathy Miles have set out to solve. With gentle introductions and step-by-step explanations, they demystify Linux and build the reader's confidence.

Help stamp out bad software in your lifetime! You can use this book to escape the Microsoft monopoly's predatory clutch, and turn your computer more to being an enjoyable tool and less of an irritation. Enjoy! And spread the word to your friends.
