# StarWriter

You can create a document from scratch or you can create one using Star Office auto pilots which will guide you through creating a document. If you want to use the auto pilot simply click “file” the “auto pilot” and then choose letter, report, or whatever you want to create. For more information about auto pilot, read the Star Office Introduction.

# Creating, Opening, Formatting  
and Saving a New Document

StarWriter is the word processing part of Star Office Suite. To begin a new doc-ument, click “file,” “new” and “text.” A blank page will appear on your screen.

You may also open an existing StarWriter document by clicking “file” and “open.”

You can move around your directories and choose the file you want to open.
