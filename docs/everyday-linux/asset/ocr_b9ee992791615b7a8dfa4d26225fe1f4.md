# Making a Chart from Your Spreadsheet Data

Charts often make it easier to see what's going on with figures, and StarCalc makes it possible for you to graph your data in a chart that can be placed right on your spreadsheet.

First, select the columns and rows you would like to make a chart from.

![](bbox=[0.06, 0.25, 0.9, 0.69])

**Figure 13-4** *Selecting Rows or Columns.*

Once they are selected, click on the insert object button on the vertical toolbar. This is the toolbar which runs up and down to the left of your spreadsheet. When you do this, the cursor turns into a plus sign with a tiny chart attached to it. Choose a blank area on your spreadsheet, and then click and drag the cursor over the area where you want your chart to be. This will determine the initial size of your chart. When you release the mouse button, the auto format chart window will pop up.
