If you have previously saved the document as a WordPerfect document, you will have a copy of the original formatting so you needn’t worry. Click “yes” to continue converting your document.

You won’t notice much difference in your document just by looking at it in WordPerfect.

Compare figure 15-23 to 15-18. You will notice that once the document has been converted, it will be opened in the Web Editor. The Web Editor looks a lot like a WordPerfect 8 document window, but the toolbars are different. You can find what the function of the various tools are by holding the mouse over the icons.

When you are ready to save your document for the web, click “file” and “Internet Publisher” to open the dialogue box. Then choose “publish to HTML.” You will be prompted for a name to save your document as. After you have typed in a name, click “ok.”
