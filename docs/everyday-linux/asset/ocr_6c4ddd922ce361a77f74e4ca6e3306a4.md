To choose the size and type of brush you want you must right click over the image then choose “dialogues” and then “brushes.”

Brush Selection.

There are calligraphic brushes, fuzzy and sharp edged circles of various sizes, as well as an assortment of other patterns you may “paint” with. Once you choose the brush you want, you may set parameters for mode, opacity and spacing. If in doubt of these options, just try the defaults.

Eraser—works opposite pencil and brush, removing areas. Choose size of area to be removed by using the brush dialogue window. You may also choose whether you want a hard or incremental edge to the eraser by double clicking on the eraser icon.
