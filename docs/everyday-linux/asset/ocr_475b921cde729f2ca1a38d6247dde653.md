# Creating a New Image

To create a new image click “file” and then “new.” You will be prompted to specify image size and what color you would like the background to be. You may choose foreground or background colors, white or transparent. You may also choose whether you want the new image to be RGB (full color) or grayscale. The default is RGB.

# Opening an Existing Image

To open an existing image, click “file” and then “open.” The Load Image window will appear.

Move around your directories in the left window and choose the specific file on the right. At this time GIMP does not have “preview image” options.
