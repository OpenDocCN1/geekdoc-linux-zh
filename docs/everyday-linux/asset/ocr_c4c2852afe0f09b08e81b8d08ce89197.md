Bookmarks represent either links to websites on the Internet or locations within your file directories. You can even have a bookmark link to a place within a document.

You can set up the mail so that Star Office will retrieve your e-mail. We will cover this in the section for StarMail.

Explorer will allow you to view your directories and files on your computer.

# Beamer

The Beamer is a small window which will open and show you the contents of directories or graphics files listed in the Explorer window. Try double-clicking on "gallery" in Explorer. Now double click on "clipart." The contents of the directory will be displayed to the lower right, and thumbnails of the images will be displayed in the Beamer.
