The controls for media player are quite intuitive and there are pop-up labels when you hold the mouse over a button. You will notice a few buttons (such as “eject media”) are grayed out. This is likely preparation for adding additional features such as playing CDs.

As with most programs which run on Linux, you may open as many windows of media player as you like.

# # Playing Midi Files

# ## Kmid

Mids are popular tunes and often played on websites. KMid can be found under “KDE menus” by selecting “multimedia” and then “Midi/Karaoke player.”

The controls for KMid are intuitive, and there is also a menu bar across the top of the screen. KMid allows you to set up collections of your favorite tunes. You could, for example, put a group of holiday mids into a holiday collection, while keeping your favorite everyday mids in another collection. This saves a lot of opening of directories!

KMid comes complete with instructions for playing songs and creating collections. It also has a “tips and tricks” section that is useful and informative.
