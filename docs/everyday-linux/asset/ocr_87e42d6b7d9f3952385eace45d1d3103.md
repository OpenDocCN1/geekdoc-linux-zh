# # Formatting Cells, Rows and Columns

You may format an individual cell, or partial or entire rows or columns of cells. Format can include type numbers (percent, currency, scientific, etc.) font, borders, and more.

To format a single cell, just click on the cell and then, from the menu at the top, click on “format,” then “cell.” When you are through formatting, press “ok.” This will affect only the cell which you had clicked on. If you add data to the cell and then hit enter, the next cell will revert back to whatever the previous formatting was.

To format multiple cells, simply click on the first cell and then, while holding down the mouse button, drag over the cells you want to format. Then click “format,” and “cells.” You can also select the entire row or column for formatting by clicking on either the row number or column letter.
