Formatting a Column.

There are two tabs on this window: "numbers" and "align." Under align, you can set alignment for left, right, center or justify. Changing alignment will only affect the column you have clicked on.

You can change column width by right-clicking the button over a column and choosing "column width."

Again, the default is automatic, which enlarges the width as the need arises. If you uncheck automatic, you will have to adjust the width if it is not wide enough.

You have the option to hide a column. You might choose to do this if you are making the database part of a presentation, and do not want all your data to be visible. To hide a column, right click the button at the top of the column and choose "hide column." When you want to show a hidden column, simply right click the button at the top and choose "show" and then either the specific column you want to show, or choose "all." This will show all hidden columns.
