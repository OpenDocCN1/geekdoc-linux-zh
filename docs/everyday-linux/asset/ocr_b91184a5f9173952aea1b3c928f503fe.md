# Turns at Play

On each "day" of the game, you can move your players and take certain actions. Each player takes a turn. If it is human versus computer, the computer will take a turn after the human. As you gain control of areas, they are added to your resources. The resources represent gold and raw materials which you may spend to improve areas under your control. The current date appears at the lower right corner of the Map screen.

# Learning the Game

As we mentioned earlier, it takes time to learn a game. Using the tutorial helps and so does reading the manual. The more you play, the better and more familiar you become with the game.
