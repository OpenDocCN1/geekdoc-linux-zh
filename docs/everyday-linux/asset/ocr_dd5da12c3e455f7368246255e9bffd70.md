# Totaling Columns of Numbers

To total the cells in a column, simply click on the bottom cell of the column and then click the sum button. The sum button is the button which has \u03a3 as a symbol. Once you do that, two new buttons will appear in its place. One is a green check mark and the other a red “x.” If you want to have the sum of that column, click on the check mark, otherwise click on the red x to exit back to data entry.

To total several columns, just click on the bottom cell of the first column and then drag it across the other columns. Then just click the sum button. You can also total rows in the same manner as you do columns.
