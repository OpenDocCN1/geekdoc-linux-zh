At first glance the GIMP main menu might not look very impressive, but don’t be misled. Additional controls show up via left mouse-click over the image. Again, a three button mouse is preferable with GIMP.

The main menu shows tool choices and foreground and background colors, as well as menu choices at the top (“file” and “Xtras”). The default fore- ground/background colors are black and white. If you hold your mouse over one of the tools, text will appear to tell you what that tool is.
