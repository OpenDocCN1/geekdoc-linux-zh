# GNOME Calendar

GNOME provides an excellent way to keep track of daily, weekly and monthly events. Choose “calendar” from the GNOME applications menu.

![](bbox=[0.14, 0.22, 0.95, 0.73])

The calendar comes up with a day view. You can see the hour blocks on the left. You can add specific events during the day by clicking on the hour block and then typing in the event. To get an hourly calendar for a different day, simply double click on whatever day you want from the calendar on the right.

You can add “to do” lists for any day by selecting that day and then clicking on the “add” button under the “to do” window at the bottom right. These will show up when you look at the weekly or monthly calendars.
