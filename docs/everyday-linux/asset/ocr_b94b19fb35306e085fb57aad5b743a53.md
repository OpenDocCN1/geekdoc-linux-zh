# # Note from the Author

Linux has, without a doubt, altered the course of my life. I hadn't thought about writing a Linux book until I found that most Linux books (at that time) were written for geeks, by geeks. Compiling a kernel might actually give some people a warm feeling, but all I wanted to do was the things I normally did with my desktop computer: write documents, edit images, play a few games and so forth. I didn't really want to have to care about things like code, kernels, and core dumps (which turned out to be rather boring). Converting me over to Linux was the task of Chuck Peters, and was no easy thing to do. I likened it on the same scale as raising the Titanic intact. Many were the times that Chuck's face turned ashen and he developed a habit of rubbing his temples as if he might just be suffering the mother of all migraines. Once I learned Linux, I came to love it. Once I came to love it, I wanted to write a book and make other folks conversion easier, but without Chuck's unending patience, this book simply would not exist.
