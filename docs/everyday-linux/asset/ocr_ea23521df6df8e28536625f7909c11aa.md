Just click the GNOME foot and then go to "games" and "Heroes 3" Remember, you will need the Heroes III CD in the CD-ROM before you can play!

When the opening screen of Heroes comes up, you will have the option of starting a new game, loading a game, seeing the high scores or quitting.

Click on single player. We suggest if you are new to the game that you then choose tutorial to familiarize yourself with it.

When you choose tutorial from the Heroes menu, one of the first things it will ask you to do is print out the tutorial document which can be found in the directory where you installed the game.
