StarCalc Spreadsheet

When you are finished with your spreadsheet and want to save it, click “file” and then “save.” The first time you save a spreadsheet, StarCalc will use the “save as” window. By default, StarCalc saves spreadsheets in its native format (.sdc). If you want to export your spreadsheet in another format so that you might view it in another spreadsheet program, use “save as” and choose from the document types window.

StarCalc will both save and open Excel spreadsheets.
