+   - **Local Directory** The local directory is the directory that you want to enter every time you connect to this FTP site. If you connect to a specific FTP site to upload documents or something similar, you might want to be in your documents directory, for instance.
+   - **Username** If you have your own FTP account on this site, you should put in your username. If not, leave this blank.
+   - **Password** If you have an account, you must access it with a username and a password. Make sure that you put in the password for your user if you have an account.
+   - **Account** You can leave this blank—it's not important to logging in. Fill this area in if you are directed to do so by the administrator of the FTP site you want to connect to.

If you don’t have an account on the FTP site you want to connect to, enable the “Log in as ANONYMOUS” option. This will send the site a username of anonymous and your e-mail address as your password. If the site doesn’t support anonymous FTP access, you won’t be able to log in unless you have an account.

# ## Logging

At the bottom of your GFTP main screen, you’ll see a small notice from the author of the GFTP program, Brian Masney. This is the *log file*. You can either read it in that small window (which serves to show you what commands are being executed on the local and remote computer and to report errors) or from the logging submenu.

There are three different options under the logging submenu. You can either view the log, save it to a file, or clear the log. When you clear it, the contents are deleted and you cannot retrieve them.

# ### Transfers

To send and receive files, you can use the transfers menu. Highlight the file you wish to send or receive. Then click on transfers. If you wish to retrieve a file, click on retrieve; if you wish to send a file, click on put.

When you begin a transfer, the transfer progress will be noted at the bottom of the screen in a dialog box. If you wish to stop a transfer, click on the transfer progress line to highlight it. Then click on transfers and click on stop.
