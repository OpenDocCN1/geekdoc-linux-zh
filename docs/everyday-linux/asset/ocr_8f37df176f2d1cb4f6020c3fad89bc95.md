You can find out what your incoming mail server is by talking to a customer service representative at your Internet Service Provider.

**Outgoing Mail Server** — On smaller systems, the outgoing mail server may have the same name as the incoming mail server. This simply means that the same computer handles both outgoing and incoming mail. The outgoing mail server is sometimes called the “SMTP Server,” and you may see it referred to as such in your version of Netscape or on the World Wide Web.

The outgoing mail server handles all of the routing necessary for your e-mail to make it from your post office (the outgoing mail server) to another (another incoming mail server). It ensures that the e-mail you send makes it to the address that you specify.
