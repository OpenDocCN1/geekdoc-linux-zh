# Modifying Global Panel Properties

You should have noticed when you right-clicked your panel that there was an option to modify global panel properties. These are properties that apply to all panels, regardless of their position, type, or make up.

Most of these are self-explanatory and likely tangential to your understanding of how to use GNOME. If you want to further customize your panels, however, you should use the options here. You will be able to modify how your icons look when depressed, how much space a hidden panel will consume on the screen, and other such properties.

# Changing Drawer Properties

Changing drawer properties is very simple. The properties are almost exactly the same as panel properties. If you right-click a drawer and then click on "properties," a menu will appear with all sorts of fun goodies to play with.

You'll notice the same information that you input when you created the drawer; you can change this if you like. You can modify the hide/button arrows for your drawer as well.
