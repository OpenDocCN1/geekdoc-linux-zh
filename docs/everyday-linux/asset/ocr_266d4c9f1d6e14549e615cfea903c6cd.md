# ### Adding Additional Slides

You may add a new slide to your presentation by clicking “insert slide” from the presentation menu, or from the top menu by clicking “insert” and “slide.” The slide will be labeled “slide two” and you may move between slide one and two by clicking the tabs directly below your slide. If your new slide has many of the attributes of another slide you have created, you may make a copy of the slide by clicking “duplicate slide” from the presentation menu.

When you insert a new slide, the “insert slide” window pops up and you can choose from an assortment of slide layouts, or you may choose a blank slide. Each layout has a brief description of text and image placement. You can always change the layout of the slide by clicking “modify slide” from the presentation toolbar.

# ### Sorting Your Slides

You can easily rearrange the order of your slides by clicking on the slide view icon. This small icon is located on the right border of your Star Desktop and has four tiny boxes in it.
