# Combine

Film— is a cute little filter I haven’t exactly found a practical use for, but find it fun to play with. It takes a series of images and joins them together to look like frames in a roll of film. Open the images you want to join and then choose “film” in the filter menu. A window will pop up and you can add and remove from a list of images which are opening in GIMP to create your film.
