Clone—allows you to use one part of an image to paint over another area of the image. Sounds strange, yes, but it's useful for cleaning up edges. To use, place the mouse over the area you want to use as a source and then press CTRL key and the left mouse button. Release both, then move to the area you want to paint over and hold only the left mouse button and drag the mouse over the area. Size is determined by brush size selected from brush dialogue window. Likewise, you may choose a pattern to paint with. To choose image or pattern, double-click on the clone icon.
