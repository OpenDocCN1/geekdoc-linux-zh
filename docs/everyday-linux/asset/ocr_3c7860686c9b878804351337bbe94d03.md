If you just want to change size or attributes without changing the font you can do that also. To change font size or to make it bold, italics or underline, you can do this from the toolbar to the right of the font window. For example: to begin typing in bold type, just click the “B” icon and begin typing. When you are through, click the icon again and bold is turned off. There are additional attributes you can change from the font attributes window we mentioned above.

If you want to change attributes of already existing text, simply highlight the text with your mouse and then right click and select “character.” The font attributes window will pop up and you can change fonts and/or attributes. See the next section on selecting text for more information.

You can easily select larger areas of text. Place the cursor at the beginning of the text you want to select. Then click “edit,” “select” and choose from “sentence,” “paragraph,” “page,” or “all.” Once the text is selected, you can then right click and choose “character” to make changes.

# Cut, Copy and Paste

To cut text from a document, or to move it to a different location, you need only highlight the text you want to remove. Once you have selected the text, click “edit” and “cut.” You can also right click over the selected text and select “cut.” The text is removed from the document and placed in memory (sometimes called the “clipboard”). If you want to move the text to another place in the document, or another document, simply place the cursor where you want the text to be inserted and then either click “edit” and “paste” or right click with the mouse and choose “paste”

If you want to copy, rather than cut text, highlight it and then click “edit” and “copy.” Likewise you can right click and choose “copy.” This action will leave the original selected text where it is at, and you can insert other copies wherever you like.

![](bbox=[0.16, 0.82, 0.28, 0.95])

It is very easy to copy and paste text from one document to another. Once the selected text is in memory, it can be pasted into any program which will accept text.
