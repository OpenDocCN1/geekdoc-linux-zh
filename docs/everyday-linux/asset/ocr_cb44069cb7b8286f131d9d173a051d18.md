# **Available Presentation Templates**

Listed below are all of the templates currently available in Star Office. There is a wide range to choose from. Following the presentation title, are the titles of the slides included in the group.

**Breaking Bad News:** Title, Current Situation, Causes, First Alternative, Second Alternative, Recommendation, Future Prospects, Summary

**Brainstorming:** Title, Agenda, The Aims of This Brainstorming, Rules, Active Brainstorming, Summary, The Next Steps

**Business Report:** Title, Goals, Important Events, Company Results, Market Share, Market Share by Divisions, Revenues, Cross Margins, Expenditure, Earnings, Capitol Structure, Cash Flow, Productivity Indicators, Future Prospects, Trends in This Sector, Internal Reactions, Company Goals, Summary

**Business Venture:** Title, Agenda, Introduction and Background, Market Situation, Differences between the Companies, Products, Services, Finances, Summary

**Company Finance Report:** Title, Intro, Contents, Figures, Revenues, Fields of Business, Balance Sheet, Branch Offices, Shares, End

**Company Meeting:** Title, Agenda, Company Goals, What Has Been Achieved, Company Structure, Major Challenges, Main Goals, Results, Sales and Profit, Main Cost Areas, Market Research, Goals for Next Period, Summary

**Concept:** Title, Goal, Next Goal, Current Situation, Development, Possible Strategies, Recommendation, Questions, Tasks

**Introducing a New Product:** Title, Long Term Goal, Customer Wishes, Fulfilling Customer Needs, Cost Analysis, Strengths and Advantages, Next Steps of Action

**Introducing the Company:** Title, General, History, Future, Product Lines, Product 1, Product 2, Product 3, Company Structure, Customer Services, Employees, Company News, Summary
