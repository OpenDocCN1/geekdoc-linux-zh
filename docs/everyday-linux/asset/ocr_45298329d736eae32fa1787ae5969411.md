The tutorial will guide you through learning how to play the game, such as controlling armies, using spells and weapons and moving about. We don't suggest trying to figure this one out by yourself unless you are very talent ed at learning games without reading about them first. The manual which comes with the game also provides a good amount of information.

When you are ready to play the game, you will have a choice between single scenairo, multi-player or campaign mode.

Single Scenario mode is played with one map against the computer. You are given a set of mission conditions and the winner is determined by who meets those conditions first.

Multiplayer mode allows you to play against other humans on one computer, over a network or even over the Internet.

Campaign mode are a series of linked scenarios marking climactic events in longer stories. Campaigns are played against the computer.
