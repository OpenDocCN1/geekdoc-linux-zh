# ### What Is Star Office?

Star Office did a great service to the Linux community with a free office suite for Linux. Originally owned by a German company, Star Division, the office suite is now the property of Sun Microsystems. Since that time the office suite has been updated and remains free. Even more exciting news came in July 2000 when Sun announced that it was making Star Office Suite open source. This will likely lead to rapid additions and updates.

There are many programs within the office suite such as a word processor, scheduler, presentation program, database program, spreadsheet and more. Entire books have been written on Star Office Suite so we will just familiarize you with the very basics so that you may get started using the office suite. If you find you like Star Office and want to learn more about what it can do, we would suggest purchasing one of the books on Star Office.
