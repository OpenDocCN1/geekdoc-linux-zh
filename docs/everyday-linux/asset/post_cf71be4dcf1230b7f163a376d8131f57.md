## Railroad Tycoon II

Loki Games (www.lokigames.com) and Pop Top Software have teamed up to provide Linux gamers with Railroad Tycoon II. This is a commercial game that allows you to assume the role of a railroad mogul. Your goal is to be smarter and better than everyone else in the railroad industry.

There are over seventy-five scenarios that include building the transcontinental railroad in the 1800s, to supplying allied forces during WWII to establishing an electric railroad through the Swiss Alps. You can play alone or against dozens of computer opponents or even against friends on a local area network or Internet.

The game provides stunningly realistic maps and graphics. The maps are actual maps and the financial models are detailed and logical. The game comes with a tutorial so it is not difficult to learn how to play.

### Installing Railroad Tycoon

To install Railroad Tycoon, you must be root, but you need not run X-Windows as root. Instead, we offer a safer way to install the game. Put the Railroad Tycoon CD into your CD-ROM and then open a terminal window. Type in this command to mount your CD-ROM:

```bash
su -c mount /dev/cdrom /mnt/cdrom
```

Now type in this command:

```bash
su -c ./setup.sh
```

The `su -c` command tells the computer to execute the command as root. You will be asked for the root password. Type it in and hit Enter. The reason we suggest using this command is because it only executes one command and makes mistakes less likely to happen. The `./setup.sh` is the file on the CD-ROM that you want to run to start the install. Once you type in the root password, an install window will pop up.

The first window which comes up asks you which parts of Railroad Tycoon you would like to install. The movies can be run off the CD and do not need to be installed on your computer. They take up a good deal of space and if you do not have a large hard drive, it is better to not install them. Choose which parts you want to install and then click "begin install."

## **Railroad Tycoon Tutorial**

Many people like to avoid reading manuals and just jump right in and figure out the game by themselves. While you can certainly do this, we suggest that you either read the manual or at least run through the tutorial from the game menu.

To access the tutorial, start from the main menu and click "single player." Then choose "load scenario."

![](img/92fb6d8ad19de3b3470ba95c7b71b82e_380_0.png)

**Figure 20-24**
Selecting the *Tutorial*.

A box will open on the right and you can choose tutorial and click Ok. The tutorial begins with a brief overview of the scenario.

When you are ready to begin your railroad, double-click "start a new company" toward the bottom of the screen. Note that tutorial tips will continue to pop up until you uncheck the box below the tip.

Using this window you can create a name for your Railroad and choose from a wide variety of logos. You must also decide how much money you want to invest in your railroad (which in turn decides how much money investors will put into your company).

Follow the tutorial in the book to learn how to lay track and move around using the map. You must also purchase trains and build stations and other buildings. You'll have to watch your finances though because you can go bankrupt if you don't plan things out.

## Starting a New Scenario

When you are ready to leave the tutorial and start a scenario of your choosing, go back to the main menu. Click on the icon at the bottom of the row on the left side. When the main menu comes back up, choose "single player" and "new scenario."

![](img/92fb6d8ad19de3b3470ba95c7b71b82e_383_0.png)

Click on "map" from the right side of the window and a window will appear on the left with a list of maps and time periods for you to choose from.

The scenarios are authentic. For instance, choosing the Northeast US around 1955 is a tough time. Competition was fierce and it was difficult to survive. On the other hand, choosing Canada about the same time was much more promising.

You can keep track of your financial situation and see an annual company report. If the company is doing well, you can raise additional money by selling shares of the company. You are limited only by your sense of adventure and your business sense!

## Heroes III

Heroes III is another commercial game produced by Loki Games and New World Computing. It is set as an epic story of kings, traitors, castles and magic. You must command armies and battle evil hordes of monsters and men of dark deeds!

### Installing Heroes III

To install Heroes III, you must be root. You needn't run X-Windows as root however. Bring up a terminal window and log in as root. Then type in:

```bash
mount /mnt/cdrom
```

Then type in:

```bash
cd /mnt/cdrom
```

and finally:

```bash
sh setup.sh
```

The install will now start and bring up a window asking you what parts of the game you want to install.

When you have made your choices for installation, click "begin install." When the install is complete you will see another window.

When the install is complete you will have the option to either begin playing Heroes III or to exit. Choose "exit" since you do not want to play the game while you are root.
