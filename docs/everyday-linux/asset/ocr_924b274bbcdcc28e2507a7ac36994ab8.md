You will see a dotted box around your image. To move the entire image around, simply place your mouse inside the box. The cursor will turn into a small crossed arrow. You can now move the image to where you want it on the screen. To resize the image vertically, click on one of the vertical dots and drag the mouse to the size you want. To alter size horizontally, click and drag on a dot on the top of bottom of the image. To maintain the ratio, use a dot on the corner of the image.

# Arranging Things: Placing an Image Behind Text

If you've already typed some text and then decide you want to place an image behind it, you can do so quite easily. Click on the text to make the dotted box appear. Now, from the top menu, click “insert,” “graphic,” and “from file.” An “insert graphics” window will appear.

You may move around directories and choose a graphic. There is a small preview window on the right side of this window. If you cannot see it, widen your Star Office main window. Choose an image and click “Open.”

The image will appear over the text (hiding it) on your slide.

To get the image to go behind the text, hold the shift and click on the arrange icon. Choose “send to back” and the graphic will be moved behind the text.
