# Xpaint

Xpaint is a drawing program as well as a basic image editor and it is found under the Another Level menus. Xpaint is best used for doing simple diagrams and drawings, though it is quite capable of loading large images.

To begin a drawing, click once and hold on “file.” Then choose “new canvas” or “new with size” if you want to specify size. You may open an existing drawing or image by choosing “open” or, if you have an image in the clipboard, choose “load clipboard.”

When the image is opened a toolbar will appear at the bottom and top of the screen. The bottom of the screen contains color palettes. If you have a three-button mouse you may make use of the middle button as a second color. Using the left mouse button, choose a color on each palette. When you paint or use a filled shape feature, you may use the middle mouse button to get your second color.
