# Find and Replace

GEdit also allows you to find and replace things in your files. This can be particularly useful when you find you've used a wrong name or something in a long file. To begin search and replace, click “edit” and “replace.” The window that pops up looks quite like the earlier “find” window. Below the line for what you want to search for, however, is a line for what you want to replace it with.

Perhaps you've written a long letter to your Great Aunt Martha and since you want to borrow a good deal of money from her, you have inquired several times about her pet cat. Then, for good measure you've run on for a bit about the merits of pet cats. It is only after you're finished with the letter that you realize that Aunt Martha has a pet spider. Easy: just find cat and replace with spider (never mind the part about spidermip!).
