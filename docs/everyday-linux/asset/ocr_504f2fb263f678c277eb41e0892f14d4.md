# # Spell Checking Your File

GEdit provides an easy way to spell check your file. Click “plugins” and “spell check.” GEdit will pop up a spell check window and list misspelled words as it finds them in the file. It will list a word and then list an alternative spelling suggestion. To the right are the actions you can take. If you want to replace the word with one of the alternatives, just click on the alternative and then click “replace.” If you want GEdit to accept the word as a correctly spelled work, click “accept.” If you want an incorrectly spelled word to just be ignored, click “skip.” If there are no alternatives, and you know the correct spelling of a work, click insert and type in the correct word.

You can set some preferences with GEdit’s spell check by clicking on the config ure tab of the spell check window. In the special configuration window you can specify minimum number of letters in a word to check, special characters in words to ignore (such as @ in e-mail addresses,) and other parameters.
