# The Basics
of Use
and Setup

In this chapter, we present conceptual understanding of some key topics in the Linux Operating system. We won't make any substantial changes to your user account or to the system itself during the course of this chapter, but we try to present a few procedures and concepts you will need to understand throughout the course of your use of Linux.

We talk about logging into your machine and logging out, the concept of groups and permissions, the root user, and shutting down the computer.

These are basic concepts and procedures that you're going to use when you use Linux, and they will provide you with a few elementary "dos and don'ts" for when you just start out. If you re familiar with all the topics listed above, you probably don't need to read this chapter-you can skip ahead to customizing your own account.
