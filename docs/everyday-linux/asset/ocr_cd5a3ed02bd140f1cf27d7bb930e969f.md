Color Picker—allows you to select a specific color in your image. Choose the tool and then move the mouse over your image until you are over the color you want, then click with left mouse button. The selected color will appear in the foreground color box of the main tool menu.

*Color Picker.*

Bucket Fill—allows you to fill entire regions with a color or pattern. Double-clicking on the icon will bring up the bucket tool options where you may choose color fill or pattern fill and some other characteristics. When using color, the foreground color will be used. To use a pattern, select pattern fill from the options box. Whichever pattern selected will be used. To choose a pattern, see the section on patterns.
