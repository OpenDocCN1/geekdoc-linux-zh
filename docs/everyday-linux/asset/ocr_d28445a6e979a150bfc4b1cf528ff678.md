Drawing a Box

Suppose we want to draw a box around the title of our slide. We would want a box with just an outline, rather than a solid color. From the left toolbar, shift and click on the rectangle icon. Select the outlined box. You will now see some different controls appear at the top of the screen, beneath the main toolbar.

The icon on the left side which looks like a calligraphy pen tip is for line style. Click on it and you may choose from various line types such as solid, dotted, dashed, or others.

The icon to the right of the line style icon is two arrows. Clicking on this will allow you to choose from various arrows or endpoints of lines. You won’t need this for drawing a box, but if you were drawing lines you might want to use this.

You may also choose line style from the pop down window to the right of the arrow style icon.
