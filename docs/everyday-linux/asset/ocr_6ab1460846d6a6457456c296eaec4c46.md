Mistakes may be corrected in two different ways. If you have made a small mistake you may simply choose “undo” under “edit.” If you’ve really bungled the project and want to start over again from the original (if there was one), you may choose “file” and then “revert.” When choosing revert, the window you have been working in will close (without saving) and a new window will pop up with the original.

When you are finished with your image or drawing, click “file” from the image window and then “save” or “save as.”
