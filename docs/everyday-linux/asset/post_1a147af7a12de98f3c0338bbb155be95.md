If you just want to change size or attributes without changing the font you can do that also. To change font size or to make it bold, italics or underline, you can do this from the toolbar to the right of the font window. For example: to begin typing in bold type, just click the “B” icon and begin typing. When you are through, click the icon again and bold is turned off. There are additional attributes you can change from the font attributes window we mentioned above.

If you want to change attributes of already existing text, simply highlight the text with your mouse and then right click and select “character.” The font attributes window will pop up and you can change fonts and/or attributes. See the next section on selecting text for more information.

![](img/92fb6d8ad19de3b3470ba95c7b71b82e_291_0.png)

You can easily select larger areas of text. Place the cursor at the beginning of the text you want to select. Then click “edit,” “select” and choose from “sentence,” “paragraph,” “page,” or “all.” Once the text is selected, you can then right click and choose “character” to make changes.

# Cut, Copy and Paste

To cut text from a document, or to move it to a different location, you need only highlight the text you want to remove. Once you have selected the text, click “edit” and “cut.” You can also right click over the selected text and select “cut.” The text is removed from the document and placed in memory (sometimes called the “clipboard”). If you want to move the text to another place in the document, or another document, simply place the cursor where you want the text to be inserted and then either click “edit” and “paste” or right click with the mouse and choose “paste”

If you want to copy, rather than cut text, highlight it and then click “edit” and “copy.” Likewise you can right click and choose “copy.” This action will leave the original selected text where it is at, and you can insert other copies wherever you like.

![](img/92fb6d8ad19de3b3470ba95c7b71b82e_291_1.png)

It is very easy to copy and paste text from one document to another. Once the selected text is in memory, it can be pasted into any program which will accept text.

# Spell-As-You-Go

WordPerfect 8 has a very good spell checker which works as you type by underlining words it believes are misspelled. You can either check each word as it is marked as misspelled or you can finish your document and then go back through it. When you want to check a word marked as misspelled, right click over that word.

![](img/92fb6d8ad19de3b3470ba95c7b71b82e_292_0.png)

Figure 15-16
Spell-As-You-Go

When you right click over the misspelled word you will see a small pop down menu. If WordPerfect finds correctly spelled words which are close matches to your misspelled word, you will see the possibilities. You may also choose to add a word which is correctly spelled, but WordPerfect does not recognize. The added word (and any other occurrences of it) is then considered correctly spelled. You may also choose "skip" and move on to the next word.

# Adding an Image to Your Document

It is very easy to add images or clip art to your documents. Place the cursor where you would like to insert or draw a picture. Click “insert,” then “graphics.” Then choose “from file” if you want to insert an existing image. Choose “draw picture” if you want to draw your own. This feature is available only with the commercial version of WordPerfect.

If you choose to insert an image from a file, a window will pop up and you can move around your directories and choose the file you want.

![](img/92fb6d8ad19de3b3470ba95c7b71b82e_293_0.png)

Figure 15-17 Insert an Image or Drawing

> WordPerfect has no image preview. We suggest that you open GQView, or another image viewer. This will make it easier to choose the image you want to add to your document.

![](img/92fb6d8ad19de3b3470ba95c7b71b82e_293_1.png)

When you choose an image to be inserted, it will appear on your document with small blocks around the edges.

You can place your mouse over the blocks and drag them to resize the image. When you have positioned your image as you want it, just click outside the image area to permanently place it. If you change your mind later, just click on the image again, the blocks will reappear and you can reposition or resize the image.

# Selecting a Printer and Printing a Document

Before you print any documents you must first select a printer for WordPerfect 8 to use. Click "file" and then "print".

When the print window pops up, choose "select" near the top right of the window. The "select printer" dialog box will pop up.

The dialog box will list whatever printers are connected to your system (our screenshot doesn't list printers because there were none connected). Choose a printer from this box and then click ok.

![](img/92fb6d8ad19de3b3470ba95c7b71b82e_295_0.png)

When you are ready to print something, click “file” and “print.” You may also click on the little printer icon towards the left side of the toolbar. The print dialogue box will pop up and you can choose to print the entire document, or certain pages of the document. You can also specify how many copies you would like to print and whether you want high, medium or draft quality.

Tip If you are just printing something to proofread, you could choose to print it in draft mode. The quality will not be as good but it will save ink and print faster. You might also choose to not print graphics which you can toggle on and off in the print dialogue box.

# Converting Your Document to a Web Document

WordPerfect 8 makes it quite easy to convert a document to one which can be used on the web. This can be very useful if you don’t know how to write in HTML. The first thing you will want to do is format your document so that it is able to be used on the web. Click “file” and then “Internet Publisher.” The Internet Publisher Dialogue box will pop up.

![](img/92fb6d8ad19de3b3470ba95c7b71b82e_296_0.png)

From this dialogue box choose “format as Web Document.” Another dialogue box will pop up warning you that WordPerfect 8 is about to convert your document for the web and you may lose some document formatting not transferable to HTML.

![](img/92fb6d8ad19de3b3470ba95c7b71b82e_297_0.png)

# Figure 15-22

*Web Conversion Warning.*

If you have previously saved the document as a WordPerfect document, you will have a copy of the original formatting so you needn’t worry. Click “yes” to continue converting your document.

You won’t notice much difference in your document just by looking at it in WordPerfect.

Compare figure 15-23 to 15-18. You will notice that once the document has been converted, it will be opened in the Web Editor. The Web Editor looks a lot like a WordPerfect 8 document window, but the toolbars are different. You can find what the function of the various tools are by holding the mouse over the icons.

When you are ready to save your document for the web, click “file” and “Internet Publisher” to open the dialogue box. Then choose “publish to HTML.” You will be prompted for a name to save your document as. After you have typed in a name, click “ok.”

# Creating a New Web Document

You can create a new HTML document with very little knowledge of HTML. Open the Internet Publisher Dialogue Box by clicking “file” and “Internet Publisher.” Choose “New Web Document.”
You can create the web document much as you would any other document but you will have to know a bit about html to create anything other than a simple page. Hold the mouse over the various tools to find out what they do.
