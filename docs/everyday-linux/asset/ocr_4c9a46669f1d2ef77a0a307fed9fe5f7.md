A dotted rectangle will appear and you may type in your text. When you are through, click somewhere outside of the dotted rectangle and the text will be anchored to the page.

While the dotted box is surrounding your text, you may change other attribut- es. If you want to center your text, click on the alignment icon and select center. If you want to add color, just click on a color box at the bottom of the window.

Any time you want to go back and change something about the text, it is pos- sible to do so. Just click over the text and the dotted box will reappear and you may then make changes. To delete the text, first click to make the dotted box appear and then hit the backspace key.

# Adding an Image to the Slide

To add an image to your slide, click “insert,” “graphics,” and “from file.” A window will pop up and allow you to navigate around directories and choose the image you wish to add. Then click “open,” and the image will appear on your slide.
