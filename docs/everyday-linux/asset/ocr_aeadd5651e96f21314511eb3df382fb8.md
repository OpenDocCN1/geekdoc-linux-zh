Transform—has four options which can be selected by double-clicking on the icon and then choosing. Once you choose an option then you may select a region of an image to alter.

Rotation—allows rotation (by dragging) of a selected region or entire image.

Scaling—allows selected area of image to be enlarged or shrunk.

Shearing—allows selected area of image to be "leaned" to one side or up/down.

Perspective—allows selected region of image to be angled toward or away from you.

![](bbox=[0.05, 0.4, 0.35, 0.5])

Flip—allows flipping selection left/right or top/bottom. Choose which by double-clicking on icon to pull up tool options box.

![](bbox=[0.05, 0.575, 0.35, 0.675])

Text—allows text insertion into image. When you choose this icon and then click on an image, the text tools window pops up. Use this window to choose font and characteristics (bold, size, etc.). Text color will match foreground color on main tool menu. When you are satisfied, click ok. The text will appear on your image and you may move it to where you wish it to be.
