# Minesweeper

The goal of Minesweeper is to uncover all the squares without clicking on a bomb. There are ten bombs hidden under the squares which you need to flag. To uncover a square, click on the left mouse button. To flag a bomb use the right mouse button.

Under some squares you will find a number. The number will tell you how many bombs are touching that square. You must deduce where the bombs are. For example, if you uncover a square with a number one, it means that it touches one bomb. This may mean above, below, sideways or diagonally.

Some squares have no number, and when you click on that square, it will turn over all other unnumbered squares near it.
