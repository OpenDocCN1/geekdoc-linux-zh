When you use slide view, all your slides will appear on the desktop and you can click on them and drag them to the order you wish to use. You can also delete slides when in this window by simply right clicking on the slide to be deleted and then selecting "delete slide" in the pop up window.

# ## Viewing Your Presentation

When you are through with your presentation and would like to view it, right-click over the first slide and choose “slide show.” You will see a full screen view of your slides.

![](bbox=[0.03, 0.34, 0.9, 0.72])

To advance from one slide to the next, just place your mouse over the image and click. When all of the slides have been displayed you will get a prompt to “click to end presentation.”

# ## Additional Presentation Options

Star Office gives you some additional options to help with your presentation. There are some additional small buttons near the one to organize your slides.
