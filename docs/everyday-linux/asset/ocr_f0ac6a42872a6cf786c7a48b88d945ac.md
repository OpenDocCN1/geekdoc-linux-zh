# ## Adding an Image to Your Document

You can add images and clipart to any StarWriter document very easily. Place the cursor where you would like the image to be and then click “insert,” then “graphics,” and then “from file.”

![](bbox=[0.05, 0.24, 0.95, 0.5])

**Figure 12-7**
*Inserting a Graphic.*

You will see a preview of the graphics you click on, and once the image is loaded into your document you can resize it or move it around to just where you want it.

> > **tip**
> >
> > Remember that adding larger images will add to the size of the document. Adding two or three images can make a huge bulky document.
