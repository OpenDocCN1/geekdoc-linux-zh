When the “new from template” window pops up, click on “miscellaneous” in the box on the left and then choose “month/year” calendar on the right. Click “more” to see a preview, and click “ok” to begin creating your calendar.

After clicking “ok” to the “new from template” window, a new window will come up called “StarOffice Calendar.”

You can choose either year or month. If you choose month, you will need to specify which month. To enter in events you want to remember, click “personal data.”

Using this window, you can enter the event (a birthday, or some other special event you want to remember). Enter the day and month of the event and then click “insert.” You may enter as may events as you like. When you are done, click “ok.” StarCalc will create your calendar.

You will notice that the calendar is divided up into rows and columns just like a spreadsheet. You can move around the calendar just as you do with a spreadsheet, using arrow keys to add events as needed.
