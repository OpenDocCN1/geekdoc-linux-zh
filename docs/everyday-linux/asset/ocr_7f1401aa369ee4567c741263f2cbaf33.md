If you live in an area where power outages during storms are common, you might choose to do automatic backups more often.

When either your power is interrupted or WordPerfect 8 crashes, you are at risk to lose your work. When you restart WordPerfect 8 you will see a small window pop up telling you that backup files exist and you can start WordPerfect's file manager to select the files, or click continue to ignore the backup files.
