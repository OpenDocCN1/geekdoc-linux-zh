The dialog window will show the word in question, and its suggestions (if it has any) for the correct spelling. On the right are buttons which allow you to "ignore," "ignore all," "replace," and "always replace." Ignore all will ignore all occurences it finds of that particular word in that document. Replace all will replace all occurences it finds of the misspelled word. You also have the choice to add a word to the dictionary.

The other way to spelcheck is to do it as you type. By clicking "tools," "spellcheck," and then "autospelcheck," you can see a misspelled word as soon as you type it. When you misspell a word, Star Office will place a squiggly red line underneath the word. You can then click on the word with the right mouse button and see suggestions for correct spelling, ignore all, or to add the word to the dictionary.

Find and replace can be used to replace a word which is not misspelled with another word. Suppose you have written a personal letter to someone and then realize that you have used the wrong name and typed "Burgers" instead of "Burgess." If you have used the name multiple times, it can be very time consuming to go back through the document and hunt and replace the names. You will find it much easier to choose "edit" and then "find and replace."
