To change colors, double-click on either the foreground or background color blocks at the bottom of the main menu. The color selection window pops up separately. The narrow vertical bar shows a rainbow of hues. Click on the general color you want and a larger range of hues of that color will appear in the box on the left. Click on the color you want and click “close.” Alternatively, you may move the slide bars around on the right to choose a color.
