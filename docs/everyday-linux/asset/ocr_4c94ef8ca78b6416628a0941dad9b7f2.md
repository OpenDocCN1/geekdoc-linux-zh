You will see a list of existing printer drivers in the lower part of the window. Scroll down through the list and see if your printer is there. If you find your printer, double-click on it and it will appear at the top portion of the window under “installed printers.” There is an existing choice of “generic printer.” Click once on your printer in the upper window and then click “test page.”

If you do not find your printer in the existing printer drivers, just try the generic printer as it usually works.

Click once on the printer you want to use as your default printer and then click “default.”

You can also click “configure” and set default values for your printer such as paper size, margins and paper orientation.
