As you can see, the output looks pretty similar whether it's in HTML or even plain text. This means that your document should be easily followed by no matter the medium it's rendered in.

The GUIButton element, as its name implies, indicates text displayed to the user on a button. Whether the GUIButton text is rendered differently from the regular text in your document depends on the stylesheets you use.
