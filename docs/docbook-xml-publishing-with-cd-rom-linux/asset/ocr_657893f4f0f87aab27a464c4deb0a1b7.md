# gPhoto

One of the neatest inventions in the past 10 years, in my opinion, is the digital camera. Unfortunately, many digital cameras come with software for the MacOS and Windows, they usually don't come with software for Linux or other operating systems. Fortunately, gPhoto (see Figure 3.10) comes to the rescue with support for (at the time of this writing) more than 105 camera models on the following operating systems:

+   * Linux
* FreeBSD
* HPUX
* NetBSD
* OpenBSD
