Why would you want to include the part number information about
book? For those who are making use of DocBook XML, this sort of information
can be used to streamline your production systems, or the inventory
information included in the document used to produce the book,
specialized stylesheets can be made to help generate catalogs, or
information, and much more. This has the potential to save
company money on data entry and other tasks, too.
