# # Selecting an Internet Service Provider (ISP)

Because of the meteoic rise in the popularity of Linux, many ISPs are training their support staff in the ways of Linux. If you already have an ISP, give one of them a call to let them know of your Linux pursuits. Chances are, that person already has information pertinent to Linux subscribers and can provide you with that information. If you’re shopping for a new ISP, this section offers some practical selection advice.

Some ISPs provide their own proprietary software that you must install on your PC to connect to the Internet. The software they provide is likely to run only on Windows.
