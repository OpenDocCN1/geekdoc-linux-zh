Now, just as octal and hexadecimal, there can be identity problems
when binary. The number 101 in binary is not the same as 101 in
hex, or 101 in decimal. For this reason, always append the suffix "B" to
our binary values to make sure people reading your programs (incl-
ing you, six weeks after the fact) know what base you are working from.

the 16s column, and so on. In binary, a column's value is either
(1 time) or not present (0 times).
