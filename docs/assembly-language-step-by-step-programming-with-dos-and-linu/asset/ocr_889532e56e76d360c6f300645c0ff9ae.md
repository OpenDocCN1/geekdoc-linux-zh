# An Uneasy Alliance

In short, segments may begin at any segment address. There are 65,536 segment addresses evenly distributed across real mode's full megabyte of memory, 16 bytes apart. A segment address is more a permission than compulsion; for all the 64K possible segment addresses, only five or six are ever actually used to begin segments at any one. Think of segment addresses as slots where segments may be placed.
