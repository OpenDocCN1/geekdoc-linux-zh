There is a cost to this speed, and the cost is in extra memory used, especially if the macro is called a great many times. Notice in Figure 9.3 that three invocations of the macro generate a total of 12 instructions in memory. If the macro had been set up as a procedure, it would have required the four instructions in the body of the procedure, plus one RET instruction and three CALL instructions to do the same work.
