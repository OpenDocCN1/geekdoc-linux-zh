# CMP Arithmetic Comparison

Flags affected:

| O D I T S A P C | OF: Overflow flag TF: Trap flag AF: Aux carry |
| F F F F F F F | DF: Direction flag SF: Sign flag PF: Parity flag |
| * * * * * * * | IF: Interrupt flag ZF: Zero flag CF: Carry flag |

Legal forms:

+   CMP r8,r8
CMP m8,r8
CMP r8,m8
CMP r16,r16
CMP m16,r16
CMP r16,m16
CMP r32,r32 386+
CMP m32,r32 386+
CMP r32,m32 386+
CMP r8,i8
CMP m8,i8
CMP r16,i16
CMP m16,i16
CMP r32,i32 386+
CMP m32,i32 386+
CMP r16,i8
CMP m16,i8
CMP r32,i8 386+
CMP m32,i8 386+
CMP AL,i8
CMP AX,i16
CMP EAX,i32 386+

Examples:

+   CMP BX, DI
CMP EAX,5
CMP AX,0FFFFH ;Uses single-byte opcode
CMP AL,42H ;Uses single-byte opcode
CMP BP,17H
CMP WORD [BX+SI+Inset],5
CMP WORD ES:[BX],08B00H

Notes:

CMP compares its two operations, and sets the flags to indicate the results of comparison. The destination operand is not affected. The operation itself is identical to subtraction of the source from the destination without borrow (SUB), that the result does not replace the destination. Typically, CMP is followed by one of the conditional jump instructions; that is, JE to jump if the operands were equal; JNE if they were unequal; and so forth.
