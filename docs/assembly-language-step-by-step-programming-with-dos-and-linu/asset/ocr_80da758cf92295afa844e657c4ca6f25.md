That part of it, however, is hidden from you so that you don't have to worry about it. Once you tell all those heaps of transistors what to do, they'll know how to do it.

This plan, this list of machine instructions in memory, is your assembly language program. The whole point of this book is to teach you to correctly arrange machine instructions in memory for the use of the CPU.

With any luck at all, by now you'll have a reasonable conceptual understanding of both what computers do and what they they are. It's time to start looking closely at the nature of the operations that machine instructions force the CPU to perform.
