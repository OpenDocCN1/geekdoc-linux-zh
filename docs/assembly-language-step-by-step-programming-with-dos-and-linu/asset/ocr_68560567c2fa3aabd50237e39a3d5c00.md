# Notes:

By default these instructions make a short jump (127 bytes or 128 bytes back) if
s condition is true, or fall through if the condition is not true. The conditions all involve
flags, and the flags in question are given to the right of the mnemonic and
description.

The mnemonics incorporating "above" or "below" are for use after signed comparisons,
 the mnemonics incorporating "less" or "greater" are for use after signed compar-
is. "Equal" and "Zero" may be used after unsigned or signed comparisons.
