# A Strategy for Designing the GUI

There's no need to worry if you don't have much experience in creating GUIs. The Developer's Exam recognizes there are lots of ways to put together a graphical interface. There is no "correct" GUI. You will not be graded on your talents as a graphics designer. Moreover, you are not expected to have mastered all the conventions and principles that are second to an experienced, full-time GUI programmer. Even if you have no GUI development experience at all, you can go very wrong by applying common sense. The worst that can happen is that you might lose some points. A submission with a proper GUI that does well in other areas is likely to pass.

The next four sections present a simple four-step approach to help you organize your GUI thinking. The four steps are

+   1. Identify needed components.

+   2. Isolate regions of behavior.

+   3. Sketch the GUI.

+   4. Choose layout managers.
