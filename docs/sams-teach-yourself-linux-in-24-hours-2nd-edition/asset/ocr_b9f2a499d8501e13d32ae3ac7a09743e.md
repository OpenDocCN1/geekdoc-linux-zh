# FIGURE 12.8

The krn newsreading window features scroll bars and a point-and-click interface for reading, replying to, or following up on newsgroup articles.

![](bbox=[0.26, 0.16, 0.83, 0.62])

The numerous buttons in the window's menu bar let you

+   - Navigate through the newsgroup's articles
+   - Search for subjects
+   - Print
+   - Save to disk
+   - Reply to by email
+   - Follow up with a post
+   - Reply and follow up
+   - Forward by email
+   - Mark as read or unread

![](bbox=[0.09, 0.84, 0.15, 0.92])

Click your mouse button on the Sender, Date, Lines, or Subject column headings to sort the article list.
