The xf86config command configures X11 through a text-based interface. Before you begin, take some time to read documentation under the /usr/X11R6/lib/X11/doc directory. This directory, summarized in Table 3.1, contains numerous documents with information about accelerated video graphics, different video monitors, various graphics chipsets, different video modes, and several helpful text files filled with tips on configuring X11.
