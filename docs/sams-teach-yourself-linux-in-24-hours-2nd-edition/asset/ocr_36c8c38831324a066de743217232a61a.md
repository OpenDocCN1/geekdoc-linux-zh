To perform quick totals of your data, click in the bottom cell of a column of numbers, and then click the Sum button (the Σ) next to the equal(=) sign in the data entry field above your work area. An X and a check mark appear next to the data entry field, which has the SUM formula inserted. Click the check mark to accept the entry, or click the X button to cancel.

When you finish, press Ctrl+S, or open the File menu and click Save to save your work. StarCalc can import and export a variety of spreadsheet document formats, as shown in Table 19.3.
