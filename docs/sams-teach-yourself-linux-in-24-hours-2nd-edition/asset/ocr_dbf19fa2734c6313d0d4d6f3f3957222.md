# Q&A

Q What other drawing and graphics editing programs come with OpenLinux? A The classic graphics editor included with X11 is the bitmap client. For editing more complex bitmaps, try the xpaint client. For technical drawing, such as blueprints, try the object-oriented editors tgif and xfig. However, you need a display with a resolution of at least 1024×768 in order to use these clients.
