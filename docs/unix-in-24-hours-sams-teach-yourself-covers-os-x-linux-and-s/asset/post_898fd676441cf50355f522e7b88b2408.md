# Task 11.2: The Colon Commands in vi

Without too much explanation, you have learned a couple of colon commands—that is, commands that have a colon as the first character. The colon immediately zooms the cursor to the bottom of the screen for further input. These commands are actually a subset of quite a large range of commands, all part of the ex editor on which vi is based.

Remember what I told you at the beginning of the last hour about how vi is the visual shell to the ex editor? Turns out that ex is still a part of the system after all.
