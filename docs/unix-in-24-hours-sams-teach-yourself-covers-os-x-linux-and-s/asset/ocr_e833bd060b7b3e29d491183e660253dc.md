> **Note**
> 
> In this table, I use the simple shorthand notation introduced in [Hour 7](#), “Looking into Files.” Unix users often use a caret (^) followed by a character instead of the awkward Ctrl- notation. Therefore, ^f has the same meaning as Ctrl-f. Expressing this operation as ^f does not change the way it’s performed: You still press and hold down the Control key and then type f. It’s just a more succinct notation.
