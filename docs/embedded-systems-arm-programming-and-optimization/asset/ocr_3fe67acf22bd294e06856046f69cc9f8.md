# # Acknowledgments

Several students assisted me in the development of this book.

During spring and summer 2013, undergraduate students Benjamin Morgan, Jonathan Kilby, Shawn Weaver, Justin Robinson, and Amadeo Bellotti evaluated the DMA controller and performance monitoring on the Raspberry Pi5's Broadcom BCM2835 and the Xilinx Zynq 7020.

During summer 2014, undergraduate student Daniel Clements helped develop a uniform approach for using the Linux perf-event on the ARM11, ARM Cortex A9, and ARM Cortex A15. Daniel also evaluated Imagination Technology's OpenCL runtime and characterized its performance on the PowerVR 544 GPU on our ODROID UX Exynos 5 platform.

During summer 2015, student Friel "Scottie" Scott evaluated the Mali T628 GPU on the ODROID-UX3 platform and proofread Chapter 5.

Much of my insight about many optimizations for computer vision algorithms were an outgrowth of my graduate student Fan Zhang's dissertation on auto-optimization of stencil loops on the Texas Instruments Digital Signal Processor architecture.
