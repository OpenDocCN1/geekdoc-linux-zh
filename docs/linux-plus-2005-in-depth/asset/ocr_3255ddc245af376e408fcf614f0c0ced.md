After the POST has completed, the BIOS looks for an operating system on a floppy, CD-ROM, or hard disk. Most settings used by the BIOS are configurable as they are stored on the CMOS chip, and after the POST has completed, most BIOSs allow you to choose these settings by pressing the Delete key, as shown in Figure 2-5. If you enter the BIOS setup utility, a screen similar to Figure 2-6 appears where you can configure devices and observe hardware settings.

NOTE

The system BIOS setup utility looks very different from manufacturer to manufacturer; however, each utility roughly contains the same general types of information and configurations.
