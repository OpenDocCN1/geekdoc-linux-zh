# # Common Uses of Linux

As discussed earlier, an important feature of Linux is its versatility. Linux can provide services meeting the needs of differing companies in a variety of situations. Furthermore, configuring these services is straightforward given the wide range of documentation freely available on the Internet; simply choose the services that are required and then customize Linux to provide those services. These services can be used on the local computer **workstation**, or they can be configured to allow other computers to connect to it across a network. Services that are used on the local computer are referred to as **workstation services**, whereas services that are made available for other computers across a network are known as **server services**.
