All links were active websites at the time of this writing, but may change over time. Some are single wordlists; some pages have numerous wordlists. Of these, the Ignis, Clem9669, and the Hashmob lists are my favorites. I feel these three are some of the best collections currently available. They also work extremely well in combination with other wordlists and with rules. Even though there is a new "Rock You 2021" wordlist out, I personally feel it is too large to be used for combination, hybrid or rules type attacks. Though if you want a good large wordlist, you can easily Google for it.
