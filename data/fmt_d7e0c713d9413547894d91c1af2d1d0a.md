+ * `/dev/rdsk/c0t3d02` 分区 map

* 维度:

  * 512 bytes/sector

  * 126 sectors/track

  * 4 tracks/cylinder

  * 504 sectors/cylinder

  * 4096 cylinders

* 标志:

  * 1: 不可挂载

  * 10: 只读
