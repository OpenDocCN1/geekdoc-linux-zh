一旦我们有了密钥文件，就可以创建 CA 证书。

```
[root@alpha CA]# openssl req -new -x509 -days 365 -key /etc/pki/CA/private/ca.pem -extensions v3 ca -out /etc/pki/CA/certs/ca.pem
Enter passphrase for /etc/pki/CA/private/ca.pem:
```
