- UNIX System Architecture by Prabhat K. Andleigh (Prentice-Hall, Inc., ISBN 0-13-949843-5).
- UNIX Shell Programming, revised edition, by Stephen G. Kochan and Patrick H. Wood (Hayden Books, 06-672-48448-X).
