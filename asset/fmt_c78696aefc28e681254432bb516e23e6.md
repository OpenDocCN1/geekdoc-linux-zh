
"The states of motion of nuclei and electrons in a molecule, or of electrons in an atom, are restricted by quantum mechanics to special forms with definite energies. The state of lowest energy is called the ground state; all others are excited states. In analogy to water levels, one speaks of energy levels. Excited states exist only momentarily, following an electrical or other stimulus. See QUANTUM CHEMISTRY; QUANTUM MECHANICS."

分子中核和电子的运动状态，或原子中电子的运动状态，受量子力学的限制，只能处于具有确定能量的特殊形式。最低能量状态称为基态；所有其他状态均为激发态。类比水位，人们称之为能级。激发态仅在受到电刺激或其他刺激后短暂存在。参见 [量子化学](https://example.org/quantum_chemistry)；[量子力学](https://example.org/quantum_mechanics)。 (Again, translating "See X" as "参见 X")

"Excitation of an atom consists of a change in the state of motion of its electrons. Electronic excitation of molecules can also occur, but alternatively or additionally, molecules can be excited to discrete states of vibration and rotation."

原子的激发是指其电子运动状态的变化。分子的电子激发也可以发生，但分子还可以被激发到振动和转动的离散状态，或者同时发生。

The quantities Eᵣ, Eₜₕ, and Eₕᵢₒ denote rotational, fine-structure, and hyperfine-structure energies. The last two appear as small or minute splittings of the rotation levels. The spacings ΔE of adjacent discrete levels of each type are usually in the order given in notation (2). The Eₕᵢₒ term in"

任意分子的总能量可表示为式 (1)。电子能量 `E₀` 和振动能量 `Eᵥ` 均可以是离散或连续的。量 `Eᵣ`、`Eₜₕ` 和 `Eₕᵢₒ` 分别表示转动、精细结构和超精细结构能量。后两者表现为转动能级的小或微小分裂。每类相邻离散能级的间隔 `ΔE` 通常遵循符号 (2) 中给出的顺序。式 (1) 中的 `Eₕᵢₒ` 项

$$E = E_0 + E_v + (E_r + E_{th} + E_{hio}) \quad (1)$$

$$\Delta E_{th} \gg \Delta E_r \gg \Delta E_v \gg \Delta E_{hio} \quad (2)$$

"Eq. (1) refers to additional fine structure which appears on subjecting molecules to external magnetic fields (Zeeman effect) or electric fields (Stark effect). See FINE STRUCTURE (SPECTRAL LINES); HYPERFINE STRUCTURE; STARK EFFECT; ZEEMAN EFFECT."

指的是当分子受到外部磁场（塞曼效应）或电场（斯塔克效应）作用时出现的额外精细结构。参见 [精细结构（谱线）](https://example.org/fine_structure)；[超精细结构](https://example.org/hyperfine_structure)；[斯塔克效应](https://example.org/stark_effect)；[塞曼效应](https://example.org/zeeman_effect)。

"Polyatomic molecules have much more complicated patterns of vibrational and (usually) rotational energy levels than diatomic molecules."

多原子分子具有比双原子分子复杂得多的振动能级和（通常的）转动能级模式。

### Subsection: Molecular spectra

**分子光谱。**

"The frequencies ν (*c* = speed of light) of electromagnetic spectra obey the Einstein-Bohr equation (3),"

电磁波谱的频率 `ν`（`*c*` = 光速）遵循爱因斯坦-玻尔方程 (3)，

$$hc\nu = E' - E'' \quad (3)$$

"where *h* is Planck’s constant. Molecular emission spectra accompany jumps in energy from higher to lower levels; absorption spectra accompany jumps from lower to higher levels. Molecular spectra can be classified as fine-state or low-frequency spectra, rotation spectra, vibration-rotation spectra, and electronic spectra. See ELECTRON PARAMAGNETIC RESONANCE (EPR) SPECTROSCOPY; MAGNETIC RESONANCE; MICROWAVE SPECTROSCOPY; MOLECULAR BEAMS; SPECTROSCOPY."

其中 `*h*` 是普朗克常数。分子发射光谱伴随能量从高能级向低能级的跃迁；吸收光谱伴随能量从低能级向高能级的跃迁。分子光谱可分类为精细状态或低频光谱、转动光谱、振动-转动光谱和电子光谱。参见 [电子顺磁共振（EPR）光谱](https://example.org/epr_spectroscopy)；[磁共振](https://example.org/magnetic_resonance)；[微波光谱](https://example.org/microwave_spectroscopy)；[分子束](https://example.org/molecular_beams)；[光谱学](https://example.org/spectroscopy)。

"Transitions between energy levels differing only in rotational state give rise to pure rotation spectra. These typically consist of a sequence of lines spaced almost equidistantly, and lying in the far infrared or the microwave region."

仅在转动状态不同的能级之间的跃迁产生纯转动光谱。这些光谱通常由一组间距几乎相等的谱线组成，位于远红外或微波区域。
