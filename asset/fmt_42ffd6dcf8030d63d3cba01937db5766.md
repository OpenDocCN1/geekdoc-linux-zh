## 10 初始化

```python
addon = xmcaddon.addon()

filtering = filter.getgetboolean("Filtering")
filter_threshold = filter.getinteger("FilterThreshold")
```

为了读取你类型的设置，你需要使用：

```python
filter = addon.getboolean("filter") == "true"
```
