
# 骨细胞与骨重建

骨中的原始细胞是那些产生成骨细胞和骨细胞的细胞，它们负责矿物质的重吸收（破骨细胞），并由多能性间充质细胞和骨髓形成。在适当的刺激下，这些原始细胞变得成熟并留在骨中；它们还能够分化为脂肪细胞（脂肪细胞）、肌肉细胞和软骨细胞（软骨）。骨细胞是并入骨骼的成骨细胞，居住在称为骨骨腔的空间内。它们通过骨中微小通道（骨小管）的延伸进入矿化骨，以便与其他骨细胞和内骨膜表面通信。因此，骨细胞处于理想位置，可以感知骨上的应力和载荷，并将信息传递给表面的成骨细胞，从而实现新骨的机械重建。成骨细胞也被认为是检测并引导骨中发生的机械损伤频率的细胞，用于修复骨中出现的裂纹和骨折，或者当这些损伤以可能导致结构性故障的速度累积时进行修复。正是这些蛋白质与胶原相互作用，且其中几种非胶原蛋白可以特异化。

骨中的 `primary cells` are those that result in `osteoblasts` and `osteocytes` and that are responsible for resorption of mineral (`osteoclasts`) and form from the multipotent stromal cells and the bone marrow. Under the appropriate stimuli, these primitive cells become mature and stay in the bone; they are also capable of differentiating into `adipocytes` (fat cells), muscle cells, and `chondrocytes` (cartilage). `Osteocytes`, which are `osteoblasts` that become incorporated into the bone, reside in spaces called `lacunae`. They reach into the mineralized bone with extensions through tiny channels (`canaliculi`) in the bone to communicate with other `osteocytes` and the endosteal surface. `Osteocytes` are therefore ideally placed to feel stresses and loads on the bone and to convey information to the `osteoblasts` on the surface, enabling the mechanical remodeling of new bone. `Osteoblasts` are also thought to be the cells that detect and direct the mechanical frequency of damage that occurs in bone, to repair the cracks and fractures that occur in bone, or when this damage accumulates at a rate that could cause the structural failure.
